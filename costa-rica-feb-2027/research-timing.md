## 2. Crowds, holidays, booking
- Feb is peak tourist traffic; "Book early"; coincides with local school holidays per https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/costa-rica-in-february/. CONFLICT: https://www.vacationscostarica.com/best-time-to-visit-weather/february/ says school resumes second week of Feb and describes Feb as mid-range pricing, "not as expensive as peak season".
- Lead time: 4-6 months advance recommended for best prices (vacationscostarica.com Feb page, same URL). Busy places named: Tamarindo, Jaco, La Fortuna, Manuel Antonio.
- Costa Rica 2027 official public holidays: 1 Jan, 25 Mar (Maundy Thu), 26 Mar (Good Fri), 11 Apr, 1 May, 25 Jul, 2 Aug, 15 Aug, 15 Sep, 25 Dec. NO national holidays in Feb 2027, none moved to Mondays. Sources: https://www.holidays-info.com/costa-rica/holidays/2027/ ; https://www.calendarlabs.com/holidays/costa-rica/2027 (via search summary).
- Canadian Family Day: Monday 15 Feb 2027 in AB, BC, NB, ON, SK. Source: https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/canada/family-day ; https://canada-holidays.ca/provinces/ON/2027. Expect more Canadian family demand around 13-15 Feb. GAP: no source quantifying the booking effect.
- Local events: Fiestas Civicas Liberia (late Feb); Puntarenas Carnival (typically last weekend, not held in 2024); Envision Festival near Uvita/Dominical (7 days, book a year ahead; dates for 2027 not verified; GAP). Sources: https://www.vacationscostarica.com/best-time-to-visit-weather/february/ ; https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/costa-rica-in-february/. Fishing tournaments at Los Suenos and Marina Pez Vela (mytanfeet).

## 3. Wildlife calendar for February
- Humpbacks: Ballena gets two populations. Northern (California/Alaska/BC) Dec-Mar/Apr, Jan-Feb "typically offering excellent sightings"; southern (Antarctic) Jul/Aug-Oct/Nov, Aug-Sep peak and most consistent. One source says "roughly equal chances of amazing encounters" in both. Sources: https://vamosrentacar.com/blog/marino-ballena-national-park/ ; https://www.visitcostarica.com/where-to-go/protected-areas/ballena-national-marine-park ; explorewithalec: "From December to March, California whales come down to mate and give birth. Then, from July to October, Antarctic whales..." https://explorewithalec.com/things-to-do-in-costa-rica/. Other Feb whale spots: Gulf of Papagayo, Catalinas, Drake Bay (https://www.vacationscostarica.com/best-time-to-visit-weather/february/). GAP: no numeric sighting probability found for Feb vs Aug-Oct; qualitative only. Also GAP: northern-pop counts are generally thought smaller than the southern; not verified by a source here.
- Turtles: Olive ridley arribada at Ostional is Aug-Dec (best Sep-Nov), so NOT Feb. Leatherbacks at Playa Grande mid-Oct to mid-Feb, peak Dec-Jan, Feb is the tail end. Source: https://www.entercostarica.com/travel-guide/popular-activities/turtle-nesting ; https://costa-rica.com/en/best-time-for-turtle-watching-in-costa-rica/ (search summary). Another source says leatherbacks and Pacific green turtles nest in Feb, esp. Guanacaste (https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/costa-rica-in-february/). GAP: Caribbean turtle season (green turtles at Tortuguero, Jul-Oct; leatherbacks Mar-Jun) not researched; Feb is outside both from my memory, unsourced.
- Quetzals: breeding mid-Feb into Jun/Jul; mid-Feb to April is peak viewing; late Feb-Apr recommended, March especially. Monteverde and San Gerardo de Dota/Savegre. Curi Cancha Reserve named as one of the best at Monteverde. Source: https://costa-rica-guide.com/nature/wildlife/resplendent-quetzals/ ; https://misticopark.com/blog/costa-rica-travel-tips/where-and-when-to-watch-quetzals-in-costa-rica/ ; https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/costa-rica-in-february/. Caveat: 13-24 Feb is the very start of breeding; later in Feb/March is better.
- Scarlet macaws: nest and are most visible in dry season Dec-Apr; Carara (north of Jaco) and Osa/Corcovado best; beach almond trees; early morning and late afternoon. Source: https://www.costaricafocus.com/best-places-for-spotting-macaws-in-costa-rica/ ; https://www.costarica.com/wildlife/scarlet-macaws.
- Sloths/monkeys: Corcovado/Osa has all four monkey species, tapir, sloths; dry season easier for wildlife spotting and river crossings. Source: https://www.adventure-life.com/costa-rica/articles/hiking-in-corcovado-national-park. GAP: no Feb-specific sloth/monkey data; no source on dry-season waterhole concentration.
- Migratory birds: Monteverde ~100 migratory species plus ~400 residents (https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/costa-rica-in-february/).
- Other Feb extras: manta rays at Catalinas and Monkey Head Rock; bioluminescence "one of the best months", Punta Cuchillos, Nicoya (https://www.vacationscostarica.com/best-time-to-visit-weather/february/).

## 5. Rio Celeste and Blue Falls
- Rio Celeste: best Dec-Apr; driest months Feb-Apr, Mar-Apr most reliable. Colour is lost by heavy rain sediment; "previous 24 hours matter more than the day itself". Another source: Feb "most sky blue" thanks to sunny weather and lower water. Sources: https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/the-enchanting-rio-celeste/ ; https://www.costaricadaytrip.com/guides/rio-celeste-hike ; https://www.vacationscostarica.com/best-time-to-visit-weather/february/ (search summaries).
- Blue Falls (Bajos del Toro): best Dec-Apr, Feb and Mar usually driest, water bluest in dry season; rainy season brings high, dangerous water. Sources: https://www.dirtyshoesandepicviews.com/blog/how-to-visit-blue-falls-bajos-del-toro-costa-rica ; https://www.sunchasingtravelers.com/bajos-del-toro/ ; https://www.blue-falls-of-costa-rica.com/warning/ (search summaries).
- GAP: explicit "lowest volume in Feb" not stated, but implied by dry-season language. Note Bajos del Toro and Rio Celeste can still get showers (Caribbean-influenced); the author's Feb trip saw heavy overnight rain raising flow (explorewithalec).
