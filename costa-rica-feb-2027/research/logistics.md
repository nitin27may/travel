Ruta 606 Monteverde mountain road narrow with drop-offs, not at night. ## 3. Car rental

- Mandatory TPL/SLI cannot be declined or covered by credit card. Vamos: TPL USD 15.95/day (sedan to fullsize), 17.95 premium, USD 2,000 deposit hold. Basic CDW USD 9/day (USD 750 deductible, USD 750 hold). Full CDW USD 18-21/day, zero deductible, USD 100 hold, glass and tires. Basic CDW + TPL = USD 24.95/day. https://vamosrentacar.com/car-rental-insurance-explained/
- Trap: online aggregator quotes of ~USD 10/day exclude TPL; generic TPL range USD 10-20/day, base rate USD 10-20 low season but USD 50-100+ in Dec-Apr, full CDW USD 30-40. https://drinkteatravel.com/renting-a-car-in-costa-rica-tips/ . Vamos "Elusive Rental Quotes Exposed": https://vamosrentacar.com/rental-quotes-exposed/ (not opened, GAP on content).
- Credit-card CDW: allowed in place of in-house CDW only with written proof letter naming renter and last 4 digits, covering vehicle class and duration. Vamos does not accept third-party internet insurance programs. https://vamosrentacar.com/car-rental-insurance-explained/ . ## 4. Entry and practicalities

- Passport: Costa Rican law one month beyond arrival (visitcostarica says valid and ICAO machine-readable); many airlines enforce 6 months. Canada's advice: valid for the entire stay. https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica , https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/entry-requirements , https://tamaluxury.com/entry-requirements-for-costa-rica/ (search-snippet on airline rule)
- Visa: none for Canadians, up to 180 days (stamp at arrival). https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica
- Onward ticket: required (return/onward ticket within 90 days; mandatory for one-way, return more than 90 days out, or exit via another country). Funds: USD 100 per month of stay. https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/entry-requirements . Open-jaw with a return flight from either airport satisfies it.
- Travel insurance: not an entry requirement since April 2022 (Covid rule removed); officers keep discretion to ask. Canada recommends comprehensive medical insurance. https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/costa-rica-required-travel-insurance/ (search-snippet)
- E-health forms: no current requirement found. GAP: not verified directly on Costa Rica government site; Covid-era health pass appears removed.
- Yellow fever: only if arriving from listed at-risk countries; new rule from 31 Aug 2026 applies to Costa Rican citizens and residents travelling to at-risk destinations, not inbound Canadians. https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/entry-requirements , https://www.fragomen.com/insights/costa-rica-updated-yellow-fever-vaccination-requirements-for-certain-costa-rican-citizens-and-residents-forthcoming.html
- Health advice: Hep A and B, routine immunizations (Canada); dengue, Zika, chikungunya present, protect against bites all day (dengue is a daytime biter); CDC Level 1. https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica , https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/International-Travel-Country-Information-Pages/CostaRica.html . Emergency 911; Canadian Embassy San Jose +506 2242-4400.
- Driver's licence: valid Canadian licence accepted for 180 days; carry passport (or copy with entry stamp). https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica
- Phones: Canadian roaming about CAD 15-16/day (Rogers/Bell/Telus); eSIM cheaper; Telus Easy Roam covers Costa Rica. GAP: Kolbi/Claro airport SIM prices unverified. https://esim.holafly.com/roaming/best-international-phone-plans-canada/ , https://www.telus.com/en/mobility/travel
- Power: Type A/B, 120V 60Hz, same as Canada, no adapter. https://esim.holafly.com/travel-tips/costa-rica-plug-type/
- Tap water: potable in Central Valley/SJO and most towns; use filtered/bottled on Caribbean coast, Osa; Guanacaste beach towns (Tamarindo, Coco, Hermosa) have dry-season shortages. GAP: Fortuna, Monteverde, Uvita, Manuel Antonio not individually confirmed. https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/costa-rica-water-safe-to-drink/ , https://ticotravel.com/costa-rica-drinking-water-where-to-trust-it-and-when-to-use-bottled/ (search-snippets)
- Money: restaurants add 10% service + 13% IVA (23% on menu price); extra 5-10% optional. USD widely accepted, change comes back in colones; ATMs dispense both; BAC and Promerica friendliest to foreign cards, about USD 5-10 fees; cards widely accepted. About 455 colones/USD (May 2026). Canada: USD easier to exchange than CAD. https://costaricatravelblog.com/tipping-in-costa-rica/ , https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/using-atms-credit-cards/ , https://costaricatravelblog.com/spending-money-in-costa-rica/ , https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica

## 6. Safety

- Rental-car break-ins are the most-reported tourist incident; happen at beaches, trailheads, roadside stops, national-park lots; rentals are recognisable (same models). Leave nothing visible, use paid guarded lots, never leave bags; flat-tyre "helper" scam. https://qcostarica.com/avoid-getting-your-car-broken-into-in-costa-rica/ , https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/safety-tips-costa-rica/ , https://drinkteatravel.com/is-costa-rica-safe/ (search-snippets)
- Canada advice: petty theft frequent; check door locks manually; swim with caution, riptides, lifeguards rare. https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica
- Riptides cause about 80% of 150-200 drownings a year. Playa Hermosa (Jaco) no lifeguards, fatalities incl. a Canadian in 2017; Jaco swim at lifeguard tower; Dominical 18 drownings 2001-2015, not recommended; Tamarindo rip currents plus estuary crocodiles; Manuel Antonio Playa Espadilla occasional rips, park beach calmer; Uvita rips common. Lifeguards: Manuel Antonio, Tamarindo, Bahia Ballena, Ventanas, Esterillos Oeste, Caldera. https://villascostarica.com/2011/04/costa-rica-safety-rip-tides/ , https://news.co.cr/the-most-dangerous-beaches-in-costa-rica/34316/ , https://jacocostarica.com/jaco/2024/can-you-swim-in-jaco-beach/ (search-snippets)
- Navigation: Waze is the local standard (live hazards, closures, police); Google Maps with offline download works without signal in remote areas; many run both. https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-travel-tips/apps-for-traveling-in-costa-rica/ , https://www.goingpuravida.com/googlemaps-or-waze.html (search-snippets); explorewithalec backs offline Google Maps. Check Transito closures before mountain days.
