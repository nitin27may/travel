# Costa Rica Feb 2027 - Adventure lane (raw data)

Collected 2026-10-06. Method: WebFetch of explorewithalec + WebSearch result summaries. Most figures below come from search-result summaries, NOT from reading the operator pages directly; treat as "reported 2026" and re-verify at booking. Prices USD; many operators add 13% IVA on top (noted where stated). Family composition/ages unknown, so age/weight limits are listed in full.

## 0. Author picks (explorewithalec)
Source: https://explorewithalec.com/things-to-do-in-costa-rica/
- Top 5: white water rafting, Nauyaca Waterfalls (near Uvita; arrive ~noon), Blue Falls hike (Bajos del Toro, $25 combo, 4-5 h, 1,350 ft gain), Sky Adventures zipline, full-day Manuel Antonio.
- Arenal: Sky Adventures (5 main lines, groups 10+), Arenal Rafting Company (Class 3/4 half day), Birding Fortuna night walk, Mistico hanging bridges, Tabacon $360/couple, Baldi $80.
- Pacific: Tamarindo surf lessons (2-3 h), Playa Potrero catamaran + snorkel with meal/sunset. Quepos mangrove kayak (morning better). Ballena Marino NP free per author (CONFLICT: other source says ~$18 foreigner fee, see 6). Prices were mostly "not specified" in the page.
- GAP: no prices for rafting/zip/surf on that page.

## 6. Uvita: whales and Cano
- Humpback (northern hemisphere population) season Dec-Apr, peak Jan-Feb. Tours $75-95 pp, 3-4 h: Marino Ballena Tours and Info $95, Localbird $85, Ballena Tour Costa Rica $85, another from $78. Park fee ~$18 foreigners, may or may not be included (author says free; CONFLICT). https://www.costarica-spirit.com/guides/uvita-whale-watching-season/ ; https://localbird.io/product/whale-watching-snorkeling-tour-uvita/ ; https://ballenatourcostarica.com/ ; https://www.getyourguide.com/uvita-costa-rica-l187790/uvita-marino-ballena-national-park-whale-watching-cruise-t859712/

## 7. Surfing (Feb)
- Feb = dry season, offshore winds, waist to head high; Tamarindo beginner/intermediate friendly, water 27-28C, Papagayo winds Dec-Apr; Playa Hermosa (Jaco) is most consistent Apr-Oct, Dec-Feb gets less north swell; Dominical suits advanced, winter offshore barrels. https://www.costarica-spirit.com/guides/surf-seasons-by-region/ ; https://www.witchsrocksurfcamp.com/costa-rica-surf-calendar ; https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/best-surf-spots-in-costa-rica
- Lesson prices: GAP (search returned none). Beginner spots near Jaco/Dominical (Playa Hermosa is a powerful beach break, not beginner): GAP.
- Safety: rip currents kill more tourists than ziplines (Outside article above).
