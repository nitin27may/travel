## Author view: explorewithalec (read first)
Source: https://explorewithalec.com/things-to-do-in-costa-rica/
- Best hike of whole trip: Blue Falls + Catarata del Toro (Bajos del Toro). Far off beaten path, little English. Catarata del Toro 1.7 mi RT, 540 ft gain, steep stairs ~1.5x normal height. Blue Falls combo 4.5 mi, 1,350 ft gain (La Pintada, Tepezquintle, Las Gemelas falls). Combo $25.
- Mirador El Silencio: "nice, but nothing overly special", ~3.1 mi RT, poor visibility on their day.
- La Fortuna waterfall: skipped (time/weather); >100,000 visitors/yr; go early.
- Manuel Antonio: wildlife "endless", guide essential for spotting; park closes 4pm, rangers clear beaches by 3:15pm.
- Ballena (Uvita): Whale's Tail seen at low tide; stingrays, macaws.
- Nauyaca: top-five activity; shuttle $38 pp due to steep road; arrive ~noon.
- Skipped: Rio Celeste, Monteverde (says similar experience near La Fortuna; 3-4 h off route), Rincon (last erupted 2017 per author, now outdated), Corcovado (full-day commitment, multi-hour travel from Uvita).
- Not mentioned: quetzals, Tortuguero.

## 1. Arenal Volcano NP (La Fortuna)
- Fee: $15 + 13% tax = ~$17 foreign adult; child $5 + tax. Source (snippet): https://wellness.alibaba.com/outdoor/arenal-volcano-entrance-fee-guide ; base $15 at https://costaricatravelblog.com/costa-rica-entrance-fees/
- Hours (snippets): 8:00-16:00 daily, last entry 15:30 (one source); another: Volcan sector last entry 14:30 close 16:00; Peninsula sector open 8-18. Source: https://ecoterracostarica.com/arenal-national-park-guide-2026/ , https://www.costarica-spirit.com/guides/arenal-volcano-national-park/ . CONFLICT; GAP on last entry.
- Trails: Los Miradores 1.2-2.4 km (sources disagree); Las Coladas 1.8 km one way; El Ceibo; park page says two short trails 3.4 km and 2 km. Source: https://www.arenal.net/arenal-volcano-national-park ; https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/hiking-in-arenal-national-park/
- Volcano dormant since 2010: no lava glow, views of cone and old lava. Source: https://www.arenal.net/arenal-volcano-national-park
- Cerro Chato (crater lake) closed since 2017, still closed as of Apr 2026. Source: https://ecoterracostarica.com/arenal-national-park-guide-2026/
- Online booking: costaricatravelblog says not required (https://costaricatravelblog.com/costa-rica-entrance-fees/). GAP: confirm on SINAC.
- Elevation gain, guide requirement, capacity: GAP (not found).
- Arenal 1968 (private reserve, not SINAC): Lava Flow 1968 trail 2.5 km (1.5-2 h), Forest 1968 4.5-4.7 km (2-2.5 h), combined ~7 km; $25 adult, $15 child 6-11; 8-17, last entry 16. Source (snippet): https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/arenal-1968-trail/ ; AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/costa-rica/alajuela/arenal-1968-volcan-sendero-bosque (title shows "[PRIVATE PROPERTY]" tag; verify access/fee before going). Elevation: GAP.
- Mirador El Silencio (private): ~$10 pp (older figure); 8-17 daily; ~3.1 mi RT loop per Alec. Sources: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/costa-rica/alajuela/mirador-el-silencio-circuito-completo ; https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g309226-d10033998-Reviews-Mirador_El_Silencio-La_Fortuna_de_San_Carlos_Arenal_Volcano_National_Park_Provin.html . Fee review titled "Expensive entrance fee" so GAP: current price.
- Feb: dry season but Arenal cone often cloud-covered; poor visibility on Alec's day.

## 2. La Fortuna Waterfall
- Fee $18 adult (range $18-20 across sources); child 5-11 $5; under 5 free. Hours 7:30-16:00 (another source 7-17). ~500 steps down, 15-20 min down, 76 m (246 ft) fall, swimming allowed in marked area. Sources: https://www.arenal.net/la-fortuna-waterfall-costa-rica ; https://www.costaricavibes.com/la-fortuna-waterfall/
- Feb dry season = optimal, reduced slick steps. Closed days: none found. Booking: not required (GAP, not explicitly stated).
- Elevation figure: ~500 steps only. Distance from town: short drive.

## 3. Rio Celeste / Tenorio Volcano NP
- Fee: $12 (+IVA ~ $13.56 foreign adult); child 2-12 $5. Sources: https://mytanfeet.com/activities/tips-visiting-rio-celeste/ ; search snippet quoting 13.56.
- Hours: 8:00-16:00 daily, last entry 13:45 (mytanfeet) or 14:00 (unusualnomad); must exit by 16:00. Online timed ticket REQUIRED, none at gate. Sources: https://mytanfeet.com/activities/tips-visiting-rio-celeste/ ; https://unusualnomad.com/how-to-hike-rio-celeste-trail-tenorio-volcano/
- Trail: ~6-7 km round trip, 3-4 h, moderate (mud, roots, stairs). Waterfall stairs 253 steps. Elevation gain: 413 ft (~126 m) is for waterfall segment per AllTrails-sourced snippet (2 mi); whole-route gain GAP. AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/costa-rica/alajuela/catarata-rio-celeste
- CLOSURE: since Aug 2025 landslide, descent to waterfall viewpoint closed/partly closed (still partial as of March 2026). Waterfall viewable from above only. Sources: https://mytanfeet.com/activities/tips-visiting-rio-celeste/ ; https://unusualnomad.com/how-to-hike-rio-celeste-trail-tenorio-volcano/ . GAP: status for Feb 2027; check park Facebook.
- Blue colour in Feb: dry season Dec-Apr best; brown only after heavy rain. mytanfeet says stays blue in typical Feb rains, loses colour only in severe storms. Sources: https://mytanfeet.com/activities/tips-visiting-rio-celeste/ ; https://ticotimes.net/2015/04/21/rio-celeste-dont-it-make-my-brown-river-blue . Caveat: Feb on Caribbean-slope side can still bring rain; unverified reliability percentage = GAP.
- Swimming prohibited inside park. Guide not required. Standard car OK, ~1.5 h from La Fortuna. Source: unusualnomad / mytanfeet above.

## 4. Rincon de la Vieja NP
- Fee: $15 (some sources $16-17) adult; child $5. All tickets online in advance. Sources: https://costaricatravelblog.com/costa-rica-entrance-fees/ ; https://ticotimes.net/2026/07/27/rincon-de-la-vieja-volcano-alert-level
- Las Pailas sector: Tue-Sun 8:00-15:00, last entry 14:00; closed Monday. Source: https://rincondelaviejapark.com/las-pailas-trail/ ; https://drinkteatravel.com/rincon-de-la-vieja-national-park-costa-rica/
- Las Pailas loop: 3 km (AllTrails 2.4 mi), easy, 1-1.5 h; fumaroles, boiling mud pots, volcanito. AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/costa-rica/guanacaste/fumarolic-lagoon-and-water-pots-loop . Elevation: GAP.
- Waterfall (Catarata Escondida/La Cangreja): 10.2 km RT; must enter before 12:00 (one source). "Oropendola waterfall": GAP, not found in any source (Oropendola is not named in results; the Santa Maria sector has other falls, unverified).
- ALERT: major phreatic eruption Apr 2026; alert raised to level 3 "caution" 24 Jul 2026 after 38 phreatic eruptions since 2 Jul, three produced lahars on north flank. Most trails open including Las Pailas. Active crater trail closed since 2011. Grey water in Azul/Penjamo rivers = lahar warning. Sources: https://ticotimes.net/2026/07/27/rincon-de-la-vieja-volcano-alert-level ; https://www.alltrails.com/trail/costa-rica/alajuela/crater-volcan-rincon-de-la-vieja ("[CLOSED]"). GAP: status in Feb 2027.
- Guide requirement, capacity: GAP.

## 5. Llanos de Cortes
- $7 adult 13+, $4 child 7-12, free <=6; cash; includes parking. 8:00-16:00, last entry 15:30. ~5 min walk from car park (some steps), swimming in rope area with lifeguard. Source: https://vamosrentacar.com/blog/llanos-de-cortes-waterfall/ ; https://costaricatravelblog.com/llanos-del-cortes-waterfall/ . Feb dry season flow lower: GAP (not stated). Closed days: GAP.

## 6. Bajos del Toro
- Combo (Blue Falls + Catarata del Toro) $25 adult, $12.50 child 4-12; Blue Falls only $20; Catarata del Toro alone ~$15. Blue Falls open Mon-Sat 7-17, SUNDAY CLOSED, last entry 16:00; no reservations, cards accepted; free parking; combo ~4 h. Source: https://www.blue-falls-of-costa-rica.com/prices/ ; https://www.catarata-del-toro.com/ticket/blue-falls-of-costa-rica/
- Catarata del Toro: 1.7 mi RT, 540 ft gain, steep stairs. Blue Falls: 4.5 mi, 1,350 ft gain. Source: https://explorewithalec.com/blue-falls-costa-rica-catarata-del-toro/ (also the Alec main article).
- Road: Route 708 north approach paved, hilly, foggy, narrow, some large potholes; any car OK but SUV more comfortable. Source: https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/bajos-del-toro/ ; search snippets.
- GAP: Catarata del Toro own hours/closed days (assumed same operator pattern, unverified).

## 7. Monteverde / Santa Elena
- Feb is dry/windy season: wind and fine mist, 22-26 C day, 13-17 C night, less lush. Source: https://www.touristinmonteverde.com/monteverde-costa-rica-weather ; https://blog.ocoteahotel.com/a-year-in-monteverde-what-to-expect-from-each-month ; https://www.whereandwhen.net/when/central-america/costa-rica/monteverde/february/ . Locals call early-Feb wind-mist season beautiful; wind can limit zip/bridge visibility (GAP, no source on bridge closures).
- Santa Elena Reserve: $18 adult 13+, $9 student/child 4-12; 7:00-16:00 daily; walk-in OK. Source: https://vamosrentacar.com/blog/santa-elena-cloud-forest-reserve/ ; https://www.22places.com/santa-elena-cloud-forest-reserve/ (fee/hours from search snippet)
- Selvatura hanging bridges self-guided: $49 adult, $44 student, $34 child 4-11; 6 bridges 157-774 ft long, up to 164 ft high. Source: https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/product/selvatura-hanging-bridges-self-guided/ ; https://www.paradisecatchers.com/hanging-bridges-monteverde-costa-rica/
- Hanging bridges comparison with Arenal-area bridges (Mistico etc.): GAP, not researched with a source. Alec says Monteverde-type experiences exist near La Fortuna.

## 8. Manuel Antonio NP
- Closed TUESDAY; open Wed-Mon (hours per sources 7:00-15:00 or 7:00-16:00; beaches close 15:00 per thoroughlytravel). CONFLICT on closing time. Sources: https://www.thoroughlytravel.com/manuel-antonio-national-park/ ; https://www.ict.go.cr/en/top-news/1870-manuel-antonio-to-open-mondays-beginning-april-1.html ; Alec says park closes 4pm.

Sources: https://www.thoroughlytravel.com/manuel-antonio-national-park/ ; https://www.earthtrekkers.com/manuel-antonio-national-park/

- Feb: dry season, best weather, crowds peak.

## 10. Ballena Marine NP (Uvita)
- $6 + tax foreign adult; under 12 and 65+ free. Hours 7:00-16:00 (some sectors to 18:00); Whale's Tail only visible at low tide, which shifts ~1 h/day and sometimes falls outside open hours. Humpback seasons Aug-Oct and Dec-Mar, so Feb is whale season. Sources: https://costaricadivers.com/blog/destinations/whale-tail-marino-ballena-national-park/ ; https://mytanfeet.com/costa-rica-national-park/marino-ballena-national-park-in-costa-rica/ ; official page https://www.sinac.go.cr/EN-US/ac/acosa/pnmb/Pages/default.aspx (not fetched). Closed days: GAP. Booking: not required per costaricatravelblog fee page.

## 11. Corcovado and Isla del Cano
- Corcovado: guide mandatory (ICT-certified naturalist) since 2014; advance SINAC permit; fee $15 base. Sirena day trip from Drake Bay: ~1 h boat each way, 4-5 h walking, ~7.5 h total, ~08:00-16:00; book 2+ weeks ahead Dec-Apr. Sirena day capacity cited ~500, overnight ~80 (figures from tour blogs). Park closes all of October. Sources: https://www.gocorcovado.com/how-to-visit-corcovado-national-park/ ; https://www.gocorcovado.com/corcovado-sirena-station-day-trip/ ; https://www.costarica-spirit.com/guides/corcovado-national-park/
- From Uvita: private Sirena tour ~9 h incl 2.5 h boat RT and ~3 h hiking: https://osatravels.com/en/tour/private-tour-to-corcovado-sirena-station-from-uvita/ . Drive Uvita to Drake Bay 106 km (~2 h 46 m) per snippet, 141 km per distancesfrom. Source: https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Uvita-Station/Drake-Bay-Costa-Rica ; https://www.distancesfrom.com/cr/distance-from-Uvita-to-Drake-Bay-Costa-Rica/DistanceHistory/17278158.aspx
- Assessment (inference, not sourced): realistic only with a Drake Bay overnight or a private Uvita boat day; otherwise skip in a short trip.
- Isla del Cano: reserve 8:00-16:00, fee $15 (some say $10); tours $145-200 pp, ~1.5 h boat from Uvita, ~30 min from Drake Bay. Source: https://www.entercostarica.com/attractions/national-parks-and-refuges/cano-island ; https://www.getyourguide.com/uvita-costa-rica-l187790/uvita-snorkeling-tour-in-the-isla-del-cano-biological-reserve-t860114/ . Weekly closure: GAP (none found; tours listed Mon-Sat). Feb sea conditions: GAP.

## 12. Savegre / Chirripo / quetzals
- Quetzal breeding: mid-Feb into June/July (wild avocado fruiting); Feb-May many nesting pairs; one source says Apr-Jun is easier. Savegre/San Gerardo de Dota considered best, drive ~2 h from San Jose via Cerro de la Muerte, potholed sections. Dec-Apr driest; avg ~20 C (nights cold, GAP actual lows). Sources: https://misticopark.com/blog/costa-rica-travel-tips/where-and-when-to-watch-quetzals-in-costa-rica/ ; https://www.paradisecatchers.com/san-gerardo-de-dota-quetzal/ ; https://www.thelatinamericatravelcompany.com/destinations/costa-rica/places-to-visit/san-gerardo-de-dota/
- GAP: guided quetzal tour price, Savegre Hotel reserve entry fee, Los Quetzales NP fee ($10 per costaricatravelblog) specifics.
- Rio Chirripo hike (Cerro Chirripo): permits 90 days ahead 8am CR time via sinac.go.cr/chirripo; 40 hikers/day; Dec-Apr fills within hours; park ~$18-22/day; Crestones lodge ~$30-35/night. Source: https://jameskaiser.com/costa-rica-guide/hiking-chirripo/ ; https://www.costarica-spirit.com/guides/chirripo-national-park/ . Needs 2-3 days: not short-trip friendly (inference). Feb 2027 permits would open ~Nov 2026.
- Rio Savegre rafting/other options: GAP.

## 13. Tortuguero
- Turtles not in Feb (user-supplied; nesting Jul-Oct green; unverified here). Canal boat tours 2-3 h morning, $35-75 pp. Park fee ~$15-17 foreign adult, child $5; advance SINAC online ticket. Source: https://vamosrentacar.com/blog/tortuguero-national-park-guide/ ; https://www.viator.com/tours/Tortuguero/Canal-Tour/d50227-143776P1
- Access: no road. Self-drive to La Pavona (via Route 32, Guapiles, Cariari, Route 247), parking ~$10/day, boats 6:00, 7:30, 9:00, 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 16:30, 4000 CRC, 1-1.25 h. Sources: https://www.costaricavibes.com/transportation-to-tortuguero/ ; https://lapavonatortuguero.com/en/ ; https://costaricatravelblog.com/tortuguero-transportion-la-pavona-or-cano-blanco-dock/ . Feb is on the Caribbean side so rain possible: GAP.
