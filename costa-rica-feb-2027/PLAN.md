# Costa Rica, February 2027 — plan

Party: two adults and a son aged 10. Open-jaw: fly into Liberia (LIR), out of San Jose (SJO).
Main plan **10 nights, Sat 13 – Tue 23 Feb 2027**. Alternative **8 nights, Sat 13 – Sun 21 Feb** (drops Uvita).
Source files: `research-*.md` in this folder. Everything priced here is a 2026 figure — no Feb 2027 quote exists for anything.

## Architecture rules

1. Never drive Ruta 2, Ruta 606 or the Monteverde road at night. Sunset is ~17:45; be at the next bed by 17:00.
2. Shut days decide the order (see closure table). Manuel Antonio is shut **Tuesday**; Las Pailas is shut **Monday**; Blue Falls shut Sunday.
3. Whale's Tail is walkable at low tide only, so Uvita days are fixed by the tide table, not by preference.
4. Nothing on the route needs a 4x4. A compact SUV is the sensible rental.
5. One big activity per day for a ten-year-old. The two long drives (Arenal to Manuel Antonio, Uvita to SJO) carry nothing else.
6. Anything he is near a limit on (140 cm tubing, 10 kg to 40 kg harness) gets measured and weighed before booking.

## Getting there and around

- Flights: Air Canada, Air Transat, WestJet, Porter nonstop. Kayak typical return YYZ–LIR C$522–848, YYZ–SJO C$622–1,004, Feb ~10% above average. Open-jaw price GAP. Exit tax $29 (may not be included).
- Car: third-party liability is mandatory and cannot be covered by a credit card ($15.95/day at Vamos). Card CDW covers the deductible only, needs a letter, and any agency CDW voids it. Open-jaw drop fee ~$50 (Adobe waives it for 3+ days). Roughly $880 for 10 days.
- Entry: Canadians need no visa (up to 180 days); onward ticket rule applies; no e-health form; travel insurance no longer required. migracion.go.cr not read (GAP).
- Canadian Family Day is Mon 15 Feb 2027: expect the outbound weekend to be busy. Costa Rica has no national holidays in Feb 2027.

## Drive times

LIR to Guachipelin 34 km, 45–60 min (unpaved access, length GAP). Rincon to La Fortuna ~155 km, 3–3.5 h. La Fortuna to Manuel Antonio ~215–245 km, 4–6 h. Manuel Antonio to Uvita ~64 km, 1 h 10–1 h 25. Uvita to SJO 3.5–4 h via Ruta 34 and 27 (not Ruta 2 on a flight day). La Fortuna to Rio Celeste ~1.5 h.

## Decisions and why

- 10 nights main, not 8: Uvita is where the tide, whales and macaws are; 8 costs all three.
- Skipped Monteverde: the extra 3 h each way buys cloud forest the family can get nearer.
- Rio Celeste is a bonus, not a plan: the waterfall trail has been shut since mid-2025.
- Sky Trek and balsa float are alternatives on day 5, not both.
- Tabacon only as an evening soak; the rest of the springs come free with the hotel.
