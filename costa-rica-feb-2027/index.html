Before the days start

What this trip physically is

Costa Rica is a narrow country with a spine of volcanoes. Rincón de la Vieja and Arenal are both active-arc volcanoes, which is why there are hot rivers and steaming ground in the north and why the springs here are fed by the mountain, not heated in a tank.

The route is a line down the country, not a loop: fly into Liberia (LIR), fly out of San José (SJO), and drive about 600 km between. February is the dry season on the Guanacaste side and around La Fortuna. The Pacific south is not especially dry — Uvita sees roughly 60 to 150 mm over a week in February, so pack a rain layer for the last leg.

What is specific here is the humpback season. Whales come up from the Northern Hemisphere between December and April, and Ballena Marine National Park is where the Whale’s Tail sandbar shows at low tide. That, not the beaches, is the reason the plan ends in Uvita.

Costa Rica sells itself on adventure and nature, and both are here. The skill is choosing: with a ten-year-old, one big activity a day, and two long drives that carry nothing.

Rincón de la Vieja

Nights 1–2

Active volcano, steaming ground at Las Pailas and rivers warm enough to bathe in. The easy start, 45 minutes from the airport.

La Fortuna & Arenal

The adventure base: canyoning, a balsa float, ziplines and a night in the springs. Arenal has not glowed since 2010.

Manuel Antonio

Nights 6–7

Rainforest that runs down onto a beach, with sloths and capuchins on the path. Shut on Tuesdays.

Uvita & Ballena

Nights 8–10

The Whale’s Tail sandbar, humpbacks offshore and scarlet macaws in the almond trees.

Driving

  • Third-party liability is mandatory and a credit card cannot cover it.
  • Card CDW covers the deductible only, needs a letter naming the renter and last 4 digits, and any agency CDW voids it. Scotiabank caps at 48 days and excludes trucks and vans.
  • Compact SUV is the common choice; nothing on the route needs a 4x4.
  • Never Ruta 2, Ruta 606 or the Monteverde road at night.

Entry

Canadians need no visa for up to 180 days and an onward ticket may be asked for. The e-health form was repealed on 1 April 2022 and travel insurance is no longer required (travel.gc.ca, 31 Aug 2026). GAP: migracion.go.cr not read.
