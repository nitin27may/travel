### 1.2 Children
- Official exempt list includes "Ninos de 0 a 6 anos inclusive" (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/131189). An earlier news piece said under-5 (https://www.anbariloche.com.ar/noticias/2026/05/31/107272-a-partir-de-este-1-de-junio-se-actualizan-las-tarifas-de-ingreso-a-los-parques-nacionales); the official page says 0-6, use that.
- GAP: no official child/teen rate for ages 7-17 found. The "student" column requires Argentine DNI and a student certificate issued within 12 months (https://www.infoviajera.com/?p=173325), so a foreign 11-year-old almost certainly pays the full General rate. This is my inference, not stated by a source. Assume full price for children aged 11+ in budgeting.

### 1.3 What the ticket covers, validity, and where to buy
- Official wording is only "Pase diario", "Flexipass 3 dias", "Flexipass 7 dias". The page gives no explicit validity text for the daily pass (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/131189). GAP: explicit validity rules for daily pass not found; a daily pass is presumably one calendar day.
- Second-day discount: 50% off the second day, valid within 72 hours of first entry. In Tierra del Fuego it applies only to tickets bought on the web sale (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/131189).
- Flexipass: described by iProfesional as personal, non-transferable, for ONE park, valid 6 months; pay 2 days get 3, pay 3.5 days get 7 (https://www.iprofesional.com/turismo/454919-aumentan-las-entradas-a-parques-nacionales-cuanto-cuesta-ir-a-cataratas-glaciares-y-mas). Arithmetic check: 100,000 = 2 x 50,000; 175,500 = 3.5 x 50,000. Consistent. GAP: whether Flexipass days must be consecutive not found.
- Annual pass: ARS 300,000, all parks, 12 months, online only, equal to 5 days of the applicable category (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/131189).
- Where to buy: official online portal https://ventaweb.apn.gob.ar (named on the official page). Bring passport to prove category at entry (same source).
- Online purchase beforehand: the El Chalten sector requires a ticket bought online, shown digitally (QR) at booths at Portal Los Condores, Portal Base Fitz Roy and Portal Rio Electrico (2024 info, https://www.iprofesional.com/turismo/416143-el-chalten-comenzo-cobrar-para-acceder-a-senderos-cuanto-sale-entrada ; charging began 21 Oct 2024). Card only / QR per https://nomadicsamuel.com/travel-blog/mirador-de-los-condores-hike-guide-short-steep-sunset-classic . Recommend buying online in advance because there is no mobile signal on the trails (https://thephotohikes.com/laguna-torre-hike/ ). Perito Moreno glacier: ticket can be bought online or at the checkpoint on the road about 45 km from El Calafate (https://thephotohikes.com/perito-moreno-glacier-walkways/ ).
- Does one Los Glaciares ticket cover both the glacier (south) and El Chalten (north)? GAP: official page lists them as separate rows with identical prices and does not say whether one ticket covers both zones (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/node/131189 ; same unresolved in https://www.infoviajera.com/?p=173325). Treat as two separate daily entries until confirmed on ventaweb. The Flexipass might be a way to cover both zones; GAP unverified.

## 2. EL CHALTEN

General notes
- Wind: "in the austral summer (Dec 21 - Mar 20) days without wind are rare" (https://www.elchalten.com/v4/en/the-weather-in-el-chalten.php via search snippet). Gusts matter more than the average (https://nomadicsamuel.com/travel-blog/hiking-safety-in-el-chalten-weather-navigation-when-turn-around). Calm morning in town can mislead once exposed.
- Mobile signal: often none on trail; plan offline maps (same nomadicsamuel article; https://thephotohikes.com/laguna-torre-hike/).
- Season/crowds: Nov-Mar is high season and most crowded; Oct and April quieter; early to mid April strongest autumn colours (https://thephotohikes.com/el-chalten-hiking-landscape-photography-guide/). Another guide: lenga colour peak late March to early April; "first week of April usually strong bet" (https://thephotohikes.com/laguna-torre-hike/ , search snippet ursulasweeklywanders.com). So 26 Feb - 11 Mar is just BEFORE peak colour; expect the first yellows/reds at best (my inference). Weather often more stable from late March into early April (photohikes guide).
- Services wind down from mid-April (search snippet, nomadic sources).
- Visitor centre of Los Glaciares is at the village entrance; check trail conditions there (https://www.elchalten.com/v4/en/laguna-torre-trek-el-chalten.php).
- Town dogs may follow hikers; rangers ask you not to let them (nomadicsamuel safety article).
- Trailhead naming: "Fitz Roy trailhead" = north end of Av. San Martin (parking lot, park dome/sign), serves Laguna Capri, Laguna de los Tres, Chorrillo del Salto start. Laguna Torre trailhead = west side of town (behind Hotel Los Cerros area / Calle Riquelme), coordinates 49.3314 S, 72.8931 W per https://thephotohikes.com/laguna-torre-hike/ . Visitor centre trailhead = Condores, Aguilas, Pliegue Tumbado, Huemul circuit start. Other coordinates: GAP, not verified; use the Google Maps queries below.
- Official trail table for Los Glaciares north zone: GAP. The argentina.gob.ar Los Glaciares senderos page returned 404 and the parquesnacionales search returned no official trail pages. All trail figures below are guides/blogs. Official figures are therefore unverified.

### 2.2 Per-trail detail

#### Chorrillo del Salto
- Distance: 6.4 km / 4 mi RT (https://www.switchbacktravel.com/el-chalten-hikes); 7 km / 4.3 mi (https://thephotohikes.com/el-chalten-hiking-landscape-photography-guide/ , https://www.elchalten.com/v4/en/chorrillo-del-salto-trek-el-chalten.php); "less than 4 km from town" one way (same elchalten page). Time 1h45-2h15 (photohikes) or about 3 h (elchalten.com). Gain about 70 ft / 21 m (switchback). Easy.
- Trailhead: north end of Av. San Martin, right-hand path then Ruta 41 gravel, signs on the right for a bike trail (do not enter), left at next sign. Car park exists near the final stretch; bikes stop at the car park; last section foot only (elchalten.com page above).
- From town: walk from trailhead.
- OB. Water/toilets: GAP not stated.
- See: 20+ m waterfall in ñire/lenga forest; orchids in season (elchalten.com).
- Hazards: walks beside Ruta 41 and a gravel road for a stretch (switchback).
- Light: midday to early afternoon for sun through trees (photohikes).
- Season: easy anytime; crowds moderate. Autumn colour starts late March.
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Chorrillo%20del%20Salto (GAP: not verified). Official: GAP. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Chorrillo+del+Salto+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: yes; good rest-day or arrival-day hike.

#### Laguna Torre (and Mirador Maestri)
- Distance: 17.8 km / 11.1 mi (photohikes, https://thephotohikes.com/laguna-torre-hike/); 20.1 km / 12.5 mi (switchback); about 19 km / 11.8 mi (elchalten.com Torre page); 18 km / 11 mi (guide listings via search). Video claim 11 mi: matches the lower range.
- Gain: 400 m / 1,312 ft (photohikes); 1,700 ft = 518 m (switchback); about 240 m / 800 ft (elchalten.com); other snippets 250-510 m. Video claim 1,600 ft = 488 m: within range, upper end. Honest range 250-520 m; most precise-sounding tracks say about 400 m. Max altitude 660 m (photohikes).
- Time: 5-6.5 h (photohikes) to 7-8 h (elchalten.com). Moderate.
- Trailhead: west side of town, 49.3314 S 72.8931 W; 15 min walk from most of town, 20 from bus terminal; unofficial parking 15-20 cars (photohikes). Switchback says reach via black metal staircase from Av. Rojo to Calle Riquelme. Two trails from town merge within about 5 min (elchalten.com).
- OB. Water/toilets: GAP none stated; carry all water. De Agostini campground at about halfway has camping (elchalten.com).
- See: Cerro Torre with ice mushroom, Glaciar Torre, Glaciar Grande, small icebergs; first viewpoint about 15 min in over Rio Fitz Roy gorge; Mirador Torre at halfway (elchalten.com, photohikes).
- Mirador Maestri: from moraine crest continue about 2 km along ridge (elchalten.com). Photohikes gives no distance. Trail is closed beyond the lagoon's lower end (photohikes).
- Hazards: steep rocky moraine final climb, rocky riverbed-like section, remote GLOF warning boards, river runs high and fast, no mobile signal (photohikes, switchback).
- Light: sunrise from Mirador Torre or lagoon; granite walls glow red for a few minutes at sunrise (elchalten.com). Late Feb/Mar sunrise about 07:20-07:40 (computed), so a start at 03:30-04:30 for sunrise. Otherwise start before 8 a.m. (elchalten.com); light lasts to mid-afternoon (photohikes).
- Season: crowds very high in Feb; better in March. Early April peak colour.
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Laguna%20Torre%20El%20Chalten (not verified, 403). Official: GAP. Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Laguna+Torre+trailhead+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: probably, if used to 5-7 h days. Flat for most of the way; steepest part is the first 3 km (photohikes: "most gain in the first 2.9 km").

#### Laguna Capri and Mirador Fitz Roy
- Distance: 5 mi / 8 km, gain 700 ft / 213 m, "less than two hours" one way (switchback). 5.4 mi, 1,348 ft / 411 m, 2.5-3.5 h (10adventures via search snippet). elchalten.com: 8 km, 4-5 h, "best light in the morning" (https://www.elchalten.com/v4/en/laguna-capri-trek-el-chalten.php). Komoot Mirador Fitz Roy + Capri loop 11.7 km, 3.5 h, 390 m (search snippet).
- Trailhead: end of Av. San Martin, near park dome; steep first kilometre.
- Facilities: outhouse and camping at lake shore (switchback). See: Fitz Roy over lagoon, ñire/lenga forest, Mirador Rio de las Vueltas.
- Hazards: steep start. Snow on final stretch may close it April-Oct (elchalten.com). Also the natural return leg for Laguna de los Tres day.
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Laguna%20Capri%20El%20Chalten (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Laguna+Capri+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: yes. Best easy "Fitz Roy view" alternative on a poor-weather or tired day.

#### Laguna de los Tres (and variants)
- Distance: 13 mi / 20.9 km (switchback); 21 km / 13 mi (https://triptins.com/laguna-de-los-tres-fitz-roy); 22 km / 13.67 mi (photohikes); 28 km (Chalten Mountain Guides snippet, outlier). Video claim "12-13 mi": OK.
- Gain: 3,100 ft / 945 m (switchback); +1,103 m / 3,620 ft (triptins); about 800 m (guides). The last 2.4 km climbs about 450 m / 1,475 ft (triptins). Video "1,300 ft final scramble": plausible for the last pitch (switchback: "steep scree climb of over 1,200 feet") but total gain is 2.5-3x that. It is a steep loose-rock climb, not a technical scramble; triptins says "no scramble described" and advises poles and slow descent.
- Time: 7-10 h incl. rests (triptins); 7h30-8h30 (photohikes); 8-10 h guides. Hard.
- Trailhead: Fitz Roy trailhead, north end Av. San Martin. Alternative one-way from El Pilar (needs transport) per triptins; Howlanders offers El Pilar -> Poincenot as a 2-day route.
- Route: Mirador Fitz Roy, then Rio Blanco crossing is a bridge and warning sign appears after it (triptins), Poincenot campsite at base of final climb (booking required, paid).
- Variants: (a) Return via Laguna Capri (recommended by triptins). (b) Piedras Blancas detour: about 2 km each way, about +4 km (triptins). (c) Laguna Sucia spur from Poincenot: 4.55 km / 2.8 mi, 1.5-2 h, quieter (photohikes). (d) Via Rio Electrico/El Pilar one-way.
- Toilets/water: not stated; Poincenot camp has facilities (inference, GAP verify).
- Hazards: exposed slope; steep loose final climb; weather changes quickly; choose a clear, low-wind day (switchback, triptins).
- Light: sunrise glow ("Fitz Roy fire") needs 4-4.5 h pre-dawn start or a Poincenot night. Computed sunrise 07:15-07:40; sunrise light on the east face is the drama, so a pre-dawn start of about 03:00-03:30 from town. Triptins says "5:30 to 7:00 AM in summer", true only for mid-summer.
- Crowds: most popular hike in town; start soon after sunrise or overnight to avoid (triptins).
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Laguna%20de%20los%20Tres%20El%20Chalten (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Laguna+de+los+Tres+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: GAP no source addresses children (triptins and others silent). Inference: possible for a fit child used to 8-10 h days with trekking poles, but it is the hardest day-hike here; plan it only on a clear day and keep Laguna Capri as the bail-out. Poincenot overnight splits the load.

#### Mirador de los Condores / Aguilas
- Distance: about 2.6 km / 1.6 mi (photohikes); 2-3 mi / 3.2-4.8 km (switchback, appears overstated); about 2.5 km (distance matrix in other guide); with Aguilas 5-6 km, gain 180-300 m (https://nomadicsamuel.com/travel-blog/mirador-de-las-aguilas-is-it-worth-adding-condores-hike).
- Gain: 130-160 m for Condores alone (nomadicsamuel); 300 ft / 90 m (switchback).
- Time: 45-90 min (Condores); +1-1.5 h with Aguilas.
- Trailhead: Los Glaciares visitor centre at the village entrance. The Aguilas fork is about 10 min before the Condores viewpoint (nomadicsamuel).
- See: 360 degrees over Fitz Roy, Torre, Adela, Rio de las Vueltas valley; Aguilas adds Lago Viedma.
- Hazards: strong wind, "wind is the main difficulty factor". Windproof layer essential. Ticket needed (north zone). Bring headlamp for sunset descent.
- Light: afternoon to sunset (photohikes); sunset about 20:30-20:50 in this window (computed), so start about 19:00. Sunrise quieter (nomadicsamuel).
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Mirador%20de%20los%20Condores%20El%20Chalten (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Mirador+de+los+Condores+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: yes. Good arrival or rest-day activity.

#### Loma del Pliegue Tumbado
- Distance: 18.8 km / 11.7 mi, 1,132 m, 9 h (operator via search snippet); 20.3 km / 12.6 mi, 5.5-6.5 h (photohikes); 13 mi, 3,500 ft (switchback); 11 mi, 3,000 ft (theoutbound: https://theoutbound.com/argentina/hiking/hike-to-loma-del-pliegue-tumbado). One automated outlier 4.65 km is wrong, ignore.
- View: Fitz Roy range, Lago Viedma, Rio Tunel valley; summit viewpoint at about 1,500 m. Last 500 m very exposed.
- Hazards: windy, exposed, loose in places; cows in field. Lesser-known so fewer crowds (switchback).
- Trailhead: visitor centre. Light: early morning to afternoon with clear skies.
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Loma%20del%20Pliegue%20Tumbado (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Loma+del+Pliegue+Tumbado+El+Chalten
- 11-yo: GAP. Inference: long and sustained uphill, only for a strong teen.

#### Piedras Blancas glacier mirador
- 8 km / 5 mi, 230 m, 4-5 h, easy, from the Rio Blanco bridge (Ruta 41, about 17 km from town near El Pilar) (https://www.elchalten.com/v4/en/piedras-blancas-glacier-trek-el-chalten.php). Photohikes: 11.4 km / 7.1 mi, 3-4 h. Viewpoint is about 2 km from the glacier. Private land at first; no public toilets. Needs transfer (taxi/shuttle).
- 11-yo: yes.

#### Piedra del Fraile / Rio Electrico / Lago Electrico
- Piedra del Fraile: 13 km / 8 mi, +100 m, 5-6 h, easy, Nov-Apr; trailhead at the Electrico river bridge on RP 41, 16-17 km from town; taxi recommended. Private refugio/camp with dorms and small restaurant; viewpoint 10 min past camp with Fitz Roy north face, Guillaumet, Mermoz (https://elchalten.com/v4/en/piedra-del-fraile-trek-el-chalten.php). No signal. Pollone glacier extension 4-5 h from camp, unsafe moraine beyond.
- Lago Electrico: about 19 km / 12 mi RT, 660 ft gain, about 6 h guided (search snippet, toursbylocals).
- 11-yo: yes for Piedra del Fraile. Transfer cost: GAP.

#### Lago del Desierto / Huemul glacier
- Lago del Desierto is 37 km / 23 mi north of town on Ruta 41 gravel, about 1 h (switchback). Shore walk about 1.6 km, easy. GAP: boat/Kaiken schedule not found.
- 11-yo: shore yes; Huemul probably.

#### Huemul Circuit (note only)
- 64+ km, 4 days, 16,000 ft gain/loss, glacier crossing, two Tyrolean traverses, registration at visitors centre, experienced backpackers only (switchback). Not for this trip.

#### Cerro Electrico and others
- GAP: no source for Cerro Electrico as day hike found; skip. Extra options with sources: Cascada Escondida 13.8 km, 3.5-4.5 h easy; Loma de las Pizarras 25.2 km, 8-9 h hard (photohikes guide).

### 2.3 Extra El Chalten day hikes the video missed (4-6)

### 2.4 El Chalten recommendation (for synthesis)
Must-do: Laguna de los Tres (weather-permitting) and Laguna Torre; Condores at sunset; Chorrillo del Salto as a short day. Keep Laguna Capri/Mirador Fitz Roy as the lower-effort alternative. Budget at least 3 full days and keep spare days for wind or cloud.

## 4. USHUAIA / TIERRA DEL FUEGO

General notes
- Park is 12 km west of Ushuaia; shuttle from bus terminal (Maipu y Juana Genoveva); taxi from rank (https://thatbackpacker.com/hiking-tierra-del-fuego-national-park/). GAP: bus fare and schedule not found.
- Alakush visitor centre has cafeteria; toilets at Rio Pipo, Ensenada and Laguna Verde campsites (thatbackpacker). Another source gives the centre hours 10:00-17:30 (solsalute snippet). GAP: park gate hours.
- Official trail table (APN, authoritative): https://www.argentina.gob.ar/senderos-del-parque-nacional-tierra-del-fuego
- Climate early March: highs about 8-10 C, lows 2-4 C, rain about 90-109 mm and about 12 rain days (weatherapi/other aggregated, sources inconsistent; https://www.weatherapi.com/history/march/q/ushuaia-111984). Autumn colours (lenga/ñire turn copper/gold, March start of autumn per https://www.selectlatinamerica.co.uk/Ushuaia-and-Tierra-del-fuego-holidays snippet); a mid-April visitor found full colour with emptier trails. So the early-March window gives early colour only (my inference). Daylight about 13.4 h on 5 Mar (computed).
- Season windows for park trails: "November-April" for most (APN); Laguna del Caminante Dec-Mar.

### 4.2 Per-trail notes (mi/km, suitability)
- Senda Costera: 8 km (5 mi) one way, 70 m, 3-4 h (thatbackpacker; APN 4 h), medium, linear (not loop). Recommended direction Zaratiegui Bay to Alakush because the cafe is at the end (thatbackpacker). Needs transport back from Alakush (shuttle stops at Lapataia). Sees Beagle Channel coast, forest, bays. Light: any. 11-yo: yes. AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Senda%20Costera%20Tierra%20del%20Fuego (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Senda+Costera+Tierra+del+Fuego+National+Park
- Pampa Alta: 4.7 km, 252 m, 2 h (APN). 11-yo: yes.
- Lago Roca (Acigami shore walk): official page equates the "Hito XXIV" route: 3.5 km one way, 10 m gain, 3 h RT, Nov-Apr (APN). Hito XXIV is the Argentina-Chile border marker at the lake end. 11-yo: yes.
- Cerro Guanaco: 900 m gain, 6 km one way (APN), strenuous, wind-exposed, check weather. 11-yo: only a strong, experienced child. GAP: no source speaks to children.
- Laguna Negra: 500 m peat-bog boardwalk (APN), easy; some sections under repair.
- Lapataia / Bahia Lapataia: Mirador Lapataia 980 m, Turbal 1,350 m, Paseo de la Isla 1,300 m, Castorera 200 m, Baliza 1,200 m (APN); combined about 2 h back to Alakush (thatbackpacker). Southern end of Ruta 3 and the shuttle stop. All easy; ideal for 11-yo.
- Castor beaver trails: Castorera (APN 200 m); beavers are invasive; also beaver dams on the Laguna Esmeralda river (solsalute).
- Isla Redonda: boat/Zodiac tour, not a free-walk; two trails each about 20 minutes; Expedia listing $40 per adult for 4.5 h sailing/trekking with park entry noted as not required (as stated in listing; https://www.expedia.com/things-to-do/sailing-and-trekking-in-isla-redonda-ushuaia-national-park.a50145020.activity-details , price date unknown, GAP).
- Hito XXIV: see Lago Roca.
- Beagle Channel walks: GAP; none researched beyond Senda Costera and boat tours.

### 4.3 Laguna Esmeralda
- Distance: 9 km RT (5.6 mi) (https://solsalute.com/blog/trekking-laguna-esmeralda-ushuaia/); range 8.8-11.5 km / 5.4-7.1 mi across other sources (search snippets: lisagermany, hikingproject, explore-share). Video "5 mi RT": inside the range.
- Gain: about 100-214 m (sources 100 m, 158 m, 204 m per Hiking Project). Time 4-5 h walking (3-5 h range).
- Trailhead: on Ruta 3 about 18-20 km northeast of Ushuaia, large parking lot, coordinates 54 43'18.8"S 68 07'18.4"W (search snippet, source unclear; GAP verify). Getting there: shuttle from bus terminal near tourist info at 9, 10, 11 a.m. returning about 16:00, minimum 3 people (search snippet); rental car; taxi (arrange pickup time, no cell signal at trail) (solsalute).
- OB. Mud in the first forest and peat bog sections (logs and raised boardwalks help), beaver dams, steep muddy third-forest section, rocky end; no toilets mentioned; carry water (solsalute).
- See: lenga forests, peat bogs, Esmeralda river, turquoise lagoon (colour from glacial silt), Ojo del Albino glacier.
- Best time Oct-Mar; start by 15:00 in summer (solsalute). Autumn: not covered.
- Official: Laguna Esmeralda is outside the national park (it is on Ruta 3 inland of Ushuaia), so the park fee likely does not apply. GAP: not stated explicitly by any source; I did not find the provincial/private fee, if any.
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/search?q=Laguna%20Esmeralda%20Ushuaia (not verified). Maps: https://www.google.com/maps/search/Laguna+Esmeralda+trailhead+Ushuaia
- 11-yo: yes, with boots for mud.

### 4.4 Glaciar Martial and Laguna de los Tempanos
- Glaciar Martial: 8.4 km / 5.2 mi RT, +588 m, about 3 h (guide via search snippet) vs a guided listing "5 mi, 1,300 ft, 5 h". Lift runs from base about 7 km from town; elevation of glacier 1,050 m; GAP: chairlift operating dates and price in March 2027.
- "Laguna de los Witches": GAP. I found no trail by that name; the nearby lake route is Laguna de los Tempanos and Vinciguerra glacier: about 9 km (5.6 mi) to the lagoon in about 3 h per one operator, or 14 km with +550-600 m per others; intense wind; reachable by taxi/remis, free entry per a review site (all from search snippets, https://lisagermany.com/hiking-argentina-laguna-de-los-tempanos-and-vinciguerra-glacier/ ). Treat distances as inconsistent.
- 11-yo: Martial yes via chairlift option; Tempanos probably.

### 4.5 Extra Ushuaia/TDF hikes the video missed
1. Laguna del Caminante (hard, 24.4 km, avalanche risk; not suitable).
2. Pampa Alta + Cascada Rio Pipo combination.
3. Cerro Guanaco (strong hiker only).

5. Laguna de los Tempanos/Vinciguerra (needs verification).

### 4.6 Ushuaia recommendation
Must-do: Senda Costera (or Pampa Alta) + Lapataia loops in one park day; Laguna Esmeralda second day. Optional: Glaciar Martial with chairlift.
