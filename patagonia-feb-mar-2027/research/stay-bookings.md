## PART 1 WHERE TO STAY

### 1A. Buenos Aires
Source for area safety (blog/rating sites, not official): https://www.buenosairesfreewalks.com/practical-information/where-to-stay-in-buenos-aires/ ; https://www.isitsafetovisit.com/cities/buenos-aires ; https://www.frommers.com/destinations/buenos-aires/hotels/neighborhoods/ ; https://www.savacations.com/best-neighborhoods-buenos-aires-palermo-puerto-madero/
- Recoleta: most consistently called safest/upscale, day and night. Con: expensive, poor subway access, expect taxis/Uber/Cabify. [RESELLER/blog]
- Palermo (Soho/Hollywood sub-areas): best food/nightlife with a safety margin; walkable on main streets day and night. Con: popular parts pricey, on the fringe of other sights, subway not great. [blog]
- San Telmo: most atmospheric (Sunday market, tango); sources urge caution at night, side streets get sketchy; use app rides after dark. [blog]
- Puerto Madero: sources conflict (one "safest, most modern", one "deserted evenings/weekends", one "very safe, patrolled"). Quiet at night; Rojo Tango is in the Faena hotel here. [blog]
- Avoid at night/areas to watch: Retiro station surroundings, La Boca outside tourist strip. Pickpocketing/phone snatching is main risk; Line D subway rush hour. Use Uber/Cabify.
- Recommendation basis: Recoleta or Palermo for a family with 11+ kids. Apartment-hotel with 2 bedrooms is the family fit.
- Named options with prices: GAP. Searches returned no BA hotel price data. Candidate names are [MODEL-MEMORY], unverified: budget = Palermo apart-hotels/hostel private rooms; mid = Hotel Les Lavandes / Palermo Hollywood boutique class; splurge = Alvear Palace (Recoleta), Faena (Puerto Madero). Needs a real price pull on Booking.com/Hotels.com before use.
- Nights: BA is the arrival/departure hub; 2-3 nights at the start is typical to absorb long-haul jet lag, plus 1 night on return if the international flight is evening. Evidence for that count: none retrieved; this is planning judgement.

### 1B. El Calafate
Source: https://www.patagonline.com/en/where-to-stay-in-calafate/ ; thehotelguru https://www.thehotelguru.com/en-us/best-hotels-in/argentina/el-calafate
- Centre: most restaurants/shops/tour offices; can be crowded in peak season. Best for a 2-night glacier stay without a car.
- Lakeside / Bahia Redonda / edge of town: views, quieter; a taxi or short drive to centre. Design Suites Calafate described as lakeside but not far from centre. Posada Los Alamos on northern edge nearer the lake, pool and spa, ~USD 110 on one Expedia snapshot (undated) https://ie.hotels.com/ho3668232416
- Estancia: no estancia price data retrieved. GAP.
- Kau Yatun Hotel Boutique: pine-forest, top of town, rated ~9.2/10 on several sites; USD ~143-187 snapshots (dates unknown); a guest notes a walk to centre and no air conditioning. https://nz.trip.com/hotels/el-calafate-hotel-detail-3572634/kau-yatn-hotel-boutique
- Tiers: mid = Posada Los Alamos (~USD 110, undated); upper-mid = Kau Yatun (~USD 143-187, undated). Budget and splurge options with prices: GAP. Breakfast/heating inclusion: GAP for all.
- Nights: 3 nights is what the glacier set (Perito Moreno + a boat or Estancia Cristina day + transfer day) needs; a weather buffer day matters. Source for needing a weather buffer: none retrieved. Planning judgement.
- Crowd note: Lake Argentino Festival cited as straining hotels around mid-February (one source, https://championtraveler.com/dates/best-time-to-visit-el-calafate-ar/), date not verified.

### 1C. El Chalten
Source: https://elchalten.com/v4/en/where-to-stay-accommodations-in-el-chalten.php (local directory; says each property has its own site, book direct, no commission)
- Town is small and walkable; the two main trailheads (Fitz Roy via Rio Blanco/Cerro Torre via Chorrillo del Salto) start at the ends of town. Noise/wind specifics per area: GAP (no source).
- Cabins/apartments/houses are recommended for families (garden, grill, kitchen) by the directory.
- Rio de las Vueltas Cabins: 3 independent cabins, private parking, single storey, rates by 2/3/4 passengers, open Oct-Apr. https://www.elchalten.com/riodelasvueltas/en/
- Cabanas Austral: kitchen, wifi, BBQ, children welcome, but listing summary reads as 2 people; confirm sleeping setup. https://elchalten.com/austral/en/
- Lo de Trivi: double ~USD 90-98 (Kayak snapshot, early April date), 0.8 km from centre; hostel beds from ~USD 10. https://www.kayak.com/El-Chalten-Hotels-Lo-de-Trivi.655931.ksp
- Tiers: budget = Lo de Trivi (private double ~USD 90, hostel from ~10); mid/family = Rio de las Vueltas or similar cabins (price: GAP); splurge: GAP. Breakfast/heating: GAP.
- Sell-out: only inference. Aggregators show very small cabin counts (about 7 cabins + 13 apartments on one page), so cabins are the first thing to go. One widget labels March "high crowds, rainy most days". Book the cabin NOW with free cancellation.
- Weather: one source says Feb driest (~18 rain days?) and March/April rainiest; sources conflict; treat as unverified.
- Nights: 3 nights (2 full hiking days + a weather day) is standard; evidence none retrieved.
- Practical: no petrol station in town; fill in Calafate/Tres Lagos (see Part 3).

### 1D. Ushuaia
Source: https://destinationlesstravel.com/where-to-stay-in-ushuaia-argentina/ ; https://lorenzigo.com/best-hotels-in-ushuaia/
- Centre (Centro): practical, walkable to port and restaurants, recommended for most travellers.
- Hotel at Martial / Las Hayas side (about 15 min from port with shuttle; Las Hayas Resort): quieter, mountain views, needs transport. No Martial-specific hotel data found.
- Cerro Castor: ski area ~35 min away; only sensible in ski season; dinner transfers USD 30-40 each way blogger claim. Not useful for late Feb/March.
- 2026 snapshots (all different dates, Jan 2026 for the 4-5 star ones, per 2 adults): Los Calafates (budget, downtown, breakfast) USD 75-200; Los Acebos 4-star USD 145; Canal Beagle Hotel 4-star USD 183; Arakur Resort & Spa 5-star USD 379; Los Cauquenes Resort & Spa USD 255 (late Apr), 6.6 km from centre. Source: lorenzigo and hotels.com/kayak results above, https://kayak.com.ph/Ushuaia-Hotels-Los-Cauquenes-Resort-Spa.160272.ksp
- Caution: some budget places charge per guest, so ask whether a rate is per room or per person.
- Family room/cabin: GAP (no family-config data).
- Nights: 3 nights suits Beagle/penguin/TDF park/train; evidence none retrieved.

## PART 3 CROWDS, PRICES, INSURANCE, MEDICAL

Crowds and pricing
- Feb and March both flagged as the two most popular/crowded months (trekking guide); one source says March tourism volume is about a quarter lower than the Dec-Feb rush; early March often cited as shoulder season with better accommodation deals. Another widget rates March "high crowds". Medical / insurance / cash (El Chalten)
- El Chalten has a basic health facility; serious cases go to a clinic/hospital in El Calafate about 2-3.5 h away. Old tour-operator page: https://www.ladatco.com/webalizer/zzzOLD/Journeys%20Conditions.htm . Current Chalten clinic name/hours: GAP.
- Hospital SAMIC El Calafate: public, centre of town, basic services; expat guide says not ideal for serious emergencies/major surgery; sources mention governance issues and labour disputes. https://www.expatexchange.com/article/9315/Health-Care-in-El-Calafate-Health-Care-in-El-Calafate
- Evacuation: upfront payment often required; one French insurer estimates a Fitz Roy helicopter evacuation up to EUR 12,000 and advises cover without a ceiling, with trekking cover. https://www.globalrescue.com/common/blog/detail/patagonia-traveler-safety-guide/ ; https://hellosafe.com/fr/travel-insurance/to-argentina ; https://swoop-patagonia.com/visit/advice/essential/insurance
- Argentine-specific insurance rules for foreign visitors: GAP (none retrieved). Policy should cover trekking, medical transfer, pre-existing conditions.
- ATM: one ATM (Red Link, Banco Santa Cruz) that is often empty; bring pesos from Calafate; no exchange offices; card payment sometimes fails due to weak signal. https://elchalten.com/apartsaltamontana/en
- Pharmacy: exists near the main supermarket per one apartment listing; hours/stock GAP. Bring prescriptions.
- Fuel: none in Chalten; fill in Calafate/Tres Lagos. https://kimkim.com/ab/el-chalten-to-puerto-natales
- Pickpocketing in BA: see 1A.
