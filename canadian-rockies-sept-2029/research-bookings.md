# Lane 4: Bookings and passes, Canadian Rockies, 1-15 Sep 2029 (raw findings)

Checked 2026-10-12. Party: 2 adults + child ~6, rental car, hotels. Labour Day 2029 = Mon 3 Sep. Thanksgiving (Canada) 2029 = Mon 8 Oct (computed, INFERENCE; used because 2026 shuttle/cruise/gondola seasons ended on Thanksgiving weekend, 12 Oct 2026).
All figures are 2026 unless stated. 2029 data does not exist; anything labelled INFERENCE is projected from the 2026 pattern. Many web-search summaries were cross-checked by fetching the official page; where only a search snippet exists, it says so.

## MUST DO for a September family trip (book first)
1. Lake Louise / Moraine Lake Parks Canada shuttle (no private cars on Moraine Lake Road). 2026 opened Wed 15 Apr 8:00 MDT; only 40% of seats at launch, 60% released 8:00 MDT two days before each date. INFERENCE 2029: mid-April 2029 launch; be logged in on the day; set a reminder for the 2-day-ahead release for each wanted day.
2. Lodging in Banff / Lake Louise / Canmore / Field for 1-3 Sep (Labour Day long weekend) and the weekends of 8-9 and 15 Sep. No lead-time data found (GAP); book as soon as the property opens its 2029 calendar, with free cancellation.
3. Parks Canada front-country campgrounds (only if camping instead of hotels). 2026 launches Jan 20 - Feb 12. INFERENCE 2029: Jan-Feb 2029.
4. Lake O'Hara day bus lottery (only if wanted). 2026 application window 2-23 Mar, $10/application, random draw. Campground (Lake O'Hara) opens Feb 10 2026. INFERENCE 2029: lottery March 2029.
5. Mount Assiniboine Lodge / huts (only if wanted): lodge requests taken in an August window 14+ months ahead; Naiset huts open in January. See below. A 6-year-old makes this a hard fit; policy not stated (GAP).
6. Rental car Calgary: no verified data (GAP). Reserve when lodging is fixed.

## CAN WAIT (weeks before, or no booking)
- Parks Canada Discovery Pass / daily pass: buy online or at the gate. Child (0-17) enters free.
- Kananaskis Conservation Pass (only if driving into Kananaskis): buy online the same day.
- Johnston Canyon, Takakkaw Falls drive, Icefields Parkway driving, Maligne Lake cruise (buy a few weeks ahead for chosen day; not reservation-gated), Jasper SkyTram, Banff Gondola, Lake Minnewanka cruise, Radium pools: walk-up or short-notice booking, but check hours (post-Labour Day reductions below).
- Larch Valley group rule: no booking, but plan the day around it.

## Parks Canada passes
- Discovery Pass 2026 (annual): adult $83.50, senior $71.50, family/group $167.50. Daily 2026: adult $12.25, senior $10.75, family/group $24.50 (up to 7 in one vehicle). Youth 0-17 free. Sources: https://banffbound.com/blog/parks-canada-discovery-pass-price-2026 ; https://thebanffguide.com/banff-park-pass-kananaskis-conservation-pass/ . Official Parks Canada fee pages returned 404 to the fetch, so these are secondary (two consistent sources; search summary cited the official schedule).
- Fees rose 1 Jan 2026; next scheduled increase 1 Jan 2028 (per a search summary of travelbanffcanada/banffbound; not verified on an official page). INFERENCE: 2029 prices equal or above the 2028 schedule; the family/daily pass is cheaper than the annual pass for a trip under ~7 days (family daily $24.50 vs annual family $167.50), but a daily pass covers only the park of the day and may not carry across the Rockies parks; the annual Discovery Pass covers all national parks.
- Canada Strong Pass: ran 19 Jun - 7 Sep 2026; free admission to Parks Canada places plus 25% off camping and roofed accommodation (per search summaries); it does not cover parking, reservations, hot springs entry. Official page lists only that one period and says nothing on future years: https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/canada-pass.html . A summary also mentions an earlier Dec 2025 run (https://refdesk.ca/blog/canada-strong-pass-december-2025-free-travel-parks-guide, not verified). INFERENCE: do not plan around a 2029 free-admission window; GAP: no information on 2027-2029 editions. In 2026 Labour Day (7 Sep) was the last day.

## Lake Louise, Moraine Lake, Banff shuttle (Parks Canada official: https://parks.canada.ca/lakelouise-transit ; https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/parkbus/louise?gccf=true)
- Moraine Lake Road closed to personal vehicles year-round. Access only by Parks Canada shuttle, licensed commercial operators (list at CarFreeBanff.ca), and Moraine Lake Lodge guests.
- 2026 season: Lake Louise Lakeshore shuttle 15 May - 12 Oct; Moraine Lake shuttle 1 Jun - 12 Oct (weather permitting); Lake Connector 1 Jun - 12 Oct, 7:00-18:00 every 30 min; Alpine Start (new in 2026) 1 Jun - 12 Oct, 4:00 and 5:00 departures from Lakeshore lot.
- Reservation launch: Wed 15 Apr 2026 08:00 MDT; 40% at launch, 60% at 08:00 MDT two days before each departure date; also book by phone 1-877-737-3783.
- Seats: ticket covers Lake Louise, Moraine Lake and Lake Connector; departs from the Park and Ride (1 Whitehorn Rd; free all-day parking for reservation holders; vehicles can enter 06:00-20:00). Check in at kiosk in the 1-hour departure window. Up to 10 seats per reservation.
- Fees: $3.50 online booking fee (non-refundable), $5.50 by phone (Parks Canada page). The Banff Blog lists per-seat fares: adult 18-64 $12.75, senior $6, youth $4 and a phone fee of $6.50 (conflicts with Parks Canada's $5.50: https://thebanffblog.com/moraine-lake-shuttle/). Parks Canada did not list the fare itself on the fetched page. Changes/cancellations cost the same fee, allowed up to 1 hour before the departure window. Whether the seat price is refunded: GAP.
- Sell-out: opening day 2026 was reported at nearly 40,000 seats booked (search-result title: https://thebanffguide.com/moraine-lake-shuttle-reservations/ ; page itself 404 to fetch, so only the title is known). Blog says tickets typically sell out. Waits at least 30 min, up to 1 hour at peak. September-specific availability: GAP.
- Lakeshore parking: often full before sunrise; about 75% of vehicles trying to reach the lot were turned away "last summer" (per Parks Canada 2026 page); paid parking in shuttle season ($36.75 for Alpine Start parking per Banff Blog).
- 2025 to 2026 changes (Banff Blog): Alpine Start new, disabled-tag road access ended, own canoe/kayak banned at Moraine Lake, Roam one-day Reservable Super Pass ($30) is the only fare with Lake Connector access. 2025 opening date: GAP (not stated on either page).
- Banff to Lake Louise: no Parks Canada shuttle from Banff town found; the route is Roam 8X (Lake Louise Express), year-round, reservations recommended in summer, $12.50 per adult (Banff Blog). Roam site roamtransit.com (page not fetched).
- Commercial shuttles: Moraine Lake Bus Company from $49 (Banff Blog); others unpriced.
- INFERENCE 2029: season ends Thanksgiving Monday 8 Oct 2029 so 1-15 Sep is mid-season; check shuttle frequency (2026 hours unchanged in September per the official page).

## Larch Valley / Sentinel Pass group access
- Parks Canada Group Access rule: legally required to travel in a tight group of four or more on Consolation Lakes, Larch Valley, Sentinel Pass, Wasatch Pass, Eiffel Lake, Wenkchemna Pass, Sheol Valley, Paradise Valley and a 3.8 km Moraine Lake Highline section when the restriction is in effect. Lakeshore and Rockpile trails exempt. Typically in effect mid-July to mid-October; trailhead sign states "mandatory" or "recommended". Source (search snippet of official page; fetch failed on redirect): https://www.pc.gc.ca/pn-np/mtn/ours-bears/gestion-management/ours-bears/moraine . Secondary: https://canadianrockiestrailguide.com/larch-valley-hike-on-the-busiest-day-of-the-year/ (post is ~2015, 2023 update; no counting rule for children).
- GAP: whether a child aged 6 counts toward the four. Official page not retrieved; confirm with Parks Canada before relying on it. Practical INFERENCE: a family of 3 cannot enter Larch Valley when the rule is mandatory unless joining others; larch colour peaks late Sep, so 1-15 Sep is before peak.
