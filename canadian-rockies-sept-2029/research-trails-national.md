# National parks research: trails and sights (raw)

Checked 2026-10-12 against Parks Canada pages. All status figures are 2026 and used as a pattern for Sept 2029. 2029 status is GAP for everything.
Structured data: data/national.json (30 sights, 36 trails, 19 adult_only each with family alternative and less-walking option).

## Key findings that change the plan (2026 pattern)
- Maligne Canyon (Jasper): closed, "Superintendent closure, no public access" (2026-10-08). https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/activ/experience/sentiers-trails/etat-sentiers-trail-conditions ; also https://www.parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/visit/ouvert-fermee-open-closed
- Edith Cavell Road, Cavell day-use, Cavell Meadows and Path of the Glacier: closed for the 2026 season (debris flow risk after fire). https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/bulletins/2601a019-af00-4e39-b10c-0985e10fa7e6
- Opal Hills Loop: closed 2026-10-08, grizzly. Bald Hills rated Poor; Skyline snow in The Notch; Wilcox Fair; Fryatt not closed. https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/activ/experience/sentiers-trails/etat-sentiers-trail-conditions
- Jasper wildfire: page lists "Closed area: wildfire impacts" without names, links to bulletins; Maligne Road reopened 11 Oct 2024; Icefields Parkway reopened to vehicles 23 Aug 2024; Pyramid Bench trails and Miette Hot Springs open; Valley of the Five Lakes and Wabasso reopened summer 2026 per a search snippet of the recovery page https://www.parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/gestion-management/serviceimmobilier-realty/retablissement-recovery/pc-recovery (not fetched directly).
- Johnston Canyon (Banff): trail to both falls closed from 13 Oct 2026 for rock slope work; Ink Pots reachable via Moose Meadows Trail. https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/randonnee-hiking
- Lake Minnewanka Trail past Stewart Canyon bridge, Aylmer Pass: closed from 16 Sep 2026 until further notice (same page). Aylmer group restriction 10 Jul-15 Sep.
- Moraine Lake Road closed to personal vehicles year-round; shuttle 2026: Moraine ran 1 Jun-12 Oct, Lakeshore shuttle 15 May-12 Oct; 40% of seats at 15 Apr 8am MT, rest 48h ahead; adult 12.75, youth 4.00, senior 6.00, +3.50 online fee, park entry separate. https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/parkbus/louise (search snippet; page not fetched directly) and the Banff hiking page.
- Waterton 2026-10-12: Crypt Lake "Not recommended, no boat shuttle access"; Wishbone Trail overgrown; Bertha Lake and Lower Bertha Falls bear warnings; Red Rock Parkway closed to motor vehicles each Oct-May; wildlife corridor caution at Park Gate, Red Rock and Akamina parkways. https://www.parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/waterton/activ/experiences/randonee-hiking/etat-sentiers-trail-conditions
- Kootenay: Dog Lake Loop and Numa Creek closed; Kindersley Pass and Sinclair Creek require group of four (grizzlies). https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/kootenay/activ/randonnee-hike/jour-day
- Yoho: Takakkaw road open mid-June to mid-Oct; Wapta Falls road closed late Oct to late May, eastbound only; hiking not recommended on snow above treeline mid-Oct to mid-June. https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/bc/yoho/activ/randonnee-hike/jour-day
