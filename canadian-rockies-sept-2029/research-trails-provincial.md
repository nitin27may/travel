# Provincial parks research (Alberta and BC), Sept 2029 trip

Raw findings, checked 2026-10-12. No 2029 data exists; every figure carries its source year (2025-2026 unless stated). Companion data: `data/provincial.json` (18 sights, 26 trails). Statuses in JSON: VERIFIED, GAP, INFERENCE. Zero values for km/gain in JSON mean GAP.

## Access, fees, reservations

- Kananaskis Conservation Pass (2026): C$15 day pass (1 vehicle), C$90 yearly (up to 3 vehicles). Needed only if you park; driving through is free. Source is travel sites, not alberta.ca directly (GAP: official page not fetched). https://www.travelbanffcanada.com/kananaskis-conservation-pass/ , https://thebanffguide.com/banff-park-pass-kananaskis-conservation-pass/
- Mount Robson / Berg Lake (2026): BC Parks page lists no day-use pass or fee. Earlier news (2023-24) described a timed day-use pass for Berg Lake; GAP: not confirmed for 2026. Camping reservations only via BC Parks (phone C$5 fee). https://bcparks.ca/mount-robson-park/ , https://www.princegeorgecitizen.com/latest/day-hikers-will-need-pass-for-berg-lake-trail-3876645
- Berg Lake Trail: fully reopened June 2025 after 2021 flood repair; 2026 reservations opened Dec 2 2025, required May 27 to Sept 29; 7 backcountry campgrounds. A 2029 season should follow this pattern (INFERENCE). https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025ENV0005-000247 , https://richmondsentinel.ca/article-detail/55317/camping-reservations-will-open-for-entire-berg-lake-trail-in-mount-robson-park , https://canadianrockiestrailguide.com/berg-lake-trail-reopens/
- Mount Assiniboine: hike in or helicopter (Wed, Fri, Sun, may change on summer long weekends, so Labour Day weekend flights could differ). A valid camping reservation is required to fly in. Flights booked through Assiniboine Lodge. Hind Hut C$30 pp. GAP: nightly camping fees, helicopter fares and child pricing not found. Magog/Og booking windows disagree between sources (3 vs 4 months), and sell out within seconds. https://bcparks.ca/mount-assiniboine-park/ , https://kootenayrockies.com/planning-for-your-mount-assiniboine-adventure
- Mount Shark to Magog by foot: about 26-27 km one way, about 1,000 m gain; 1.5-2 days. https://besthikesbc.ca/a-backpacking-guide-to-mount-assiniboine-provincial-park/

## Roads

- Official Alberta Parks chart (page updated 2026-10-02): Hwy 40 Peter Lougheed to Highwood House Junction closed Dec 1 to June 14; Hwy 66 west of Elbow Falls and Powderface closed Dec 1 to May 14; Hwy 546 same; Bow Valley Park Road west of Middle Lake closed mid-Oct to Apr 30. A May 2025 version of the chart said June 20 (conflict, newer official page says June 14). September is open. https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/kananaskis/kananaskis-country/advisories-and-public-safety/annual-road-closures/
- Smith-Dorrien (Hwy 742): not on the closure chart (GAP). Gravel, dusty, washboard, no cell service; a rockslide closure near Grassi Lakes turnoff was reported (date unknown). https://hikebiketravel.com/chester-lake-hike/
- Highwood Pass parking: construction/limited parking advisory, later overflow lot added. https://www.albertaparks.ca/albertaparks-ca/learning/parks-blog/golden-larch-season-guide-to-fall-hiking-in-alberta-parks/
- Elk Lakes: about 67 km Forest Service Road, medium clearance, radio recommended, yield to industrial traffic (May 2026 report). https://nrs.objectstore.gov.bc.ca/kuwyyf/elk_lakes_trail_report_a59d6de1b6.pdf?d=20260515
- Whiteswan/Top of the World: unpaved logging road, heavy trucks. https://thecanadianrockies.com/whiteswan-lake-provincial-park/

## Larches and early snow

Alberta Parks: larches turn mid-Sept to early Oct, varies yearly. Chester Lake: end of Sept to early Oct. Ptarmigan Cirque: easiest larch loop (4.5 km, about 200 m). Rawson: sources do not confirm larches. For a 1-8 Sept 2029 trip colour is probably not yet started (INFERENCE), so plan for green with possible early snow at altitude. https://www.albertaparks.ca/albertaparks-ca/learning/parks-blog/golden-larch-season-guide-to-fall-hiking-in-alberta-parks/ , https://explorekananaskis.com/the-complete-guide-to-larch-hikes-in-kananaskis/

## Bears and closures (2026 pattern)

- Upper Kananaskis Lake circuit (North Interlakes to White Spruce) closed until further notice for grizzly with cubs; Rawson/Sarrail advisory effective July 5 2026; Lower Lake item effective July 8 2026. Mount Shark: min group of 4 plus bear spray (Sept 2025). Hike in tight groups of 4+. Call 403-591-7755 to report; info line 403-678-0760. https://www.albertaparks.ca/parks/kananaskis/kananaskis-country/advisories-and-public-safety/trail-reports/peter-lougheed/1982-canadian-mount-everest-expedition-interpretive/ , https://cms.albertaparks.ca/parks/kananaskis/spray-valley-pp/advisories
- Mount Robson: elk, moose aggressive in Sept-Oct rut; permanent boil-water advisory on Berg Lake trail. https://bcparks.ca/mount-robson-park/
