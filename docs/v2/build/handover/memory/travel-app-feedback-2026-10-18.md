After seeing https://nks-travel.pages.dev live (2026-10-18) Nitin said: (1) trips must not show past ones by default: status planning/booked/done, hide done by default, but a done trip is a reusable template for anyone (the product is opening to everyone); (2) the forest-green palette "does not go with everything": he wants a sleek design, wants me to take bolder decisions, render better, and show examples to choose from (not step-by-step iteration); (3) he cannot find any trail map: every trail needs one, use AllTrails-style maps for every trail if nothing better exists, with link-out; (4) more relevant images and a gallery for trails and destinations, found by research and addable; (5) trip pages (naming conventions, layout, photo gallery) should be done better and sooner.

**Why:** the first live version is functional but not appealing; trails and media are what make a plan feel real, and strangers will use the trips.

**How to apply:** do not keep building plan pages on the green theme: the design restyle (phase D1, tokens-driven) comes before P5a. Show design options as standalone HTML previews first (see [[mydesispots-ui-previews-before-build]]). Trail pages with maps (T1), media/gallery (M1) and trip status/templates (P3c) are being planned in Revision 5 of docs/PLAN-platform.md; design directions are in docs/design/.
