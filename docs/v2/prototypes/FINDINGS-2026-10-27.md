# Prototype findings and enhancements (27 Oct 2026)

Check-in for the three clickable prototypes (`parks-app/`, `builder/`, `planner/`). They are throwaway and the owner called them
"an OK starting point, not what he is looking for" (PLAN R-280). This page records what testing found, what is still wrong, and
what to build next. Placeholder data throughout: places, drive times, airports, prices and counts are invented, so none of it is
a finding about the product's data.

How it was tested: each app was built by a Sonnet agent and walked in Chromium at 390 and 1440 wide, light and dark, with no
console errors. The coordinator then ran the whole chain (park page, Plan a visit, builder, build, edit, review, planner trip
home, Edit plan, My trips) on the local server and through a public tunnel. Not tested: a real phone, real print output, a screen
reader, keyboard-only use. Agents read only a fraction of their own screenshots (about 8 of 52 parks, 4 of 88 planner).

## 1. Bugs found while testing (all fixed)

| # | Finding | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | The first frames page showed one frame, not 15 | The mock shows one screen at a time chosen by the URL hash | Gallery page that embeds every frame |
| B2 | Trip frames had no fonts on any other machine | Font paths were `file://` | Fonts served from `design/redesign/fonts` by `serve.py` (no copies, CLN-5 rule 7) |
| B3 | Planner trip home was blank for every trip made in the builder | Builder wrote `dates.month` as a number, planner expected `YYYY-MM` text | Planner accepts both; the builder writes numbers only |
| B4 | A Banff weekend from the park page started from Toronto | One default start for every park | Each park carries its nearest public hub (Algonquin Toronto; Banff, Lake O'Hara, Castle Calgary) |
| B5 | Plans with few picks left free days; Day 1 on air trips was empty | Day filling had no fallback | Light stop on Day 1 when it fits, "Arrival day" and "Departure day" labels, "Fill free days" sheet, spare-nights note before Build |
| B6 | Phone header crowded long titles at 390 and 360 | Two icons plus the count on one row | Title ellipsis, icons 44 px, sub-line below; checked at 320, 360, 390 |
| B7 | Park "Add to a trip" used a link key the builder did not read | Agents built in parallel | Contract agreed: `add=<park>--<place>&p=<park>`; builder reads it |
| B8 | Base label read "Huntsville 1" | Index appended to the town name | Now "Base: Huntsville, 2 nights" |
| B9 | Quick tunnels returned 404 | A stale `~/.cloudflared/config.yml` pointing at a deleted tunnel | Run with a minimal `--config` (tunnel hosting only, not product) |

## 2. Findings that stood (status after the fix round)

Status added after each finding on 27 Oct 2026; 304+ prototype specs pass. "Product work" means a prototype cannot fix it.

### Trip Builder (`builder/`)
- **Free days can stay empty** when nothing nearby fits. The sheet says so but the plan still has a thin day. **Status: Partly. Fixed in builder: two-pass fill (strict, then relaxed, then half a day) and "Remove this day" when nothing fits. Remains: the half-day path is not reached with the placeholder data, so it is covered by a unit-style spec only; the Fill sheet offers only what the placeholder places hold.**
- **Departure days get no light stop.** **Status: Fixed (builder). A light stop is added when the departure time leaves 3 h or more (`departSlack`).**
- **Far-park card** appears on Start, Where and Pick but not in the editor, and a park pinned in trip mode still starts from Toronto. **Status: Fixed (builder). The card shows in the editor and a park pinned in trip mode starts from the arrival airport.**
- **A second trip cannot be added to a day-trip kind**; "Surprise me" only offers Italian regions. **Status: Fixed (builder). A day trip accepts a second trip and Surprise me covers every country with data.**
- **Drag and drop is desktop only.** Phones use the item sheet. Keyboard drag and the single-letter shortcuts from FLOW 7.1 are not built (Esc and Cmd+Z only). **Status: Fixed (builder). Long-press drag on phones, the keyboard move model and single-letter shortcuts limited to the focused row. Not tested on a real phone.**
- **"Ask us for more" only shows a toast.** There is no request route. **Status: Partly. Fixed in builder: a request sheet writes `proto.requests`. The real request route and status are product work (needs a REQ task).**
- **Map is a schematic SVG**, not tiles and routes. **Status: Partly. Fixed in shared: `shared/map.js` draws day routes with numbered pins you can tap. Remains: pins overlap in clusters; tiles are product work (the prototype has no network).**
- **Convert notice departs from the spec:** it adds a switch that frees "from our trip" stops so a driving or pace change visibly re-flows the plan. FLOW keeps them placed. Decide which is right. **Status: Fixed in builder, decision still the owner's. Default D1 follows FLOW: copied stops stay placed, and the switch became an opt-in link in the notice. See the prototype notes in FLOW section 12.**
- **Packing is a short checklist**; sign-in is not simulated; school holidays, camper, train and stay data are marked later. **Status: Product work. Needs data that does not exist yet (sign-in, school holidays, camper, train, stays).**
- **Not checked:** the header icons at widths 391 to 760 px, a real phone, `window.print()`. **Status: Partly. Fixed: layout and print specs cover the header widths and print media in Chromium. Remains: a real phone and real paper output.**

### Parks (`parks-app/`)
- **Six parks are list-only.** Their pages use the compact template, and the index chips (Open in, Entry) come from `data.js`, so the index and the page can disagree on detail. **Status: Fixed (parks). One data source feeds the chips and the pages, and full pages exist for Jasper, Yoho and Killarney. Remains: placeholder pages for 7 parks.**
- **Placeholders:** Algonquin, every camping row, airports, drive times, Algonquin's closure, cover photos (gradient only). **Status: Product work. Real records, fees, closures and photos need research and the park data (enhancement 15).**
- **Index:** Cards and Map views are simple; no pager; no drive-time sort for parks without a hub value; header nav items other than Parks are inert; the phone bottom bar is not built. **Status: Partly. Fixed in parks: cards, map, pager, drive-time sort, header nav and the phone bottom bar. Remains: map pins overlap in clusters. The `pinLabel` hack is replaced by the shared map option `numbered:false`.**
- **Directions, Share and external links** only show a toast. **Status: Partly. Fixed in parks: Share and Directions work and carry Placeholder tags. External links stay placeholders.**

### Planner (`planner/`)
- **Travellers edits do not rewrite the plan.** They update checks and the "what this affects" panel only; the plan is re-run in the builder through a link. **Status: Partly. Fixed in planner: Step-free changes re-flow the plan and show a diff. Remains: other needs do not re-flow; bookings are not removed on a re-flow; trips without engine place ids cannot be re-flowed.**
- **Dates** are one start date plus nights. No flexible-month picker. The calendar file has all-day events, not timed items. **Status: Fixed (planner, shared). One date model (`planner/dates.js`), flexible month, and timed calendar events through `shared/ics.js`.**
- **Map is a placeholder** with generated points; sun times are an approximation, not `core/src/sun`; weather is static text; the offline badge is a flag with no service worker. **Status: Partly. Fixed: shared map, sun times from `shared/sun.js`, a service worker for offline. Remains: weather is static text (product work); pins overlap in clusters.**
- **Deleting the trip in context** leaves the header chip stale until the next navigation. **Status: Fixed (planner). The header chip updates at once.**
- **Demo data disagrees with the builder:** the seeded weekend is dated Jun 2027, the builder's "next weekend" is November. **Status: Fixed (planner). Seeds are built by the engine and dated the same way as the builder.**
- **"Sign in" opens an explanatory sheet only.** **Status: Product work. Needs the account backend; the owner checks Google sign-in himself.**
- **Dark-mode contrast** was eyeballed on a few shots, not measured. **Status: Fixed (builder, parks, planner). Contrast specs measure light and dark.**

### Cross-cutting
- The three apps share `localStorage` (`proto.trips`, `proto.saved`, `proto.draft`). Nothing here proves the real contract (plan-draft/1, account sync), only the user flow. **Status: Product work. The contract (plan-draft/1, sync) is built in the product. Planner-only fields still live in `proto.planner.ext` until the planner moves to `trip.ext`.**
- Distances are coordinates times a factor. Plan quality in the product depends on the drive-time model (BACKLOG 15.344) and the per-place metadata (`features/trip-builder-location-metadata.md`). **Status: Product work (15.344).**
- Algonquin from Toronto at a 3 h reach shows the far-park card, which depends on invented distances. **Status: Product work. Depends on real distances.**

### Infrastructure and process
- `~/.cloudflared/config.yml` points at a tunnel that no longer exists; `cloudflared tunnel info Macbook` errors because of it. Remove or fix it. **Status: Owner action.**
- `ssh.sampleapp.cloud` has no DNS record. A CNAME for `app.sampleapp.cloud` was added to the `Macbook` tunnel on 27 Oct 2026. That tunnel's saved route sends the host to `localhost:4200` and a locally-written config cannot override it. **Status: Owner action.**
- DNS resolvers cache a missing record (Google's kept answering empty after the record existed). Use a fresh hostname when a new record does not show. **Status: Note only.**
- A relayed "owner decided" is not accepted by the other session for a table rename; the owner confirmed in that session. Record owner decisions where the doing session can see them. **Status: Process note only.**
