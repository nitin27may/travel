# Prototype findings and enhancements (27 Oct 2026)

Check-in for the three clickable prototypes (`parks-app/`, `builder/`, `planner/`). They are throwaway and the owner called them
"an OK starting point, not what he is looking for" (PLAN R-280). This page records what testing found, what is still wrong, and
what to build next. Placeholder data throughout: places, drive times, airports, prices and counts are invented, so none of it is
a finding about the product's data.

How it was tested: each app was built by a Sonnet agent and walked in Chromium at 390 and 1440 wide, light and dark, with no
console errors. The coordinator then ran the whole chain (park page, Plan a visit, builder, build, edit, review, planner trip
home, Edit plan, My trips) on the local server and through a public tunnel. Not tested: a real phone, real print output, a screen
reader, keyboard-only use. Agents read only a fraction of their own screenshots (about 8 of 52 parks, 4 of 88 planner).

## 1. Bugs found while testing (all fixed)

| # | Finding | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | The first frames page showed one frame, not 15 | The mock shows one screen at a time chosen by the URL hash | Gallery page that embeds every frame |
| B2 | Trip frames had no fonts on any other machine | Font paths were `file://` | Fonts served from `design/redesign/fonts` by `serve.py` (no copies, CLN-5 rule 7) |
| B3 | Planner trip home was blank for every trip made in the builder | Builder wrote `dates.month` as a number, planner expected `YYYY-MM` text | Planner accepts both; the builder writes numbers only |
| B4 | A Banff weekend from the park page started from Toronto | One default start for every park | Each park carries its nearest public hub (Algonquin Toronto; Banff, Lake O'Hara, Castle Calgary) |
| B5 | Plans with few picks left free days; Day 1 on air trips was empty | Day filling had no fallback | Light stop on Day 1 when it fits, "Arrival day" and "Departure day" labels, "Fill free days" sheet, spare-nights note before Build |
| B6 | Phone header crowded long titles at 390 and 360 | Two icons plus the count on one row | Title ellipsis, icons 44 px, sub-line below; checked at 320, 360, 390 |
| B7 | Park "Add to a trip" used a link key the builder did not read | Agents built in parallel | Contract agreed: `add=<park>--<place>&p=<park>`; builder reads it |
| B8 | Base label read "Huntsville 1" | Index appended to the town name | Now "Base: Huntsville, 2 nights" |
| B9 | Quick tunnels returned 404 | A stale `~/.cloudflared/config.yml` pointing at a deleted tunnel | Run with a minimal `--config` (tunnel hosting only, not product) |

## 2. Findings that still stand

### Trip Builder (`builder/`)
- **Free days can stay empty** when nothing nearby fits. The sheet says so but the plan still has a thin day.
- **Departure days get no light stop.**
- **Far-park card** appears on Start, Where and Pick but not in the editor, and a park pinned in trip mode still starts from Toronto.
- **A second trip cannot be added to a day-trip kind**; "Surprise me" only offers Italian regions.
- **Drag and drop is desktop only.** Phones use the item sheet. Keyboard drag and the single-letter shortcuts from FLOW 7.1 are not built (Esc and Cmd+Z only).
- **"Ask us for more" only shows a toast.** There is no request route.
- **Map is a schematic SVG**, not tiles and routes.
- **Convert notice departs from the spec:** it adds a switch that frees "from our trip" stops so a driving or pace change visibly re-flows the plan. FLOW keeps them placed. Decide which is right.
- **Packing is a short checklist**; sign-in is not simulated; school holidays, camper, train and stay data are marked later.
- **Not checked:** the header icons at widths 391 to 760 px, a real phone, `window.print()`.

### Parks (`parks-app/`)
- **Six parks are list-only.** Their pages use the compact template, and the index chips (Open in, Entry) come from `data.js`, so the index and the page can disagree on detail.
- **Placeholders:** Algonquin, every camping row, airports, drive times, Algonquin's closure, cover photos (gradient only).
- **Index:** Cards and Map views are simple; no pager; no drive-time sort for parks without a hub value; header nav items other than Parks are inert; the phone bottom bar is not built.
- **Directions, Share and external links** only show a toast.

### Planner (`planner/`)
- **Travellers edits do not rewrite the plan.** They update checks and the "what this affects" panel only; the plan is re-run in the builder through a link.
- **Dates** are one start date plus nights. No flexible-month picker. The calendar file has all-day events, not timed items.
- **Map is a placeholder** with generated points; sun times are an approximation, not `core/src/sun`; weather is static text; the offline badge is a flag with no service worker.
- **Deleting the trip in context** leaves the header chip stale until the next navigation.
- **Demo data disagrees with the builder:** the seeded weekend is dated Jun 2027, the builder's "next weekend" is November.
- **"Sign in" opens an explanatory sheet only.**
- **Dark-mode contrast** was eyeballed on a few shots, not measured.

### Cross-cutting
- The three apps share `localStorage` (`proto.trips`, `proto.saved`, `proto.draft`). Nothing here proves the real contract (plan-draft/1, account sync), only the user flow.
- Distances are coordinates times a factor. Plan quality in the product depends on the drive-time model (BACKLOG 15.344) and the per-place metadata (`features/trip-builder-location-metadata.md`).
- Algonquin from Toronto at a 3 h reach shows the far-park card, which depends on invented distances.

### Infrastructure and process
- `~/.cloudflared/config.yml` points at a tunnel that no longer exists; `cloudflared tunnel info Macbook` errors because of it. Remove or fix it.
- `ssh.sampleapp.cloud` has no DNS record. A CNAME for `app.sampleapp.cloud` was added to the `Macbook` tunnel on 27 Oct 2026. That tunnel's saved route sends the host to `localhost:4200` and a locally-written config cannot override it.
- DNS resolvers cache a missing record (Google's kept answering empty after the record existed). Use a fresh hostname when a new record does not show.
- A relayed "owner decided" is not accepted by the other session for a table rename; the owner confirmed in that session. Record owner decisions where the doing session can see them.

## 3. Enhancements (proposed order)

### A. Make the plan trustworthy (before any real build)
1. Real drive-time matrix (ORS, cached, haversine fallback) in place of coordinates times a factor (15.344).
2. Real opening hours, closures and booking windows per place, feeding "Fill free days" and warnings.
3. Daylight from `core/src/sun` for every day and every hike.
4. Engine from the spec: p-median bases, largest-remainder nights, TOPTW-lite days, `scheduleDay`; deterministic tests with known picks and expected order.
5. Coverage gate per region: a region with too few researched places says "Ask us" and never pads (TB9). Ontario is not ready.

### B. Trip Builder experience
6. Phone drag by long-press, keyboard move model, single-letter shortcuts limited to the focused row (WCAG 2.1.4).
7. Real map with the day route and a day colour per leg; tap a pin to open the item.
8. "Ask us for more" becomes a request route (needs a REQ task) and shows request status.
9. Rain plan: one tap swaps an outdoor item for an indoor place on the same day (uses the existing `indoor` data).
10. Compare two or three destinations before committing (needs per-region summaries).
11. "Surprise me" for every country with data, by season, drive time and interests.
12. Decide the convert-notice behaviour (free "from our trip" stops or keep them placed) and write it into FLOW.
13. Interest profile that ranks and never gates; party "needs" field in the account.
14. Later, once data exists: budget band and running estimate (price on 60% of candidates plus lodging bands), school holidays (TK-H), camper, train and EV, stay-type filter, per-item votes and comments.

### C. Parks
15. Fill the park records for the pilots (Banff, Lake O'Hara, Castle, Algonquin) with entry, fees, camping and permit windows, and add the Algonquin park record (PA-R-ON).
16. Alerts and closures feed with a printed "as of" date; revalidate daily.
17. Passes wallet: show which pass covers which parks and the number of parks it covers.
18. Drive-time filter on `/parks` once the hub list and drive model exist; list beside the map view if usage supports it.
19. Trails and camping sub-pages for the pilots; kid-friendliness and step-free on the park glance.

### D. Planner and Mine
20. Travellers edits re-flow the plan with a diff of what moved.
21. Flexible-month dates; timed calendar events; book-by reminders by email or push when the account exists.
22. Today view with real sun times, a service worker for offline, and a live "leave by" from the device position.
23. Post-trip notes and photos that flow back into the place data (F14).
24. Shared-link view for viewers with the private-places rule, print or PDF export, account sync (TRIP-EDIT-I, `plan_draft`).
25. Fix the stale in-context chip after a delete.

### E. Quality and process
26. Test on real phones (small, mid, large) and with a screen reader and keyboard only; measure contrast instead of eyeballing.
27. Turn the three walkthroughs into Playwright specs for the real build; keep the `/plan/` payload budget (90 KB gzip).
28. Usability sessions with two or three real families on the clickable prototype before TB-W1 starts; capture the owner's gap list first (R-280).
29. Fix `~/.cloudflared/config.yml`; use a named hostname with Cloudflare Access for design reviews instead of a quick tunnel; decide whether to keep the `app.sampleapp.cloud` record.
