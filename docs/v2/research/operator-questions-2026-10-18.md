# Applicability questions (fields the lanes think are marked wrongly)

For the coordinator to settle in `data/schema/suitability-applicability.json`; not for operators.

## Algonquin

- **Dwight Beach sunset** is mapped as a `viewpoint`. In summer it would be a `beach` (lifeguard, entry, water temperature). For an October sunset visit the viewpoint profile fits; a second record would be needed for swimming.
- **Ragged Falls** is a `waterfall`; `water_safety.current_or_tide` is A and would matter (fast water beside the lookouts), but no source states it.
- **Solitaire Lake** sits on a private reserve with a compulsory waiver. The `hike` profile has no field for a waiver unless `operated`; the waiver is in `access_season.note`.

## Canadian Rockies (activities)

- **`mtb_xc` on a network.** `effort.drivers.distance_km` and `gain_m` are R, but the Canmore Nordic Centre is a 100 km network with no single route. Both are stored as `not_applicable` with a note. Recommend that a network record take the `area` treatment, with child trail records carrying effort.
- **`canoe_kayak` `water_safety.lifejacket`.** The enum has no value for "provided, sizes not stated", which is what most operators say. Recorded as `searched_none` with that note. Recommend adding `provided_sizes_unknown`.
- **`stroller_on_board` for advice.** Banff Gondola "does not recommend" strollers. The enum has no advice value, so it is `no` with a note. Recommend `not_recommended`.
- **`rafting` `water_safety.class` with a range.** The grade must be a single class, so "II-III" is stored as III with a note. A range type would keep the operator's words.
- **`hot_spring` `ext.water.clarity`** is A. Parks Canada pools are clear and chlorinated; no source says so, so it is left open.
- **`sight` `access_season` (Skywalk, Dark sky)** is A on `sight`. For the Skywalk it is decisive (operator shuttle, May to October), so it was filled.

## Canadian Rockies (trails and sights)

Fields or kinds that look wrong for a record; the data was left as `kind-map.json` has it.

1. **`sights/canmore-nordic-centre` is `activity / mtb` (M4, M5)**, but this sight row is the provincial park, used by the plan as the way to Grassi Lakes; the bike product is the adventure row R2b covers. It fits `sight / park` (the `area` profile, B14). I did not change `kind-map.json`, because `migrate --check` would then fail on S0's `migrated.json`, which this lane does not own. The record holds mtb_xc R fields mostly as `searched_none`.
2. **`trails/mary-schaffer-loop` and `trails/troll-falls` should be `walk`.** M1 kept them `hike` on the plan's climbs (84 m, 155 m); the official figures are 3.2 km with no gain and 1.4 km (one way?) with 0 m.
3. **`sights/kinney-lake-view` as a `lake` record** is a lake 7 km up a trail; `walk_m` 7000 reads oddly as "the walk from the car". The trail record carries the effort; the lake record could take the area profile, or `walk_m` could allow a trail ref.
4. **`ext.drive.vehicle_limits` is R for every drive.** The Icefields Parkway, Highwood Pass and Vermilion Lakes have no private-vehicle limit on the land manager's pages, so the answer is a `searched_none`. A `none_stated` would be more useful, but no page says "no limit" in words.
5. **`stroller` as a surface against a land manager's advice.** Johnston Canyon is 100% paved to the Lower Falls, but Parks Canada says strollers are not recommended. I stored `none` (`official_info`) with the reason. A separate advisory flag may be cleaner.
6. **`hazards` is A on the `walk` profile.** The Athabasca Glacier toe walk has a deciding hazard (stepping onto the ice); it was filled anyway as an A field.
7. **Exposure on railed viewpoints.** A railed canyon edge (Athabasca, Sunwapta) set to `significant` lifts terrain to 4 through the drivers table even when fenced; I stored `some, fenced`. The drivers rule may want to read `fenced`.

## Dolomites

1. **`group_min` is R on `hike`, but no Italian or South Tyrol day hike has a group rule.** Thirteen records carry the same `searched_none`. Suggest A on `hike` and R only where a land manager publishes group rules (Parks Canada).
2. **`max_weight_kg` is R on `coaster`, but coaster operators publish a load per sled, not a rider weight** (Funbob: 127 kg per sled, which is `ext.coaster.max_load_kg`). Suggest A for `max_weight_kg` on `coaster`.
3. **`effort.drivers.gain_m` is R on `cycle_path`, but downhill rides publish a height difference only** (Drava: 500 m of descent). Derive should accept `loss_m` alone for a one-way downhill ride, or `gain_m` should be A when `ext.ride.one_way_return` is set.
4. **One `source_grade` per hike hides a harder section.** Piz Boè is CAI E to Rifugio Boè and EEA:F for the summit path; derive maps E to terrain 3 and gives FE3. Suggest a "hardest section" rule or a second grade field, so the summit reads as FE4 or FE5.
5. **`wheelchair` and `stroller` are R on `town`**, but no town in scope has an accessibility page; all nine are our inference. Fine as R, but the UI should show them as "our judgement" clearly.
6. **`water_safety.child_sizes_from_kg` is R on `rafting`**, and no operator here publishes it; it will be `searched_none` almost everywhere. Consider A.
7. **`hazards` is N on `town` and on `lake`'s rowing-boat use at Braies.** The Braies boats are a rental with their own rules (an `activity/boat-tour`-like record would be needed). Not added.

## Iceland

1. **`exposure` "some" lifts a roadside waterfall to family effort 3 (Moderate).** Gullfoss, Godafoss, Skogafoss, Seljalandsfoss, Hraunfossar, Kirkjufell and the Thingvellir walk all derive FE3 from one driver ("rough path or exposure some"). For a viewpoint 100 m from a car park that reads wrong on a chip. Suggest: for waterfall and viewpoint profiles, terrain from exposure only when `walk_m` is over about 500 m, or mark exposure as a warning only.
2. **`water_safety.entry` and `ext.water.swimming` are R on `beach`.** On Icelandic surf beaches nobody enters the water. The honest answer is `drop_off` / `unsafe`, which works, but a "no-swim beach" subkind (or `entry: not_used`) would read better.
3. **`hazards` is A on `waterfall` and `lake`.** At Dettifoss (landslide) and Jokulsarlon (ice floes) the hazard decides the day; consider R where `exposure` is not `none`.
4. **`area` has no `exposure` or water fields.** Fine for the Reykjanes lava fields; volcanic gas went into `hazards` as `health`.
5. **The hazard vocabulary has no `volcanic` category.** Eruption risk went in as `other`. Suggest adding `volcanic`.
6. **Snaefellsjokull view** has no defined spot in the plan. It is a viewpoint with no place; consider dropping it or tying it to a named stop (Arnarstapi or the Malarrif visitor centre).

## Patagonia

1. **Wildlife (operated): `participation.guide`, `medical` and `pregnancy` are N.** The Martillo landing has a required guide, a reduced-mobility exclusion and a pregnancy warning. I removed them from the data (W_SUIT_NA_FIELD) and kept the facts in `kid_note`, `hazards` and `wheelchair`. Recommended: make them A on `wildlife` when operated.

3. **BMI limit.** Hielo y Aventura excludes BMI 30+. There is no field (MEDICAL has no `obesity`). Proposed: add `obesity` to MEDICAL, or a `max_bmi` limit.
4. **Laguna Nimez as `viewpoint`.** It is a 3 km self-guided reserve loop for birds. A `walk` or `wildlife` (self) profile fits better. I left it as is because the change needs a person's call on the ref's meaning.
5. **"Evening Fitz Roy glow"** has no fixed viewing spot. If it means Mirador de los Cóndores at sunset (the plan's Day 6), it duplicates that record. If it means the village, `walk_m: 0` is right.
6. **Family effort without official distance.** Laguna de los Tres derives FE3 from drivers only, because its distance and gain are community conflicts. A reviewer override to FE4 (21 km, about 1,000 m) is warranted. Override is a person's call.

## Utah

1. **`stroller_on_board` is N on the `drive` profile.** The Zion Canyon Scenic Drive is seen only from the shuttle for most of the year, and NPS has a stroller rule (load it on the front bike rack yourself). The rule exists and decides the day for a family. Suggest A on `drive`, or a `shuttle` subkind.
2. **`ext.drive.vehicle_limits` cannot say "no private vehicles".** For a shuttle-only road the honest value is a ban, not a size. Stored as `{}` with a note. Suggest a `private_vehicles: banned | seasonal | ok` member.
3. **`water_safety.water_temp_c` is one range.** River water varies by season (5-12 C in fall, 13-20 C in summer, 3-7 C in winter). A by-month form, like `class_at_level`, would hold it.
4. **Area designations and the relevance kind.** Goblin Valley is suitability `sight`/`park` (the area profile), but relevance accepts an `own` area designation only when the relevance `kind` is `park`, `region` or `protected_area`. The relevance record uses `kind: park`. The two specs should share one rule.
5. **`hazards` is A, not R, on `viewpoint`.** The rim viewpoints (Dead Horse, Grand View, Sunset Point) have unfenced drops; it is captured under `exposure`, which is enough, so this is a note, not a request.
6. **`walk` has no `hazards` as R and no `ext.trail.navigation` as R.** Fine for these walks.
