Moraine Lake

The Valley of the Ten Peaks over a turquoise lake; the Rockpile view is a short walk.

Our judgement · read 18 Oct 2026

No ratings or review counts are used. How we judge this

A glacier-fed lake under ten peaks, reached only by shuttle or bus since the road closed to private cars. The classic view is from the Rockpile, a short climb from the shuttle stop.

Hike · Banff National Park · Alberta

Larch Valley to Sentinel Pass

To Sentinel Pass · 2,611 m · photo and judgement from the pass

Larches turn gold under the Ten Peaks for three weeks; the pass looks down on both valleys.

From the Moraine Lake shore, switchbacks climb through forest to the larch meadows, then on to the pass. Turn at the meadows for a shorter day.

To Sulphur Mountain summit · 2,281 m · judgement from the summit

A turquoise lake under Victoria Glacier, the image of the Canadian Rockies.

The lakefront is a short walk from the lots and the shuttle stop, and the level lakeshore trail runs on for 2.3 km.
