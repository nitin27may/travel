# Globe: owner feedback on the L2 preview (18 Oct 2026)

Nitin tried the L2 build through the preview tunnel. Every item below goes into the L2 fixes or the L2b spec.

| # | What he saw | Cause (checked) | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | "The whole experience can be better": rendering, spin smoothness, overall look, and how the selected details are shown. "It was created quickly." | Built for parity first. The experience review (`experience-review.md`) covers it. | L2b, whole spec |
| F2 | Concentric rings across the Arctic when Russia is hovered | Russia's border crosses the 180 degree meridian in 4 places (lat 65.0, 69.0, 71.0, 71.5) in `countries-50m.json`. The hover and selected line layers draw each jump of more than 180 degrees of longitude as a full parallel around the globe. Other countries that cross the meridian (Fiji, USA Aleutians, Kiribati, Antarctica) are likely affected too. | L2 must-fix: cut or unwrap rings at the antimeridian when the TopoJSON becomes GeoJSON, and add a test that no segment jumps more than 180 degrees |
| F3 | A selected city (Whitby) shows no area: only a card with "Fly here" | We have no city boundary polygons. OpenFreeMap gives a label point only. | L2b: city area from OSM admin_level 8 polygons. Pre-build them for cities with our content. For the rest, use a soft ring around the point plus a highlight on the basemap's admin_level 8 boundary lines, and say "boundary not loaded" honestly. |
| F4 | "District Toronto" covers Whitby and Oshawa, and "Toronto" appears 3 times | Canada ADM2 from geoBoundaries is the 76 economic regions, not the 293 census divisions. Its "Toronto" region includes Durham, York, Peel and Halton. Some names are truncated with `*` ("West Coast--Northern Peninsul*"). The polygons are heavily simplified: the Lake Ontario shore is cut by straight lines. | L2b data: replace with Statistics Canada census divisions (Open Government Licence), so Whitby is in Durham. Audit other countries' ADM2 for the same problems: level meaning, truncated names, simplification against the coastline. |
| F5 | "There is no option to reset the globe" | The "World" breadcrumb chip is covered by the selection card at city zoom, and there is no reset control beside + and -. | L2 must-fix: a Reset view (globe icon) button in the zoom stack, always visible; Home key and Esc behaviour; breadcrumb never covered |
