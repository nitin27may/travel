# Changes from the Trailhead previews (18 Oct 2026)

What the foundation changes relative to `docs/v2/design/redesign/trailhead/*.html` and directions.md
section C, and why. Where a preview is listed as superseded, build from the foundation docs, not the HTML.

| # | Change | Why | Previews affected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Amber only for selected, now, primary action.** Countdown chips are neutral; "to book" is a neutral outline chip (caution when the window is open, urgent inside 7 days); only the focused day sign is amber, the rest are quiet; base pins are ink signposts; cover kickers are white at 80%; globe controls fill only the active option. | Amber meant nine things on one screen (review C1). | all; home, plan, bookings-switch, public-trip, trail superseded for colour |
| 2 | **New caution and info roles** (`--warn` ochre text on a pale well, never a fill; `--info` slate). Danger fill `--crit-fill` with white text in both themes. | The previews had only urgent and done; "window open", "over pace", "offline" and "read-only" had no colour of their own. | tokens only |
| 3 | **Green stays only for done, booked, fits.** Provenance dots on trail facts become text labels. Kind glyphs are ink on a well, not teal. | Owner: no green palette; teal glyphs collided with the Costa Rica identity. | trail, places, home (Peru idea card) |
| 4 | **Identity swatches get a 1 px `--line-2` keyline.** | Yellow (1.3:1) and sky (2.3:1) vanished on white. | home, bookings-switch, phones |
| 5 | **Public header variant**: Trips · Places · Globe, search, theme, Sign in; no trip chip or bell for visitors. "Discover" is renamed Trips; the household's own trips are Home. | Read-only without login is the primary surface. | home, public-trip, trail, places headers |
| 6 | **Trip bar is a fixed 88 px band**, one-line truncating name and meta; drawers open below it. | Patagonia's meta wrapped and the drawer covered the tabs. | plan, bookings-switch |
| 7 | **Public trip page is categorised and chronological**: category strip of six (Stays, Trails, Sights, Activities, Food, Logistics; every research kind mapped in patterns.md 3.4), By day / By category segment, fourteen uniform day rows instead of six cards plus eight rows. | Owner's product direction; the preview was chronological only and changed shape halfway. Activities is its own chip because the kinds store adventures and lifts as `activity`, and they are the items a family checks limits and windows on. | public-trip superseded for structure |
| 8 | **Globe specified** (full-bleed, floating controls, side panel, selection card, fly-in rules) instead of a screenshot of the live page. | It is the public entry point and was not designed. | places (globe section) superseded |
| 9 | **Map: All-days view uses 6 px dots and routes; numbered pins only for the picked day; co-located stops merge with a "3 · 5" badge; the picked route gets a 1 px ink casing; dark-map contour, water and halo tokens.** | 40 overlapping numbered pins were unreadable; dark contours were invisible. | plan, public-trip, plan-phone maps |
| 10 | **Drag handle has a 24 x 44 hit area (28 x 44 phone); insertion pill reads "to Day 3 · before 11:30".** | 17 px glyph with no target. | plan, plan-phone |
| 11 | **Mono never wraps**: card meta is one truncating line; section subtitles are serif. Lower-casing of place names removed. | "Sat 24 Oct 2026, day trip from whitby" wrapped and was mangled. | home, places |
| 12 | **Readiness is one labelled bar** that splits into four stages on hover. | Four unlabelled 4 px bars. | home |
| 13 | **Table status**: Planned = filled ink dot, Idea = dashed ring, Done = check; density is a Compact / Comfortable segment. | Amber status dot (see 1); the toggle did not show its state. | places |
| 14 | **Phone bottom bar**: 11.5 px labels, 28 px photo with ring and keyline, 56 px items. | 10.5 px labels and a 26 px switcher. | plan-phone, today-phone |
| 15 | **Input and button borders** `#7A8794` light / `#5E748E` dark. | Preview `#8C99A8` was 2.9:1 on white, under the 3:1 UI minimum. | all |
| 16 | **Focus, pressed, disabled, loading, error and empty states defined** for every component, with the focus ring token. | None were drawn. | all (additive) |
| 17 | **Type scale closed at nine display sizes** (96, 72, 40, 32, 24, 21, 19, 15, and 78 for leave-by), plus phone variants 56, 44, 26. The previews' 18, 20, 22, 30, 34, 44 and 52 map to 21, 32 or 40 (tokens.md 6). | Ten ad-hoc sizes in the previews; an open scale becomes ad-hoc sizes again. | all |
| 18 | **Links on the trail page become tabs by group**; hazards move above the fold. | 12 links pushed hazards down a 400 px column. | trail |
| 19 | **No infinite scroll**: 48 cards / 100 rows per page with a pager and URL `?page=`. | Credits and attribution must stay reachable; CSV and group collapse need a bounded set. | spec only |
| 20 | **"Held" chip** is an outline with a clock, not dashed. | Dashed is reserved for Idea and honesty markers. | bookings-switch |
| 21 | **One urgency rule** (tokens.md 3.1): coral within 7 days or when a window opens within 48 h, ochre 8 to 30 days or window open, neutral beyond. The switcher's 14-day coral is gone; "Book by Sat 31 Oct" is ochre on 18 Oct. | Two thresholds meant two meanings for the one colour a parent scans for (gate B2). | home, bookings-switch (rebuilt), recommendation.md 2 |
| 22 | **One URL table** (patterns.md 1.2): public `/trips/<trip-slug>`, `/places/<place-slug>`, `/globe/...`; planner `/t/<trip-slug>/<tab>/<item-id>`; shared links `?share=<token>`. Slugs carry the start year (`patagonia-2027`); item ids are prefixed and opaque (`bk_7f3a`). | `/t/<slug>` meant a public trip in one page and the planner in another (gate B1). | recommendation.md 2, 5, 6; trip-scope-scenarios rule 9 |
| 23 | **Nothing amber at rest on public surfaces**: globe country pins are ink discs with surface counts, amber only when hovered or selected; the elevation profile is an ink line and area, amber only for the scrub cursor and its position dot. | Gate B4; the two most-seen public components carried "selected" colour with nothing selected. | places (globe), trail (profile) superseded for colour |
| 24 | **Side sheet is 460 px with a 140 px photo header and a 32 px title**; its kicker is white at 80% (`--kicker-on-photo`), like the cover kickers. The previews' amber `#F0B356` kicker is gone from covers and the drawer. | components and specimen disagreed (420/120/30 vs 460/140/34); amber on a photo was low contrast on sky (review I8). | all covers and drawers (rebuilt) |
| 25 | **Done check is `--ok-soft` fill, `--ok` border and glyph.** | A white glyph on `--ok` is 1.8:1 in dark. | spec and specimen only |
| 26 | **No hex in components**: `--accent-hover`, `--on-crit-fill`, `--kicker-on-photo` added. Radii only 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 18, 999 (booking and leave-by cards 12; banners, legend, lifted rows, late toggles and day strip 8). Minimum text 11.5 px with no exceptions (badge 18 px disc with 11.5 digits; attribution, axes, credits 11.5). Inputs, including search, are 16 px. | Consistency findings in scenario-check.md 5.2. | specimen |
| 27 | **Globe hover and selection are cased amber lines** (tokens 5.1): hover `--accent` 35% fill plus 1.5 px amber over 0.5 px `--map-casing`; selected 2 px over 1 px; siblings under `--globe-dim`. New tokens `--map-wood` (replaces every green park and wood fill), `--map-casing`, `--globe-ocean`, `--globe-halo`, `--globe-edge`, `--globe-dim`. `?f=` selects a basemap feature; `?c=` carries pitch and bearing. | The "`--accent-soft` at 35%" hover fill was 1.01:1 over land (review L2-4); amber alone was 1.14:1 on water. | globe (spec and components 7.2) |
| 28 | **Public trip page gets a party profile and per-day timelines** (patterns 3.4 rewritten from timeline-design 10): "Plan it for" replaces any per-day Track A / B / C control; a sticky plan bar with a day rail; "The route" becomes "The trip at a glance" with the map in a sticky column; day rows become day cards whose timelines are always open. Timeline URL keys `p`, `pace`, `a`, `walk`, `split`, `d`, `add` in patterns 1.2. | A family wants one answer for the whole trip, not a choice on each of fourteen days (timeline-design 1). | public-trip superseded for structure (again) |
| 29 | **Suitability chips** (components 2.8): rule, advice, value, gap, fits, advice fail, rule fail, with existing tokens; card chip rows wrap rather than truncate; chips may wrap on a phone timeline entry. | Age and effort rules need to be readable without colour and without a legend (suitability-display 3). | cards, day rows, place pages |

Preview slips fixed in the generator (`../redesign/build/trailhead.py`, `data.py`) and rebuilt, so a rebuild keeps them: "Sunday 18 October"; Patagonia's 13-day deadlines ochre, not coral; places count 57 (Italy 10 + Canada 22 + United States 25); Iceland meta says nights once. Still as drawn in the previews and superseded by the foundation: the "Discover" header tab, amber "To book" chips, the amber elevation line on trail.html.

Not changed: the palette base (paper `#F5F6F8`, slate `#172230`, navy header with the amber rule, dark
`#0F1620`), the three typefaces, the card anatomy, the day sign, the item row, the switcher menu, the
form drawer with the trip photo header and "Files under", the Needs-you columns, the Today frames
(content and layout), the season band, the credits footer. Build those from the previews as drawn.
