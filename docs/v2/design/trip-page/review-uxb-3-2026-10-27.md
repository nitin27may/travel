# Review: UXB-3, the trip's shape replaces the glance (27 Oct 2026)

Reviewer: `travel-design-reviewer`, Opus 5.5. Branch `public/UXB-3`, commit `da339451` (builder: Sonnet).
Against: spec [trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md](../../features/trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md) 4.1 to 4.7, 13.6, UXF-1, UXF-2,
UXF-I1 to I3 (owner answers Q293 to Q307); wireframe W5 in
[ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md](ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md); [uxb-components-2026-10-26.md](uxb-components-2026-10-26.md)
3.1 and 3.2; foundation components 6.4; principles P1 to P9; trip-scope-scenarios; keep-from-live-site; product direction
(public site first, no green). Standard applied: the UXB-1 and UXB-2 reviews.

**Verdict: approve with changes.** The shape is the right replacement. Collapsed, it answers "where do we sleep, for how
long, and what kind of trip is this" in one look; open, it is a real table that answers "what is each day like" with
words, not a clock. The clock bar, the 06 / 12 / 18 scale, the drive-share segment and "The plan's day" are gone
(I1 to I3 fixed). Keyboard, focus, remembered state, print and axe are clean. Three things must change before merge: a
row can say "Light day" when the page does not know the walking (C1), the ribbon's short names make some trips unreadable
(M2), and the first open waits about a third of a second with nothing happening (M3).

## 2. Findings

### C1. Critical: "Light day" is claimed on days whose walking is not known, and on flight days

Screens: the open table, every trip with condensed days or stops that are not trails. Patagonia Day 2 "Fly to Ushuaia"
and Day 13 "Fly to Buenos Aires" both read **Light day** while the same row says On foot **Not checked yet**; Patagonia
Day 1, Chamonix Days 2, 4 (Mer de Glace), 7 (Emosson) and 8, Vermont Day 2 the same. Dolomites Day 7 "Lagazuoi and
Scotoni" reads **Light day**, while the Days tab shows 3 h 30 in the Lagazuoi tunnels with a headlamp, then a 2 h stop at
Rifugio Scotoni.

Why it matters: a family picks the light day to rest a tired child. The row contradicts itself ("Not checked yet" beside a
confident "Light day"), and on Lagazuoi it is simply wrong. The shape exists to answer "which are the light days"; a wrong
answer here is worse than none (P6, and the "Not checked yet" honesty rule of spec 8).

Cause: `core/src/trips/shape.ts` counts only `trail` entries for the on-foot half of the rule, treats a day with no
`checkin`/`checkout` entry as not a travel day, and runs the rule on condensed days (`blocks`), which have no entries.

Fix (in `core/src/trips/shape.ts`, which this task may edit as the shape's owner, with tests in
`web/tests/trips/shape.test.tsx`):

1. No Light day when the timeline is condensed (`tl.blocks`), or when `walkKm` is unknown.
2. No Light day on a day with a flight leg or a flight word in its title or entries (the same test as "Flight home"), or
   on the first day.
3. The on-foot half counts every timed activity stop, not only trails: the sum of `durationMin` of entries that are not
   travel, meals, rest, free time, check-in or check-out. Under 2 h with driving under 1 h stays the rule.
4. Unit cases: Patagonia Days 1, 2, 13; Dolomites Day 7; Chamonix Day 4 all without Light day; Rockies Days 10 and 11
   keep it.

Related, not in this diff: the Days tab says "No walk planned" on Dolomites Days 7, 8 and 12 (Lagazuoi tunnels,
Nuvolau, Seceda) because `walkKm` counts trails only. The shape repeats it in its On foot column. Open a row for the
timeline owner: a day whose stops include a walk with no distance says "Walk not measured", not "No walk planned".

### M2. Important: the ribbon's short names make several trips unreadable

Screens: the collapsed shape, all widths.

- Iceland at 1440 (`r-iceland-closed-1440-light`): all 11 one-night segments are about 90 px, so every name is cut:
  Rey, Sel, Vik, Ska, Hofn, Egi, Myv, Aku, Gru, Rey, Kef. Nine of eleven are codes a visitor cannot read, at desktop.
- Iceland at 390 (`r-iceland-closed-390-dark`): eleven segments of about 30 px; "Hof" and "Myv" are **clipped** mid-word.
- Patagonia at 1440: Buenos Aires becomes "Bue" twice. Dolomites at 1440: Bolzano becomes "Bol" in a 75 px segment.
- Rockies at 390: Banff becomes "Ban" (wireframe W5 shows "Banff" whole at 390), Kananaskis becomes "Kan".

Why it matters: "Where do we sleep" is the first question the ribbon answers. A code needs a manual (P4), and the ring-road
trip is exactly the "many short stays" case the shape must scale to (P1).

Fix:

1. Shorten a name only when the name does not fit, not when the segment is under a fixed width. Keep the container query,
   but key it per segment: the server sets `data-fit="s|m|l|xl"` from the name's length (up to 6, 10, 16, more
   characters), and the stylesheet hides `.shape-full` under 56, 96, 140 and 200 px for those classes. At 1440 that keeps
   Selfoss, Vik, Hofn, Myvatn, Akureyri, Bolzano, Banff whole.
2. The short form: a single word keeps up to 6 letters whole ("Banff", "Myvatn") and longer single words take 4 letters
   and a full stop ("Grun."); a name of two or more words takes its initials ("BA", "LL", "KV"). Drop the leading-article
   rule only if "El Chalten" then reads "EC" in a segment where "Chalten" fits; keep it otherwise.
3. Never clip: a segment narrower than its short form shows the nights number only.
4. When a trip has more than 6 bases, or any segment would fall under 40 px, add one line under the ribbon in `--t-meta`
   `--muted` that names them in order with nights: "Reykjavik 1 · Selfoss 1 · Vik 1 · ...". The ribbon then carries only
   nights. Owner decision below (section 4).

### M3. Important: the first open waits about 330 ms with nothing but "Loading the days"

Screens: Summary, first press on "Show all 15 days" or any count phrase, every width.

Measured on a local server with the chunk already fetched (pointer resting on the button for 600 ms first): the body stays
at 0 px for 316 to 336 ms, then grows for about 185 ms (full at 515 to 521 ms). Reduced motion: the same 316 to 326 ms
wait, then a jump. The wait is the same with and without the preload, so it is not the network: it matches React's
reveal throttle on a `Suspense` boundary that shows its fallback first.

Why it matters: P5 asks for a smooth linked open. Half a second from press to content, a third of it blank, reads as a
dead button on a phone, and it will be longer on a real network.

Fix: in `TripShape.tsx`, set `wanted` and `open` inside `startTransition` (React keeps the closed state on screen until
the rows are ready, with no fallback and no throttle), or keep the resolved module in a module-level variable on intent
and render it directly once loaded. Keep "Loading the days" as the status for a slow network only (show it after 300 ms).
Acceptance: with the chunk preloaded, the body starts to grow within 50 ms of the press.

### S1. Should fix: phone rows are four to six lines, not three

Screens: open table, 390 and 320 (`03-...-390-*`). The DaySign column takes about 80 px, so the plan wraps to 3 or 4 lines
(Day 6: five lines); a 15-day table is 1,972 px tall at 390. The plan column also starts at a different x per row (the
sign is narrower for "DAY 11" than "DAY 02"), so the left edge of the text is ragged.

Fix: under 640 px, grid areas `'day base drive' 'title title title' 'notes notes notes'`, with the title and notes starting
at the row's left padding; first column a fixed width (the small DaySign's width) so every base aligns. That gives W5's
three lines. Put the On foot value into the notes line, first, as "1.9 km on foot" (or "Walk not checked yet"): the
spec hides the column on phones, but walking is the figure a family most wants on a phone (P6).

### S2. Should fix: phone count phrases take three rows of pills

Screens: 390 closed and open. Five pills over three rows, about 150 px, before any day. W5 at 390 uses short phrases:
"15 days · 4 long drives · 5 with a booking · 8 highlights · 6 warnings". Fix: `tripShape` returns a `short` text per
phrase; under 640 px render the short text (visible text and accessible name stay the same string, WCAG 2.5.3). Two rows
at 390, two at 320.

### S3. Should fix: each row link is named only "DAY 06"

The link holds only the DaySign, so a screen reader's links list reads "DAY 01, DAY 02, ...". Fix: add an `sr-only` span
inside the link: "Day 6, Jasper townsite: Kinney Lake, open in Days".

### S4. Should fix: the airport test in core is written for one trip

`AIRPORT = /\b(?:airport|flight|fly home|YYC|YEG)\b/i` in `core/src/trips/shape.ts` hard-codes Calgary and Edmonton.
It is right today for Rockies, Dolomites, Iceland, Patagonia and Chamonix, but the next trip will need another code (P1).
Fix: drop the codes; use a `flight` leg, the schedule's day `flight` field, or an entry whose place kind is an airport,
then the words "airport" and "flight".

### S5. Should fix: spec text

Spec 4.2 and the 13.6 acceptance say "7 highlight days" for the Rockies; the right number is 8 (section 3). Update both,
the Rockies day list (Days 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14) and 4.4's 64 px (see section 3).

### P1. Polish: "Warning n" is the colour of the buttons

`--warn` and `--accent-text` are 1.09:1 apart in light (#7A5200, #8A5300) and 1.04:1 in dark (#E0B15C, #E8A94A). In the
Notes column "Warning 1" in bold reads as a link, beside count phrases of the same colour. Fix: give the note the warn
glyph from the Icon set at 14 px before the word (the foundation's warning pattern is glyph and word), and keep the colour.

### P2. Polish: live region wording

"Showing 5 days with a booking of 15 days" reads backwards. Use "Showing 5 of 15 days: days with a booking".

### P3. Polish: base name data

"Kananaskis Village / Peter Lougheed PP" wraps to two lines in the ribbon and repeats on four rows; "PP" is not explained.
A data fix for the researcher ("Kananaskis Village"), not this task.

### Out of scope, seen on this branch

axe's `target-size` flags the Summary highlight cards' day links ("Day 3, Johnston Canyon", five nodes at 1440, one at
390). That is UXB-1's code, not this diff; add it to the UXB-1 fix list.

## 3. Deviations ruled

| Deviation | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Short names under 96 px, not the spec's 64 px | **Accept 96 px as a threshold only with M2's per-name fit.** 64 px cannot hold "village" beside 8 px padding, so the builder was right that 64 is too narrow; but any fixed width is wrong for a short name like "Banff". Update spec 4.4 to "a name shortens only when it does not fit its segment". |
| 8 highlight days, not 7 (Rockies) | **Accept 8.** The wireframe's own Rockies facts list Maligne Lake and Spirit Island on Day 5 among the 13 highlights, and the Summary's highlight card links "Day 5, Maligne Lake and Spirit Island". The spec's 7 left Day 5 out. |
| Dolomites: "+ the flight home on Day 14", Day 14 "Bolzano to Venice" noted Flight home | **Accept.** The Days tab ends Day 14 with "At the airport 3 h before an international flight" at Venice Marco Polo. Day 14 has no base, so its row shows the title only: correct. |
| On foot hidden under 640 px | **Accept the column hidden**, as the spec says, with the value moved into the notes line (S1). |
| Count phrases drawn as pills on phones, dotted-underline text buttons from 640 px | **Accept.** They are filters, and the pill gives a 44 px target with a visible pressed state (fill, border and weight, not colour alone). Shorten the text (S2). |
| Explicit `role="table"`, `rowgroup`, `row`, `cell` on a real `<table>` | **Accept.** Under 640 px the table is `display: block`, which drops table semantics in WebKit and Chromium; the roles keep them. |
| Count phrases use `aria-expanded`, and the phrases not chosen say "collapsed" while the table is open | **Accept** as the components note specifies (each phrase is a disclosure for its filtered view). The live region says what is shown. |

## 4. What the owner should decide

**Short names in the ribbon (proposed as Q310; the coordinator confirms the number).** The approved W5 shows "LL" and "Kan" on phones. With M2's fix, desktop
shows almost every name whole, and phones show initials or a short word for narrow stays. For trips with many one-night
stays (Iceland, 11 bases), even that is a row of codes. Options:

- A. Short forms on narrow segments (M2, steps 1 to 3), nothing else.
- B. **Recommended:** A, plus a names line under the ribbon when a trip has more than 6 bases or a segment under 40 px.
- C. On phones always draw the ribbon without names and always add the names line.

## 5. Checks that passed

- **Glance.** Collapsed: ribbon, tail, count line; the heading and the toggle on one row. The ribbon is the largest
  element, the count line second in body size, the toggle smallest: the right order. Open: a table, caption "The trip's
  shape, 15 days", columns Day, Base and plan, Driving (bar and value), On foot, Notes. No "The plan's day" anywhere.
- **Party.** With `p=family-7` the rows follow the party (Day 6 becomes the family version, "Adults' hike" goes); the
  count line recomputes from the timelines on screen.
- **Filters.** A phrase opens the table filtered, its pill or text shows pressed, focus stays on the pressed phrase, the
  caption reads "The trip's shape, 8 highlight days of 15" with "Show all days"; clearing moves focus to "15 days".
- **Keyboard.** Closed: toggle, five phrases, then the next section (the hidden body is `visibility: hidden`, so it takes
  no Tab stops). Open: toggle, phrases, then each row link. The row has a 2 px focus ring (`:has(:focus-visible)`).
  Enter on Day 6: Days tab, `?view=day#day-6`, focus on the day's `h3`. Back: Summary, shape still open, scroll restored.
- **Remembered.** `travel.tripui` gains `shapeOpen` for the trip only; a reload opens it with no animation; another trip
  stays closed. Nothing else is stored.
- **Motion.** Normal: about 13 frames over 185 ms after the wait in M3. Reduced: one jump. The chevron turns in
  `--d-base` and stops under reduced motion.
- **Targets (WCAG 2.2 AA, 2.5.8).** Toggle 130 x 44, phrases 64 to 176 x 44, "Show all days" 44 tall, each row a full-width
  hit area 58 to 130 px tall. axe `wcag2a, wcag2aa, wcag21aa, wcag22aa` (and best-practice when open) on `#trip-shape`:
  0 violations closed and open, 1440 and 390, light and dark.
- **Print.** The ribbon, tail and count line print as text; the toggle, the buttons and the table do not (the days follow).
- **Tokens.** Colour from `themes.css` only; no colour per base; no green, no purple.
- **Privacy.** No party, ages or home data in the shape's HTML or storage. The visitor's rows are computed in the browser.
- **Budget.** The builder's before and after reports agree with the diff: start JS 190.3 to 190.1 KB, lazy within 1 s
  132.8 to 123.4 KB on Summary and 429.0 to 419.6 KB on Days. Days fell too because the old `ssr: false` clock chunk
  loaded on every tab. Nothing is missing from Days: the strip has lived only in the Summary since UXB-1; each day's span
  is in its Days header, the "Worth the journey" flag became Highlight, the version column became the version note.
