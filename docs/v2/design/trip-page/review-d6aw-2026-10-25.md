# Design review: D6a-W, seasons for the six overview-only trips (25 Oct 2026)

Branch `public/D6aW`, commit `ba0023d` (not merged). Reviewer: `travel-design-reviewer`, Opus. BACKLOG 15.113, PLAN R-111.

## Verdict

**Ship after fixes.** Reading `data/seasons/<trip>.json` through the adapter is right (P9: one boundary, build time, no new
client load; P8: only the light season block reaches list rows), the six band layouts hold at 390 and 1440 in both themes,
contrast passes everywhere, and the month filter counts are correct. Three things block the merge: the season chip
overflows its card on the trips list and on the map card, the "Valid for" claim is shown without saying it is only the
plan's own window when opening dates were never checked, and one label is two calendar days written in words.

## MUST (fix before merge)

**M1. The season chip overflows the card (trips list cards and the map card).** Critical at 390 and 1440.
- Seen: `card-norway-summer-2028-{390,1440}-{light,dark}.png` cut the chip at "(dates still" and "(date";
  `card-quebec-2026-1440-*.png` cuts "early Octob"; `review/mapcard-norway-1440-light.png` cuts the chip at "early Jul"
  and pushes the whole text column past the card, so the meta line ("Lofoten, Se") and the "Open the plan" button are
  clipped too.
- Cause: `.chip` is `white-space: nowrap` (`web/components/ui/ui.css:167`); the longer D6a labels are wider than the
  card column. Seed labels happened to fit.
- Why it matters: a family glancing at the card (P3) reads half a season and, on the map card, loses the button that
  opens the plan.
- Fix (smallest): a season chip may wrap. Add a modifier, for example `.chip-wrap { white-space: normal; height: auto;
  min-height: 24px; padding-block: 3px; text-align: left; }`, and use it for the calendar chip in
  `web/lib/entities/descriptors/trip.tsx:29` (card chips), `web/components/trips/TripsMap.tsx:132` and
  `web/components/home/Strips.tsx:37`. Do not truncate with an ellipsis: the season is the claim, it must be read whole.
  Also fixes M3's longer chip text. Add the Norway map card at 390 and 1440 to the e2e no-overflow check.

**M2. A plan-only window says "Valid for" with no marker, while openings were not checked.** Important.
- Seen: hero stat "VALID FOR / Last days of September into early October" (`quebec-2026-page-*`), band kicker "THIS PLAN
  IS VALID FOR", table column "Valid for", card chips: all identical to the researched seed windows next to them
  (`table-1440-light.png`: "Mid-February" sits in the same column as Dolomites "Mid-July to end of August").
- The data says otherwise: every one of the six has `verdict: null` and unknowns such as "Opening seasons of the plan's
  sights in this window" (Quebec), "Lift and rail season dates for the plan's lifts" (Switzerland), "Opening dates of the
  plan's buses, ferries and roads" (Norway). The seed windows are reasoned from openings ("All lifts and high trails are
  open"). Here the window is when the plan happened to be written for.
- The why line carries "The plan's own ..." on five trips, but it is prose under a large heading, and it is absent from
  the hero, the table, the chips and the strip's screen-reader name. Owner rule: a gap is never a yes.
- Fix (smallest, no new component):
  1. The adapter passes the basis: add `basis: 'plan' | 'researched'` to `Season` (`web/lib/public/types.ts`), set from
     `s.months.basis` in `web/lib/public/seasons.ts:77` and `'researched'` for seed seasons in `trips.ts:200`.
  2. Hero stat: label "Planned for" instead of "Valid for" when `basis === 'plan'`
     (`web/components/trips/TripCover.tsx:18`).
  3. Band: keep the kicker, add one line under the label, in `.trip-why` style or a `notice` Banner:
     "The window this plan was written for. Other months and opening dates have not been checked yet."
     (`web/components/trips/SeasonBand.tsx:27`). Then drop the regex prefix at `seasons.ts:77`: the marker comes from
     the basis, not from sniffing for the word "plan" (a why line that says "plan long drives" would pass the regex today).
  4. Strip name: `SeasonStrip` says "Open February" (`web/components/ui/SeasonStrip.tsx:18`), which reads as "the place
     is open only in February". Accept an optional `label` prop and pass "Months this plan was written for: February".
- Decision on the builder's open question: a visible marker is a **MUST** on the trip page (items 2 and 3) and for the
  strip name (item 4). On the card chip and the table cell it is a **SHOULD** (S1): the "Overview only" honest chip
  already sits next to it, so the card does not lie, but it does not say why the window is narrow.

**M3. "Last day of September and first of October" is two calendar dates in words.** Important.
- `data/seasons/new-hampshire-stopover-2026.json:22` (and the window label at `:36`). It passes `DATE_TEXT` because there
  is no numeral, but the dateless rule (`docs/v2/design/public-trips-dateless.md` lines 3 and 13) says a template shows a
  window ("a long weekend in mid-October"), never days. A family copying a template reads this as the only two days it
  works.
- Fix (data lane, not the builder, who may not edit `data/seasons/`): label "Two days at the turn of September and
  October". Same pass: move "(dates still open)" out of the Norway label (`norway-summer-2028.json:28`) into the why
  line; the label becomes "Last week of June into early July", which matches its own window label at `:43` and shortens
  the chip.
- Follow-up (SHOULD S4): extend `DATE_TEXT` in `core/src/privacy/dateless.ts` to catch "(first|last) day of <Month>",
  "<ordinal> of <Month>" and "first of <Month>", with tests, so the rule enforces this rather than review.

## Checks that pass

- **Band at 390 and 1440, both themes:** label, why, strip, weather tiles and notes lay out cleanly for all six. The long
  label "Last week of June into early July (dates still open)" wraps to two lines at 390 with no clipping and one line at
  1440 (`norway-summer-2028-band-*`). The hero stat card wraps it to three lines at 390 and stays inside the card.
- **"Not converted to days" banner:** shown under the band on all six (Switzerland, Costa Rica, New Hampshire, Niagara,
  Norway, Quebec), and the old "Overview only. No days and no season ..." notice is gone. Order is right: the claim,
  then the gap.
- **Season filter:** options come from the data, so "Season not set yet" (`none`) disappears when no trip lacks a
  season, and returns by itself if one does (`trip.tsx:93-94`). Counts in the When popover (Feb 2, Mar 1, Apr 1, May 1,
  Jun 2, Jul 4, Aug 4, Sep 8, Oct 4) match a recount from the snapshot plus the six files. Jan, Nov and Dec are absent
  because no trip covers them; correct.
- **Sun ranges:** no new sun text appears on the six pages beyond the daylight tile from the file. `sunMonths` now holds
  the six trips' months, but overview trips have no days and no schedule (`trips.ts:225`, `:391`), so nothing else uses
  it. The daylight tile times (06:40, 18:30 and so on) are clock times, which the dateless rule allows.
- **Dateless:** every public string from the six files was read on the served pages. No numeric dates; only M3 breaks
  the rule in spirit.
- **Keyboard and names:** the band is a `section` named by its `h2` ("This plan is valid for"); notes list is named
  "Worth knowing"; the strip has a name (see M2 item 4 for its wording). No new interactive element.
- **No green, no new colour:** nothing added to `themes.css`.

## Answers to the builder's three questions

1. **Is a visible "plan's own window" marker needed?** Yes. MUST on the trip page: hero stat label "Planned for" and one
   line in the band, driven by a `basis` field rather than the regex; MUST for the strip's accessible name. SHOULD on
   the card chip and the table cell. See M2 and S1.
2. **Zod in `web/`, or keep the hand-written validator?** Neither as it stands. Do not add `zod` to `web/package.json`:
   the repo rule is that core owns schemas (CLAUDE.md "Platform"; overview spec 2.2 puts `trip-windows/1` in
   `core/src/schema/windows.ts`), and core already depends on `zod` 4.6.7. Put `trip-season-basic/1` in
   `core/src/schema/seasons.ts`, export it with the other schemas to `data/schema/generated/`, validate
   `data/seasons/*.json` in check-all, and have `web/lib/public/seasons.ts` import the parser from `@travel/core` (web
   already imports `@travel/core/...` on the server side, for example `web/lib/server/source.ts`). Bundle impact: none on
   the client either way, since the loader uses `node:fs` and runs only at build time; the cost of a web copy is a second
   schema that can drift from core, not bytes. The hand-written validator can ship now as a stopgap; the core move is a
   follow-up row under 15.113 (or with OVR-C, which writes the windows schema next to it).
3. **Does the card chip need the word "plan"?** Yes, as "· our plan", the same suffix the suitability chips on the same
   card use (S1). It depends on M1 so the longer text wraps instead of being cut.
