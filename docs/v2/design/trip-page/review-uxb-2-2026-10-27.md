Reviewer: `travel-design-reviewer`, Opus 5.5. Branch `public/UXB-2`, commit `c9d06008` (builder: Sonnet 5.5).
Against: spec [trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md](../../features/trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md) 5.2 to 5.5, 10.2, 13.5 (owner
answers Q293 to Q307); wireframe W3 in [ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md](ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md);

**Verdict: approve with changes.** The bar is the right shape: one quiet line, one Edit, an inline editor with no
"Who is going" toggle and no Youngest stepper (I4, I5 fixed), ages that never leave the device, and a clean axe run.
Three things must change before merge (section 2). None is a privacy or safety failure.

## 2. Must fix (before merge)

**M1. Important. A baby reads as "0".** Screens: the bar ("3 adults · 3 children (4, 7, 0) · Standard", shot 04 and
mine F "1 adult · 1 child (0)"), the live line ("youngest 0"), and downstream the day rows ("Fits age 0", "Not suited to
age 0", mine E 1440). The select says "Under 1", so the page contradicts itself, and "0" next to "children" looks like a
bug to a parent. Fix: in `core/src/trips/party.ts` `childrenText`, write `under 1` for 0 ("3 children (4, 7, under 1)");
in `PartyEditor.profileLine` say "youngest under 1"; add a unit test. The day rows' "age 0" belong to the timeline's
suitability words: log it in `docs/v2/build/UI-NOTES.md` for UXB-F ("Fits a child under 1"). Spec 5.3 already says 0
reads "under 1".

**M2. Important. The copy sheet does not share the party (brief 13.5 steps 5, acceptance "the copy sheet opens with
both ages").** `CopySheet.tsx`, `Travellers.tsx`, `timelineState.ts` and `copies.ts` are untouched. Today a family that
sets 2 adults and children 7 and 10 on the page and then taps Copy to my trips gets a sheet that knows only the youngest,
and a sheet that caps adults at 10 while the page allows 12 (a party of 11 cannot be copied as set). That breaks spec 5.4
"the copy sheet and the codec stay consistent" and P4 (no manual needed: the visitor would have to enter the party twice).
Fix, either: (a) do it in this branch: extract `web/components/actions/Travellers.tsx` using the new `ui/Stepper`
(adults 1 to 12, children 0 to 8) and native age selects, prefill from `travel.party` through `readParty`, write back
through `writeParty`; or (b) the coordinator opens a row UXB-2b now (Sonnet, about 2 h, same owner files) and the owner is
told the copy sheet still asks separately. I recommend (a): the owner will test Copy straight after the bar.

**M3. Important. Phones never say "Your party".** `.pb-note` is `display: none` under 900 px, so a visitor on a phone
who lands with a kept party (Q295) sees "3 adults · 2 children (4, 7) · Standard" and a Reset link, with nothing saying
this is their device's party and not the author's. The owner accepted Q295 on the trade-off "the bar says 'Your party'".
Fix: under 640 px put the note in the label row: "PLAN IT FOR · YOUR PARTY" (`--t-cap`, `--muted`, 6.94 light / 7.45
dark on `--surface`); for the plan as written keep the label alone (the cover already says "From about 6").

## 3. Should fix (this round or UXB-F)

- **S1. The bar's Edit/Done is 30 px tall** (measured 73 x 30 at every width). W3 and uxb-components 4.3 say a 44 px
  target. It passes WCAG 2.5.8 (24 px), and axe is clean, but it is the one control of the bar and sits beside 44 px
  Reset and offer buttons. Fix: `Button size="md"` or `.pb-edit { min-height: var(--touch-min) }`.
- **S2. Every editor tap is a history entry.** Two taps on More adults and one age change pushed three entries; Back then
  walks `?p=family-5&a=4` to `?a=4` to `?a=3` before leaving the trip. A parent who sets a family of four presses Back
  five or six times (P5: Back returns to the previous view, not the previous keystroke). Landing is right (the device
  party is applied with a replace; Back from it leaves the page). Fix: in the editor, push on the first change of an open
  session and replace after it; Done or Escape ends the session.
- **S3. The rail says a different party from the bar on a shared link.** Bar: "Shared plan: family, youngest 6";
  rail: "2 adults, 1 child · Standard" (D 1440 dark). Fix: `setRailParty` carries a `shared` flag and the rail says
  "Shared plan · Standard".
- **S4. Phone shared state is crowded at 320.** Four lines of value, Reset in its own column, and the offer wraps to two
  centred lines (07-320). Fix: under 640 px hide Reset while the offer shows (the offer and Edit are the choices that
  matter on a shared link, and Reset comes back once the visitor changes anything), and left-align the offer text with `text-align: start`.
- **S5. Focus after closing an editor opened from the rail** goes to the bar's Edit, far above where the visitor was on
  Day 9. Fix: remember the opener and return focus there (rail Edit), scrolling the rail back into view.
- **S6. The lazy-JS line rose** (+0.6 KB Summary, +1.4 KB Days, gzipped, within 1 s of hydration; start JS fell by
  250 B, so D12 holds). Cause: `lib/storage/keys.ts` imports `cleanParty` from core `party.ts`, and the tab shell's lazy
  `import('@/lib/storage/keys')` therefore pulls `party.ts` into the Summary line; `PlanBar.tsx` imports
  `PACE_WORD` and `partyShortText` statically, which pulls it into the Days line. Right fix: split by reader, not by
  bundler trick. (1) `web/lib/storage/keys.ts` becomes `tripUi.ts` (imports `tabs.ts` only) and `partyKey.ts` (imports
  `party.ts`), with `keys.ts` re-exporting both for tests; the tab shell imports `tripUi.ts`. (2) The words move to
  `core/src/trips/partyWords.ts` (counts, `childrenText`, `partyShortText`, `PACE_WORD`, no landing rule, no clean),
  re-exported from `party.ts`; `PlanBar` imports only that. Then the `DEVICE_KEYS` copy in `ProfileBar.tsx` can import
  the key names from the light module. Owner: the UXB-C seat (B2) because `keys.ts` and `party.ts` are its contract;
  Sonnet is enough with this note as the spec (mechanical split, boundary test unchanged). Not a merge blocker.
- **S7. Dead CSS.** `web/styles/timeline.css` still carries the old card: `.tl-profile`, `-head`, `-h`, `-sub`, `-edit`,
  `-line`, `-controls`, `.tl-stepper` and its phone rules (lines 9 to 35 and 203 to 212). Only `.tl-profile-note` is
  still used. Remove the rest (it is UXB-2's former block, spec 12.6), keep `.tl-profile-note`.

## 4. Polish

- P-1. The pace segmented stretches to about 640 px at 1440 with "Full" left-aligned in a wide third segment (04-1440,
  C-1440). Cap `.pe-pace .seg` at `max-width: 24rem`.
- P-2. The unset-age honesty note is said twice in the open editor ("Until an age is chosen the plan uses 10" under the
  selects and "Age not set for a child, planning for 10" in the live line, 05 and 09). Keep the live line; drop the hint's
  second sentence. Also drop "0 to 17; 'Under 1' for 0", which the select already shows.
- P-3. Label levels mix: "ADULTS", "CHILDREN", "AGES (NEEDED FOR EACH CHILD)" in caps, "Child 1 age" in mono sentence
  case. Fine at 1440; on a phone the caps heading over the ages reads louder than the controls. Rename it "Ages" and
  keep the per-child labels.
- P-4. With youngest under 1 the live line gives no version counts ("Showing the plan for 3 adults and 3 children
  (youngest 0), standard pace. Written for families with a child from about 6."): `versionCounts` hides a single
  version. Say it: "Easy version on all 15 days."
- P-5. The collapsed value is the display face (`--t-row`, Big Shoulders 800) on desktop and serif `--t-small` 600 on
  phones. Both are legible; one face would read as one component. Leave until UXB-R1 sees the whole top of the page.

## 5. What passes, with evidence

- **Hierarchy (P3, P7).** Collapsed: label, the party as a sentence, one note, one Edit, on one 56 px row at 1440 and two
  rows at 390. The editor opens inline under it, never a modal. The sticky rail repeats the words in short form and
  truncates the pace first ("12 adults, 8 children · Stand…", E 320), as W3 asks.
- **Editor.** Steppers 44 x 44 at every width; bounds use `aria-disabled` and keep focus ("Fewer adults" at 1 and "More
  children" at 8 kept focus; ArrowUp from the bound stepped to 2). Adding a child moves focus to its select; removing one
  leaves focus on "Fewer children". "Choose age" is offered only until an age is chosen, and the bar says
  "2 children (10, age not set)", the live line "Age not set for a child, planning for 10": honest, and matches FAMAGE10.
  Escape and Done close and return focus to Edit with `aria-expanded="false"`. The live line and the stepper outputs are
  `aria-live="polite"`.
- **Shared link and "Plan as written" (Q295).** `?p=family-6` shows "Shared plan: family, youngest 6" with "Use my party:
  2 adults, 2 children"; the kept party is never applied over the link; using the offer writes `p=family-7` and no ages.
  Reset is "Plan as written" in its title and stops the kept party applying again to that trip (`asWritten`).
- **Storage blocked.** No page error; the editor works for the visit and says "This browser is not keeping data, so your
  party works for this visit only."
- **Privacy (P9, navigation-and-mine 2.3, spec 15).** 87 requests while editing: no age, no `travel.party`, no children
  list in any URL or body. The URL carries only `p=family-<youngest>` and `a=<n>`. `localStorage` holds only
  `travel.party` and `travel.tripui` (no ages in the latter). No household data in any default or shown text; the
  default bar words are the trip's own profile.
- **Motion.** The editor grows by grid rows over `--d-base` with `--e-std`; with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the
  transition is `0s` (G).
- **Accessibility.** axe with `wcag22aa`: 0 violations collapsed, shared and open, at 320 and 1440, light and dark.
  Targets: steppers 44 x 44, offer 44 tall, Reset 54 x 44, rail Edit 28 px visual with a 44 px `::after` hit area; the
  bar's Edit is the exception (S1).
- **Colour, tokens only, no green or purple.** Approved pairs (light / dark):

| Pair | Where | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ink` on `--surface` | bar value, stepper values | 16.06 | 13.83 |
| `--ink-2` on `--surface` | live line, age labels | 12.09 | 10.76 |
| `--muted` on `--surface` | label, note, hints, device note | 6.94 | 7.45 |
| `--accent-text` on `--surface` | offer, Reset | 6.33 | 7.99 |
| `--accent-text` on `--bg` | rail Edit | 5.85 | 8.83 |
| `--ink` on `--bg` | rail party words | 14.85 | 15.28 |
| `--line-2` on `--surface` (non-text, 3:1) | stepper and offer borders | 3.67 | 3.42 |
| `--focus` ring on `--surface` | focus | 16.06 | 13.83 |

  The bound state at 45% opacity is an inactive control (exempt from 1.4.3) and is also announced by `aria-disabled`.
