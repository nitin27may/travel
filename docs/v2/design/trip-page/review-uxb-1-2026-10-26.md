## Verdict

**Approve with changes.** The tab shell is sound: ARIA tabs with manual activation, roving focus, Home and End, Back
returning to the previous tab, the remembered tab, storage blocked, the tab bar holding its sticky line on a switch, the
one-sticky-row rule on phones, five tabs on one row at 320 with no page overflow, the right tab sets per trip state, and
colour from tokens only. Three things must change before merge: a safety line the spec names is hidden behind "2 more"
(P2), the small Days fixes I12 and I13 were not built, and deep links now flash the Summary before switching (a
regression for every `#day-N` link and for Mine's Open). Two data problems in the Summary should be fixed before the
owner sees screenshots, because the owner will see them first: half the highlight cards say "No licensed photo yet" while
the Photos tab holds photos of those exact places, and Moraine Lake (the place the owner named) is not in the four cards a
phone shows at rest.

## 2. Findings

Ranked critical, important or polish. Must-fix are the important ones that block the merge; should-fix are important
or polish items for UXB-F or the named owner.

### Must fix before merge

**M1. Important. Summary, "Know before you go", Rockies: the Moraine Lake road ban is hidden behind "2 more".**
- What: `knowBeforeYouGo` tests the word list `SAFETY_NOTE` against the note text only. The Moraine Lake line's danger is
  in its name ("Moraine Lake Road private vehicle ban and commercial/Roam options"); its note ("Have the Parks Canada
  shuttle reservation as primary...") matches nothing, so it falls into the disclosure with the Lake Louise parking line.
  Measured in the built HTML: the `details` holds exactly those two lines.
- Why it matters: a family that does not open "2 more" drives to Moraine Lake and is turned away at the road, on the
  day the owner calls the trip's highlight. Spec 3.5 names this line as always at rest (P2 safety exception).
- Fix: (a) test `name + ' ' + text`, and add `ban|banned|not allowed|no private|closed to` to the list; (b) a unit test
  that pins the Rockies: Larch Valley and Moraine Lake road in `atRest`; (c) keep the UI-NOTES row so a `safety` flag on
  the booking (UXB-C, from the capture record) replaces the word list. On the heuristic itself: acceptable as a stop-gap
  only because its failure mode is pinned by that test; a word list cannot be the long-term rule for P2.

**M2. Important. Days tab, every day header: findings I12 and I13 are not fixed.**
- What: the header still reads "07:05-07:27 · 19:43-20:16" with only a sun glyph, and transfer days still say "no
  walking" (6 times in the Rockies HTML; 0 "No walk planned"). Brief 13.4 step 3 assigns both to UXB-1; the hand-back
  does not mention them.
- Why it matters: a reader cannot tell which range is sunrise and which is sunset without decoding the glyph (P4, no
  manual needed); "no walking" on a travel day reads like a rule.
- Fix: in `DayTimeline.tsx` line about 198 and the sun line: "Sunrise 07:05 to 07:27 · Sunset 19:43 to 20:16" and "No
  walk planned". `DayTimeline.tsx` is not in UXB-1's owned list, but the brief's step requires it and no other open task
  owns it; the coordinator should grant the two lines.

**M3. Important. Any deep link to a tab (`?view=day`, `#day-N`, `?view=bookings`, a remembered tab, Mine's Open once
UXB-2 adds `view=day`): the Summary paints first, then the page switches.**
- What: the static page always renders the Summary; the tab is decided after hydration. Measured per animation frame:
  Summary selected at 33 ms, Days at 187 ms on this Mac; with 4x CPU throttling (a mid phone) Summary at 79 ms and Days
  at 766 ms; `#day-11` switches at 695 ms and then jumps 18,000 px. Before UXB-1 a `#day-N` link painted the days
  directly, so this is a regression.
- Why it matters: every shared day link and every highlight day link opened in a new tab shows the wrong content for most
  of a second, then a full-page jump (P5: open and close are smooth and linked). Families share "look at Day 11" links.
- Fix: the builder's own UI-NOTES idea, done now: a tiny inline script before the tab bar that reads `view` and the hash
  and sets `data-tab` on `.trip-tabs` (or the `html` element), plus CSS that shows the matching panel and hides the
  Summary when `data-tab` names another tab; hydration then confirms the same state. It costs a few hundred bytes of
  HTML and no start JS. It cannot apply the remembered tab before paint without reading storage; that case may keep the
  switch (it is a returning visitor, and the URL is rewritten). Acceptance: no frame shows the Summary for `?view=day`
  and `#day-11` under 4x CPU.

### Should fix (before the owner sees the screenshots)

**S1. Important. Summary, Highlights, Rockies and Dolomites: half the cards say "No licensed photo yet" while the Photos
tab has photos of those places.**
- What: 4 of 8 Rockies cards (Athabasca Glacier and Columbia Icefield, Icefields Parkway, Peyto Lake Lookout, Maligne
  Lake and Spirit Island) and the Dolomites' Tre Cime loop show the glyph placeholder. The matcher in
  `core/src/trips/summary.ts` (UXB-C) accepts a gallery section only if the section names the whole place name; the
  sections are "Icefields Parkway: Bow Lake and Peyto Lake", "Columbia Icefield and the Athabasca Glacier", "Jasper,
  Maligne Lake and Maligne Canyon", and the photos inside carry captions such as "Peyto Lake from the viewpoint" and
  "Spirit Island on Maligne Lake".
- Why it matters: the Summary is the 90% visitor's first screen (P7); four grey boxes in the first highlight row read as
  a broken page, and the owner asked for "highlights of the trip (Lake Louise, Moraine Lake etc.)" with photos.
- Fix (UXB-C owner, core): match on the photo, not only the section: take the place name's core (drop "Lookout",
  "scenic drive", "and ..."), find the first photo whose caption or whose section names that core as a whole phrase, and
  store that photo's index. Acceptance: Rockies 8 of 8 cards at rest with a photo; Dolomites 5 of 5 where the gallery
  holds one.

**S2. Important. Summary, Highlights on phones: Moraine Lake, the place the owner named, is behind "Show 4 more".**
- What: `highlightsAtRest` picks photo cards first, then sorts the chosen eight back into day order; the phone shows the
  first four by day (Johnston Canyon, Lake Louise, Athabasca, Icefields Parkway), two of them without a photo. Once S1
  gives every card a photo, the same rule picks the first eight by day and drops Moraine Lake (Day 11) entirely, so the
  builder's stated reason for the rule stops holding.
- Why it matters: the phone visitor sees two placeholders and not the trip's signature place (P7, P2: the four at rest
  must be the best four).
- Fix: rank by the curator's order, which the gallery already records (its sections run Moraine Lake, Lake Louise,
  Banff, Johnston Canyon, Icefields Parkway and Peyto Lake, Columbia Icefield, Maligne Lake, ...), then photo, then day;
  draw in rank order, not day order (W2 draws Moraine Lake first). The day links stay on each card. Pin the Rockies'
  first four in `summary-tab.test.tsx`: Moraine Lake, Lake Louise, Johnston Canyon, Peyto Lake Lookout (or whichever
  four the curator order gives).

**S3. Important. Process: the five owner questions Q303 to Q307 are unanswered, and the build implements all five
recommendations.** Q303 (one sticky row on phones), Q304 (no Days count), Q305 (print adds Bookings), Q306 (phone
highlights 2 x 2 with "Show 4 more"), Q307 (Photos tab from 6 photos). Nothing on `main` records an answer. The choices
are the recommended ones and I would keep them, but the coordinator must put Q303 to Q307 to the owner with these
screenshots and say they show the recommendations. Not a blocker for showing screenshots; it is what the screenshots are
for.

**S4. Important. Summary, the Bases card and the cover disagree on the same screen: cover "5 bases", card "4 bases",
standfirst "Five bases from Banff to Kananaskis".** The card counts distinct bases (Lake Louise village twice), the cover
counts stays. A planner reading both wonders which is right (T1 honesty). Fix in `text.ts`: card sub "5 stays, 14
nights" so the two numbers reconcile; the cover can stay until UXB-3's ribbon, which shows the five stays.

**S5. Important. Summary, "See all 13" next to Highlights: opens Stops unfiltered (95 rows, starting with Stays).** It
promises 13 and delivers 95. Until OVR-2 adds the "Worth the journey" filter, drop the link and keep "13 places worth the
journey" as text (P4, never a control that misleads), or word it "Open Stops".

**S6. Important (accessibility). Summary on desktop: two invisible focus stops.** The lead's "More" and the highlights'
"Show 4 more" are `sr-only` checkboxes that stay in the tab order at every width, while their labels are `display: none`
from 640 px. A keyboard user on desktop tabs onto a control with no visible focus that does nothing; a screen reader
hears "Show the whole summary, checkbox". Fix: use the static disclosure the foundation chose (components 6.4,
`details`), or at least `display: none` on `.sum-toggle` from 640 px. The checkbox pattern is also announced as a
checkbox rather than a disclosure on phones; `details` fixes both.

**S7. Important (accessibility). Highlight day links: the link text is the bare number.** "Days 3 and 10" renders the
links "3" and "10"; a screen reader's link list reads "3, 10, 4, 8, ...". The tap area on a coarse pointer is about 20 x
40 px. Fix: link text "Day 3" (and "Day 10" in "Days 3 and 10" as two links "Day 3", "Day 10"), with
`aria-label="Day 3, Johnston Canyon"`; extend the coarse-pointer hit area to 44 px tall and at least 24 px wide (WCAG
2.5.8).

### Polish

- **P-1. 1440, count cards:** six cards wrap 5 + 1, leaving "38 photos" alone on a second row. Use `repeat(6, 1fr)`
  from 1100 px or a 3 x 2 grid.
- **P-2. 390, Costa Rica:** with two tabs, `flex: 1 1 auto` stretches each tab to half the row and the underline to 170
  px. Stretch only when five tabs need it; two tabs sit left at content width as on desktop.
- **P-3. Dolomites, phone:** "Show 1 more" hides a single card. Show all when the hidden count is 2 or fewer.
- **P-4. Summary standfirst** repeats the cover's standfirst one screen below it (Rockies, Costa Rica). The spec asks
  for it (W2), so this goes to the planner: drop it from the Summary, or drop the lead's first sentence instead.
- **P-5. Lazy panels:** `aria-busy` is never set while a chunk loads (spec 3.4); the plan bar's loading shell is an empty
  `<nav aria-label="Days">` landmark; Photos' `aria-controls` points to no element until the tab is first opened (probe:
  "Photos 38" has a missing target). Render each panel's empty `TripPanel` shell always, set `aria-busy` on it while

- **P-6. Prefetch:** only `onFocus` prefetches. Safari does not focus a button on click, so on iPhone the first Days or

- **P-7. The glance pops in below the fold** after hydration ("Loading the trip at a glance", then about 1,300 px of

- **P-8. No-script:** the tab bar is hidden rather than turned into in-page links (spec 3.4); Stops and Photos are absent

- **P-9. Trip HTML:** Rockies grew 13,984 B (1,143,077 to 1,157,061, against `main`'s build, which also has TRP-GL-F),
  Dolomites 3,714 B. The spec estimated 4 to 6 KB for the Summary plus about 1 KB for Bookings. Not budgeted; report the

## 4. Start JS: is the 10,000 B headroom real?

**The number is measured, not a cap.** `web/scripts/budget-report.mjs` has no cap or rounding on start JS: headroom is
`round((200 - kb) * 1024)`, with `kb` the gzipped sum of the unique same-origin `<script src>` tags in each trip page's
HTML (`startScripts`). On this branch's build it reports **190.3 KB, headroom 9,940 B** (Algonquin is the heaviest trip
page). On `main`'s current build (which includes TRP-GL-F) it reports 199.7 KB, headroom 309 B. So about 9.6 KB gzipped
left the HTML script tags.

**What it measures.** Only scripts named in the HTML. A `next/dynamic(..., { ssr: false })` chunk is not in the HTML (no
script tag, no preload link in this build), so the gate does not see it, even when the page renders it at once. On
landing:

| Landing (390 px) | JS transferred, `main` | JS transferred, UXB-1 |
|---|---|---|
| Rockies, no `view` (Summary on UXB-1, Days on `main`) | 340,131 B, 26 files | 331,646 B, 27 files |
| Rockies, `?view=day` | 340,131 B, 26 files | 341,828 B, 29 files |
| Algonquin, no `view` | 336,039 B, 25 files | 331,646 B, 27 files |

(Uncompressed bytes as served by `next start`; same method on both builds.) On a Summary landing the page now downloads
about 8.5 KB less (about 2.5 KB gzipped): Stops and the plan bar really are deferred. On a Days landing it downloads
slightly more than before, because the glance and the plan bar arrive as separate chunks straight after hydration.

**Ruling.** The headroom is real for what the gate protects: the code that must parse and run before the page is
interactive, and the shell now hydrates about 9.6 KB lighter. It is **not** 9.6 KB of new room: most of it is code that
still loads on the first view (the glance on the Summary, the plan bar on Days). Two consequences:
1. Do not spend the freed headroom on start code in UXB-2 to UXB-6. Treat the working headroom as the 392 B the brief
   started from plus the real Summary saving (about 2.5 KB gzipped), until UXB-3 replaces the glance chunk.
2. The gate can be satisfied by wrapping any component in `ssr: false`, which also costs a pop-in. Ask the BUD owner to add
   a second line to `budget:report`: "JS loaded within 1 s of hydration on the Rockies Summary and Days landings", so the
   next task cannot move bytes and call it a saving.

## 5. Principles

- **P1 (scale):** fine at 13 highlights (8 at rest), 95 stops (count only), 7 bookings (no filter row, as the note says).
- **P2 (progressive disclosure):** right shape; fails on the safety line (M1).
- **P3 (a glance, then a few clicks):** the count cards answer "how many trails, how many bookings" at rest and each is
  one link into its tab. Pass, with S5.
- **P4 (no manual needed):** labels are plain; the bare-number day links (S7) and the cover and card base counts (S4)
  need fixing.
- **P5 (linked open and close):** Back and the sticky line are right; the deep-link flash (M3) is the exception.
- **P6 (right metadata at the right moment):** "none has a published opening date yet" is honest and at rest. Pass.
- **P7 (the 90% goal):** the Summary is the first tab and answers "what is this trip"; S1 and S2 weaken its first
  impression on the two full trips.
- **P8 (protect the catalogue):** the Summary reads slugs and looks the cards up on the server; no new bulk file.
- **P9 (server-ready):** one adapter (`tripSummaryFor`); no new client-side loads of data.
- Trip-scope rule 2 (each trip remembers its last tab): met through `travel.tripui`.
