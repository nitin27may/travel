## 1. Verdict

**HOLD. Close the round after B1 to B3 below.**

The map work is done well. The stubs, the mixed-day key, the All days swatch, the chip wording, the leg hold and the hover all render as asked. This round is not finished because of three problems:

- The new per-profile route export writes a 27 km drive that the family's day never takes.

The 0-track adults export for the Rockies is **not** a bug. It is the correct result: the adults' Rockies days are each a single hike, and the bases have no position. Section 3 explains this.

## 3. The route export risk: what the numbers mean

The builder flagged that the Rockies adults export now has 0 tracks, and family-6 about 8 where there were 23 before. I checked each profile against what the map draws:

| Trip, profile | Legs the map draws as routes | Tracks the export writes | Before this change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rockies, adults | 0 | 0 | 23 for every profile |
| Rockies, family-6 (trip default) | 7 | 8 | 23 |
| Rockies, family-4 | 9 | 10 | 23 |
| Dolomites, every profile (trip default adults) | 34 | 37 | about 37 |

- **The old 23 was wrong.** It wrote every stored leg, including legs between the stops of versions B and C and pairs the page never shows. Filtering by profile was the point of SHOULD-6, and the large drop is mostly that filter doing its job.
- **Adults, 0 tracks, is correct.** Each Rockies day in the adults' version is one hike, with nothing to pair inside the day, and the bases have no point. The map draws no routed legs for adults either. A file with no route lines and points only is the honest export. The menu should say so, as in PL2 below.
- **Normalisation of place identity mostly works.** The `e.at.placeSlug` used in the timeline and the slug in the model's item meet in `place:<slug>`, as they do in `legGeometry`.
- The remaining difference, one extra track for family-6 and family-4, is a real bug. That is B1.
- The Dolomites' extra 3 are the airport legs (day 1, airport to Lago di Landro; day 14, Bolzano to the airport). The map does not draw them because the airport is not a pin. That gap is acceptable; see PL4.

## 5. Findings

Each finding is ranked critical, important or polish.

### B1. Important, and a blocker: the export writes a leg the family never drives

- **Screen.** Export menu, GPX and KML, on the Canadian Rockies (2029) trip page, profile family-6 (the trip's default).
- **What is wrong.**
  - The export writes "Day 3, Whole family: Paint Pots Walk to Johnston Canyon (drive, 27.4 km, 29 min)".
  - `routeLines` keeps a stored leg when both of its ends are places on that day's timeline. It does not check that the leg is a step in the day's order.
  - The 3-B-5 to 3-B-1 leg comes from the authored order. The family's schedule goes the other way: Johnston Canyon, then Marble Canyon Walk, then Paint Pots Walk, then the base.
  - The same rule adds one extra track for family-4.
- **Why it matters.** A parent loads the GPX into a car or phone app and sees a 27 km drive back down the valley that is in no plan. Either they follow it, or they stop trusting the file the trip page gave them (T5 and P6). A wrong track is worse than a missing one.
- **Fix.**
  - Build the export from the same join the map uses: `legGeometry(legs, dayMapOf(timelines), bases, slugOf)`.
  - Write only the legs it returns as routed: one track for each consecutive pair in the day's timeline that has stored geometry.
  - If the airport legs are ruled in (PL4), add them by the same consecutive-pair rule.
  - Keep a single join in one shared module, so the browser export and a later API export cannot drift apart (P9; this sets up SHOULD-9).
- **Acceptance checks.**
  1. For every trip and profile, the set of `(day, from, to)` exported equals the map's routed legs from `legGeometry`. The only allowed difference is the airport legs, if they are ruled in. Test this in `web/tests/export/trip-routes.test.ts`.
  2. Rockies family-6: no track named "Paint Pots Walk to Johnston Canyon". Every track's `from` comes before its `to` in that day's timeline.
  3. Rockies adults: 0 tracks is asserted as the expected value, not just allowed. For a Rockies adult, the menu says there are no route lines (see PL2).
  4. The e2e asserts exact counts for each profile, not "more than 5".

### B2. Important, and a blocker: print does not match the page for durations, notes, "~" and empty days (rest of MUST-5)

- **Screen.** Print and the PDF from the export menu, on both trips.
- **What is wrong.**
  1. **Durations and notes come from the authored model, not the timeline.**
     - Dolomites day 3, family-4: the page shows 8 h 05 for the Tre Cime loop; the print says "270 min".
     - Rockies: durations and notes are missing entirely. Athabasca Falls shows 25 min and a note on the page, and nothing on paper, because the matched stop has none.
  2. **The approximate marker is lost.** Entries whose `time.confidence` is `suggested` show "~08:45" on the page and "08:45" on paper. This breaks rule T3: a suggested time must never look like a fixed one.
  3. **A day with no visits prints "Not checked yet".** Dolomites day 14, "Bolzano to Venice", is a travel day, not a gap. The marker tells the reader something is missing when nothing is.
- **Why it matters.** The printed sheet is what goes in the car door and is read at a trailhead with no signal (S-scenarios for travelling, and P3: a glance should be enough).
  - A 270-minute loop that the page calls 8 h 05 changes whether a family with a 4-year-old starts it at all.
  - A firm-looking 08:45 invites a family to plan around a time the site only suggested.
- **Fix.** In `TripPrintView.tsx` `rowsOf`, read every printed field from the timeline entry, not from `r.stop`:
  - the duration from `e.durationMin`, in the page's own formatter (the same one the day list uses)
  - the prefix "~" when `e.time.confidence === 'suggested'`, as the page does
  - the note from `e.time.note`, then the stop's note only if the entry has none
  - for a day with no visit entries, print the day's travel line (for example "Bolzano to Venice") and no gap marker
- **Acceptance checks.**
  1. Dolomites day 3, family-4: the print shows the same duration string as the page for the Tre Cime loop.
  2. Rockies day 2, family-6: the print shows Athabasca Falls with 25 min and the page's note.
  3. Every entry shown with "~" on the page is shown with "~" in the print. Compare the page text with `pdftotext` output in an e2e.
  4. Dolomites day 14 prints no "Not checked yet".
  5. Pagination stays clean: no stop split across pages. Dark theme still prints as light.

### B3. Important, and a blocker: on a phone, opening the key shrinks the map to a strip

- **Screen.** Trip page map at 390 px, key open, on a mixed day (Dolomites day 4, Rockies day 5), in light and dark.
- **What is wrong.** The open key and the four-line mixed-day sentence push the map down to about 85 CSS px. The Fit control is lost, and the pins sit under the copyright chip.
- **Why it matters.** The phone is what the family uses on the road. A parent opens the key to ask "is this dashed line a real path?", and the map they wanted to read disappears. That fails P3 and P5: opening a detail should not destroy the context it explains.
- **Fix.** Use one of these, in order of preference:
  - (a) open the key as an overlay sheet over the lower part of the map, with a close button and Escape, so the map keeps its height
  - (b) give the map a `min-height` of 240 px at phone widths, and let the key scroll inside a max height
  - In both cases, shorten the mixed-day sentence to one line in the key ("Solid: road or path route. Dashed: straight line, not a route.") and put the longer explanation behind "More".
- **Acceptance checks.**
  1. At 390x844, 2x, with the key open on Dolomites day 4 and Rockies day 5, the map canvas is at least 240 CSS px tall.
  2. Fit and zoom stay visible and can be tapped (44 px targets).
  3. No pin sits under the chip. Shots in light and dark.

### B4. Important, recommended for this round: in the Rockies, a place and its trail entry are different stops to the route join (SHOULD-5)

- **Screen.** Trip map and day list, Rockies, family profiles. This affects the export too, through B1.
- **What is wrong.**
  - The legs file pairs place items. The scheduler and the map use the trail item for the same stop.
  - These pairs are at the same point, 0 m apart: marble-canyon and marble-canyon-walk, takakkaw-falls and takakkaw-falls-walk, peyto-lake-lookout-banff and peyto-lake-lookout, overlander-falls and overlander-falls-trail.
  - These pairs have no point on one side: emerald-lake and emerald-lakeshore, lake-louise and lake-louise-lakeshore, paint-pots and paint-pots-walk.
  - The result: legs that have a stored route are drawn dashed with "Straight line, not a route", and left out of the export. For example, Rockies family-6 day 3, Johnston Canyon to Marble Canyon Walk.
- **Why it matters.** The key tells the family there is no route to follow when one is stored. They may plan a drive by guesswork that the site already worked out (T5).
- **Fix.**
  - In the data, key the Rockies legs by the slugs the scheduler uses (re-run `legs-ors.mjs --check` and, where needed, the fetch), or add an explicit alias map `place ↔ trail` in the trip's schedule data.
  - In code, add a fallback in the shared join: two ends match when they fall in the same `cellOf` cell, or within 150 m of the pin.
  - Do not match by name.
- **Acceptance checks.**
  1. Rockies family-6 day 3: Johnston Canyon to Marble Canyon Walk is drawn as a route and exported.
  2. A unit test lists the seven pairs above and asserts that each joins.
  3. The routed-leg count for each profile does not drop for any Dolomites day.

If B4 slips, record it as must-fix before go-live, with these checks.

**N1. Important, not a blocker: the open phone key covers almost the whole map.**

- **Screen.** Trip map at 390, key open. Rockies day 5 in `phone-key-open-390-dark.png`, and Dolomites day 4.
- **What is wrong.**
  - The panel runs from the top of the map almost to its bottom. On Rockies day 5 it ends about 7 CSS px above the map's edge.
  - The map keeps its 247 px, but the reader cannot see a swatch and the line it names at the same time.
  - The Map key button also jumps from the right of the row to the left when the key opens, because the one-line key item is removed.
- **Why it matters.** A parent opens the key to ask which line on the map is the dashed one. Now they must close the key to look, then reopen it to compare. That is better than the 85 px strip it replaced, but it is still a toggle where P5 wants both in view.
- **Fix.**
  1. Anchor the panel to the **bottom** of the map, with `top: auto; bottom: 8px` inside the map box.
  2. Cap it at `max-height: 50%` of the map surface, about 120 px, and let it scroll.
  3. Set the four swatches in two columns.
  4. Keep the toggle in place: render the collapsed item with `visibility: hidden` instead of removing it.
- **Acceptance check.** With the key open on Rockies day 5 at 390x844, at least 50% of the map surface stays visible, and the dashed line and its key entry are both on screen.

**N2. Important, not a blocker: on a travel day, the print drops the arrival time and reads as two drives.**

- **Screen.** Print and PDF, Dolomites day 14, "Bolzano to Venice".
- **What is wrong.**
  - The page shows the day as a sequence: leave and check out (~08:45), drive 1 h 20, a stretch break at ~10:25, drive 1 h 20 "on to" the airport, then "~12:00 At the airport 3 h before an international flight".
  - The print shows only the two drive halves: "~09:05 · Drive to Venice Marco Polo Airport · 1 h 20" and "~10:40 · Drive on to Venice Marco Polo Airport · 1 h 20".
  - There is no break and no arrival time.
- **Why it matters.** On the last morning, the one fact the family needs on paper is "be at the airport by 12:00". Without the break between them, the two rows look like two separate drives to the same place.
- **Fix.**
  - In `rowsOf`, on a travel day (no visit rows), print every non-nested entry that is not a start or a check-out. That covers `leg`, `break` and the end or anchor entry (`kind === 'end'`), in timeline order, with their times.
  - Alternatively, print one merged row ("Drive 2 h 40 to Venice Marco Polo Airport, with a 15 min break") followed by the arrival row.
- **Acceptance check.** The Dolomites day 14 PDF contains "12:00" and "At the airport".

**N3. Important, for the trip-page owner, outside TRP2b2: a split drive repeats the whole distance.**
