**Verdict: approve with changes.** The Bookings tab now answers "how much do we have to book, and by when" in one line
and one list, and every count on the page agrees: tab 7, Summary card 7, panel heading 7, CSV toast "35 stops, 7 to book
ahead", print 7 (Dolomites 3 everywhere). A check, a pass or a task is listed and never counted, the window reads in the
page's own words ("Opens 30 days before Day 3", no stray "local"), there is no filter row for 3 or 7 rows, and the prices
line is honest. The Photos tab groups by the gallery's sections with day signs and a credit under every tile at rest.
Axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) is clean on both tabs at 320 and 1440 in light and dark; no sideways scroll at any width.

Two things must change before the owner sees the Photos tab: one section carries a wrong day (M1), and at phone widths
the licence is the part of the credit that is cut off (M2). The rest is should-fix or polish. The real-data gap, that
every public trip's rows are still all `booking` until PA-W wires `bookKind`, makes "7 to book ahead" an overcount today;
that is not UXB-6's fault but it must be said when the owner is shown the tab (G1).

## 2. Findings

### M1. Important (must-fix): the Kananaskis section says "Day 10"; the plan is in Kananaskis on Days 12 to 14

Screen: Photos tab, Rockies, every width. The heading reads "Kananaskis Country and Peter Lougheed Provincial Park ·
Day 10". The plan's Kananaskis rows are "Kananaskis Village / Peter Lougheed PP" (Days 12, 13, 14) and "Upper and Lower
Kananaskis Lakes" (Day 13). The Day 10 comes from "Lake Louise Lakeshore parking and Park and Ride": `nameCores` splits
it on "and" into "park" and "ride", and "park" is a phrase of "... Provincial Park". A family planning from the photos
reads Day 10 as the day they see Kananaskis, and the day link elsewhere on the page says otherwise (P6, T1: a wrong
"when" is worse than none).

Fix (`web/components/trips/tabs/photoGroups.ts`): never match on a core of one generic word. Drop cores that are a
single word from a stop list (park, ride, lake, lakes, pass, road, trail, view, falls, canyon, village, lot, parking),
or require a core of two or more words unless the single word is a proper name not in that list; expand "PP" to
"provincial park" and "NP" to "national park" in `norm` before matching. When nothing reliable matches, draw no day sign
(never a guessed one). Add a unit test on the real Rockies rows: Kananaskis reads "Days 12, 13 and 14" (or no day sign),
never "Day 10"; and a test that "Park and Ride" matches no section.

### M2. Important (must-fix): at phone widths the licence is what the ellipsis cuts

Screens: Photos tab, 320 and 390, both trips; at 1440 too for long author strings. The rest line is "author · licence"
in one `nowrap` line with `text-overflow: ellipsis`, so the end goes first: "Tobias Alt · CC BY-…", "Wingchi Poon · CC
B…", "Douglaspperkins · C…", and at 1440 "InSapphoWeTrust from Los Angeles, California, USA · CC BY-SA …". 19 of 38
Rockies credits are cut at 390. The point of the 4.5 amendment (uxb-components 8.2) is that a phone, with no hover,
still shows who made the photo and under what licence; the licence is the half that is lost. The full credit stays in
the lightbox and the page's Credits, so this is not a licence breach, but the at-rest line fails its own purpose.

Fix (`Gallery.tsx` and `trip-tabs.css`): render the rest line as two spans, `<span class="gal-credit-by">{author}</span>`
and `<span class="gal-credit-lic">{licence}</span>`, in a `display: flex; gap: var(--s-1)` line with a " · " separator;
the author span `min-width: 0; overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis; white-space: nowrap`, the licence span
`flex: none`. The author truncates, the licence never does. Keep `--t-chip` `--muted`. Add an e2e check at 320 that every
`.gal-credit-lic` has `scrollWidth <= clientWidth`.

### S1. Important (should-fix): a multi-place section heading links to whichever place happens to come first in the rows

Screen: Photos tab, Rockies. The whole heading is the link, so its name is the section's name, but the target is the
first matched row with a place page in the plan's row order: "Johnston Canyon and Lake Minnewanka" opens Lake
Minnewanka; "Yoho: Emerald Lake and Takakkaw Falls" opens the Takakkaw Falls walk; "Kootenay: Marble Canyon and Radium
Hot Springs" opens the Marble Canyon walk. A visitor who taps "Johnston Canyon" lands on another place (WCAG 2.4.4 in
spirit, P5).

Fix: order the hits by where their core first appears in the section name and link the first; or, better, link each
matched name inside the heading text ("Johnston Canyon" and "Lake Minnewanka" as two links) and leave the rest plain.

### S2. Important (should-fix): the lightbox stops at the end of each section

Screen: Photos tab, any trip, any width. Each section is its own `LazyGallery`, so the lightbox counts "3 / 3" and
ArrowRight at the last photo does nothing. Before UXB-6 the 38 photos were one sequence; now a visitor browsing the
trip closes, scrolls and reopens 13 times. The Photos tab is the inspiration tab for the 90% visitor (P7, P5).

Fix: one `Gallery` instance for the tab that renders the section headings between its tiles (a `sections` prop of
`{ heading, start, end }`) and one lightbox over all photos, with the section name in the caption and the counter
"4 / 38 · Lake Louise and Lake Agnes". Tile labels keep "Open photo 4 of 38".

### S3. Important (should-fix): "13 places" counts a theme and a nearby park

Screens: Photos tab note and the Summary's Photos card, Rockies: "38 photos · 13 places". Two of the 13 are "Larches and
early autumn" (a theme) and "Mount Assiniboine and the Sunshine Meadows" (labelled nearby, not in this plan). Dolomites
"8 places" counts "Sella group and the passes". The number contradicts the tab's own labels.

Fix: say "38 photos in 13 sections" in both places, or count only sections matched to the plan ("38 photos · 11 places
in this plan"). One helper for the card and the tab so they cannot drift.

### S4. Important (should-fix): the prices line counts passes, the headline does not

Screen: Bookings tab with a pass (fixture 06, and every trip after PA-W): "2 to book ahead ... also 1 pass" then
"Prices: not checked yet for these 3." "These 3" points at nothing the reader just counted.

Fix (`pricesLine`): "Prices: not checked yet for the 2 bookings and 1 pass." When only bookings: keep "for these 7".

### S5. Important (should-fix): the Passes note asserts a fact the row may contradict

Screen: Bookings tab, Passes group: "Bought any time before the trip; not counted as a booking to watch." A pass row can
carry a rule with a close ("Buy by 23:59 on Day 12", `anytime` with `closes`), and some entry passes are bought on
arrival. A family that reads "any time" can miss a deadline the row states one line lower (P2).

Fix: "Entry passes. Not counted as a booking; the line under each says when to buy." Let the rule say the when. Same
review of the other two notes, in visitor words: To check, "Rules and conditions to know before the day; nothing to
book."; Tasks, "To-dos before you go; nothing to book." Drop "not counted" (internal language) from all three.

### S6. Important (should-fix): no real row links to its place page

Screen: Bookings tab, Rockies and Dolomites. Spec 3.6: "A row links to its stop's place page." The link is drawn only
when the booking row has a `placeSlug`, and the logistics rows have none, so on real data 0 of 10 rows link
("Lago di Braies P4 parking", "Tre Cime toll road slot", "Seceda lift", "Maligne Lake cruise", "Larch Valley / Sentinel
Pass" each name a place row in the same trip). Only fixture 06 shows the link.

Fix: after M1's matcher fix, resolve a booking row without a slug to the trip's place row whose core it carries
("Lago di Braies P4 parking" to `lago-di-braies`), only when exactly one place matches; else no link. Or record the gap
in UI-NOTES against PA-W, which can set `placeSlug` on the rows it writes. Either is acceptable; silence is not.

### G1. Important (gap, not UXB-6): "7 to book ahead" overcounts until PA-W wires `bookKind`

Screen: Bookings tab and Summary card, Rockies. Of the 7 rows, "Parks Canada Discovery Pass or daily entry" is a pass,
"Larch Valley / Sentinel Pass group-of-four rule" is a rule to know (a check), and "Icefields Parkway status and Columbia
Icefield Adventure" mixes a road check with a tour booking. All count as bookings because every public row is still
`booking` by default. The safety rule is not hidden (it sits at rest in the tab and in Know before you go, P2 holds),
but it is labelled as something to book. Algonquin's Summary says "Algonquin day permits sell online only a few days
ahead" and the trip has no Bookings tab at all, because no row carries the note. Owner: PA-W sets the kinds and splits
the mixed row; research adds the Algonquin permit and the Kananaskis Conservation Pass rows. The UI is ready for both
(fixture 06).

### P1. Polish: the day and booking links sit lower than the row's name at 760 px and up

Screens: Bookings 1440, both trips. `.bk-day a` is a 44 px flex box with centred text, so "Day 2" sits about 14 px below
the baseline of the name and the window. Fix: at 760 px and up, `.bk-foot a { align-items: flex-start; padding-top: 2px }`
(the box keeps 44 px; the text aligns with the first line).

### P2. Polish: two wordings of the same fact

Summary card "none has a published opening date yet"; tab "none with a published opening date yet". Use the tab's words
in both, from one function.

### P3. Polish: the print's second list prints the raw kind

"Download offline maps · task", "Parks Canada Discovery Pass · pass". Print the group headings instead (Passes, To
check, Tasks), one sub-list each, from `BOOKING_GROUPS`. Also: the print's "Book ahead" list sits before the days; Q305
said after the days. That order predates UXB-6; record it in UI-NOTES rather than move it in this round.

### P4. Polish: note text from core

"...unless joining others; Whether a young child counts is not known yet." (capital after a semicolon) and "Maligne Canyon
trailhead and Cavell Road closed for 2026." (a dated closure on a dateless plan). The first is `publicBookingNote`'s
capitalisation after a stripped marker (core); the second is research data. UI-NOTES rows for both.

### P5. Polish: the single-group case drops the group's note

When a trip has only passes (or only checks), the heading is `sr-only` and the group note is not drawn, so the reader
loses the line that says what the rows are. Draw the note under the summary line when there is one group.

## 4. Accessibility, contrast, tokens, privacy, budget

- Axe (WCAG 2.2 AA) on `#trip-panel-bookings` and `#trip-panel-photos`, Rockies, 320 and 1440, light and dark: 0
  violations in 8 runs. The builder's e2e runs the same at page level.
- Keyboard: from the Bookings tab, Tab reaches "Day 1, Day 1, Day 4, Day 5, Day 10, Day 11"; from Photos, the section
  link then each tile ("Open photo 1 of 3: Moraine Lake, turquoise ...") then the next section; 2 px solid focus ring on
  every stop. Day links are 44 px high. A screen reader hears the sr-only "To book ahead" heading in the one-group case,
  the group headings with their counts in the many-group case, and "Booking page for <name> (opens in a new tab)".
  The credit span is no longer `aria-hidden` in the tab, so a reader hears the author and licence after each tile; right.
- Contrast on `--bg`, each pair used here: `--ink` 14.85 (light) / 15.28 (dark); `--ink-2` 11.18 / 11.88; `--muted`
  (group notes, counts, prices line, credits, day signs) 6.42 / 8.23; `--accent-text` (day and booking links) 5.85 /
  8.83. The place-name link is `--ink` with a `--line-2` underline (3.39 / 3.78 against `--bg`); the underline alone
  marks it as a link, which meets 1.4.1. All AA or better; approved.
- Tokens only: every colour, space and type value in the new CSS is a token; no raw colour; no green.
- Licences: every tile carries author and licence at rest; the lightbox carries title, author, the linked licence and
  the source; the page's Credits list is unchanged. Booking links pass `safeLink` (http and https only), open in a new
  tab with `noopener noreferrer`. Prefer `https:` only; an `http:` official page is rare enough to drop (polish).
- Privacy: notes pass `publicBookingNote` (party clauses dropped); place links are drawn only for public place pages
  (`getPlace`); nothing about the household reaches the tab.
- Budget: both panels render on the server; the gallery stays in the lazy chunk. Start JS on the heaviest trip page is
  190.1 KB of 200 KB (10.1 KB headroom); no claim in the commit is contradicted.
