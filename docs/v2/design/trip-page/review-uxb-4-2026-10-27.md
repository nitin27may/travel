# Review: UXB-4, When to go in the trip Summary (27 Oct 2026)

Reviewer: `travel-design-reviewer`, Opus 5.5. Branch `public/UXB-4`, commit `294bde3f` (builder: Sonnet). `main` already
carries it unchanged (merge `c3ff202a`), so every fix below is a follow-up commit on `main`'s line.
Against: spec [trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md](../../features/trip-and-places-ux-fixes.md) 6.1 to 6.5, 13.7, UXF-7 to
UXF-10, UXF-I6 to I9, the UXB-W / UXB-WL update in 6.4 (owner answers Q293 to Q307); wireframe W4 in
[ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md](ux-feedback-wireframes-2026-10-26.md); [uxb-components-2026-10-26.md](uxb-components-2026-10-26.md)

site first, no green). Standard applied: the UXB-1, UXB-2 and UXB-3 reviews.

**Verdict: approve with changes.** The section now answers "when should we go and what will it be like" in one look on a
desktop: the window in large type, the reason, a strip that says which months, and four equal cards. The honesty work is
mostly right: a plan's own window is never called best, the cover stat reads "Planned for" in that case, crowds say "Not
checked yet", the advice glyph is gone from advice lines, the strip is pattern plus word, the screen-reader summary and
ordered list are clean, axe is clean in 28 runs, and nothing is fetched at run time. Two things must change before the owner
sees it: the Rockies, the trip the owner will open first, says "Not checked yet" and "not researched yet" about crowds and
other weeks that **are** researched in its windows file (C1); and on phones the Iceland river-crossing warning is folded
away as advice (C2). Five important fixes follow, mostly on phones.

## 2. Findings

### C1. Critical: the Rockies says crowds and other weeks are not checked, but they are

Screen: Rockies Summary, every width. The Crowds card reads **Not checked yet**, "Other weeks that work" reads **Other
weeks have not been researched yet**, and June is a blank "not checked" month. `data/windows/canadian-rockies-2029.json`
holds four researched windows with 22 sources: second week of September best, crowds lower; first week of September good,
crowds high; last week of June good, crowds rising; second week of June workable, crowds lower, each crowd level sourced to
Parks Canada visitation.

Why it matters: the Rockies is the trip the owner opens first and the one family the windows research was done for. A
family reads "not researched" and either distrusts the plan or goes looking elsewhere for exactly what we already know
(P6, the honesty rule: "Not checked yet" means we did not check, never "we did not wire it"). For the other 13 trips the
same lines are true and stay.

Fix (preferred, inside UXB-4's scope; the brief allowed the gap line only when no payload exposed windows):

1. A server-only adapter `web/lib/public/windows.ts` in the same shape as `weatherLinks.ts`: read
   `data/windows/<trip>.json` at build, no zod, return light rows `{ label, verdict, crowdLevel, crowdBasisText,
   plus: string | null, minus: string | null, months: number[] }` or null.
2. Crowds card: the crowd level of the window whose verdict is `best` (here "Lower", with "Parks Canada visitation,
   2019 pattern" as a second line in `--muted`).
3. Other weeks that work: one line per other window, verdict, crowd, one plus, one minus (spec 6.2 item 4), no `w=` link
   (that is OVR-1).
4. Strip: pass months covered by a `good` window as `extra` to `monthStates` (June becomes "good"); `workable` draws
   nothing.
5. The hero stays the season label until OVR-1.

Fallback if this cannot be done in the fix round: for a trip that has a windows file, the Crowds card reads "Researched,
not shown here yet" and the other-weeks line reads "Other weeks have been researched; they arrive on this page with the
next update." Do not ship "not researched" on the Rockies.

### C2. Critical: a real hazard is folded away on phones

Screen: Iceland, 390 and 320. "No river crossings: rental cover excludes them" is classed as advice and hidden behind
"Show 2 more"; only "About 50 campsites close during September" shows. River fords on Iceland's roads are a known way to
lose a vehicle or worse, and the insurance exclusion makes it a money risk too.

Why it matters: spec 6.2 and P2 say a hazard line stays visible at rest. The regex in `whenModel.ts` knows "closed",
"unsafe", "flash flood", "avalanche", but not fords, rockfall or weather events. I classed all 33 season notes on the 14
trips (table in section 4): one hazard is missed (Iceland), one closure is missed (Patagonia "Penguin colony season ends in
March", which matters to a trip running to mid-March), and the false-positive risk is "close" used for distance ("close to
the river"), which no note uses today.

Fix: (a) add `river cross|ford|rockfall|landslide|wildfire|smoke|lightning|tide|rip current|hypotherm|heat stroke|season
ends|ends in` to the word list, and exclude `close to|closer`; (b) pin all 33 notes and their expected kind in
`web/tests/trips/when-to-go.test.tsx` so any data change that flips a kind fails a test; (c) open a data row for an
authored `kind` on season notes (hazard, closure, advice) so the regex becomes the fallback only.

### I1. Important: "Worth knowing" over nothing on phones, and folds that hide one line

Screens: Norway, Costa Rica, Algonquin, New Hampshire, Quebec, Switzerland (all advice) at 390 and 320 show the
heading, an empty list, then "Show 2 notes"; the accessibility tree announces an empty list. Rockies, Chamonix and Utah
show "Show 1 more" to hide one line.

Why it matters: the heading over nothing looks broken, and a fold that hides one line costs a tap to save one line (P2,
P3). No trip has more than three notes today.

Fix: fold advice on phones only when a trip has more than three notes; with three or fewer, show every note at rest.
Keep the counted disclosure for longer lists, hazards first at rest.

### I2. Important: the weather links take 600 to 900 px on a phone

Screens: Utah 390 (11 pills, about 900 px), Norway 390 (12), Iceland 390 (9), Costa Rica 390 (long two-line pills).
W4's 390 frame drew the weather as one line.

Why it matters: on a phone the links push the section's end a full screen down, below the Summary's next blocks; a parent
checking the forecast wants the forecast row, not the climate PDFs (P2, P6).

Fix: under 640 px, show the publisher and its Forecast row at rest; fold Climate averages, Mountain weather and Aurora
behind one counted disclosure ("More weather pages (6)") with the same no-JS checkbox pattern. Desktop unchanged.

### I3. Important: the weather gap line is wrong for both trips that show it, and one link breaks the data contract

Screens: Dolomites and Patagonia, all widths. Both read "Not every base has its own forecast page yet."
- Dolomites: Cortina and San Vito have forecast pages; the gaps are the Veneto service (ARPAV) and a climate page for
  Cortina.
- Patagonia: no base has its own page and there is no climate link. The one link drawn, `https://www.smn.gob.ar/`, is
  marked `check.reachable: false` (HTTP 403) in `data/reference/weather-links.json`, whose `selection_rule` says such a row
  is not drawn. A request with a browser user agent from here also got 403. `partial` is also true for any `gaps` entry,
  including "Chile DMC not needed".

Why it matters: a family taps the one Argentine link and may get an error page; the line under it describes a different
problem (P6, no invented or unchecked links).

Fix: in `weatherLinks.ts`, drop rows with `check.reachable === false` and count only `gaps` entries that start with
"GAP:". Say "Some weather pages for this trip could not be checked yet, so they are not linked." When a trip ends with no
row (Patagonia), draw that sentence alone under the Weather heading. The SMN link comes back when a person opens it in a
browser and the data seat records it as a manual check.

### I4. Important: the strip calls whole months "best" when only part of them is

Screens: Chamonix (June and September best, for "Late June to first week of September", while the reason says Brevent and
Planpraz lifts close mid-September), Patagonia (March, for "to mid-March"), Dolomites (July, for "Mid-July").

Why it matters: at a glance the strip, not the label, is what people read. "September: best" in Chamonix sends a family
into closed huts in the third week.

Fix: needs data. Open a row for day-precision windows on `trip.season` (or `partMonths`), and a sixth strip state "part
of the month" (half `--ink` fill, word "part") in foundation components 4.7. Until then the hero label above the strip is
the source of truth; acceptable to show the owner with this noted.

### I5. Important: on a desktop, blank months are not explained

Screens: every trip from 705 px. The legend is hidden there because the state words are in the cells, but blank cells
have no word, so "Jan to Aug" reads as "bad months" rather than "not checked".

Fix: keep one legend item at 705 px and up: "Blank: not checked for this plan". Components 4.7 needs a one-line edit when
this lands.

### Polish

- **P1.** Phones 340 to 400 px (most phones, 375 and 390): the three-letter names fill 26 to 27 px cells edge to edge.
  6 x 2 at the same width is 91 px tall against 88 px for 12 x 1 plus its legend, and carries the words. Move the 6 x 2
  switch from 340 to 400 px of strip width (components 4.7 and uxb-components 5.1 change with it).
- **P2.** At 1440 the strip stops at 705 px and the cards run to 62rem; the right edges do not line up. Cap `.wtg-cards`
  at the strip's width or let the strip span the card row.
- **P3.** Two caps kickers stack ("WHEN TO GO", "BEST TIME FOR THIS PLAN"); add `--s-3` between them.
- **P4.** Long link labels wrap inside full-radius pills (Norway climate, Costa Rica PDFs, Chamonix's Bourg-Saint-Maurice);
  use `--r-3` when a link wraps, as the gap chip in the cards already does, and shorten the stored labels.
- **P5.** The fold checkbox keeps `aria-label="Show 2 more"` when it reads "Show fewer" (WCAG 2.5.3 for voice users). Drop
  the `aria-label`; the `<label>` already names it with the visible text.
- **P6.** A non-English link says the language twice to a screen reader (the visible chip and "page in Italian"). Keep the
  chip, drop it from the hidden text, and add `hreflang`.
