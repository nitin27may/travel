## 1. Verdict

**NO-GO for turning `planBuilder` on, even for the owner's own testing.** The flow screens (stages 0 to 4) are close:
clean, consistent with the foundation, AA in both themes, keyboard-reachable, fast, deterministic and private in the
ways that matter most. But the thing the builder exists to produce, the plan, is wrong in every one of the five realistic
trips I built: picks are spread over a week with most days empty or crammed into one day, a second base is never added
when the picks need it, most Italian picks "have no location", must-sees are dropped, and the details page forgets the
child the person entered. An owner test today would judge the engine, not the screens, and the first impression would be
"it does not work". Fix the six must-fix rows (RB-001 to RB-006), then re-run this review's five trips; the screens can
ship behind the flag with the should-fix rows open.

Counts: **6 must-fix, 20 should-fix, 8 nice** (34 rows).

## 4. What is right

- **Speed**: build answers in 35 to 125 ms on the server, 40 to 50 ms by `curl`; the landing renders 2 to 260 ms after
  the URL changes; stage changes take 20 to 120 ms (250 ms on phones the first time stage 4's chunk loads). Under the 1 s
  bar everywhere.
- **Determinism**: the same build body posted three times gives identical answers (apart from `builtAt`).
- **Flag off**: `/plan/`, `/t/<id>/` and all four `/api/v1/plan/*` routes answer 404 (POST included); no builder string is
  in any static chunk of the flag-off build; public pages make no plan requests. With the flag on, the home page carries
  "Plan your own trip →" and prefetches `/plan/`; no other public page does.
- **Privacy**: the decoy home point (10.5, -140.5) appears in no URL, log line, storage key or analytics call; full ages,
  `adults`, `children` and `walkKm` travel only in the build POST body; the device draft holds `party: "device"`; no
  third-party calls other than map tiles. RB-010 is the exception.
- **Accessibility**: axe-core finds no WCAG 2.2 AA violation on stages 0 to 4 or the landing at 1440 or 390. Each stage
  moves focus to its h1 and announces "Step 1 of 4: Start" in a polite live region; the just-built line is in a live
  region; Segmented controls are one tab stop; all controls have names ("Pick Lake Louise", "Must see Lake Louise",
  "Edit who is going, now 2 adults, 1 child").
- **Stages 0 to 4 visually**: consistent with the foundation in both themes; the phone focused flow (step header, count,
  progress, one action bar with the named primary and the reason above it) matches FLOW 7; desktop uses both columns at
  1024 and up; no green, purple or emoji; parks show "Area · we choose its stops"; Remove on stage 0 with Undo works.
- **Plan API**: errors carry codes and the UI words each (rate limit, paused, not available, too large); `starts` error
  has a Try again.
