## 1. Verdict

**Not approved yet.** Three must-fix items (M1 to M3), all small. With them done, the shell is approved: the phone
header, the bar, the dot and the Mine page match the spec in both themes and both builds.

What is right and stays as built:

- **Phone header (D1, D2).** Brand, Search, account, Theme on one row at 320 and 390; the tools are 44 x 44 with
  centres 52 px apart (44 + 8); the last tool ends 14 px from the edge; at 320 the brand ends near x 112 and the
  tools start at x 158, which is the measured 33.6 px spare. The brand is never truncated. The signed-in ring is drawn,
  30 px, `--header-mute`. The account glyph is `--accent` on `/sign-in/`. Public build: Search and Theme only.
- **Bar (D3).** Five equal items (62.4 px wide at 320, 76.4 at 390, 56 px tall), Trips · Places · Globe · Search ·
  Mine, Mine last. The label and icon never change. Current on `/mine/` (`page`) and on `/account/`, `/sign-in/`,
  `/sign-up/` (`true`); the unit test pins `/signin/` and `/places/mine-lake/` as not current.
- **The dot (D4).** 7 px `--accent` with a 2 px `--header` ring, top right of the icon, no number, on the bar and on the
  desktop button. The name becomes "Mine, new items" and the dot clears when `/mine/` opens and in a second tab
  (e2e). Reduced motion turns off the scale.
- **Desktop (D5).** Search, Theme, Mine, then Sign in or the avatar. Mine has its icon and label at 1440, is current on
  `/mine/` (amber icon, label 600), and the slot reserves its width (CLS e2e). The avatar menu holds Account and Sign out
  only.
- **Mine page (D6, D7).** "MINE" page head; the account card under it (two lines on a phone, one line on desktop);
  segmented tabs with tab semantics, counts, 44 px and full width on a phone, auto width and 40 px on desktop; arrows,
  Home and End; `?show=` through `replaceState`; the newest list opens by default. The empty states use the spec's
  strings word for word. The device notice comes after the list, and Got it returns focus to the selected tab. Signed
  in: "Signed in as a@b.com", **Account**, and the pre-CUS-C note.
- **Copy.** Every string in spec 7 that NAV-1 renders matches; nothing says "saved"; there are no emojis and no
  exclamation marks.
- **Colour.** Every colour comes from `themes.css` tokens: no literal in `mine.css` or the new `app.css` rules, and no
  green or purple. The measured pairs are in section 5.
- **Builder deviations accepted:** `/favourites/` as a `next.config` redirect rather than a page (the app is a server
  build now, so it works; it is 307, see P5); `MineLink` mounted from `SiteHeader` rather than inside `AccountNav`
  (`AccountNav` does not render in the public build, so the brief's place would have hidden Mine there); the bar icons
  as CSS masks (they pay for the new item in place HTML, but see M3).

## 2. Must fix

### M1 (important). Trip start JS is +238 B against a written cap of +160 B

- **Screen:** every trip page, on phones and desktops.
- **What is wrong:** the hand-back calls +238 B acceptable because it is under the 200 KB hard cap, and says "spec said
  +0 B". The authority for NAV-1 is bundle A D5, which replaced +0 B with **at most +160 B**. Step 4 adds that the dot
  is dropped if it alone costs more than 120 B. The budget ledger in bundle A section 5 plans NAV-1 at 160 B, TRP-GL at 64 B and
  FAV-1 at 150 B, with at least 256 B of headroom left after FAV-1 (D11). NAV-1 has taken 78 B of that plan.
- **Why it matters:** the trip page is the page a family opens at the trailhead on poor signal (T2, P9). Spending
  budgets nobody recorded is how the 2 B headroom of 25 Oct happened. FAV-1 is the next task to hit its D11 floor, and
  it will find the floor already gone.
- **Fix (one of the two, before FAV-1 starts):**
  1. Trim to at most +160 B and quote the per-part bytes in the hand-back. Candidates: the `matchMedia` re-render in
     `AccountNav.tsx` (replace `q.onchange` and the second `setUser` with one `matchMedia(...).matches` check at the
     time the session read returns; the rotation case is already a UI-NOTES "Could"), and the `mineCurrent` regex in
     `BottomBar.tsx` (three `startsWith` calls are shorter after minification).
  2. Or the coordinator records the overrun in `docs/v2/build/briefs-bundle-A.md` D5 and section 5 ("NAV-1 spent
     +238 B, measured <date>; FAV-1's allowance is now N B with 256 B left after it"), then recomputes FAV-1's cap.
  The dot stays either way: it is lazy (in `MineLink`'s chunk), not in the start chunk.

### M2 (important). The unit test that pins the duplicated storage keys does not exist

- **Screen:** the Mine dot on every page (bar and header).
- **What is wrong:** `web/lib/mine/keys.ts` copies `travel.copies.local` and `travel.fav.local` as literals and says
  "`tests/mine/keys.test.ts` pins each literal to the core constant, so they cannot drift". That file is not in the
  commit or on `main` (`git grep keys.test` finds only the comment). `dot.ts` also hard-codes the item fields
  `copiedAt` and `favouredAt`, which nothing pins.
- **Why it matters:** if NAV-2C or a later schema bump renames a key or a field, the dot goes dark with no error. A
  family copies a trip and Mine never shows that something is new (P4, P6). The comment also tells the next reader a
  guard exists when it does not.
- **Judgement on the approach (raw JSON read rather than D6's `travel.mine.latest`): acceptable, on this condition.**
  It reads the lists themselves, so no `latest` key can go stale when a writer forgets to update it. It stays out of
  the start chunk and pulls in no Zod (P8, P9). The cost is one `JSON.parse` of at most 20 copies and 500 favourites in
  a lazy chunk, which is fine at under 100 users. Record the change as a D6 amendment in `briefs-bundle-A.md`.
- **Fix:** add `web/tests/mine/keys.test.ts`:
  - `COPIES_KEY` equals `COPIES_KEY` from `@travel/core/schema/copies.ts`;
  - `FAV_KEY` equals `FAV_LOCAL_KEY` from `@travel/core/schema/favourites.ts`;
  - a valid `copies/1` and `favourites/1` envelope built from the core fixtures, written to a stub `localStorage`, makes
    `hasNewItems()` true when `travel.mine.seen` is older and false when it is newer. This pins the field names.

  Add `notifyMineChanged()` to the NAV-2 and FAV-1 briefs as a required call after every write, because the same-tab
  dot updates only through that event.

### M3 (important). In forced colours, the mask icons disappear and the phone account link becomes invisible

- **Screen:** the phone header (the account link) and the bar, at any width below 640 px, in Windows contrast themes
  (forced colours).
- **What is wrong:** `.mi` paints the glyph as `background: currentColor` through a CSS mask. Forced colours replace
  backgrounds with the system Canvas colour, so every mask glyph vanishes. Rendered under `forcedColors: 'active'`
  (Chromium, 2x): the shipped `mi-user` and `mi-layers` glyphs draw nothing. The inline SVGs this replaced were stroked
  with `currentColor` and survived.
- **Why it matters:** the phone account link has no visible text, so a low-vision visitor gets a blank 44 px gap
  between Search and Theme and cannot find sign-in (P4). The bar keeps its labels but loses every icon and the amber
  current icon.
- **Fix** (`web/app/app.css`, after the `.mi-*` rules):

  ```css
  @media (forced-colors: active) {
    .mi { forced-color-adjust: none; background: LinkText; }
    .bottom-bar button .mi { background: ButtonText; }
    .bottom-bar a[aria-current] .mi, .phone-acct[aria-current='page'] .mi, .mine-link[aria-current='page'] .mi { background: Highlight; }
    .bottom-bar a[data-dot]::after, .mine-link[data-dot]::before { forced-color-adjust: none; background: Highlight; box-shadow: none; }
  }
  ```

  The first rule was rendered and the glyphs reappear in LinkText. Add an e2e case: with `forcedColors: 'active'` at
  390, the `.phone-acct .mi` box has a non-transparent computed `background-color`.

## 3. Should fix

**S1. Account card buttons are 30 px, not 38.** Screen: `/mine/`, every width, sign-in build. `AccountCard.tsx` uses
`size="sm"` for **Sign in**, **Create an account** and **Account**, so they draw at 30 px with a padded 44 px hit area.
Spec 5.1 and the shots of the spec mock use the 38 px secondary. Why: these are the first controls in `main` and the one
way to keep the list beyond this browser (D7, P7). At 30 px they look like a footnote next to the 44 px tabs. Fix: drop
`size="sm"` from the three `ButtonLink`s in `web/app/mine/AccountCard.tsx`. The device notice keeps `sm`, as the spec
says.

**S2. The `mine` capability flag is missing.** Screen: none visible; this is the rollback. Bundle A 7.6 step 8 says:
on unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_MINE=0`; off means four bar items, no `/mine/` link, and `/mine/` a noindex notice. Why: bundle A
releases roll back together; the flag is the only way to take Mine out without reverting NAV-2 and FAV-1 with it.
Fix: add `mine` to `web/lib/capabilities.ts` with that rule, read it in `BottomBar.tsx` (omit the item), `SiteHeader.tsx`
(omit the slot) and `web/app/mine/page.tsx` (the notice), and add a shell-flags test. Alternatively the coordinator
records in bundle A that a revert is the rollback and strikes step 8.

**S3. The interim lists show raw data.** Screen: `/mine/` with a copy (both builds, every width). The row's date
reads "2027-07-03" and a favourite's kind reads "lake". Why: when a trip starts is part of every answer and must be
readable at a glance (T1, P6), and lowercase ids look broken. Fix in `web/app/mine/MineShell.tsx`: format `start` as
"Sat 3 Jul 2027" (`Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-GB', { weekday: 'short', day: 'numeric', month: 'short', year: 'numeric',
timeZone: 'UTC' })`, with the comma removed, the form the copy sheet shows), and show the kind through
`kindLabel(kind, null)` from `web/lib/public/taxonomy.ts`, with the country after it ("Lake · Italy"). If NAV-2 and
FAV-1 ship in the same release as NAV-1, this is superseded and can be skipped. It must not reach the live site as is.

**S4. One message for two different failures.** Screen: `/mine/`, a list the browser holds but this version cannot read.
`Unreadable` says "This browser holds data this version of the site can't read" for both `newer` and `corrupt`. Why: for
a damaged list that is untrue, and for a newer list the visitor needs one action: reload (P4). Fix in
`MineShell.tsx`: pass the reason. For `newer`: title "This list was written by a newer version of the site", line
"Reload the page to see it. Nothing has been changed.", primary **Reload** (`location.reload()`). For `corrupt`: title
"We couldn't read your trips" (or favourites), line "The list in this browser is damaged. It has been left exactly as
it is.", no action.

**S5. Blocked storage stacks two empty states.** Screen: `/mine/` with `localStorage` blocked and nothing kept (not in the
builder's shots; read from the code and the e2e). The blocked `EmptyState` sits above the tabs, then the panel shows
"No trips copied yet" as a second large box. Why: two bordered 48 px-padded boxes push the tabs and the browse action
below the fold at 390, and the visitor reads two messages where one is enough (P3). Fix: when `state.blocked`, render
the blocked message as a components 5.4 notice (`Banner kind="notice"`, the same title and line, **Sign in** when the
capability is on) above the tabs, and keep the panel's own empty state. Add `mine-blocked` at 390 and 1440, light and
dark, to the NAV-V1 shot list.

**S6. The device notice lays out differently in the two builds on a phone.** Screen: `/mine/` with something kept, 320
and 390, public build. With only **Got it**, the action sits to the right of the text and the text wraps to 7 lines in
about 230 px; the sign-in build puts its two buttons under the text. Why: the notice is long, so a narrow column makes
it harder to read (P3), and the same component should not change shape between builds. Fix in
`web/app/mine/mine.css`: `@media (max-width: 639px) { .mine-notice .ban-action { flex-basis: 100%; padding-left: 28px; } }`
(28 px lines the buttons up under the text, after the 16 px icon and its gap).

**S7. The signed-in and dark proofs need to be redone for NAV-V1.** Spec section 12 asks for dark set through the
header toggle and a real local session. These shots used a `localStorage` theme and a mocked session response. The
e2e covers the chunk check with the same mock. NAV-V1 must:
- shoot the phone header at 320, 390 and 639, signed in, with a NEON-SETUP session, and confirm that no `AccountMenu`
  chunk loads below 640 px;
- set dark by clicking "Switch to dark theme";
- add desktop shots at 640, 899 and 900, signed out and signed in (the 640 px cluster gap was measured at 23 px with
  Sign in; check that it holds with the avatar);
- run the VoiceOver pass of section 9 on Safari for the header, the bar and the tabs.

## 4. Polish

- **P1. Gap before the notice.** `.mine-body` uses `--s-6` (24 px) everywhere. Spec 5.1 puts 32 px between the panel and
  the notice. Fix: `.mine-notice { margin-top: var(--s-2); }` (24 + 8), or set the gap with `--s-8` on that element
  only.
- **P2. Uneven spacing in the desktop cluster.** At 1440 the space between Theme and Mine looks wider than between Search
  and Theme. The 88 px slot centres a link about 57 px wide (15.5 px each side), while the icon buttons have 10 px.
  Fix: `.mine-link { justify-content: flex-start; padding-inline: 10px; }` and set the 900 px slot width to the measured
  link width (about 77 px). Re-run the 900 px gap row of spec 4.
- **P3. Mine is current on the public build's `/sign-in/` placeholder.** There is no sign-in in that build, so lighting
  Mine there points at nothing. Fix: `mineCurrent` returns `undefined` for the account routes when
  `capabilities.signIn` is off (pass the flag in from `BottomBar`).
- **P4. Two `role="status"` empty states.** The empty states in the hidden panel and the blocked state each carry
  `role="status"`, so a screen reader may announce both on load. Fix: drop `role` from `EmptyState` when it renders
  inside a `tabpanel`, or pass `role={null}` from `MineShell`.
- **P5. `/favourites/` answers 307, not the 308 of D7.** Temporary is the right call while Favourites is a placeholder.
  Make it `permanent: true` in the FAV-1 commit.
- **P6. The `/sign-in/` lede says "Save and revisit your trip drafts."** This clashes with the "never saved" rule and
  the Mine vocabulary (visitor spec 3). Owner: NAV-3 or the account page lane. Fix: "Keep your trips and favourites on
  every device."

## 7. Principles applied

- **P3 (a glance, then a few clicks):** sign-in is one tap from the header and Mine one tap from the bar.
- **P4 (no manual needed):** the dot uses no number, and the empty states each say what to do.
- **P7 (the 90% goal):** catalogue items stay first in the bar, and Mine is last.
- **P8 and P9:** the bar ships no storage code and no Zod; the Mine readers are lazy; nothing new loads in bulk.
- **T1:** the interim date is not readable yet (S3).
- **T2:** 44 px targets in thumb reach (S8); the trip-page budget is at risk (M1).

Where NAV-1 falls short: M1 and M2 are process gaps that hide future breakage, and M3 is an accessibility regression
introduced by the mask change.
