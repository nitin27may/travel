# P6: globe route, coverage layer, catalog lists, area pages, backlog view

## Goal

Port the legacy globe into the app at the same `/globe/` URL with the same drill-down, hash parameters, labels and pin counts, then extend it with an entity switch, list filters and a coverage layer. Add the descriptor-driven catalog lists (activities, stays, eateries, offers), area guide pages and the backlog view, and retire `legacy/globe/` once the owner confirms.

## Prerequisites (state at start)

P3b and P4c merged on `main` (P5a, P5b1-P5b3, P5c may run in parallel).

- `legacy/globe/index.html` and `legacy/globe/assets/` (441 files: admin TopoJSON per country, data, vendored MapLibre 6.14.0, gazetteer), served at `/globe/` by `tools/publish.py`; `tools/globe_data.py` writes `legacy/globe/assets/` (heavy download steps are manual and are **not** re-run in this phase); `tools/location_views.py` `Paths.globe_places = legacy/globe/assets/data/places.json`; `tools/tests/test_globe_data.py`, `test_globe_page.py`.
- `web/`: `EntityPage` in four views, `MapView` (`maplibre-gl@6.14.0`), `DetailSheet`, URL codec with the list facets; catalog rows and `data/catalog/{coverage,backlog}.json` from P4c.
- Spec: `docs/PLAN-platform.md` 4.6 (globe), 4.11 (catalog lists, area pages, backlog, provenance line), 4.2 (descriptors), `docs/GLOBE-RESEARCH.md` (sources and licences).

## Files you own

`web/app/globe/**`, `web/components/globe/**`, `web/public/geo/**`, `web/app/{activities,stays,eateries,offers,areas,backlog}/**`, `web/lib/entities/descriptors/{activity,stay,eatery,offer,area}.ts`, `web/tests/fixtures/globe-hashes.json`, `web/tests/**` files named `globe-*` and `catalog-*`; `tools/globe_data.py` (output paths only), `tools/location_views.py` (`Paths.globe_places` only), `tools/tests/test_globe_data.py`, `tools/tests/test_globe_page.py`; the `globe` route in `tools/publish.py` (append-only change to `ROUTES`); `legacy/MANIFEST.json` (the `globe` entry); deleting `legacy/globe/` (final step, after the owner's yes).

Must not touch: `web/components/{plan,map,create}/**` (import only), `web/app/[slug]/**`, `core/**`, `data/**` except reading, `.claude/**`.

2. Do not re-run `globe_data.py base`, `admin1`, `admin2` or `gazetteer` (downloads from Natural Earth and geoBoundaries); move committed outputs only.
3. MapLibre must stay 6.14.0.
4. 441 asset files plus the app stay well under the 15,000-file test; check it anyway.
5. Deletion needs the owner's yes, stated with what is lost.
6. Tests never fetch tiles; kill servers by PID; never `cd`; never `git add -A`.
