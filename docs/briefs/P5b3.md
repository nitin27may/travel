# P5b3: bookings and review screens, notes, custom items, search and indexes, guardrails

## Goal

Finish the creation flow: S8 (bookings and costs) and S9 (review and save), notes and custom stops, one search box that finds catalog rows by name, near a point or along a route and adds anything else as a custom item, and every guardrail of the plan with its one-tap fixes. Build the static search and spatial indexes the search box, the offline cache and the routing phase read.

## Prerequisites (state at start)

P5b2 merged on `main` (P5a, P5c, P6, P7 may run in parallel).

- `/new/` S0-S7 with TripDraft, undo, autosave, Download/Import, the day board and `BoardMap`, `/edit/?trip=` through the local API.
- `core/`: operations including `note.*`, `custom_item.add`, `custom_item.update`, `trip_booking.upsert`, `trip_booking.set_status`; book-by and edition resolution; day checks (`W_*`).
- `web/lib/views/` (entity views at build), `web/lib/deeplinks/` (P5a, if merged; if not, use Booking.com and Google Maps URL builders of your own in `web/lib/create/links.ts` and report).
- Spec: `docs/PLAN-platform.md` 0B.2 (S8, S9), 0B.3 (search to add), 0B.4 (notes and stops), 0B.5 ("Corridor search against the static catalog": geohash layout, row encoding, Overpass rules), 0B.7 (guardrails table), 2.3 (`notes`, `custom_items`), 4.13.

### Step 1. Search and spatial indexes

`web/lib/index/` writer (run at build by `pnpm --dir web data:index`, also from `next build`): a name index (`/data/index/search.json`: id, type, name, local names, aliases, tags, area, "in N trips") and the spatial index of plan 0B.5: every public catalog row with coordinates in geohash-5 buckets, one file per geohash-3 prefix at `/data/index/geo/<gh3>.json`, compact rows `[id, type, lat, lon, tag_bits, kind, open_summary, quality]` (`quality` is 0 until planner v2 defines it), trails indexed by trailhead. Reader in the same module: `near(lat, lon, radius_km, types)`, `alongRoute(polyline, corridor_km, types)` (sample every 1 km, cover with geohash-5 cells, load the needed geohash-3 files, filter exactly by point-to-polyline distance). Geohash encode and decode written here (no dependency). Visibility: only rows the public export allows.
Tests: encode/decode round trip; the writer is deterministic; `near` and `alongRoute` against a fixture catalog give exact expected ids; file size per row under 100 bytes on average.

Commit `P5b3 step 1: search and spatial indexes with one reader and writer`.

### Step 2. UniversalSearch and custom items

`UniversalSearch` (one box on the board and in S6; `/` focuses it, Ctrl+K opens it as a palette): by name (prefix and fuzzy match over the name index, grouped by type); by category near here (chips: food, viewpoint, short walk, playground, fuel, rest area, museum, stay; near the map view, the selected day's base or the selected item; sorted by distance); along my route (the selected day's legs as a polyline, sorted by estimated detour); "Search OpenStreetMap too" (Overpass on demand through `web/lib/osm/`: one query per window with `around:<radius>`, results cached in IndexedDB for 30 days by query hash, at most one query every 5 seconds enforced by `web/lib/osm/limiter.ts` shared by every call, results labelled "From OpenStreetMap, not verified" with ODbL attribution, disabled offline with "needs a connection"); "Add as custom" (`CustomItemForm`: name prefilled, location by map click, pin drag or "no location", kind, notes, duration) creating a `custom_items` row and an item through `custom_item.add` and `item.add`; an OSM result becomes a custom item carrying its `osm_id`. "Suggest for the shared catalog" sets `proposed` and records a `catalog.propose` change in the draft's outbox (applied by the owner through `/review/` in Phase 1).
Tests: limiter with fake timers (two calls within 5 s: the second waits; a burst of five takes at least 20 s of fake time); Overpass client against a recorded response (no network); custom item round trip.

Commit `P5b3 step 2: universal search, OSM on demand with a tested limiter, custom items`.

## Pitfalls

1. Overpass is the one third-party call the static site makes for a user: on demand only, cached, limited, attributed; never in tests (recorded responses).
2. Index writer and reader live in one module; do not encode rows in one place and decode in another.
3. Private notes are x-private by visibility: the export filter must drop them, not only hide them.
4. Custom items are trip-local until the owner accepts a proposal.
5. Never `cd`; never `git add -A`; kill servers by PID.
