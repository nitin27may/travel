# P8: routing with breaks (Phase 1 part)

## Goal

Let the user set a driving chain over one or more days with a break interval and a daily maximum drive, and get ranked break and overnight suggestions along the route with detour minutes, each insertable with one tap. Phase 1 runs entirely in the browser over stored route geometry (or labelled straight-line estimates), using the static spatial index, with OpenStreetMap only on demand.

## Prerequisites (state at start)

P5b3 merged on `main` (P7 may run in parallel).

- `web/lib/index/` (search and spatial index writer and reader; `alongRoute(polyline, corridor_km, types)`), `web/lib/osm/` (Overpass client with the tested 5-second limiter and IndexedDB cache), `UniversalSearch`, custom items, guardrails; the day board and `BoardMap`; `/edit/?trip=`.
- `core/`: operations including `route.set`, `item.add`, `custom_item.add`; day checks (`W_DRIVE_LIMIT`, `W_DAYLIGHT`); sun; the `routes` table and the route sidecar schema `route-geometry/1` (plan 0B.5).
- Spec: `docs/PLAN-platform.md` 0B.5 (algorithm, break types, corridor search, route storage and the test fixture, D23, D25 defaults: 4 h a day, break every 2 h), 0B.6, 0B.7 ("Overnight not found"), 5.8 (`route.*`, `search.along_route`).

## Owner approval before you start

The test fixture needs one routed chain, fetched once from OpenRouteService (free plan) with the owner's key: the owner runs step 1's fetch, or tells you to run it with `ORS_API_KEY` from the environment. The key is never written to a file, a commit or Claude settings.

### Step 2. Breaks, day split, corridor, overnight, rank

`core/src/routing/` (pure, deterministic, shared with the Phase 2 Worker), per plan 0B.5 steps 2-8: day split by `daily_max_drive_min` (per-day overrides), break targets at `k * break_every_min` from each day's start skipping targets within 30 min of a planned item or the day's end, corridor windows `t ± 20 min` buffered by `corridor_km` (default 5, 1-25), candidates from `alongRoute` filtered by break types (table in 0B.5), overnight search at each day end (`corridor_km * 3`, at most 30 km; catalog stays first, then towns with stays), detour estimate `2 x perpendicular distance / 50 km/h x 1.3`, `rank = 3*type_match + 2*interest + 1*open_now - detour_min/10 - penalty` (penalty 2 when arriving after sunset minus 30 min; sun from `core`), ties by catalog id. Unrouted chains: straight line x 1.3 at 70 km/h, every number labelled "estimate".
Tests on the fixture: a 2 h interval yields targets at 2 h and 4 h with ranked candidates and detour minutes; "max 4 h a day" splits a 7 h chain into two days with an overnight suggestion from catalog stays; the pass caution appears on the hand-marked segment and the ferry caution on the synthetic fixture; no suggestion lies outside the corridor (property test over random corridor widths); ranks are deterministic; `t_s` and `d_m` lengths equal the decoded vertex count.

## Acceptance criteria

1. On the committed fixture: targets at 2 h and 4 h with ranked candidates and detour minutes; the 7 h chain splits into two days with an overnight suggestion from catalog stays.
2. Pass and ferry cautions appear on the hand-marked fixtures.
3. Inserting a candidate recomputes legs and day checks; undo restores the bytes.
4. An unrouted A-B shows estimates labelled as such.
5. Overpass is called only on demand and the second identical call is served from cache.
6. Property tests: no suggestion beyond the corridor; ranks deterministic.
7. `bash tools/check-all.sh` ends with `CHECK-ALL OK`.

## Pitfalls

1. No routing provider is ever called from tests, hooks, builds or users' browsers in Phase 1; only the owner's `tools/route.py`.
2. Encoded polylines carry no time: keep `t_s` and `d_m` in the sidecar, one entry per decoded vertex, precision 6.
3. The ORS key lives in the environment only; never in a file, a commit or Claude settings.
4. ORS geometry is ODbL-derived: keep the licence line in every sidecar and on the map attribution.
5. Mountain-road estimates mislead (R29): label every estimate and offer the Google Maps link on every leg.
6. Kill servers by PID; never `cd`; never `git add -A`.
