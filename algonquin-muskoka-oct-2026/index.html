Before the days start

What this weekend is

Muskoka and the Algonquin highlands sit on the Canadian Shield: billion-year-old granite and gneiss scraped thin by ice sheets. That is why the lakes are countless, the soil is thin and the rock ledges at Echo Rock and Cache Lake are bare enough to stand on and see for kilometres. (General background, not taken from the research files.)

Why it is famous, specifically. The colour sequence. Sugar and red maple go first — on the west side of Hwy 60 the average peak is 27 September. Then poplar and birch gold, beech bronze and last tamarack, the larch that turns gold before it drops its needles, expected to peak in mid-October. The 10th to the 12th sits in that second act, which is worth planning for rather than being disappointed by.

What is different about 2026. This is an early colour year. On 26 September Friends of Algonquin had the maples at canopy best, while Lake of Bays was still at 10–20%. So the lakeside bookends of this trip (Limberlost, Dwight, Dorset) are likelier to be at their best than the Algonquin maples on Sunday. The Sunday plan is built around the ledge and the lake, not around the red.

Six things that change how you read every day below

  • 1Sunday hangs on one booking. The Algonquin day permit is $21 per vehicle and is sold online only, up to 5 days ahead. That puts Sunday 11 October on sale Tuesday 6 October, 07:00 ET (inferred from the 5-day rule; verify). The Hwy 60 corridor sold out for 11–12 Oct in 2025 and 12–13 Oct in 2024, both by 8 October. Ontario Parks says there is no walk-up once it sells out. Plan B is below.
  • 2Light sets the order. Echo Rock faces east-southeast and is back-lit until about 13:30; the Track and Tower ledge faces west-southwest and is front-lit until about 10:30. So Solitaire is Saturday afternoon and Track and Tower is Sunday morning. This is why Limberlost is not first thing on Saturday.
  • 3Monday is thin. Closed on Mondays by regular hours: Boiler Room, Cast Iron, Lake of Bays Brewing, Henrietta’s, Family Place. LCBO and Beer Store are closed Thanksgiving Monday. Dwight Market is closed Sunday. Shop in Huntsville on Saturday. The lodge itself lists “Closed for the Season” on 12 October.
  • 4Hunting season is open. Grouse, bear and archery deer are open around Dwight, Huntsville and Oxtongue; moose (19 Oct) and gun deer (2 Nov) are after the trip. Hunting is banned inside Limberlost and, apart from three townships, inside Algonquin. Hunter orange is required only for bear hunters not in a tree stand, so for you it is advice: wear something bright at dusk.
  • 5Cold and dark, early. Mean high 12.4 °C, mean low 1.9 °C for these dates; about 40% of nights are at or below 0 °C and 54% of days see measurable rain. Sunset is 18:35–18:39 and it is dark by 19:05–19:09. New moon is Saturday. Deer and moose are on the road at dusk.
  • 6Offline on Hwy 60. Cell service is worst between km 20 and 30, which includes Track and Tower. Screenshot the permit. AllTrails offline maps are a paid tier; the Limberlost 2026 map is a free PDF.

Solitaire Lake Trail

The loop around Solitaire Lake: sandy beach, a boardwalk under cliffs, hemlock and old pine, a heron rookery. Expect mud, roots and some sections beside private cottages. It is a half-day, not an all-day hike.

Track and Tower Trail

There is no tower. The Skymount fire tower stood until the 1970s. What you walk is an old railway grade with the ruins of a trestle, a dam at Cache Lake, small falls, a wooden bridge near 5.4 km and about 130 steps up to a ledge. Flat for the first 3 km, then steadily up. Wet leaves on rock.

No closure appears on the official alerts page dated 27 September 2026, but there is no dated 2026 trail-condition report. Hardwood Lookout Trail, a different trail, is closed 9–12 October. Dogs are allowed on a leash.

Drive home — leave by 13:30 or after 19:30 decision rule

178 km, 167 minutes free-flow, so about 16:00 in Whitby if the road is clear. Thanksgiving Monday southbound traffic on Hwy 11 and 400 is unmeasured, and the only warnings found were low-quality. The rule is to leave early, or to stay for dinner in Huntsville and leave after 19:30; the in-between hours are the ones to avoid. Leaving at 19:30 means driving in the dark (sunset is 18:35), so it is also the option with the deer and moose risk.
