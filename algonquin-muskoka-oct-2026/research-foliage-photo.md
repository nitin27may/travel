# Research lane: seasonal timing and photography (raw data)

Trip: Sat 10 Oct - Mon 12 Oct 2026 (Mon 12 Oct = Canadian Thanksgiving). Base: 1679 Port Cunnington Rd, Dwight ON P0C 1J0 (Lake of Bays). Stops: Solitaire Lake Trail (Limberlost, Huntsville) and Track and Tower Trail (Algonquin, Hwy 60 km 25.0).
Data gathered 2026-09-27. Format: raw claims, each with a source URL. `GAP:` = could not verify, with the failed source named. `INFERENCE:` = my own derivation from cited data, not stated by any source.

Source-quality note: the WebSearch summaries repeatedly mixed 2024/2025 pages with 2026 numbers (for example attributing 2026 "Huntsville 50%" to Thanksgiving 2025). Every dated statement below was re-read from the fetched primary page or a web.archive.org snapshot, not taken from search summaries, unless flagged "search snippet only".

## 0. Flags the synthesizer should not miss

1. The 2026 Algonquin colour season is running EARLY. On 26 Sep 2026 maples were already at canopy best and the park average maple peak is 27 Sep. By 10-12 Oct the maples will very likely be past peak. INFERENCE (see section 1.5). The Oct 10-12 look is "golden encore": poplar/birch gold, maple understory orange, beech bronze, tamarack starting.
2. Track and Tower's lookout overlooks Cache Lake only. Lake of Two Rivers is a different lake about 8 km further east (OSM lake centroid 45.5786, -78.4794 vs Cache Lake 45.5404, -78.5915). The brief's "Cache Lake/Lake of Two Rivers" framing is wrong for this trail.
3. Algonquin day-use permits for Thanksgiving weekend sell out days early (2025: Oct 11, 12 sold out on Oct 8; 2024: Oct 12, 13 sold out on Oct 8). Permits can be bought only 5 days ahead. So Oct 10 opens ~Oct 5, Oct 11 ~Oct 6, Oct 12 ~Oct 7. See 1.7.
4. Limberlost is open 9:00-17:00 daily (its own site). Sunset is ~18:37, so no sunrise or sunset shoot inside Limberlost, and Echo Rock is front-lit only after 17:00 (section 2.4).
5. Huckleberry Rock is NOT in Dwight. It is at 1057 Milford Bay Rd, Bracebridge/Muskoka Lakes Township, 46 km straight-line from Dwight Beach (section 3).
6. Dorset Scenic Tower: the only hours/fees/reservation info I could read are the 2024 seasonal ones (reservations were required for all visits in fall 2024). 2026 terms are a GAP (section 3).
7. New moon is Fri 10 Oct 2026 at 15:50 UTC (11:50 EDT). All three nights are moonless. Aurora chance is weak on the current NOAA outlook (Kp 3 on 11-12 Oct).

## 1. Fall colour timing

### 1.1 Typical timing, Algonquin west side / Hwy 60 (Friends of Algonquin Park)
Source: https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/general_park_info/fall-colour-report.php (fetched 2026-09-27; page content dated to 26 Sep 2026).
- Average peak of the Sugar Maple canopy, western Hwy 60 corridor: 27 September (observations since 1973, per a search snippet of the Ontario Parks blog https://blog.ontarioparks.ca/fall-colours-at-algonquin-provincial-park/; not read on the page).
- Earliest recorded peak 15 Sep 1982; latest 9 Oct 1996. 2016 peak 6 Oct ("almost record late", warm fall and late frost). 2025 peak 25 Sep.
- Sequence stated by the Friends: mid-Sep to early Oct = Sugar and Red Maple (canopy). Early to mid Oct "including Thanksgiving" = "Golden Encore": yellow-orange poplar and birch, plus orange Sugar Maple understory (after canopy leaves drop). Mid to late Oct = Tamarack at peak yellow. "By late October or early November ... all deciduous trees are bare".
- Tamarack sites named: Spruce Bog Boardwalk, Mizzy Lake Trail, Opeongo Road.
- Friends' 2026 statement (26 Sep): poplar "traditionally shows its best colour in early October"; tamarack "expected to show its best fall colour in mid-October"; maple canopy "may slightly improve over the next several days, but strong winds could increase leaf fall".
- Park is higher than surroundings (up to ~600 m); "best colour is observed several weeks (or more!) prior to" Toronto/Ottawa and even nearby communities outside the park.
- East side of Hwy 60 corridor is better for poplar/birch encore; west side is maple-dominated. Friends: "The eastern portion of the Highway 60 Corridor and the Park's East Side is a great location to view this colour."

### 1.2 Species order past maple peak: beech, oak, tamarack (verified)
Source: Ontario Parks blog, "Algonquin's second fall colours peak: the golden encore", published 8 Oct 2024: https://blog.ontarioparks.ca/algonquins-second-fall-colours/
- Golden encore "usually takes hold about a week after the maple peak and can last through much of October and sometimes even into early November."
- American Beech: peak "golden-yellow to yellow-brown". Yellow/White Birch and Trembling/Largetooth Aspen/Balsam Poplar: golden. Red Oak: "brownish-red to deep scarlet". Tamarack: deep golden-yellow.
- Where (Hwy 60): birch/poplar at Centennial Ridges Trail, Lookout Trail, hills between Centennial Ridges Road and East Gate, Visitor Centre viewing deck. Tamarack at Mizzy Lake Trail, Hemlock Bluff Trail parking lot, Mew Lake, Big Pines Trail parking lot, Opeongo Road, Algonquin Logging Museum area.
- The Friends' 2024 archived page adds: "Poplar dominated fall colour at the Visitor Centre peaks after the best Sugar Maple has occurred, typically in early to mid-October." (https://web.archive.org/web/20241004184020/https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/general_park_info/fall-colour-report.php)
- Friends' recommended side roads: Centennial Ridges Road (km 37.6) and Rock Lake Road (km 40.3) "best for early/mid-October viewing"; Opeongo Road (km 46.3) "best for late season foliage viewing". Same 2026 page as 1.1.

### 1.5 What this implies for 10-12 Oct 2026 (INFERENCE, not sourced)
- 2026 is an early-to-average year: near-freezing nights 22-25 Sep, frost 24 Sep, maple canopy at best 26 Sep (Friends). 2025 (peak 25 Sep) is the closest analogue: 16 Oct 2025 Algonquin was 80% leaf fall. Applying the 2025 timeline shifted about 2 days later gives 10-12 Oct 2026 = maple canopy largely dropping, understory maple orange, poplar/birch/beech gold-bronze at or near best, tamarack turning (peak due later, around mid-Oct per the Friends' 2026 sentence).
- Lake of Bays / Dwight, being ~10 days behind Algonquin at 26 Sep, is more likely to be at or just past maple peak on 10-12 Oct (Discover Muskoka's own guess for 2026 is peak maple 4-8 Oct, encore 9-18 Oct). So the lakeside colours are likely better at Dwight/Limberlost than the high Hwy 60 maples, while Algonquin offers the poplar/tamarack encore.
- Main risks to flag: wind and rain knocking leaves (Friends: "High winds, rain, or even snow can sometimes quickly result in fragile leaves being knocked off"); The Weather Network fall outlook published 9 Sep 2026 says mid-Sep to mid-Oct "may run surprisingly close to seasonal", below-normal precipitation overall (https://www.theweathernetwork.com/en/news/weather/seasonal/ontario-2026-fall-forecast). It gives no colour or Muskoka detail.
- GAP: no 2026 colour forecast for Muskoka/Algonquin beyond Discover Muskoka's date guesses. Instant Weather's Ontario page (https://instantweatherinc.com/ontario/fall) has only the same 25 Sep observation.

### 2.1 Method (reproducible)

### 2.3 Light direction rule used
Front-lit = sun behind the photographer (angle between view bearing and sun azimuth more than 120 deg); side-lit 60-120 deg; back-lit under 60 deg (sun in front). Foliage colour saturates best front- or side-lit; overcast lifts all colour and removes glare but flattens a wide view. The overcast/soft-light preference is standard photographic practice and is not from a fetched source (GAP: no source located; Discover Muskoka's pages carry no photo advice).

### 2.4 Per-spot results (Oct 11 sun path; Oct 10 and 12 shift by about 1 min)
1. Track and Tower lookout over Cache Lake. View: open water lies W to SW of the OSM viewpoint (bearings 210-310 deg at 1-1.5 km; centre about 255 deg). INFERENCE: it looks west-southwest.
   - Front-lit (sun behind you): sunrise until about 10:30 (sun az 100-135). This is the best window for colour on the far ridge: 07:30-10:30, and the lookout is ~4.1 km into the loop (adventurereport.ca https://www.adventurereport.ca/the-track-and-tower-trail-a-7-5-km-hike-in-algonquin-provincial-park/) or "4-5 km" (https://roadtripsandsuitcases.com/ontario-algonquin-track-and-tower-trail/), so with a 07:00 permit start a hiker reaches it about 08:15-09:00 (INFERENCE from those distances and ~4 km/h, sun 8-15 deg elevation).
   - Side-lit 10:30-14:00; back-lit 14:00 to sunset (sun az 214-260 in front of you; sunset az 260 sits almost in the centre of the view).
   - Sunset from the lookout: sun 15 deg at 17:00, sets 18:35. Golden hour starts 17:56. A visit for sunset means a 4+ km walk out in the dark: Friends allow it (permit valid to 22:00) but "ensure you are off any trail prior to darkness"; civil dusk 19:05.
   - Elevation: trailhead ~460 m, lake ~420 m, lookout ~525 m (http://mcelroy.ca/bushlog/20081105.html, 2008 GPS log). Lookout is on a rocky face reached by about 130 steps (roadtripsandsuitcases). Wind: "the wind was WHIPPING in this open section" (adventurereport.ca, a spring visit).

## 4. Wildlife on Hwy 60 in mid-October

- Moose: the Friends' fall page says: "you'll have a good chance of seeing a big bull Moose, its antlers now full-grown and polished, ready for the rut (mating season), in late September and early October." https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/general_park_info/fall-in-algonquin-park.php
- Road-salt pools are a spring effect, not a mid-October one. Sources: Gone Camping Again says salt draws moose "during April and May" and that in the rut moose become "less visible and significantly more dangerous", bulls "known to attack humans, cars, and even buildings" (https://gonecampingagain.com/how-to-see-moose-in-algonquin-park/). SeeTheWild: "Late May through mid-June is the most reliable window ... September is also a strong secondary window" and "early mornings along Highway 60 (especially between km 10 and km 30)" (https://seethewild.org/see-wolves-moose-loons-algonquin-provincial-park/; note it wrongly places the West Gate "near Dwight"). GAP: no source supports moose at road-salt pools on 10-12 Oct. Treat the salt-pool idea as unsupported for this date; the Friends' fall statement (rut, late Sep and early Oct) is the only official claim, and 10-12 Oct is at the tail of it.
- Time of day: Friends, "Go out in the evening or, better still, at dawn and the two or three hours which follow. Early morning is the best time to see birds and mammals." Habitats: "low-lying, wet areas such as bogs, ponds, and beaver meadows"; trails named: Beaver Pond Trail, Spruce Bog Boardwalk, Mizzy Lake Trail. Pull onto the shoulder and "stay at a respectful distance". https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/recreational_activites/wildlife-watching-in-algonquin-park.php
  Sun geometry: sunrise 07:27; civil dawn 06:57; permits valid from 07:00, so a dawn drive is legal only from 07:00. Wetland stretches at low sun: sun az 95-105 through 07:00-08:00 (the eastern sky glows behind eastbound traffic; it is behind the photographer when facing west).
- Birds: Gray Jay, Spruce Grouse, Boreal Chickadee and Black-backed Woodpecker are year-round Algonquin residents (https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/recreational_activites/bird-watching-in-algonquin-park.php). Spruce Bog Boardwalk is at Hwy 60 km 42.5, 1.5 km loop, northern spruce bogs (https://www.algonquinpark.on.ca/visit/recreational_activites/spruce-bog-boardwalk-trail.php). ## 8. Code used (Python, run with `uv venv && uv pip install astral ephem`)
```python
import datetime as dt, math
from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo
import ephem
from astral import Observer
from astral.sun import sun, azimuth, elevation, time_at_elevation, SunDirection
TZ = ZoneInfo("America/Toronto")
f = lambda t: t.astimezone(TZ).strftime("%H:%M")
SITES = {"Dwight Beach": (45.32881, -79.0112), "Track&Tower lookout": (45.54226, -78.57055)}
for name, (lat, lon) in SITES.items():
    obs = Observer(latitude=lat, longitude=lon, elevation=0)
    for d in (10, 11, 12):
        date = dt.date(2026, 10, d)
        s = sun(obs, date=date, tzinfo=TZ)
        o = ephem.Observer(); o.lat, o.lon = str(lat), str(lon)
        o.elevation = 0; o.pressure = 0; o.horizon = "-0:50"      # cross-check
```
