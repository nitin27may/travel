Whitby -> Port Cunnington Lodge (Lake of Bays, Dwight) -> home. Sat 10 to Mon 12 Oct, two nights, one base.

## Orientation

Background, not from the research files: the Algonquin highlands and Muskoka sit on the Canadian Shield, billion-year-old
granite and gneiss scraped thin by ice sheets. That is why the soil is thin, the lakes are countless and rock ledges like the
Cache Lake lookout and Echo Rock are bare. Sourced facts: the Dorset tower stands 30 m and 142 m above Lake of Bays; the
Cache Lake lookout is about 4.1 km in.

Why it is famous, specifically: **the colour sequence.** Sugar and red maple peak first (Algonquin 27 Sep on average),
then poplar and birch gold, beech bronze and, last, **tamarack**, the larch that turns gold and drops its needles, expected
to peak mid-October (research-foliage-photo.md s1). 10-12 Oct sits in that second act.

What you cannot get here elsewhere in this folder: a national-park-scale corridor (Hwy 60) two hours from Whitby, with a
lake-country base where the shoreline itself is the second half of the trip.

Sub-regions:
1. **Limberlost** (Sat) - private forest reserve, free, 9:00-17:00, signed online waiver. Solitaire Lake, Echo Rock.
2. **Lake of Bays / Dwight / Dorset** (base, Sat evening, Mon) - lodge, Dwight Beach, Dorset tower.
3. **Algonquin Hwy 60** (Sun) - Track and Tower, km 25. Optional Visitor Centre deck, km 43.
4. **Oxtongue / Ragged Falls** (Sun) - 80 ft falls, park open to 18 Oct.
5. **Huntsville** (Sat lunch, Mon) - Lions Lookout, murals, food when the small towns are shut.

## Six things to know before reading the days

1. **The Sunday permit is the only real point of failure.** $21 per vehicle per day, 07:00-22:00, online only. Release is 5 days
   ahead, so Sat 10 = Mon 5 Oct, **Sun 11 = Tue 6 Oct**, Mon 12 = Wed 7 Oct (inferred; verify on Ontario Parks). Ontario
   Parks says there is no walk-up entry once permits sell out. Refund terms: GAP.
2. **Colour: early year.** Do not expect peak red maples on Hwy 60. 2025 calibration: 16 Oct Lake of Bays "past peak, 20%
   leaf fall, still nice colour"; Algonquin 80% leaf fall. Check the reports on 8 Oct.
3. **Light sets the order.** See the table above.
4. **Monday is thin.** Closed Mondays on regular hours: Boiler Room, Cast Iron, Lake of Bays Brewing (Baysville), Henrietta's,
   Family Place. LCBO and Beer Store closed Thanksgiving Monday. Dwight Market closed Sunday [secondary source]. Buy
   groceries and alcohol Sat or Sun in Huntsville. The lodge calendar says "Closed for the Season" on Mon 12 Oct.
5. **Hunting is open.** WMU 50 (Dwight, Limberlost), 53A (Huntsville, Baysville) and 54 (Oxtongue): grouse, bear, archery
   deer. Moose (19-25 Oct) and gun deer (2 Nov) are after the trip. Hunting is banned inside Limberlost and, except in
   three townships, Algonquin. Hunter orange is legally required only for bear hunters not in a tree stand; for you it is
   advice. Wear something bright on the road at dusk.
6. **Cold, dark, offline.** Mean 12.4 C high, 1.9 C low for 10-12 Oct (Dwight/Oxtongue station); about 40% of nights at
   or below 0 C; 54% of days have measurable precipitation. Sunset 18:35-18:39, dark by 19:05-19:09, new moon 10 Oct.
   Cell service on Hwy 60 is worst between km 20 and 30, which includes Track and Tower.
