# Research — the Green Mountain gaps + a full viewpoint audit
Researched Sept 7, 2026. Triggered by: Lincoln Gap and Appalachian Gap appear nowhere in PLAN.md.

## 1. The two gaps, honestly compared

### Appalachian Gap — VT-17, 2,375 ft (Buels Gore)
- **Drive-up view. No walking.** Large overlook parking lot on the right just over the crest coming
  from Waitsfield / on the left just before the crest coming from Bristol.
- View is **west**: Champlain Valley, Lake Champlain, the Adirondacks. => **afternoon / golden hour**,
  not morning. Morning puts you shooting into the sun.
- Road: sustained 15%+ pitches both sides, hairpins. Fine for a normal car; no trailers.
- Long Trail crosses here — north side to Baby Stark / Molly Stark, south side to Stark Mountain.
- Lot fills in foliage season; arrive early or late, not 11–3.
- Mad River Glen is immediately south on the east side.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appalachian_Gap · https://www.komoot.com/highlight/6146814 ·
  https://vermontvacation.com/7-seasonal-scenic-drives-in-vermont/

### Lincoln Gap — 2,424 ft, Warren ↔ Lincoln
- **The DRIVE is the attraction, not a viewpoint.** Highest road pass in Vermont; contains the
  **steepest paved mile of public highway in the US**, max grade ~24%, ~1,800 ft of climb.
  Paved on the Warren (east) side, **dirt on the Lincoln (west) side**, changeover at the top.
- **The gap itself is wooded.** Two small lots ~100 yd apart at the height of land; the western
  lot has the better vista but it is not a panorama. **The rocky-summit photos on the Google
  listing are Mount Abraham, reached by hiking — not visible from the car.**
- Not recommended for trailers. Narrow, twisty, no guardrail in places.
- **SEASONAL: closes for winter ~Oct 15.** Opened May 14, 2026; tentative 2026 winter closure
  **Oct 15, 2026**. Our window Oct 1–4 is **open, with ~11 days of margin.**
- Open flag: a temporary culvert repair was in place spring 2026 with a **full culvert replacement
  planned for spring/summer 2026**. Neither warrenvt.org nor lincolnvermont.org carried a 2026
  construction notice as of this research. **Verify before driving it** — Warren highway dept
  802-496-2709, or https://trailsnh.com/roads/status/58/lincoln-gap-road
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Gap_(Vermont) ·
  https://www.mychamplainvalley.com/news/local-news/vermont/lincoln-gap-road-closes-for-the-2025-2026-winter-season/ ·
  https://www.fs.usda.gov/r09/gmfl/recreation/lincoln-gap-road · https://lincolnvermont.org/highway
- NOTE: vermontvacation.com lists Lincoln Gap as "open year-round". **That is wrong** — the town
  posts a hard winter closure. Trust the town, not the tourism board.

### Which view is better — the direct answer
- **View per minute spent: Appalachian Gap wins outright.** It is a car door and a 20-metre walk.
- **Best single view of the two areas: Mount Abraham** — but it costs 4 hours and 2,500 ft.
- **Best compromise: Sunset Ledge** — 75 minutes for a Champlain/Adirondack ledge view.
- **Lincoln Gap as a stop, with no hike, is not worth an 85-minute drive.** Its value is the road.
- Both prime views face **west** → both are afternoon/sunset assets, neither is a morning stop.

## 3. Viewpoint audit — what else the plan is missing
Ranked by (value added) ÷ (cost to the schedule).

### A. Barr Hill Natural Area, Greensboro — **best ratio found, add it**
0.7 mi / 88 ft / 30–60 min. **The panorama is from the open field at the trailhead** — you barely
have to walk. Sweeps from Groton's Spruce & Signal Mountains across the southern Greens, Woodbury
Mtn, **Camel's Hump, Elmore Mountain and Mount Mansfield**, plus Lake Caspian and Long Pond.
~15 min from Craftsbury Common, **which is already on the Saturday route.**
https://www.nature.org/en-us/get-involved/how-to-help/places-we-protect/barr-hill-natural-area/ ·
https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/barr-hill-natural-area

### B. Burke Mountain Toll Road — **the real find**
3-mile paved historic CCC toll road to a **summit parking area**, then a short walk to the fire
tower: **360°, and it looks straight at Willoughby Gap** and the whole Kingdom. Picnic pavilion
midway. Universally accessible at the top.
- Fee: **$5–$10 per vehicle** (sources disagree — northstarmonthly says $5/carload, vermontexplored
  says $10). Hours quoted 8:00–17:00 daily. **Season ends mid-October** → Oct 3 is inside it, barely.
- ~20 min south of Lake Willoughby (Westmore → East Burke).
- **This is the drive-up substitute for Mount Pisgah** on a wet day or tired legs — same view
  neighbourhood, 360° instead of Pisgah's treed summit, $10 and 20 minutes instead of 3h45.
- **VERIFY 2026 season end date + fee: 802-626-7300.**
https://vermontexplored.com/drive-up-vermont-mountains/ ·
https://www.northstarmonthly.com/features/driving-the-toll-road-for-a-picnic-on-burke-mountain/article_3a12355c-80ec-11ef-801f-1b55bf595ddc.html

### C. Mount Elmore fire tower — **15 minutes from where we sleep, and it wasn't in the plan**
4.2 mi loop / 1,470 ft (or ~3 mi RT direct), moderate, ~3 h. Fire tower on the National Historic
Lookout Registry: Mount Mansfield, Camel's Hump, the Worcester Range, Lake Elmore below, and the
Presidentials east. Elmore is nicknamed the "Beauty Spot of Vermont"; the lake-with-mountain
reflection is one of the most photographed scenes in New England in October.
Elmore State Park, ~15 min from 1592 Center Rd. On the GMC's 31-foliage-hikes list.
https://www.vtstateparks.com/content/hike-mount-elmore-fire-tower · https://fpr.vermont.gov/elmore-state-park

### D. Prospect Rock, Johnson — **the cheap local win**
2.6 mi RT / ~460 ft / ~1 h 10. Long Trail north from VT-15, over a **suspension bridge across the
Lamoille River** at Ithiel Falls. Flat bedrock ledge over the Lamoille valley and the Sterling
Range. ~15 min from base. Fits the existing Saturday 16:00 slot better than the rail trail does.
https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/prospect-rock--2 ·
https://www.greenmountainclub.org/hiking/hiking-recommendations/lamoille-river-valley/

### E. Jay Peak aerial tram
Weekends **through Oct 11**, 10:00–17:00, **$15/person**, to ~4,000 ft — the only aerial foliage
view in the north. Oct 3, 2026 is a Saturday, so it runs. ~50 min north of base.
Third the price of the Stowe gondola. Costs the NEK day, so: alternative, not addition.
https://jaypeakresort.com/summer/tram-rides · https://skivermont.com/scenic-lift-rides

### F. Mad River Glen single chair foliage rides
The world's last single chairlift, up to **Stark's Nest** on Stark Mountain at App Gap.
$20 adult / $15 junior+senior, **10:00 first chair, 14:30 last chair loaded**, foliage weekends
late Sept → mid Oct. Stark's Pub and the General Store open on those days.
**2026 dates not published in a form I could confirm — call 802-496-3551.** Only relevant if a
gaps day happens, and only on a weekend.
https://www.madriverglen.com/calendar/

### G. Laraway Lookout, Waterville
3.6 mi / 1,390 ft, moderate. Rock ledges with Mansfield and the Sterling Range. ~25 min from base.
A local substitute if the NEK day gets scrapped for weather and we don't want to drive far.

### H. Smugglers' Notch Boardwalk / Barnes Camp Loop
0.66 mi boardwalk (1.4 mi full loop, 350 ft). Wetlands, beaver dam, Notch walls overhead.
A 20-minute add to the existing Friday Notch drive — no schedule cost.

### Checked and deliberately rejected
- **Great Cliff of Mount Horrid, Brandon Gap** (1.4 mi / 650 ft, big S/E view, peregrine closure
  lifts Aug 1) — genuinely good, but 2 h+ each way from Hyde Park. Out of range.
- **Middlebury Gap (VT-125), Roxbury Gap** — Roxbury Gap's roadside overlook over the Mad River
  Valley is well regarded, but it is a gravel town road and only makes sense inside a big central-VT
  loop we are not doing from this base.
- **Mount Hor** (5.3 mi / 1,325 ft) — duplicates Pisgah's Willoughby view from the opposite wall.
- **Belvidere Mountain, Lowell** (7.9 mi / 1,980 ft) — 360° with Jay Peak, but too long for any slot.
- **Owl's Head / Groton, Spruce Mountain** — south-east, wrong side of the state for this base.

### Barr Hill Natural Area — 1521 Barr Hill Rd, Greensboro
256 acres, Nature Conservancy. **0.3 mi or 0.8 mi self-guided loop**; the near-panorama is from the
open field at the trailhead. Directions: north out of Greensboro on East Craftsbury Rd, bear right
onto Lauredon Ave at the town hall, 0.6 mi to the first fork, left onto Barr Hill Rd, past a dairy
farm and a red barn.

### Mount Elmore fire tower — Elmore State Park (15 min from base)

past Balancing Rock; the two link into a ~3.7–4.2 mi loop. Tower built 1939 by the CCC, still
climbable, on the National Historic Lookout Registry.
