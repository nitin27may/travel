Four decisions to make

Everything else is settled. These four change the shape of the trip, and each one is a genuine trade rather than an obvious call.

1. Friday is overloaded

12 h 30 door to door, ~9 h on site, 9.7 km on foot. It carries the 3-hour hike and the gondola and the Notch.

  • Cut the gondola recommended — score 7, 90 min, $40–43, foliage queues. Frees the day without touching a 9 or a 10.
  • Keep it, cut Emily's Bridge and Barnes Camp — saves only 35 min. Doesn't really solve it.
  • Keep everything — accept a 13-hour day on the back of Thursday's 8.75-hour drive.

2. Mount Pisgah — how far up

Currently trimmed to Pulpit Rock, 3.5 km / 274 m / 2 h. The full hike is 7.1 km / 503 m / 3 h 45.

  • Pulpit Rock only current — the ledge 650 ft above the lake is the shot. The summit is treed and has no view.
  • Full hike — adds the North and West overlooks and the full-length lake panorama. Costs Barr Hill, which scores 9 for 1.1 km.

3. Saturday — Willoughby or the gaps

The Two Gaps day is currently the weather backup, triggered by the Sept 29 check.

  • Willoughby stays primary current — it holds the trip's only other 10, and the gaps day peaks at 9.
  • Swap them — a real choice, but you would be trading a 10 for a 9.
  • Note: the NEK Fall Foliage Festival runs Sept 27 – Oct 4 — Peacham Fri, Barnet Sat, Groton Sun. Wrong corner of the Kingdom, 1 h 15+ away, and it conflicts with Willoughby.

4. Sunday — bridges or the islands

Cold Hollow is dropped, so the Waterbury detour is gone either way.

  • Covered-bridge route current — five bridges plus Montgomery, on the road you were driving anyway, and it reaches the border ~30 min earlier.
  • Lake Champlain Islands — the islands peak exactly Oct 4, but +1 h 30–2 h puts you in the Sunday afternoon border queue. Home ~20:30.
Two things to verify by phone before any of this is fixed

Warren highway dept 802-496-2709 — a full Lincoln Gap culvert replacement was scheduled for 2026 and neither town site posted a resolution. The road otherwise closes ~Oct 15, so the dates are fine.
Mad River Glen 802-496-3551 — 2026 single chair foliage dates, only if the gaps day happens. Last chair loads 14:30.
