## 4b. The quick read — boots or no boots

**No walking at all (13 stops).** Smugglers' Notch · **Appalachian Gap** · Willoughby South Beach ·
VT-5A shore · Fisher Bridge · all five Montgomery bridges · Gates Farm · Emily's Bridge ·
Great Eddy Bridge · Cold Hollow · the gondola and the Mad River Glen chair.
**Two of these score 9 or 10 — the best view-per-effort on the whole trip is a car door.**

**Under 1.5 km — a stroll, any footwear.** Barr Hill (1.1) · Barnes Camp (1.1) · Moss Glen Falls
(0.8) · Bingham Falls (0.8, but **wet stone stairs — grip matters more than distance here**) ·
Sunset Rock (0.8) · Warren Falls (0.3).

**3–4 km, needs proper shoes and a real slot.** Pulpit Rock (3.5 / 274 m) · Sunset Ledge
(3.5 / 119 m) · Prospect Rock (4.2 / 140 m).

**5 km+ — one per trip, and it is Friday's.** Stowe Pinnacle (5.5 / 457 m).

**Walking totals: Fri 9.7 km · Sat 5.9 km · Two Gaps 4.9 km · Thu 0.8 km · Sun 0.5 km.**
Trip total **~17 km on foot over four days**, of which one day carries 57%. That is the shape you
want: one committed day, one moderate, two easy.

## 5. How the scores work

Two things only: **how good is it**, and **what does it cost you**. A 10 is something you would
regret missing and can have cheaply. A 5 is fine but forgettable, or good but expensive.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **10** | Unmissable and cheap. Smugglers' Notch, Willoughby South Beach |
| **9** | Unmissable, but costs real time or effort. Pinnacle, Pulpit Rock, App Gap, Barr Hill |
| **8** | Strong. Would not cut it, would not restructure a day for it |
| **7** | Good, worth the stop, first to go if the day slips |
| **6** | Pleasant. Only because it is already on the road |
| **5** | Filler. Cut without a second thought |

### What the scores say about the plan
- **Every 9 and 10 is now scheduled.** Nothing top-tier is sitting in the reserve list except
  Mount Abraham and Mount Elmore, and both are excluded for stated reasons (effort, and a 10:00 gate).
- **Friday is the heaviest day by far** — 12 h 30 door to door, 9 h on site. It carries the 3-hour
  hike AND the gondola AND the Notch. If any day needs trimming it is this one, and **the gondola
  (score 7, 90 min, $40–43) is the item to cut**, not the Notch or the Pinnacle.
- **Saturday is now the best-balanced day**: one moderate hike, a 10, a 9, and home at 15:45.
- **Sunday scores modestly (5–7 across the board) and that is correct** — it is a driving day, and
  the bridges are worth stopping for without being worth a detour. The route was chosen because it
  is *on the way*, not in spite of the distance.
- **The Two Gaps day peaks at 9 and averages ~7.** It is a good day. It is not better than the
  Willoughby day, which is why it stays as the backup and not a swap.
