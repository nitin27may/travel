# Research: Driving route, border, logistics (agent output, Aug 23 2026)

## Route A (RECOMMENDED): via Montreal / A-30 / A-35 / Highgate Springs
- Whitby -> 401 E -> A-20 -> A-30 E bypass (toll bridge $4.40 CAD, card/transponder in lane — avoid plate-invoice fee) -> A-35 S (freeway to Saint-Armand since Sept 2025; last 6.5 km is 4-lane Rte 133) -> Highgate Springs VT (I-89 S) -> exit 19 St. Albans -> VT-104 -> VT-15 E -> Hyde Park.
- ~650-665 km, ~6h30-6h50 driving. 8 AM Whitby departure hits A-30 bridge ~12:30 (between Montreal peaks). ON-407 not needed.
- Border: Highgate Springs is the quiet crossing (0-15 min typical Thu midday). Live: https://bwt.cbp.gov/details/05021201/POV ; return (CBSA): https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/bwt-taf/menu-eng.html
- Alternatives: Thousand Islands/I-81/US-11 (~60-90 min slower), Cornwall-Massena/NY-37/US-11 (~45-75 min slower). Use only if QC disrupted.

## Documents / customs
- Canadian citizens 16+: passport / NEXUS / EDL. PRs: passport + any required US visa/ESTA + PR card for re-entry.
- 30+ day US registration rule (Apr 2025) NOT applicable to 3-night trip.
- Return exemption (48h+): CAD $800/person + 1.5 L wine OR 1.14 L spirits OR 8.5 L beer.

## Fuel / stops / cell
- ONroute EB: Port Hope, Napanee (~10:15), Mallorytown North (~11:15, last big one). St. Albans VT fuel ~20 min past border.
- Gas (Aug 2026): ON ~159-166 c/L; QC ~180 c/L; VT ~US$4.13/gal ~ 1.50 CAD/L. Fill in Ontario, skip Quebec, fill in Vermont.
- Roaming: Rogers ~$15-18/day, Bell/Telus $14/day; US eSIM cheaper for 4 days. NEK/Notch/VT-15 have real dead zones — download offline Google Maps for northern VT.

## Sun (Stowe)
- Oct 1: rise 6:47, set 6:33 (civil twilight 7:01). Oct 2: 6:49/6:31. Oct 3: 6:50/6:30.
- 4:30-5:00 PM arrival = ~1.5-2 h usable light — Moss Glen Falls Stowe is feasible on arrival day.

## Local drive times (from hotel, 1592 Center Rd Hyde Park)
- Stowe village: 11 mi / ~18 min. Stowe Pinnacle TH: ~13-14 mi / ~25 min.
- Stowe -> Notch top (VT-108): ~9-10 mi / 20-25 min (hairpins).
- Mount Pisgah TH (VT-5A): ~42-45 mi / ~1h00-1h10 via VT-15 E -> VT-16 N.
- Pisgah -> Craftsbury Common: ~50 min (VT-5A N -> VT-58 W -> VT-14 S). Craftsbury -> Hyde Park: ~35 min (VT-14 -> VT-15).
- Cold Hollow (VT-100 Waterbury Center): ~18 mi / ~30 min.
- Route 100 loop Hyde Park->Granville Gulf: ~52 mi one-way, ~1h25; RT ~3h driving before stops (+20-30% foliage traffic).

## Gotchas
- A-35 now freeway (old trip reports about slow Rte 133 villages are outdated). Pay A-30 toll by card in lane. Check VT-108 status day-of (vtrans.vermont.gov/notch). Sunday leaf-season afternoons = northbound border queue risk — check CBSA before leaving. Offline maps essential.
