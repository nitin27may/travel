# Research: Attractions, hours, food, crowds (agent output, Aug 23 2026)

## Gondola SkyRide (Stowe) — OPERATING fall 2026
- 1991 Mansfield gondola NOT replaced/demolished; cabins upgraded + heated barn for 2025-26; connector-to-Smuggs gondola is proposal only.
- Fall ops: daily ~mid-June to mid-Oct, 10:00-4:30 (last ticket 4:15), weather permitting. ~$40-43 adult RT, dynamic pricing; buy advance on stowe.com in foliage season. Status: stowe.com/alerts, ph 1-800-842-8062. Top = Cliff House (~3,625 ft).
- https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/gondola-skyride

## Mansfield Auto Toll Road (alternative)
- Late May - mid-Oct, ~9:30-4:30 daily. $37/vehicle + $14/extra passenger (verify; payment reports conflict — carry cash AND card). 4.5 mi gravel, ~20 min up, uphill has right of way; no motorcycles/campervans. Top = the Nose, 3,850 ft; LT ridge walk 1.4 mi to the Chin (4,395 ft, VT's highest). No food at top. ~90 min total.
- https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/auto-toll-road | https://plannedwanderings.com/stowe-auto-toll-road/

## Cold Hollow Cider Mill
- 3600 Waterbury-Stowe Rd (VT-100), Waterbury Center. Daily 8-6, free. Cider donuts, press demos, cafe (breakfast 8-10:30, lunch 11-4). 30-45 min. https://coldhollow.com/

## Stowe village
- Community Church photo: "Stowe Community Church View Point" (Google-mappable, next to Salon Salon off Main St), golden hour best.
- Stowe Foliage Arts Festival = Oct 9-11 (AFTER trip). Farmers Market Sundays 10-2 thru Oct 18 — runs Sun Oct 4 at Topnotch Field.
- Trapp Family Lodge: grounds free; von Trapp Brewing Bierhall daily 11:30-9 (lagers, brats, mountain views). https://www.vontrappbrewing.com/bierhall.htm

## Lake Willoughby
- South Beach (5646 VT-5A): iconic fjord view; free but limited parking, arrive early. North Beach: bigger lot, porta-lets. Parking fee was only "in talks" Apr 2026 — assume free, check signage. VT-5A: ~5 shoulder-less miles on east shore, limited pull-offs.

## Craftsbury Common + route
- From Willoughby: VT-5A N -> VT-58 W (Orleans/Irasburg, paved) -> VT-14 S -> Common (~45-60 min). Home: Craftsbury -> Hardwick (VT-14) -> VT-15 W to Morrisville/Hyde Park (~35-40 min). 20-30 min stop.

## Crowds
- Stowe peak typically ~Oct 6-12; Oct 1-4 = just before peak crush, before Arts Festival (Oct 9-11) and Indigenous Peoples Day (Oct 12). Sat Oct 3 still heavy.
- Mountain Rd crawls 10-4 on peak weekends. Do Notch/gondola/toll road before 9-10 AM; village late afternoon; NEK day is the low-crowd day — good for Saturday.
