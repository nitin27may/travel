Moss Glen Falls, Stowe golden hour

7:15 – 10:15 AM

Stowe Pinnacle main hike

The panoramic payoff of the Stowe side: an open rock knob facing Mansfield, Camel's Hump, and the Worcester Range, with morning sun front-lighting the ridgeline and valley color. Steep, stair-like, rocky in the upper third. The Upper Hollow Rd lot holds ~9 cars and fills before 9 even on weekdays — be there by 7:10.

~3.4 mi RT~1,500 ft gain2.5–3 hOverflow: Pinnacle Meadows lot
Map AllTrails GoStowe Trail Finder
Trail notes
  • Blue-blazed; half a mile of gentle meadow, then the staircase begins.
  • Note the fork about a mile up so you take the same branch down.
  • Light scrambling near the top; grippy shoes for dew-wet rock.
10:30 AM

Emily's Bridge added

Gold Brook Covered Bridge, 1844 — five minutes from the trailhead in Stowe Hollow, spanning rocky ledges. Vermont's most storied "haunted" bridge and a fast, worthwhile photo stop on the way into town.

Gondola SkyRide, Mt. Mansfield

Confirmed operating fall 2026 (the gondola was not replaced — cabins upgraded for 2025–26). Daily 10:00–4:30, last ticket 4:15, ~US$40–43 adult. Buy online in advance — foliage-season queues are real. Cliff House at the top, ~3,625 ft, foliage from above.

Alternative: Auto Toll Road

Rather drive the mountain? Mansfield Auto Toll Road, 9:30–4:30, $37/vehicle + $14/passenger (payment reports conflict — carry cash AND card). 4.5 mi gravel to the Nose at 3,850 ft; walk the Long Trail ridge toward the Chin (4,395 ft, Vermont's highest). ~90 min. No food at the top.

3:00 PM

Bingham Falls

Half-mile round trip off VT-108 to a 25-ft plunge in a carved gorge with emerald pools. The steep rock staircases down are notoriously slick — doubly so under fallen leaves. Dirt pull-offs on both sides of the road, ~15 cars.

0.5 mi RT~45 minWet rock warning

4:00 PM

Smugglers' Notch — VT-108

The signature drive: hairpins threading giant fallen boulders under 1,000-ft cliffs, at peak color up high. Late afternoon is the quiet window (midday is gridlock), and the shaded slot actually photographs better in even light. Pull-offs along the climb; park at the top and walk among the boulders. Optionally roll down to Jeffersonville and loop home via VT-15 — no backtracking.

Open thru ~mid-NovNo vehicles >40 ftBest light even/shaded

Sterling Pond — optional, read first

From the top-of-Notch lot: 2.2 mi RT but a hard ~1,000 ft on eroded, slippery rock staircase, with an active bear situation at the pond shelter. Worth it only as a substitute for the gondola, from the Notch top at ~7 AM on a clear morning — not appended to a full day. AllTrails

7:35 – 11:00 AM

Mount Pisgah, South Trail main hike

The marquee hike. Pulpit Rock at ~1 mile hangs 650 ft directly over Lake Willoughby — the classic vertigo shot. Then the key move: the summit itself is treed, so continue 0.3–0.4 mi past it to the North and West overlooks, and past the FIRST North Overlook to the second, where the full fjord panorama opens. Depart hotel 6:15 with the picnic; trailhead lots sit on both sides of VT-5A.

4.4 mi RT~1,650 ft gain3–3.5 hDrive ~1 h 05

Conditions

  • No closures — peregrine restrictions end Aug 1 and Pisgah isn't on the 2026 list anyway.

11:15 AM

South Beach + picnic

The classic shot: looking north up the glacial trench between the cliffs of Pisgah and Hor — Vermont's fjord. Picnic on the beach. Parking free (a fee was only "in talks" spring 2026 — check signage).

Early afternoon

Granville Gulf — the other Moss Glen Falls

The steep-walled Gulf "glows from within" at midday. Moss Glen Falls (Granville) is a 35-ft roadside cascade with a paved pull-off and a 0.1-mi boardwalk — do not let the GPS confuse it with Stowe's. Turn here and run the color back north. ~104 mi round trip, ~3 h driving before stops.
