# Research: Foliage timing, photo spots, light, webcams (agent output, Aug 23 2026)

## Timing by region (historical + 2026 forecast)
- NEK (Willoughby/Craftsbury): turns FIRST, peak often last week of Sept; Oct 1-4 = at/just past peak. Safest peak bet.
- Stowe/Lamoille (Hyde Park, Morrisville): high elevations peak late Sept-early Oct; valley floor peaks 1st-2nd week Oct. Oct 1-4 = peak at elevation (Notch, upper Mansfield), near-peak valley (~60-90%).
- Mad River Valley / Route 100 (Waterbury-Waitsfield-Warren-Granville): peaks early-mid Oct; Oct 1-4 = pre/near-peak valley, Granville Gulf further along. Hold for last day / backup.
- Elevation effect: color starts high and moves down; north->south. Peak lasts ~7-10 days per spot.
- 2026 forecast (Jeff Foliage, only detailed one published): slightly LATER season, potentially vibrant; northern peak breaking out ~Oct 1. Drought-free north = good color.

## Light plan
- Sunrise: Lake Willoughby (fog/reflections; Hor cliffs catch first light), Stowe Pinnacle (front-lit Mansfield), LVRT, Craftsbury (morning)
- Sunset: Sunset Rock over village, Trapp meadows, Community Church warm late light
- Midday/overcast: Smugglers' Notch slot (shaded, even light + early = parking), Granville Gulf, waterfalls, covered bridges
- Pulpit Rock: mid-morning for fog burn-off
