# Norway — what it actually costs

Researched 2026-08-31. NOK figures as published; conversions approximate.

---

## Per-person daily spend, excluding flights

| Tier | NOK/day | USD/day |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | 970–1,250 | 100–130 |
| **Mid-range** | **1,850–2,460** | **190–255** |
| Luxury | 3,000+ | 310+ |

**A 14-night mid-range trip for two, on the ground, lands around NOK 52,000–69,000
(~CAD 6,900–9,100)** before flights and before the car. That is the number to plan against.

## The costs people forget

- **Ferries are not bus fares.** Hellesylt–Geiranger is **~NOK 1,530–1,650** for a car and
  passengers, one way. Andenes–Gryllefjord ~925. Flåm–Gudvangen NOK 550 pp one way.
  A fjord-heavy fortnight can easily run **NOK 4,000–6,000 in ferries alone**.
- **Attraction pricing is per-head and steep.** Loen Skylift return is NOK 650 adult;
  Via Ferrata Loen with a guide is NOK 2,380 per person. Two people doing three signature
  activities is a NOK 8,000–12,000 line.
- **Parking is monetised everywhere now.** Preikestolen NOK 250–275/day. Lofoten hotspots
  NOK 100–200/day. Reine harbour NOK 25–40/hour.
- **The 3% visitor levy** applies to overnight stays in adopting municipalities from
  summer 2026 — Lofoten and Tromsø included.
- **One-way car drop fees** can add 20%+, and a Bergen→Ålesund one-way has been quoted at
  over USD 1,000. Two shorter rentals usually beat one long one-way.

## The three levers that actually move the number

1. **Self-cater breakfast and lunch.** Groceries at NOK 112–195/day against restaurant
   mains at NOK 190–350 each. Book accommodation with a kitchen; this is the single
   biggest saving in Norway and it is worth more than any pass or card.
2. **Buy alcohol at Vinmonopolet, not in bars.** NOK 35–60 versus 90–130 is a 2–3×
   multiple. Anything **above 4.7% ABV is Vinmonopolet-only**; supermarkets sell beer up
   to 4.7% with time-of-day restrictions.
   **Hours: roughly Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri 08:00–18:00, Wed 09:00–18:00, Sat 10:00–16:00,
   closed Sunday and all public holidays.** Stores in small fjord villages keep shorter
   hours than this. **Plan Saturday afternoon or go without on Sunday** — this catches
   people out on exactly the night they have a cabin and a view.
   `GAP:` per-branch hours in the specific towns on the route; they vary a lot.
3. **Pick the free set pieces deliberately.** Ørnesvingen, Flydalsjuvet, Aksla's 418
   steps, Stegastein on foot from the shuttle, the entire National Tourist Route
   viewpoint network, and every hike above — all free. Norway's best hours cost nothing;
   it is the transport and the food that bleed.
