# 💧 Spencer Gorge — Tew's Falls, Dundas Peak & Webster's Falls

> The top pick for this crew. Two of Hamilton's most dramatic waterfalls plus the Dundas Peak clifftop lookout, on one moderate 5–6 km loop with stairs and gorge views. As of 2026 there's **no summer reservation** (only Sept 27–Nov 9), so it's an easy yes. ~1h40 each way. Pair with a splash pad and ice cream in Dundas.

## Trip profile

- **Outing type:** Sightseeing & scenic + active hike
- **Drive radius:** ~145 km / ~1h40 each way
- **Time:** Full day (or relaxed half-day-plus)
- **Group:** Family, kids 9–12
- **Transport:** Car

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | Whitby, ON |
| **Drive time** | ~1h40 each way (~145 km) |
| **Best for** | Two big waterfalls, a marquee lookout, moderate hiking |
| **Best season** | Spring–early summer for strong falls; great year-round |
| **Rough cost** | ~$90–130 for a family of 4 |

## The plan

> [!map] **Route:** [Open the day in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Whitby%2C%20ON%2C%20Canada&destination=Dundas%20Driving%20Park%2C%2071%20Cross%20St%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON&travelmode=driving&waypoints=Tew%27s%20Falls%2C%20581%20Harvest%20Rd%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON%7CWebster%27s%20Falls%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON)

**Leave Whitby by 7:30 am.** Spencer Gorge is popular; arriving by mid-morning beats the crowds and the parking crunch.

**~9:15 am — Park at Tew's Falls lot** ($15/vehicle + driver, $5 per passenger, under 5 free)
- [Tew's Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tew%27s%20Falls%2C%20581%20Harvest%20Rd%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON) — a 41-metre ribbon waterfall, the tallest in the Hamilton area and a mini-Niagara in spring. Viewing platforms right by the lot.

**~9:45 am — Hike to Dundas Peak** ⭐ (the big lookout)
- [Dundas Peak](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dundas%20Peak%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON) — ~15-min walk from Tew's Falls to a rock outcrop with a sweeping view over the Dundas valley and Webster's gorge. The marquee photo of the trip.

**~10:30 am — Continue the loop to Webster's Falls**
- Take the **Spencer Adventure Trail** connecting Tew's/Dundas Peak to Webster's (or drive the short hop). Full loop comes to ~5–6 km, moderate, with stairs.
- [Webster's Falls](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Webster%27s%20Falls%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON) — a 22-metre classical curtain waterfall with a stone staircase down to the base, an old stone bridge, and a shaded picnic area. The prettiest spot to stop.

**~12:00 pm — Picnic lunch** at the Webster's Falls picnic grounds.

**~1:30 pm — Kids cool off (pick one):**
- [Dundas Driving Park](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dundas%20Driving%20Park%2C%2071%20Cross%20St%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON) — free splash pad and playground right in town. Easiest option.
- [Christie Lake Conservation Area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Christie%20Lake%20Conservation%20Area%2C%201000%20Hwy%205%20W%2C%20Dundas%2C%20ON) — beach swimming and picnic grounds ($16.50/vehicle). Better for a longer water stop.

**~3:00 pm — Wind-down in downtown Dundas** 🍦
- King and Cootes streets have cafes and ice cream. Browse the shops, grab a cold treat before the drive.

**~4:00 pm — Drive home** (~1h40).

> [!tip] **Interactive add-on:** [Royal Botanical Gardens](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Royal%20Botanical%20Gardens%2C%20680%20Plains%20Rd%20W%2C%20Burlington%2C%20ON) is ~15 min away — gardens, nature trails, and exhibits if you want a relaxed, interactive afternoon instead of a second water stop.

> [!warning] **Backup (rain):** The falls are stunning in light rain (bring grippy shoes — stairs get slick), but if it's a washout, swap the afternoon for Royal Botanical Gardens or downtown Dundas cafes and shops.

## Booking & gotchas

- **No reservation needed in summer 2026.** Timed reservations only apply Sept 27–Nov 9 for fall colour. Just pay parking at the gate.
- **Fees:** $15/vehicle + driver, $5 per passenger, kids 5 and under free.
- **Footwear:** real shoes — there are stairs and clifftop edges. Keep kids back from unfenced lookout edges at Dundas Peak.
- **Falls flow:** Twenty/Spencer Creek is rain-fed; falls are fullest in spring and after rain, thinner in late summer.
