# 🌊 Elora Gorge — Day Trip from Whitby

> The adventure-water pick. Hike the limestone gorge rim above the Grand River, then tube the rapids through the canyon, cool off at the Elora splash pad, and wind down with ice cream in Ontario's prettiest village. Big-fun day for tweens. ~2 hrs each way, so an early/full-day start.

## Trip profile

- **Outing type:** Active & outdoorsy + a bit of town charm
- **Drive radius:** ~180 km / ~2 hrs each way (edge of the 200 km range — worth it)
- **Time:** Full day / sunrise-to-sunset
- **Group:** Family with school-age kids
- **Transport:** Car

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | Whitby, ON |
| **Drive time** | ~2 hrs each way (~180 km) |
| **Best for** | River tubing, gorge hiking, splash pad, village stroll |
| **Best season** | Mid-June to end of September (tubing season) |
| **Rough cost** | ~$200–330 for an example family of four — tubing rental is the big driver (see Costs) |
| **Heads-up** | Tubing requires everyone ≥48″ (122 cm) and a competent swimmer; book online ahead |

## Getting there

~185 km, about **2 hours** each way — the drive is the long part of this day, so commit to an early start.

- **Fastest:** Hwy 401 W → **407 ETR** (toll) westbound → **Hwy 6 N** through Guelph → Wellington Rd into Elora.
- **Toll-free:** stay on the 401 to Guelph Line / Hwy 6 and head north — adds ~15–20 min.
- Leave Whitby by 7:00–7:30 am: the day-use lot fills on hot weekends, and you want a late-morning tubing slot.
- Fuel runs ~$55 round trip; budget for the 407 toll if you take it. Cell service thins out near Elora — download maps offline.

## The plan

> [!map] **Route:** [Open the day in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Whitby%2C%20ON%2C%20Canada&destination=Scoop%20Ice%20Cream%2C%20Mill%20Street%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON&travelmode=driving&waypoints=Elora%20Gorge%20Conservation%20Area%2C%207400%20Wellington%20Rd%2021%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON%7CBissell%20Park%2C%20Mill%20St%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON)

**Leave Whitby by 7:00–7:30 am.** The gorge day-use lot fills on hot summer weekends and tubing slots go fast — early arrival is the whole game.

**~9:15 am — Gorge rim hike (moderate, ~5–6 km with riverside detours)**
- [Elora Gorge Conservation Area](https://maps.app.goo.gl/TVjqFTWf6kVW61wL6) — day-use opens 8 am. Do the gorge loop (~5–6 km, moderate, ~1.5 hrs): clifftop paths plus riverside trails with safety barriers and overlooks into the 22-metre gorge. Hit the three highlights — the **Hole in the Wall**, a staircase that drops halfway down through the rock to a viewing platform; the **Lover's Leap** lookout; and the **Tooth of Time**, a rock pillar standing in the river. Some stairs and uneven footing, but easy for 9–12s.

**~11:00 am — Tube the rapids** ⭐
- Float ~2 km of the Grand River through the gorge. The catch: **there's no shuttle — you walk ~1.3 km each way** along the road to the entry/exit, so block out ~2.5–3 hrs all in.
- **Book online ahead at tickets.grandriver.ca** (up to 3 days out) and complete the **waiver** before you arrive — no walk-up sales. **Last registration is 4 pm; gear back by 7 pm.**
- Requirements: **everyone ≥48″ (122 cm) and a competent swimmer**; kids must be with an adult. There are **no lifeguards and no exit points** once you're on the river — fun, but a real commitment.
- The rental package includes a helmet (chin strap required) and a PFD (worn at all times). One person per tube; dogs not allowed in the tubing area.

**~2:00 pm — Late picnic lunch** at the gorge picnic area (shaded tables by the river) — you'll be hungry after tubing. Or carry it into town.

**~3:00 pm — Drive into Elora village (5 min) + splash pad**
- [Bissell Park](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bissell%20Park%2C%20Mill%20St%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON) (Mill & Princess) — splash pad to rinse off and cool down, boardwalk along the Grand River, room to run. Splash pad runs 9 am–8 pm in peak summer.
- Stroll **Mill Street** — independent shops, the historic Elora Mill, and gorge views from town.

> [!tip] **Two Elora bonuses:** Go on a **Saturday** and the **Elora Farmers' Market** runs at Bissell Park (mornings, ~May–Oct) with live music by the river and famous cinnamon buns. You can also book a **punt/raft ride on the Grand River** — a low-key way to see the gorge from the water.

**Alternative to tubing — Elora Quarry swim:** [Elora Quarry](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Elora%20Quarry%20Conservation%20Area%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON) is a spring-fed former quarry with a sandy beach and cliffs, 5 min away. Calmer and cheaper than tubing — a good swap for a hot lazy day or weaker swimmers. **Separate property and ticket — per-person admission + timed reservation, and it sells out.** Slot it in place of the tubing block; you won't have energy or time for both.

**~4:30 pm — Wind-down: ice cream & a bite** 🍦
- Ice cream: [Scoop Ice Cream](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Scoop%20Ice%20Cream%2C%20Mill%20Street%2C%20Elora%2C%20ON) (made on site) or **Sugar Skulls** (colourful Mexican-themed) on Mill Street.
- Family-friendly food: **The Friendly Society** (comfort plates), **Lost and Found Café**, or **Wild and Free Café** (coffee and treats) — all easygoing with kids.
- Special-occasion dinner: **Elora Mill Restaurant** (upscale, riverside) if you want to make an evening of it.

**~5:30 pm — Drive home** (~2 hrs).

> [!warning] **Backup (rain / high water):** Tubing closes when the river runs high, cold, or fast — check the GRCA tubing status the morning of. Rain plan: skip tubing, do the rim hike in rain gear, then spend the afternoon in Elora's shops and cafes, or detour to the indoor **Elora Mill** for a treat.

## Booking & gotchas

- **Tubing season:** mid-June to end of September, **10 am–6 pm** (weather permitting), last registration 4 pm. Book at **tickets.grandriver.ca** up to 3 days ahead and do the **online waiver** first.
- **Check the river before you commit:** tubing closes when flow tops ~8 m³/s or after heavy rain. Check GRCA's **Conservation Area Activity Status** and **River Flows** pages the morning of.
- **Heights/swimming:** the 48″ (122 cm) minimum and competent-swimmer rule are firm — staff can turn you away. Confirm each kid clears it before booking.
- **Deposit is credit card or cash only** ($75, refundable) — **debit is not accepted**.
- **Footwear:** secured sandals or running shoes — **no flip-flops** (you also walk 1.3 km each way to the river).
- **Elora Quarry ≠ Elora Gorge:** different properties, tickets not interchangeable; the Quarry has its own per-person admission and **timed reservation**, and sells out on hot days.
- **Capacity & connectivity:** the gorge can hit its day-use cap on hot weekends — arrive by opening — and cell service is patchy, so download maps offline.
- **On the river:** no lifeguards, no exit points, submerged rocks; one rider per tube; dogs not permitted in the tubing area.

## Costs (example family of four: 2 adults + 2 kids, approx.)

Day-use admission is **per person**, not per vehicle: adult (13–64) $9.50, child (4–12) $4.00, senior $7.50, 3 and under free.

| Item | Estimate |
|------|----------|
| Day-use admission | 2 × $9.50 + 2 × $4.00 = **~$27** |
| Tubing — full rental (helmet + PFD) | $55.50 × 4 = **~$222** (+ $75 refundable deposit) |
| — or registration only (bring tubes/PFD/helmet) | $21.50 × 4 = ~$86 |
| Splash pad / farmers' market | Free |
| Ice cream / snacks / lunch | ~$25–40 |
| Fuel (~370 km round trip) + 407 toll if used | ~$55 + toll |
| **Total with tubing rental** | **~$330** |
| **Total if you skip tubing** (gorge hike + splash + Quarry swim) | **~$120–150** |

> [!tip] **Budget lever:** tubing rental for four is the single biggest cost. Swapping it for the **Elora Quarry swim** (or just the splash pad) drops the day to ~$120–150. Confirm current gate and tubing prices at 519-846-9742.

## Packing

- Swimsuits under clothes; **secure footwear for tubing** (running shoes or strapped sandals — no flip-flops); towels and a full change of clothes each
- Sunscreen, hats, sunglasses **with a strap/tether** (loose ones are lost on the river)
- Picnic lunch + lots of water (refill before the rim hike)
- Dry bag for phones; **leave valuables in the car — GRCA doesn't store them**
- **Credit card or cash for the $75 tubing deposit (no debit)** plus gate admission
- Offline map downloaded (patchy cell service near Elora)

## When to go & making a weekend of it

- **Weekday** beats weekend for crowds and parking. If you want the **Farmers' Market**, go Saturday morning and accept it'll be busier.
- If ~2 hrs each way feels long for a day, the gorge has a **campground** — book a night (camping reservations via GRCA) and pair Elora Gorge + Quarry with nearby **Fergus** (Templin Gardens, the Grand River, and the Highland Games in August). That turns this into a proper weekend getaway.
