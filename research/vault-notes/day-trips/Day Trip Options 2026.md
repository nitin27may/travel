# 🗺️ Day Trip Options 2026 — Trails, Water & Lookouts from Whitby

> Five researched day trips from Whitby, all under ~200 km, built for the same brief: a **moderate hike near water**, a **waterfall or big lookout**, and a **kids' splash/play** option, with the most **interactive** stops flagged for the 9–12 crew. Each has its own detailed note (hour-by-hour plan, costs, packing, booking gotchas) and a sharable Google Maps file.

## The five options

| Option | Drive | The hike (near water) | Waterfall / lookout | Kid play / swim | Interactive | Note |
|--------|-------|----------------------|---------------------|-----------------|-------------|------|
| **Spencer Gorge** | ~1h40 | 5–6 km loop, stairs, gorge | ⭐ Two falls + Dundas Peak lookout | Dundas splash pad / Christie Lake | ★ | [[life/travel/Day Trips/Spencer Gorge Day Trip\|Spencer Gorge]] |
| **Rattlesnake Point + Crawford Lake** | ~1h30 | 5–8 km escarpment | Cliff lookouts | Kelso beach + splash | ★★ Longhouse village | [[life/travel/Day Trips/Rattlesnake Point and Crawford Lake Day Trip\|Rattlesnake + Crawford]] |
| **Warsaw Caves** | ~1h40 NE | 4 km loop + 800 m caves | Scenic lookout | Indian River shallow swim | ★★★ Cave exploring | [[life/travel/Day Trips/Warsaw Caves Day Trip\|Warsaw Caves]] |
| **Elora Gorge** | ~2h | Gorge rim + river tubing | Gorge cliffs | Bissell Park splash pad + Quarry | ★★ River tubing | [[life/travel/Day Trips/Elora Gorge Day Trip\|Elora Gorge]] |
| **Rockwood** | ~1h45 | Cliff trails (~3–5 km) | Cliffs over reservoir | On-site sandy beach | ★★ Caves + potholes + mill ruins | [[life/travel/Day Trips/Rockwood Day Trip\|Rockwood]] |

## How to choose

- **Most directly on-brief (waterfalls + lookout + moderate hike):** Spencer Gorge. No summer reservation in 2026 — easiest logistics.
- **Best pure hike + most educational:** Rattlesnake Point + Crawford Lake (the longhouse village).
- **Most adventurous and traffic-free:** Warsaw Caves — cave exploring, and it's northeast so no GTA gridlock.
- **Most fun-in-water:** Elora Gorge — tubing the rapids (needs 48″ height + advance booking). Farthest.
- **Most variety in one gated park:** Rockwood — caves, potholes, mill ruins, beach, and canoeing.

## Shared notes

- **Season:** all five are summer-ready (June). Tubing and beach swimming are open; waterfalls are fuller earlier in the season.
- **Crowds:** leave Whitby by 7:30–8:00 am for any of them — popular spots fill their lots by late morning on hot weekends.
- **Passes:** these are Conservation Authority parks (not Ontario Parks), so an Ontario Parks pass doesn't apply. Forks of the Credit — the runner-up not built out here — is the one Ontario Parks option if you want to use that pass.

## Runners-up (not built out — say the word)

- **Ball's Falls (Lincoln, ~1h45):** two waterfalls + a 19th-century historic village (interactive), but short/easy trails — more stroll than hike.
- **Forks of the Credit (Caledon, ~1h30):** Cataract Falls and moderate 5–10 km loops, but no splash/interactive element; needs an advance Ontario Parks vehicle permit and fills fast.
