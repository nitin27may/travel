# 🧗 Rattlesnake Point + Crawford Lake — Day Trip

> The best pure hike of the five, with the most genuinely interactive stop. Clifftop lookouts over Nassagaweya Canyon and a moderate 5–8 km escarpment trail, paired with a reconstructed 15th-century Iroquoian longhouse village at Crawford Lake. Both are Conservation Halton parks ~10 min apart, ~1h30 from Whitby. Closest of the five.

## Trip profile

- **Outing type:** Active hike + interactive history
- **Drive radius:** ~125 km / ~1h30 each way
- **Time:** Full day
- **Group:** Family, kids 9–12
- **Transport:** Car

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **From** | Whitby, ON |

## The plan

> [!map] **Route:** [Open the day in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Whitby%2C%20ON%2C%20Canada&destination=Kelso%20Conservation%20Area%2C%205234%20Kelso%20Rd%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON&travelmode=driving&waypoints=Rattlesnake%20Point%20Conservation%20Area%2C%207200%20Appleby%20Line%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON%7CCrawford%20Lake%20Conservation%20Area%2C%203115%20Conservation%20Rd%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON)

**Before you go:** reserve day passes for both parks at **parkvisit.ca** — Conservation Halton requires advance reservations.

**Leave Whitby by 7:30 am.**

**~9:15 am — Rattlesnake Point (lookout hike, moderate ~5 km)**
- [Rattlesnake Point Conservation Area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rattlesnake%20Point%20Conservation%20Area%2C%207200%20Appleby%20Line%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON) — loop the Nelson, Buffalo Crag, and Trafalgar lookouts along the Niagara Escarpment cliff edge, with views over Nassagaweya Canyon. You'll see rock climbers on the cliff faces. ⭐
- For more distance, the **Nassagaweya Canyon Trail** runs 7.2 km one-way and connects to Crawford Lake — do a there-and-back section, or just the lookout loops if energy is limited.

> [!warning] Cliff edges here are largely unfenced. Keep kids close at the lookouts.

**~11:30 am — Drive to Crawford Lake** (~10–15 min).

**~12:00 pm — Picnic lunch** at Crawford Lake.

**~12:45 pm — Longhouse village + trails** ⭐ (the interactive highlight)
- [Crawford Lake Conservation Area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Crawford%20Lake%20Conservation%20Area%2C%203115%20Conservation%20Rd%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON) — explore the reconstructed 15th-century Iroquoian village (longhouses open 10 am–4 pm, staffed in summer). Walk the Escarpment, Woodland, and Pine Ridge trails.
- **Crawford Lake boardwalk:** was under reconstruction into summer 2026 — confirm it's reopened before counting on the lakeside walk. The village and other trails don't need it.

**~2:45 pm — Swim & splash at Kelso** (~15 min away)
- [Kelso Conservation Area](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kelso%20Conservation%20Area%2C%205234%20Kelso%20Rd%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON) — sandy beach swimming with lifeguard, plus the **Kelso Cove Waterpark** splash features. Canoe/SUP rentals if there's time. (Also Conservation Halton — reserve.)

**~4:00 pm — Wind-down: ice cream** in Milton or the village of Campbellville on the way out. 🍦

**~4:45 pm — Drive home** (~1h30).

> [!tip] **Rainy-day swap:** The [Halton County Radial Railway](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Halton%20County%20Radial%20Railway%2C%2013629%20Guelph%20Line%2C%20Milton%2C%20ON) (vintage streetcar museum, ~10 min away) is a great hands-on indoor/outdoor backup that tweens enjoy.

## Booking & gotchas

- **Reserve both parks at parkvisit.ca.** Online booking is cheaper than the gate and guarantees entry on busy days.
- **Admission:** ~$10.50 adult, $8.25 senior, $7.25 ages 5–14, under 5 free (per park; Kelso is a separate reservation/fee).
- **Boardwalk status:** confirm the Crawford Lake boardwalk reconstruction is finished before the trip.
- **Longhouse hours:** 10 am–4 pm; may close for pre-booked school/groups — fine on a family day but worth a glance at the calendar.
