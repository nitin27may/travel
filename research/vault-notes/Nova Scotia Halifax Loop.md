# Nova Scotia — Halifax Loop (9 Days)

Fly in and out of Halifax. Self-drive loop covering Halifax, South Shore, Cape Breton, Moncton/Hopewell Rocks, and back.

**Travel dates:** 15 August – 23 August 2026 (8 nights)
**Total driving:** ~1,800 km

See [[Nova Scotia Road Trip]] for the extended 11-day version (home to home).

> [!important] Book Before You Leave
> - **Skyline Trail parking** — opens May 6, 2026 at reservation.pc.gc.ca ($13/slot). Books out fast for August weekends.
> - **Harbour Hopper Halifax** — book ahead at harbourhopper.com, summer slots sell out
> - **Auberge Gisele's Inn, Baddeck** — small inn, peak season. Book direct at giseles.com
> - **Parks Canada free admission** — June 19–Sept 7, 2026 (Canada Strong program). Your trip falls within this window. Verify at parks.canada.ca before departure.

---

## Route Overview

```
Halifax Airport → Halifax (3 nights)
   ↓ Day trips: Peggy's Cove, South Shore / Hall's Harbour
Halifax → Cape Breton / Baddeck (3 nights)
   ↓ Cabot Trail East + West, Skyline Trail
Baddeck → Moncton (1 night)
   ↓ Hopewell Rocks + Tidal Bore
Moncton → Halifax (1 night) → Airport
```

## Itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive Halifax → Peggy's Cove → Halifax (15 August)

**Stay:** Halifax downtown (3 nights, Aug 15–17)

**Airport:** Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ) — Enfield, ~35 min from downtown Halifax.

**En route from airport — Peggy's Cove** (~80 km from airport via Hwy 102 + Hwy 333)
- Iconic Nova Scotia fishing village built on glacier-carved granite outcrops overlooking the Atlantic
- Peggy's Point Lighthouse — one of the most photographed in Canada
- Walk the granite rocks down to the water. **Stay behind the black wave-wash line** — rogue waves are a real danger, especially with kids.
- Facilities: parking lot, public washrooms, a few gift shops and a restaurant (Sou'Wester)
- Budget 1.5 hours. Best light: late afternoon or early morning.
- ~45 min from downtown Halifax — easy to squeeze in before checking in.

**Check in to Halifax hotel.** Rest, dinner near the waterfront.

> [!tip] Where to Stay in Halifax
> Stay downtown near the waterfront to walk everywhere on Days 2–3. Lower Water Street / Historic Properties area puts you steps from the boardwalk, Discovery Centre, and Maritime Museum. Parking is ~$15–25/day in a parkade — factor that in vs. a suburban hotel with free parking.

---

### Day 2 — Halifax Full Day (16 August)

**Stay:** Halifax (Night 2)

Park once downtown and walk the entire waterfront (4.4 km boardwalk).

**Free:**
- **Submarine playground** on the boardwalk — one of the best playgrounds in Halifax
- **Wave monument** — kids slide down it
- Walk Historic Properties and the full Harbourfront boardwalk
- **Harbour Ferry to Dartmouth** — ~$3–4/person, 10-min crossing. Great harbour views, kids love it.

**Paid — pick 2–3 based on kids' ages:**
- **Discovery Centre** (1215 Lower Water St) — 4 floors of hands-on science, dome theatre. Youth (3–18): $7.00, Under 2 free. Budget 2–3 hours.
- **Maritime Museum of the Atlantic** — ship models, Titanic artifacts, HMCS Sackville corvette outside. Child (6–17): $5.10, Under 6 free, Adult: $10.95. Best-value museum on the trip.
- **Halifax Citadel** (on the hill, 10-min walk from waterfront) — star-shaped British fortress with Changing of the Guard ceremonies. Good for older kids. ~$10 adult, ~$5 child.
- **Harbour Hopper** — amphibious bus, drives on city streets then into the harbour. The moment it hits the water is a crowd-pleaser for all ages.
  - Adult: ~$53.72, Youth (6–15): ~$29.90, Child (2–5): ~$18.55, Under 2 free
  - 1 hour. Book ahead: [harbourhopper.com](https://www.harbourhopper.com)

**Dinner on the waterfront:** Murphy's on the Water (casual), Bicycle Thief (nicer sit-down).

### Day 3 — South Shore or Hall's Harbour Day Trip (17 August)

**Stay:** Halifax (Night 3)

Choose one based on priorities:

---

**Option A — South Shore: Lunenburg + Mahone Bay** (~1 hr south via Hwy 103)

**Lunenburg** ⭐ — UNESCO World Heritage Site
- One of only two urban UNESCO World Heritage Sites in North America. A perfectly preserved 18th-century British colonial settlement with colourful wooden buildings on a working harbour.
- Walk the waterfront, visit the Fisheries Museum of the Atlantic (hands-on, great for kids — touch tank, boat tours, schooner Therese E. Connor)
- Fisheries Museum: Adult ~$13, Child (6–17) ~$7, Under 6 free
- Budget 2–3 hours in town. Great for photos and lunch.

**Mahone Bay** (30 min from Lunenburg)
- Charming small town famous for three churches reflected in the bay. Great bakeries and ice cream. Quick 45-min stop on the way back.

**Blue Rocks** (10 min from Lunenburg)
- A tiny fishing hamlet with colourful boats on slate-blue rocky shores. One of the most photographed spots in Nova Scotia. Free, 20-min stop.

---

**Option B — Hall's Harbour + Annapolis Valley** (~1.5 hr west via Hwy 101)

**Hall's Harbour** ⭐ — Bay of Fundy tidal spectacle
- A tiny fishing village with 14–16 metre tidal swings. Boats sit on dry ocean floor at low tide.
- **Time with tides** — check [tide-forecast.com](https://tide-forecast.com) for Hall's Harbour station. Arrive at low tide, stay to watch it come in.
- **Hall's Harbour Lobster Pound** — pick a live lobster, they cook it, you eat it overlooking the water. Seasonal (May–Oct). Confirm hours at [hallsharbourlobster.com](https://hallsharbourlobster.com).
- Walk the rocky beach, watch fishing boats, browse small artist studios.

**Annapolis Valley** (en route back)
- Apple and wine country. If passing through, stop at a farm stand for local produce.

---

### Day 4 — Halifax → Baddeck, Cape Breton (18 August)

**Stay:** Auberge Gisele's Inn, Baddeck (3 nights, Aug 18–20)

**Drive:** ~350 km, ~3.5 hr via Hwy 104. Fill up in Truro — gas is cheaper on the NS mainland than Cape Breton.

**En route stop — Truro (1.5 hr from Halifax)**
- Quick leg-stretch. Tim Hortons and gas on the Trans-Canada.

**En route stop — Cape Breton Causeway + Bras d'Or Lake views** (entering Cape Breton)
- The single causeway crossing from the NS mainland onto Cape Breton Island. Brief stop for photos of the Bras d'Or Lake.

**Arrive Baddeck (~3:30–4 PM)**

**Afternoon — Alexander Graham Bell National Historic Site**
- Bell spent his summers in Baddeck and did much of his later experimental work here. Genuine working lab displays, including flying machines and early telephone prototypes.
- Adult ~$8.50, Child (6–16) ~$4.25, Family ~$21.50. Youth under 6 free.
- Budget 1.5–2 hours. Surprisingly good for kids — hands-on exhibits.

**Evening — Ceilidh in Baddeck**
- Traditional Cape Breton fiddle music and step dancing. Informal, community-run, usually in the village hall or local venue. Ask at the inn for the current schedule.

> [!note] Auberge Gisele's Inn
> 78-room 4-star inn in Baddeck village. Restaurant on-site. Walking distance to Bell NHS and the waterfront. Many rooms have fireplaces. Book well in advance for August.

### Day 5 — Cabot Trail East: Ingonish + Middle Head (19 August)

**Stay:** Auberge Gisele's Inn (Night 5)

**Drive notes:** Ingonish is ~1.5 hr from Baddeck via Cabot Trail (east side). Leave by 9 AM to get full time at the beach.

**Ingonish Beach** ⭐ (inside Cape Breton Highlands NP)
- Unique beach: a natural **barachois** separates the Atlantic Ocean from a freshwater lake. Two supervised swim zones.
- The freshwater lake is significantly warmer than the ocean — kids strongly prefer it. Full lifeguard coverage in August.
- Facilities: picnic tables, washrooms, changing areas, playground, tennis courts.

**Middle Head Trail** — 4.5 km, ~2 hr
- Peninsula trail with **Atlantic Ocean on both sides**. Rocky headland with views of Cape Smokey, Ingonish Island, seals, bald eagles.
- Easy–moderate. Fine for school-age kids. Trailhead starts at the **Keltic Lodge** grounds.
- Combine with the beach for a full day at Ingonish.
- [Parks Canada](https://www.pc.gc.ca/en/pn-np/ns/cbreton/activ/randonnee-hiking/middlehead)

**Drive back to Baddeck via east coast Cabot Trail**
- Stop at **Cape Smokey Provincial Park** — short walk for panoramic views, or take the gondola to the summit.
- **Neil's Harbour** — tiny fishing village, good ice cream stop (Chowder House).

---

### Day 6 — Cabot Trail West: Full Loop + Skyline Trail (20 August)

**Stay:** Auberge Gisele's Inn (Night 6)

**Drive counterclockwise** — Baddeck north along the west coast. Full 298 km loop, ~4.5 hr non-stop. With stops and the Skyline hike, budget 10–11 hours. Early start (8 AM) recommended.

> [!important] Cabot Trail Direction
> **Drive counterclockwise** from Baddeck. You stay on the outside lane relative to the ocean — better views throughout. West coast in morning light, Skyline Trail at golden hour.

**Morning stops heading north:**
- **Uisge Ban Waterfall** — 1.5 km easy walk to a waterfall. Gentle warm-up.
- **Margaree Valley** — scenic salmon river valley. Quick drive-through.
- **Chéticamp** (~2 hr from Baddeck) — Acadian fishing village, good lunch. La Bella Bella or Restaurant Acadian for traditional Acadian cuisine. Fuel up here — last reliable gas before the highlands.

**Afternoon through the highlands:**
- **Lone Shieling** — 15-min loop walk through old-growth maple forest. Perfect leg-stretch with kids.
- **Pleasant Bay** — whale watching zodiac tours available. Mid-point stop. Rusty Anchor restaurant for a break.
- **Clucking Hen Cafe & Bakery** — quirky bakery stop between Pleasant Bay and Ingonish. Good pastries and coffee.

**Evening — Skyline Trail** ⭐⭐ MUST DO — 9.5 km loop, ~2.5–3 hr
- Forested plateau trail ending on a dramatic **cliff-edge boardwalk** with the Cabot Trail winding 300 m below and the Gulf of St. Lawrence to the horizon.
- **Start at 5:30–6:00 PM** for golden hour on the headland. Sunset ~8:00–8:15 PM in mid-August.
- Family-friendly. Modest elevation. Keep close watch on kids near cliff edges.
- **No dogs.** Moose common in the forest section — do not approach.
- Bring headlamps. Pack snacks and water (nothing at trailhead).
- [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/trail/canada/nova-scotia/skyline-trail-loop)

> [!warning] Skyline Trail Parking Reservation — Required
> Paid reservation required June 26–October 25, 2026. $13 online. Reservations open **May 6, 2026** at reservation.pc.gc.ca. For sunset, book the latest available timeslot.

### Day 7 — Baddeck → Moncton (21 August)

**Stay:** Chateau Moncton Trademark Collection by Wyndham (1 night)

**Drive:** ~280 km, ~3 hr via Trans-Canada/Hwy 104 through Amherst into NB.

**Optional morning stop — Fortress of Louisbourg** (detour, +1 hr each way from Baddeck)
- The largest reconstructed 18th-century French fortress in North America. Costumed interpreters, working bakery and blacksmith, cannon demonstrations. Half-day attraction.
- Adult ~$18.70, Child (6–16) ~$9.55, Family ~$47.40
- **Only do this if you have an early start** — adds 2 hrs driving plus 3 hrs on-site. Means arriving Moncton late evening.
- [Parks Canada — Louisbourg](https://parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/ns/louisbourg)

**En route stop — Amherst, NS** (last NS town before NB border)
- Fill gas here — cheaper than Moncton.

**Arrive Moncton mid-afternoon.** Check in, walk downtown or rest.

**Moncton Tidal Bore** (if timing aligns — free)
- Watch the Bay of Fundy tide reverse the Petitcodiac River. Twice daily.
- **Bore Park**, 10 Bendview Court. Check exact time at [tidalboretimes.ca](https://tidalboretimes.ca). Arrive 30 min early.
- If the evening bore time works, do it today instead of tomorrow.

---

### Day 8 — Hopewell Rocks → Halifax (22 August)

**Stay:** Halifax (1 night, Aug 22)

**Morning — Hopewell Rocks** ⭐ (~45 min from Moncton)

Walk on the **ocean floor** between 15-metre flower-pot sea stack rock formations. At high tide the same spot is 15 m underwater.

- Admission: Adult $15.85, Child (5–18) $9.06, Under 5 free. **2-day ticket valid** (not needed here since you're only visiting once).
- **Tide timing is critical:** Ocean floor access open 3 hrs before to 3 hrs after low tide. Check tide table at [nbparks.ca](https://www.parcsnbparks.ca) and plan your arrival around the low tide window.
- Wear water shoes or old sneakers — not bare feet on the ocean floor.
- Facilities: café, interpretive centre, picnic areas, playground.

**Moncton Tidal Bore** (if not done yesterday — free)
- Check [tidalboretimes.ca](https://tidalboretimes.ca) for morning bore time. Bore Park, downtown Moncton. Arrive 30 min early.

**Magnetic Hill** (quick add-on if kids have energy)
- Optical illusion where car rolls uphill. ~$5/vehicle, takes 5 min.
- **Magnetic Hill Zoo** adjacent: Adult $14.99, Child (4–11) $10.99. Atlantic Canada's largest zoo.

**Drive Halifax** (~2.5 hr from Moncton via Hwy 104 and Trans-Canada). Arrive mid-afternoon.

**Halifax evening — anything missed on Days 1–3:**
- Waterfront walk, dinner at Bicycle Thief or Murphy's on the Water.

---

### Day 9 — Halifax → Airport (23 August)

**Depart from Halifax Stanfield International (YHZ)**

Airport is ~35 min north of downtown via Hwy 102. Arrive 2 hrs before domestic, 3 hrs before international.

**Morning options before flight (check departure time):**
- **Peggy's Cove** (if not done Day 1) — ~80 km from airport, ~1 hr. Early morning light is exceptional there.
- **Dartmouth via ferry** — quick 10-min ferry crossing from Halifax waterfront for a different perspective of the city skyline.
- **Point Pleasant Park** — 75-acre forested park at the southern tip of Halifax peninsula. Free, good final walk.

## Driving & Tips

**Total loop driving:** ~1,800 km over 9 days — very manageable. Max drive day is ~350 km (Day 4 Halifax→Baddeck, Day 8 Moncton→Halifax).

**Cabot Trail direction:** Drive **counterclockwise** from Baddeck. Outside lane faces the ocean — better views throughout. Puts Skyline Trail in the evening.

**Cell service:** None through Cape Breton Highlands interior. Download offline maps before entering the park.

**Moose:** Real risk on Cape Breton rural roads and any NS/NB highway after dark. Avoid driving after 9 PM on rural sections.

**Gas:** Fill up in Truro before Cape Breton (cheaper). Fill in Chéticamp before the highlands section of the Cabot Trail. Fill in Amherst before crossing into NB.

**August peak season:** Hopewell Rocks, Skyline Trail, and Peggy's Cove are all very busy in August. Hit these early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds.
