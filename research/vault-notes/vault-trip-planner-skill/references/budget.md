# Trip Budgeting

Framework for Phase 3. For day trips, a one-line per-day estimate is enough — use the full table only for weekend and multi-day trips.

## Cost categories

Build the budget from these, per person or per group (state which), then sum to per-day and total:

| Category | What's in it | Notes |
|----------|--------------|-------|
| Getting there | Flights, fuel, train/bus, car rental, airport parking, tolls | Biggest swing item on most trips |
| Local transport | Fuel for day drives, parking, transit, taxis/rideshare, ferries | Easy to forget; adds up |
| Lodging | Hotel/Airbnb/camping per night × nights | Often the second-biggest line |
| Food | Breakfast/lunch/dinner + snacks/coffee per day | Estimate per-person-per-day, then × people × days |
| Activities | Tickets, tours, rentals, guides, park fees | Where passes save real money |
| Misc / buffer | Souvenirs, tips, incidentals, contingency | Add 10-15% buffer — things always come up |

Reconcile the total against the user's target. If it's over, name the biggest drivers and offer concrete trims (cheaper lodging tier, fewer paid activities, cook some meals, shoulder-season dates). If under, suggest where an upgrade adds the most value.

## Currency

Local/Canadian trips: budget in CAD. Trips abroad: budget in the destination currency and give a CAD total at a current rough rate (note the rate and date). The user's home base is in Ontario, Canada.

## Money-saving passes worth checking

Surface these when relevant — they recur in the user's day-trip notes and pay for themselves quickly:

- **Ontario Parks Annual Vehicle Pass** (~$112 CAD) — covers all Ontario provincial parks; pays off after ~6-7 visits. Worth it for an active day-trip season.
- **Parks Canada Discovery Pass** — annual admission to national parks and historic sites; the summer "Strong/Canada Strong Pass" window has historically offered free or discounted admission — verify current-year dates and terms by web search before relying on it.
- **City/region tourist passes** — many destinations sell multi-attraction passes (museum cards, transit + attraction bundles). Check whether the planned activities make one worthwhile.
- **Timed/advance discounts** — online-in-advance tickets, family bundles, off-peak rates, and annual memberships (zoos, science centres) that beat single-visit pricing if visiting more than once.

Always confirm current prices and pass terms via live web search — these change yearly.

## Reference points

Use researched, current figures over assumptions. When a quick anchor helps:

- Day trip from home base: fuel + parking + admissions + a meal — often modest; the anchor is fuel cost (distance × 2) plus paid attractions.
- Weekend getaway: one or two nights' lodging usually dominates.
- Multi-day domestic: lodging + getting-there together are typically the majority of spend.
- International: flights and lodging dominate; activities and food are the controllable middle.
