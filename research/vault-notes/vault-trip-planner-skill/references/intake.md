# Interactive Intake

The same destination is a different trip for different people — a relaxing solo escape vs. a super-fun family blowout vs. an adrenaline weekend. Capture the traveller's profile **before** discovery so every place, the pace, and the budget are filtered through their actual preferences. Record the answers in the note so the trip is personalized and reusable.

Run intake with the **AskUserQuestion** tool (selection / multi-select menus), not open-ended prose. It's faster for the user and gives clean, structured inputs.

## How to run intake

1. **Pre-fill, don't re-ask.** Read the Travel Preferences note and the user's request first. Anything already known (home base, who travels, stated dates) is a default — don't ask it again. Only ask what's missing or what genuinely personalizes the trip.
2. **Batch into rounds.** AskUserQuestion takes up to 4 questions per call, each with 2-4 options. Group the most decision-changing questions into one round; follow up with a second round only if needed. Don't drip one question at a time.
3. **Always allow more.** Users can pick "Other" to type their own answer, and multi-select lets them combine vibes/activities. Phrase questions so combinations are expected ("pick all that apply").
4. **Offer an express path.** If the user seems in a hurry or says "just plan it", confirm the essentials in a single round and fill the rest from the Preferences note defaults. Don't force the full intake.
5. **Confirm and proceed.** Echo the captured profile in one line, then move into discovery filtered by it.

## Universal question bank

Ask the relevant subset for every trip. Recommended first-round batch: **vibe, pace, group, budget**.

**Trip vibe — what's the feel you want?** (multi-select)
- Relaxing & slow — unwind, low agenda, recharge
- Super fun & lively — energy, variety, a packed good time
- Adventure & adrenaline — hikes, water, heights, the outdoors
- Nature & scenic — landscapes, viewpoints, wildlife, quiet beauty
- Culture & history — old towns, museums, local life
- Food & flavours — markets, signature dishes, memorable meals
- Romantic — couple-focused, special spots
- Family-friendly fun — works for kids, keeps everyone happy

**Pace?** (single)
- Packed — see and do as much as possible
- Balanced — a few solid things a day, with breathing room
- Slow — do less, savour more

**Who's going?** (single — skip if known)
- Solo
- Couple
- Family with kids (ask ages in a follow-up or as "Other")
- Friends group
- Multi-generational / extended family

**Budget comfort?** (single)
- Budget — keep it lean
- Mid-range — sensible, good value
- Comfortable — happy to spend on what matters
- Splurge — make it special, cost secondary

## Activity preferences

Second-round multi-select — this is what most personalizes the place list. "Which activities are you into? Pick all that apply."

Offer the subset that fits the vibe and destination. Full menu to draw from:

- Hiking & trails
- Water — beach, swim, kayak, boat, tubing
- Adventure parks — zip lines, climbing, caves
- Wildlife & nature watching
- Museums & galleries
- Historic sites & old towns
- Food tours & markets
- Wineries / breweries / distilleries
- Shopping & local crafts
- Nightlife & live music
- Photography & scenic viewpoints
- Wellness & spa
- Theme / amusement parks
- Just relax — cafes, picnics, easy strolls

Follow up on hard constraints when they matter (a quick question or "Other"): dietary needs, mobility/accessibility, anything that's a must-include or a hard no.

## Day-trip / picnic questions

For local trips the **distance and time budget** drive everything. Make these selectable — the user explicitly wants to choose by driving distance and parameters.

**How far are you willing to drive (each way)?** (single)
- Close — under 1 hr
- Nearby — 1 to 2 hrs
- Day-range — 2 to 3 hrs
- I'll go far for the right spot — 3 hrs+

**How much of the day?** (single)
- Half day — out and back by early afternoon
- Full day — make a day of it
- Sunrise to sunset — maximize it

**What kind of outing?** (multi-select)
- Picnic & relax
- Active & outdoorsy
- Sightseeing & scenic
- Foodie outing
- Kid-focused fun

**Getting there?** (single — default from Preferences)
- Car
- Transit (GO Train / VIA Rail from the home base)
- Bike

Then research and present options that fit the chosen radius and time. When several good options exist, present them as a short ranked shortlist (with drive time, vibe, and rough cost) and let the user pick which to build out — selectable, so they choose by distance and fit.

## Multi-day extras

For weekend and multi-day trips, add as needed:

- **Lodging style** (single): budget/clean, mid-range hotel, boutique/character, resort, rental/Airbnb, camping.
- **Base strategy** (single): one home base with day trips, or move between towns.
- **Trip priority** (single, when trade-offs loom): see the icons / hit the highlights, go deep on a few areas, or pure downtime.

## Recording the profile

Save the captured answers in the itinerary note under a **"Trip profile"** section (vibe, pace, group, budget, activities, constraints) so the plan is explainable and the next trip can reuse it. If the answers reveal stable preferences (e.g. always wants slow pace, never long flights), offer to update the defaults in the Travel Preferences note.
