## 1. What was done

| Layer | Source and method | Requests |
|---|---|---|
| Wikidata check of the 148 stored QIDs | one SPARQL query per 80 QIDs on query.wikidata.org: P402 (OSM relation), P6375 (street address), P281 (postcode), P625, P31, labels in en and the country's languages; a QID counts as confirmed when its P625 is within the kind's radius of the store point and a label matches the name | 2 |
| Wikidata search for the 7 places without a QID | `wbsearchentities` on www.wikidata.org, the store name and its short form, en (and fr in Canada), then `wbgetentities` for the hits' P625 | 19 |
| OSM id candidates | Overpass, 38 cluster queries (places grouped within 250 km, at most 8 a query) plus 1 query that resolved the 64 `osm_ref` values already in records; per place: elements tagged `wikidata=<QID>` within 20 to 150 km, elements whose `name` equals the store name or a Wikidata label within the kind's radius, and car parks and trailheads within 1.5 km of natural places that have no access record. Exact tag values only (indexed); one runner, `/tmp/travel-overpass.lock`, 3 s apart, 502 and 504 retried with back-off | 56 (39 answered, 17 retried after 502 or 504) |
| Address proposals | the D7 order: Wikidata P6375 and P281 for non-natural places; OSM `addr:*` on the candidate element; Nominatim reverse (`zoom=18`, `addressdetails=1`) at the address target: the place's access record (`parks_for`), else the nearest OSM car park or trailhead candidate within 1.5 km, else the place point. One runner, `/tmp/travel-nominatim.lock`, 1.2 s apart. Towns, cities, regions and drives get none (address N, spec 4.9) | 96 |
| AllTrails | no request: a query list only (section 7), per Q322 / Q-LT1 | 0 |

User agent on every request: `travel-capture/1.0 (+https://nks-travel.pages.dev/about/)`. Every answer is cached under
`tools/.cache/lt-where/` (gitignored) before it is parsed, so a rerun hits no host. No Google, Apple, Bing, booking or review
site was used; no commercial page was fetched. No home point was read or written.

### Confidence rules

- **OSM high:** the element carries the store QID and its tags fit the kind, or it is Wikidata's P402 relation, or it is the
  record's existing `osm_ref` and resolves with the QID or the name. **Medium:** the QID-tagged element has tags or a name that
  do not fit, or an exact name with fitting tags and no rival of the same element type. **Low:** a partial name, tags that do not
  fit, rivals, or an element tagged with another QID. **Conflict:** the record's `osm_ref` does not resolve, or matches neither
  the QID nor the name, or P402 names a different element.
- **Address confident:** a proposal at medium or better whose only flags are the routine ones (the target is an OSM car park
  candidate LT-AP confirms; a door place to confirm on its official page). **Flagged:** a footpath given as the nearest road,
  the answer object more than 150 m from the target, a county or municipality as the locality, or a Wikidata line that needs
  splitting. **None found:** Nominatim returned no road (summits, glaciers, lakes far from roads) or the park has no access
  record yet.
- **QID confirmed:** P625 within the kind's radius and a label match. `confirmed_coord_only`: same place, label differs
  (a German or English label for an Italian name).
