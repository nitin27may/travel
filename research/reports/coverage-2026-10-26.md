## 5. Data-quality caveats and recommended order

Caveats:
- **Unverified points.** No row in `points.json` is person-checked: 208 are `agent_checked` and 71 have no state. 33 are approximate (catalogue 19, Rockies 8, Patagonia 4, Dolomites 1, Vermont 1), and one is pinned wrong (Grand View Point). 9 trails are shown as "unconfirmed" through `UNCONFIRMED_TRAIL_DATA`.
- **Inference share.** Of the values that carry a basis, `our_inference` makes up capture 789 of 955, suitability 1,175 of 2,394 and relevance 1,888 of 2,170.
- **Honest gaps.** Dated `searched_none`: capture 107, suitability 487, relevance 297, store columns 49, and 44 rows in run records. Open `gap` states: suitability 97, relevance 151. The Rockies README notes that no minimum height was found for 42 of 43 adventures.
- **Structural gaps that research cannot close.** 25 experience offers can never pass until RIS-H gives `duration_min`, `activity_min` and `book_url` a home (D2b-us decision 2). Trails cannot pass `geometry` until a track exists, and the fetcher only reads prototype places (D2b-ca decision 1, D2b-us decision 3).
- **Lane naming.** The 4 D2b Utah-park records sit in `data/capture/usa-southwest-rockies/` (the legacy folder name), apart from the `utah-five-parks` lane. Both validate, but per-trip counts must add the two together.
- **Stale numbers.** DATA-ROADMAP section 1 is stale: it gives 394 records, `E_PLACE_AREA` 277 and keys 3,875, where today's figures are 498, 227 and 3,731. Shown photos (60 pages) lag the photos in the data (162 records + 62 store images) until PHO-R ships.

Recommended order (visitor value first, then trips with pages, then the store):
1. Owner: D1B-Q (0.5 h), Grand-Sault, and Q88 confirmed in MASTER-ORDER. Then one data seat runs TRP-GL-D, ORPH-7 and AREA-2 on the Rockies first, the published trip (11 h).
