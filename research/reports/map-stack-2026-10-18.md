# Map stack for the public site and the planner

Read date for every source below: **18 Oct 2026** unless a row says otherwise. Prices are USD per
1,000 billable events, as published. Screenshots are in `research/reports/map-stack/`.

## Decision in one table

| Surface | Map | Distance and directions | Key | Year-one cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public globe (`/globe`, home cover) | MapLibre GL JS globe projection, OpenFreeMap vector tiles, Mapterhorn terrain (what `legacy/globe/` already runs) | none on the globe; "Open place" goes to the place page | none | $0 |
| Public trip map (`/trips/<slug>`, The route) | MapLibre, OpenFreeMap, Mapterhorn hillshade, **our** day routes drawn from stored OpenRouteService geometry | stored ORS distance and drive time per leg ("38 min · 24 km"); per day an **Open in Google Maps** link built with Maps URLs | ORS key, server side only, at publish time | $0 |
| Public place and trail page (`/places/<slug>`) | **Google Maps JavaScript API** (Dynamic Maps) with our trailhead, parking and OSM trail line drawn as overlays; Maps Embed API as the zero-cost fallback | **Directions** button = Maps URLs to the parking point (no key, no charge); "from the base" distance and time from stored ORS values | Google browser key, referrer-restricted | $0 (about 10% of the free cap) |
| Planner day map with drive times | MapLibre, OpenFreeMap, Mapterhorn (the prototype's `MapView.tsx`) | ORS Directions on drop (not during drag), results stored on the leg; ORS Matrix for "optimise order" | ORS key, server side | $0 (about 10% of the daily cap) |
| Phone offline at a trailhead | MapLibre with a per-trip **PMTiles** extract (Protomaps basemap + Mapterhorn terrain) on Cloudflare R2, cached by the PWA | stored legs and times work offline; turn-by-turn is the user's own Google Maps app with an offline area they download (we show a reminder and the deep link) | none | $0 |

The hybrid is legal because of one page rule: **a page shows either Google maps or MapLibre maps, never
both.** Google's terms forbid using Google Maps Core Services "with or near a non-Google Map" (section
3.2.3(e), quoted below). Linking out with Maps URLs is not a map on our page.

Expected year-one bill: **$0**. Step to ~1,000 users: **$0 to about $40 a month**, explained in Usage.

## What decides it

1. **Coordinates and geometry we store must be ours.** Google route geometry, times and Places
   coordinates may only be cached 30 days and may never be drawn on a non-Google map. Our points
   come from OSM and official data (lanes/trails.md); our leg geometry and times come from
   OpenRouteService (OSM, ODbL), which we may store and export (GPX, CSV) with attribution. That
   keeps every Google product replaceable at the cost of one component.
2. **The owner likes the Google look, for pins and directions.** Place and trail pages are where a
   visitor checks "where exactly do I park and how do I get there". Google's basemap, labels and
   satellite toggle are strongest there, and the free cap covers it many times over.
3. **The globe, trip map and planner need our styling, our layers and offline.** Design tokens 5 (map
   palette) and components 7 assume full control of land, water, hillshade, routes, day colours,
   country fills and counts. MapLibre does this with no key and no metering; Google cannot be cached
   offline at all.

## Google terms that make the hybrid legal

Google Maps Platform Terms of Service, section 3.2.3 (page last modified 26 Aug 2026), and Maps Service
Specific Terms (last modified 15 Oct 2026):

| Rule | Text | What it means for us |
|---|---|---|
| 3.2.3(a) No scraping | "Customer will not export, extract, or otherwise scrape Google Maps Content for use outside the Services ... (iii) copy and save business names, addresses, or user reviews" | never copy coordinates, names or hours from Google into our data (already a researcher hard rule) |
| 3.2.3(b) No caching | "Customer will not cache Google Maps Content except as expressly permitted under the Maps Service Specific Terms" | nothing from Google goes into the database, the PWA cache or a PMTiles pack |
| 3.2.3(e) No use with non-Google maps | "Customer will not use the Google Maps Core Services with or near a non-Google Map in a Customer Application. For example, Customer will not (i) display or use Places content on a non-Google Map, (ii) display Street View imagery and non-Google Maps on the same screen, or (iii) link a Google Map to non-Google Maps Content or a non-Google Map." | **one map family per page**: place and trail pages are Google only (including the sidebar mini map); globe, trip and planner screens are MapLibre only. A planner sheet must not open a Google map beside the MapLibre map |
| 3.2.3(d)(iii) | "use the Google Maps Core Services in a listings or directory service" is forbidden | read as business listings; our place pages are research guides with independent content. Risk noted below |
| Service terms 3 Google ID caching | "Customer may cache (a) place_id from Places API, Directions API, Geolocation API and Routes API" | we may store a Google `place_id` per place to sharpen deep links (`query_place_id`); Places Text Search (IDs Only) is Essentials, "Unlimited" free |
| Service terms 19 Routes API | "19.2 ... must not use Google Maps Content from the Routes API in conjunction with a non-Google map. 19.3 ... may temporarily cache latitude (lat) and longitude (lng) values ... for up to 30 consecutive calendar days" | Google drive times cannot feed the MapLibre planner; ORS does |
| Service terms 14 Places API | "14.2 ... must not use Google Maps Content from the Places API in conjunction with a non-Google map. 14.3 ... cache latitude and longitude values ... for up to 30 consecutive calendar days" | no Places coordinates in our store |
| Map Tiles API policies | must not pre-fetch, store or cache except as stated; "must respect the max-age value"; "Offline uses" listed as prohibited; 3D tiles: "You must aggregate, sort, and display in a line, all attributions" | no Google tiles in offline packs |
| Overlays on a Google map | 3.2.3 forbids Google content on other maps, not our content on Google maps | drawing our OSM trail line, trailhead and parking pins on the JS API map is allowed |
| Attribution | service terms 2: "Customer will attribute all the Services in accordance with the Documentation" | keep Google's logo and data line visible; never place our credit chip over them |
| Maps URLs | "You don't need a Google API key to use Maps URLs." | free deep links; built at render time from our stored point (as lanes/trails.md `navigation` already specifies) |

What we may store, in short: our OSM and official coordinates, our ORS geometry, distances and times,
Google `place_id` values. What we may not store: anything else returned by a Google API.

**The live pages use an undocumented URL.** `legacy/` pages embed
`https://maps.google.com/maps?q=...&output=embed` with no key. It renders today (screenshots above) but
it is not the Maps Embed API, has no documentation or terms of its own, and can stop at any time. The v2
place pages replace it with the keyed JS API or `https://www.google.com/maps/embed/v1/place?key=...`.

## What the owner must do

1. **Create a Google Cloud project** for the travel site (one project; a second one for local
   development keeps quotas and keys apart). A billing account is required: "you must set up a
   billing account to set up a Cloud project." A card goes on file; new accounts may get the $300
   Cloud welcome credit.
2. **Enable only two APIs**: Maps JavaScript API and Maps Embed API. Do not enable Routes, Places,
   Map Tiles or 3D. (Places API (New) only if we later store `place_id` values; Text Search IDs Only
   is free.)
3. **Create one browser key and restrict it**:
   - Application restriction: Websites, with full referrer strings for `https://nks-travel.pages.dev/*`,
     `https://*.nks-travel.pages.dev/*` (preview deploys) and the future domain. A separate dev key
     for `http://localhost:*` in the dev project.
   - API restriction: Maps JavaScript API and Maps Embed API only.
   - Embed iframes: set `referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"` or a restricted key rejects
     them.
4. **Cap usage, because budgets do not**. Google: "Setting an alerts-only budget doesn't automatically
   cap Google Cloud or Google Maps Platform usage or spending."
   - In APIs and Services, Quotas: set Maps JavaScript API map loads per day to **300** (about 9,000 a
     month, under the 10,000 cap). When it trips, the page falls back to the Embed iframe or the
     Directions link.
   - Billing, Budgets: a budget of **$1** with alert thresholds at 1% and 100% of actual spend (any
     charge at all emails the owner), plus a forecast alert.
   - If the console offers a spend cap budget for the project ("If supported for your service"), set
     it to $1 so services pause rather than bill. Not confirmed today that Maps Platform is eligible.
5. **Create an OpenRouteService account** (HeiGIT) and a Standard key; it is used server side only
   (publish script and planner API), never in the browser.
6. **Cloudflare R2**: one bucket with a custom domain for PMTiles packs, CORS limited to the site
   origins. Already on the existing Cloudflare account.

## Risks and the switch path

| Risk | Likelihood | Switch path |
|---|---|---|
| Google cuts the Dynamic Maps free cap or prices rise (it changed in March 2025, when the $200 credit became per-SKU caps) | medium | place pages fall back to the Embed API (still listed as unlimited) with Maps URLs buttons; or move place pages to MapLibre with the same stored data. Nothing Google is stored, so nothing is migrated |
| Embed API stops being free | low | Maps URLs links plus a MapLibre mini map; one component |
| "Listings or directory service" clause read against a place catalogue | low to medium | same fallback as above; keep each place page rich in our own research so it is not a directory |
| A designer later puts a Google map beside a MapLibre one (3.2.3(e)) | medium | encode the page rule in the component layer: a page declares its map family and the build fails if it uses both |
| OpenFreeMap public instance disappears (donation funded, "I don't offer SLA guarantees") | low to medium | self-host its weekly planet build, or Protomaps PMTiles on R2, or MapTiler/Stadia paid ($20 to $30 a month); style JSON swap only |
| Mapterhorn hosted tiles have no stated terms or limits | medium | AWS Terrarium tiles (already the legacy globe's fallback), or a Mapterhorn PMTiles extract on R2 |
| ORS quota, uptime, or free-plan commercial status changes (the official page is unclear on commercial use; HeiGIT staff say higher quotas are by request) | medium | self-host Valhalla or ORS; OSRM demo stays dev only |
| The product becomes commercial | open question | MapTiler, Stadia, GraphHopper and Cesium free tiers are non-commercial, which is why none is in the recommended stack; Google, OpenFreeMap, Protomaps and R2 allow commercial use |
| Google look on place pages differs from tokens 5 | certain | a map ID style can approximate land, water and roads and hide POIs; travel-design-reviewer to decide how far to match |

## Gotchas

- The Embed API directions mode documents waypoints as names, addresses or place IDs, not coordinates;
  for a multi-stop day use Maps URLs, which take coordinates.
- Maps URLs allow 3 waypoints on mobile browsers and 9 elsewhere, and 2,048 characters; split long days
  into legs.
- MapLibre globe plus terrain shows seams at world zoom; the legacy globe turns terrain on only at zoom
  3.5 and above. Keep that gate.
- Headless Chrome rendered OpenFreeMap vector tiles for these screenshots when launched as
  `/usr/bin/google-chrome` with `--use-angle=swiftshader --enable-unsafe-swiftshader` (script in
  `/tmp/mapstack/scripts/shot.cjs`). The earlier note that headless never renders them applies to the
  default headless shell.
- ORS drive times are free-flow estimates; label them as such and offer the Google link for traffic.
