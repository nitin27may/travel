   pair claims in `data/schema/suitability-applicability.json`, so `E_SUIT_SUBKIND` refuses any suitability record and the R suitability keys
   cannot be answered. Decision 2.
2. **Dantercëpies gondola and the Southern Fuegian Railway** (ride.lift, ride.rail): route records need `geometry` at the required tier, read
   only from a `data/tracks` file, and the track fetcher reads trails only. Decision 2.
3. **Three `searched_none` osm_refs** (Skaftafell, Fosshóll, La Flégère): in the run record only, until FIX2's test change is merged.
4. **Kanarra Falls car park address**: the Nominatim road "East 200 North Street" matches the street-address detector. Until FIX2's exemption.
5. **No `covers_areas`** anywhere (no fee task for these parks), **no `run_by`** in a migrated file.

## DECISIONS NEEDED

1. **Private trips leak through catalogue pointers (builder, web, privacy).** With the Patagonia records, `web/tests/data/adapter.test.ts`
   "a private flag removes the trip from every function" fails: El Chaltén, Cerro Castor and Perito Moreno carry derived `similar` and
   `pairs` entries that name `trip/patagonia-2027/...` records, and the public layer does not drop pointers whose target belongs to a
   private trip. The same gap exists today for every trip a catalogue place points into (61 catalogue places point at trip records); the
   test only exercises Patagonia. Fix the filter in `web/lib/public/`, then merge c31fbf17; or merge without it.
2. **Profiles and routes the contract cannot fill (builder).** (a) Give the `river` suitability profile a kind/subkind pair (for example
   `lake/river` and `lake/creek`, or a `water` kind) so creeks and rivers can carry suitability; (b) let a lift or rail route's geometry come
   from its OSM way or relation (a two-station line), or have TRK-1 fetch lift and railway lines, so the gondola and the railway can be
   written. Four places wait on this.
3. **The stay promotion (coordinator, LT-C owner).** The three stay gates exit 0. Promoting needs the LT-C phase-1 tests rewritten to pin
   phase 1 for the families not yet promoted, and the PA-C fixture given a booking link or a dated `searched_none`. A builder change, then
   the four-line `types.json` edit and `capture.ts build` in one commit.
4. **Street names with numbers (FIX2).** "East 200 North Street" (Kanarra) is a street, not a house number; the detector's exemption for
   address lines should cover it. The house numbers left out here (Skaftafellsstofa, Dobbiaco station, the huts) can be added after FIX2.
5. **Skaftafell's type (planner).** Keep `national_park` (it was one from 1967 to 2008), or add a Vatnajökull National Park record and
   make Skaftafell a `zone` under it with an alias for the store's `place/skaftafell`.
6. **Point-source wording (builder, small).** `sections.ts` reads "OSM way 123" only; LT-1's 17 access points are written "OSM way/123" and
   show "Placed by hand". Accept the slash in the reader, or rewrite those quotes.
7. **Towns anchors (FIX2).** The new Icelandic, Patagonian and Chamonix points add to the points outside the towns anchors
   (`tools/tests/test_towns.py`, failing on main since LT-1). After FIX2's refresh merges, run `python3 tools/towns.py build` from the cached
   27 Oct dump again, then `capture.ts derive-near --write` (several Icelandic places now say "none within 50 km").
8. **Pre-existing, not this batch:** the five web unit failures on a clean fbc7b4cb (`address.test.ts` three, `legs-home-radius`,
   `relevance.test.ts` part_of pointer) are FIX2's.
