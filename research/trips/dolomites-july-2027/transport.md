## 0. HEADLINE FINDINGS (each evidenced below)

1. **The Guest Pass does NOT cover the three buses this itinerary most depends on.** The official 442 timetable states verbatim: *"Guestcards (Südtirol/Alto Adige Guest Pass etc.) are not valid!"* and südtirolmobil lists 442, 439 and 444 as excluded services.
2. **Three days cannot be delivered by public transport at all as written**: D2 (05:00 Braies), D6 (05:30 Sorapis — first bus 08:00), D8 (21:30 end-of-colour walk-out; sunset is 20:58 — last bus off Passo Giau 17:29).
3. **There is no verifiable staffed car-rental desk in Cortina d'Ampezzo.** Nearest confirmed physical branches are Bolzano city / Bolzano Airport. This alone probably kills the clean "car only for the Cortina block" hybrid.
4. **Every confirmed South Tyrol rental branch found publishes Mon–Fri hours only** — no Saturday-afternoon or Sunday hours listed. 18 and 25 July 2027 are Sundays.
5. **No Uber / Bolt / FreeNow anywhere in the Dolomites.** Phone-booked taxi/NCC only.
6. **Watch item:** a proposed permanent May–October through-traffic ban at **Passo Gardena** from 2027 would sit directly on D9's driving route.

## 3. PASSES AND CARDS

### 3.1 Südtirol Guest Pass
[suedtirolmobil](https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/tickets/holidays-and-recreation/suedtirol-guest-pass), [suedtirol.info](https://www.suedtirol.info/en/en/information/suedtirol-guest-pass)
- **Genuinely free** — included in the overnight rate at participating accommodation, **cannot be purchased**. Issued to everyone aged 6+ at check-in or pre-check-in, digital (wallet pass or PDF).
- **Valid 00:00 on arrival day → 24:00 on departure day.** Personalised, non-transferable.
- **Covers:** regional trains (R/RV/RE), city and regional buses, and certain public cable cars in the südtirolmobil network across South Tyrol.
- **Works in Val Gardena AND Alta Pusteria AND Bolzano — yes**, all three are South Tyrol. Val Gardena issues its own branded **Val Gardena Guest Pass** (free, all public buses in Val Gardena + nearly all South Tyrol public transport).
- **❌ EXCLUSIONS (critical):**
  - Long-distance trains: EC, Railjet, Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Italo
  - **Cross-border regional routes to Innsbruck and San Candido–Lienz**
  - Nightliners / Nightbus
  - **Service 444 (Toblach–Misurina–Auronzohütte)**
  - **Service 439 (Welsberg–Pragser Wildsee)**
  - **Service 442 (Toblach–Niederdorf–Pragser Wildsee)**
  - Bicycle and pet transport
  - **Seiser Alm Aerial Cableway** (private) — discount only
  - **Alpe di Siusi lines 10 and 11**, line 12, Nightliner N170, Tiers ropeway, Monte Pana/Saltria bus
- **On 442 the official PDF is blunt: "Guestcards (Südtirol / Alto Adige Guest Pass etc.) are not valid!"** On 444: "there is no price reduction with Südtirol Guest Pass" — only an extended seating quota.
- **→ Answering the question directly: the Guest Pass covers NEITHER the Braies shuttle NOR the Tre Cime bus.**

### 3.2 Mobilcard South Tyrol (2026)
[suedtirolmobil](https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/tickets/ticket-finder/mobilcard), [mobilcard.info](https://www.mobilcard.info/en/museumobil-card.asp)
- Adults/youth: **1 day €22 · 3 days €32 · 7 days €52**
- Children 6–13: **1 day €11 · 3 days €16 · 7 days €26**
- museumobil Card: 3-day and 7-day variants combining museums + all South Tyrol public transport. **GAP: 2026 prices not retrieved.**
- **If accommodation supplies the Guest Pass free, the Mobilcard is redundant** — same network, same exclusions.

### 3.3 Does any of this cover DolomitiBus in the Veneto (Cortina)?
**NO — confirmed.** The Guest Pass and Mobilcard are **südtirolmobil (South Tyrol / Alto Adige) products**; Cortina d'Ampezzo is in the **Province of Belluno, Veneto**, outside that transit authority. DolomitiBus is a separate operator with its own fares. The only reported exception is **SAD line 445** (South-Tyrol-operated, runs into Cortina) — **LOW confidence, verify.** [TripAdvisor thread](https://www.tripadvisor.ie/ShowTopic-g187768-i20-k15413422-Sudtirol_Guest_Pass_for_Dolomitibus-Italy.html) (snippet only, 403 on fetch)

### 3.4 Cortina's own transport

- **Public transport + Guest Pass for D1, D3, D5, D10, D11, D13, D14**
- **AlpsGo day hires for the Braies early starts (D2, D4)** from San Candido — ~€79/day
- **A conventional rental for the Cortina block, picked up at VCE or Bolzano** — which, because of the branch problem, effectively means keeping a car for the whole middle of the trip
- Net effect: you converge back on something close to model (a), just with more friction.
