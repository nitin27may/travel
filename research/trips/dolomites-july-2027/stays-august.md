**Private research notes. Not for publication.** Prepared 2026-10-24 for Nitin's brief:
a family with an older child, whole apartment/home with a kitchen, average
accommodation budget CAD 300/night (≈ EUR 200). Task: price the **last two weeks of

## 1. Ten-line summary + recommendation

1. **Concrete August window: Tue 17 Aug → Mon 30 Aug 2027** (13 nights / 14 days, VCE in/out).
   It mirrors the current Tue→Mon shape exactly and starts two days *after* Ferragosto (Sun 15 Aug).
2. The rival framing **Sat 14 Aug → Fri 27 Aug** is worse — it buys the Ferragosto long weekend
   (14–15 Aug), the single most expensive, most crowded, most minimum-stay-locked nights of the year.
3. Late August sits in the **Italian domestic peak**: Ferragosto → ~20 Aug is the costliest, busiest
   fortnight of the Dolomite summer; only the **final week (~23–30 Aug) eases**. The 17 Aug start still
   catches the tail of the peak; the back half gets the easing.
4. **Accommodation**: higher prices and the tightest availability of the year, with **7-night Sat–Sat
   minimum-stay** common in peak August — this collides with the 4-base plan. Mid-July is high season too
   but materially easier, and the EUR ~200 whole-apartment target is more findable.
5. **Crowds & parking** at Braies, Tre Cime, Seceda, Alpe di Siusi are **worse** in late August; the
   booked-slot systems sell out earlier. The access *rules* are identical (both windows sit inside the
   1 Jul–15 Sep Braies ban and the season-long Alpe di Siusi / Tre Cime schemes).
6. **Daylight** is ~1.5–2 h shorter in late August (sunset ~40–65 min earlier), but the later sunrise
   (06:11–06:28 vs 05:30–05:44) makes dawn shoots far less punishing, and real darkness returns for stars.
7. **Weather** is a near-wash: afternoon thunderstorms persist in August; the freezing level is a touch
   higher, so the high routes (Piz Boè, Croda del Becco, Alta Via 1, Lagazuoi tunnels) are reliably
   snow-free — but they already are by mid-July in this plan. Valley heat (Bolzano/Venice) hits both.
8. **Events** slightly favour late August for *driving*: the road-closing races (Maratona 4 Jul,
   Lavaredo 25 Jun, Coppa d'Oro mid/late Jul, Dolomiti Superbike ~10 Jul) all fall **before or inside
   mid-July**; late August has **no headline road-closing race** found for 2027.
9. **Switzerland join breaks.** Switzerland is 27 Jul–10 Aug 2027. Mid-July Dolomites hands off cleanly
   (26 Jul → 27 Jul). Moving Dolomites to 17–30 Aug flips the order and opens a **~7-day gap (10–17 Aug)**,
   or forces a start on 11 Aug — straight into the Ferragosto peak. This is a strong mark against moving.
10. **RECOMMENDATION: STAY WITH MID-JULY (Tue 13 → Mon 26 Jul 2027).** Late August trades a modest,
    mostly cosmetic set of gains (cleaner race calendar, easier alarm, higher freezing level, star
    darkness) for higher cost, worse crowds, tighter availability, shorter days and a broken Switzerland
    join. *If* the family specifically wants the cooler, quieter end of the season, the honest better
    answer is **early September** (out of the "last two weeks of August" scope) — not peak August.

## 2. The concrete window and why these dates

Current plan: **Tue 13 Jul → Mon 26 Jul 2027**, 13 nights, 14 days, VCE in/out, 4 bases
(San Candido 4 / Cortina 4 / Ortisei–Selva 4 / Bolzano 1). `legacy/dolomites-july-2027/PLAN.md`.

**August calendar anchors (verified from `cal`):** 1 Aug = Sun; **Ferragosto = Sun 15 Aug 2027**;
14 Aug = Sat; 17 Aug = Tue; 30 Aug = Mon; 31 Aug = Tue.

| Candidate window | Shape | Ferragosto exposure | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tue 17 Aug → Mon 30 Aug** | 13 nights, mirrors current Tue→Mon | Starts 2 days *after* Ferragosto; front half (17–22) tail-of-peak, back half (23–30) easing | **Best fit — use this** |
| Sat 14 Aug → Fri 27 Aug | 13 nights, Sat start | Buys the Ferragosto weekend itself (worst nights) | Rejected — maximal peak |
| Sat 21 Aug → Fri 3 Sep | 13 nights | Mostly clears the peak | Spills into September — **outside the brief's "last two weeks of August"** |

**Italian holiday-week effect.** Ferragosto (15 Aug) is Italy's summer peak; the **two weeks around it
are the most expensive and crowded of the Dolomite year**, with demand from the domestic exodus on top of
the DACH (German/Austrian) traffic that is already present. The **last week of August eases** as Italian
holidays wind down (schools in Bolzano province reopen ~first week of September; South Tyrol summer break
2027 runs 17 Jun–5 Sep per `timing.md` §2.1). So **17 Aug → 30 Aug** is the least-bad 14-day slice that
still honours "last two weeks of August": it skips the Ferragosto weekend and leans into the easing week.

## 4. Detail by axis

### 4.1 Accommodation price, availability, minimum stay
- **Peak structure.** Ferragosto → ~20 Aug is the most expensive, most crowded fortnight of the summer;
  the last week eases (`timing.md` §1.6, §2.2, confidence: multi-source community consensus, as-of
  2026-08-23). Mid-July is high season but *pre-peak*.
- **Minimum stay.** Dolomite apartments and B&Bs widely impose **7-night Sat–Sat minimums in high summer,
  heaviest in the Ferragosto fortnight**. This directly fights the current **4+4+4+1** base split: the three
  4-night apartment blocks may not be bookable as 4-nighters in peak August. Mid-July is more forgiving but
  can still carry 5–7-night minimums in top properties. **GAP:** not verified per property — no live
  inventory pulled this pass. (Stays lane requires `attrs.min_stay` from the property's own site.)
- **Budget target.** Indicative, **non-live** rate placeholder: ~EUR 1 = CAD 1.47 (CAD 1 ≈ EUR 0.68),
  so **CAD 300 ≈ EUR 204** — *re-date the FX at booking*. Against the directional bands in `logistics.md` §4
  (whole-town 3–4★ doubles, not apartments): San Candido/Dobbiaco **EUR 130–240**, Bolzano **130–250**,
  San Vito di Cadore **120–220**, Ortisei **180–350**, Selva **160–300**, Cortina **250–500+**. A **whole
  apartment with a kitchen for 3** sits near or above the top of these in peak August — **EUR 200 is realistic
  in the Puster Valley / Bolzano / San Vito, tight-to-unachievable in Cortina and Ortisei during Ferragosto
  week**. Mid-July is the easier window for the budget. These are **estimates, not quotes.**

### 4.2 Crowds and parking — Braies, Tre Cime, Seceda, Alpe di Siusi
- Both windows fall **inside every access scheme**, so the *mechanics* are identical; August just adds the
  domestic peak on top, so **booked slots sell out earlier and lots saturate sooner**.
- **Lago di Braies:** car ban **1 Jul–15 Sep, 09:00–16:00**, mandatory prepaid (**EUR 44** incl. EUR 22
  voucher). Both windows are fully restricted — there is **no free-Braies advantage either way** (that was
  the June window's prize, already lost in the July plan). `logistics.md` §3.1.
- **Tre Cime toll road:** mandatory online booking, **EUR 40/car**, 700-space lot; slots sell out — worse in
  August. `logistics.md` §3.3.
- **Alpe di Siusi:** private-car ban **09:00–17:00** during the cable-car season (21 May–1 Nov 2026), P2
  Compatsch reservation mandatory, **09:30 hard cut-off**; road can close before 09:00 at capacity — more
  likely on peak-August weekends. Take the Siusi/Seis cable car. `logistics.md` §3.2.
- **Seceda:** timed-slot cable car, **EUR 5/pp ridge turnstile**, ~08:30–17:30; slots scarcer in August.
  `logistics.md` §3.6. Crowd note: sunrise/sunset ridge is empty by definition (lift hours) in both windows.

### 4.3 Access-restriction and car-free dates (verify Mar 2027)
- **Braies** 1 Jul–15 Sep; **Alpe di Siusi** 23:00–06:00 night barrier + 09:00–17:00 day ban; **Tre Cime**

- **Passo Gardena pilot:** 2026 ran a 150-slot/day restriction **from 1 Sep** (2-month digital test), billed

### 4.7 Weather, freezing level, snow-free routes
- **Afternoon thunderstorms** are a daily convective pattern **through August** as in July; mornings clear,
  storms build ~14:00–17:00. The "start early, off exposed ridges/via ferrata by 14:00" rule holds in both.
  `timing.md` §3.2, `logistics.md` §6.3.
- **Hottest phase ~23 Jul–23 Aug** (Alps-wide; see the Switzerland lane's MeteoSwiss note) — **both windows
  sit in it**, so the higher freezing level is common to both. The specific high routes the mid-July plan
  unlocked — **Croda del Becco, Puez plateau, Piz Boè summit, Alta Via 1 huts, Lagazuoi tunnels** — are
  snow-free in **both** windows; August is at most marginally more certain. No route *gains* by moving.
- **Valley heat:** Bolzano can exceed 32 °C and Venice is hot/humid in **both** windows; many alpine
  apartments have **no air-conditioning** (stays-lane trap). The late-August *last week* eases slightly.

### 4.8 Rifugio opening/booking
- All rifugi in the plan are open in **both** windows (season ~mid-June–late Sept; `timing.md` §1.4).
  No opening-date risk either way (that was the June window's problem). August's only difference is
  **heavier booking demand** — reserve earlier. Locatelli etc. remain cash/bank-cheque, online-form only.

### 4.9 Flights YYZ–VCE (seasonality only — not live fares)

- Seasonality: **Ferragosto/peak-August fares typically match or exceed July**; the **last week of August

### 4.10 Car rental
- Automatics are scarce in Italy; book 3–4 months ahead, especially at VCE (`logistics.md` §2.4). **Peak
  August tightens supply and lifts rates versus mid-July** — directional, not quoted. Everything else
  (IDP, ZTL, tolls, one-way drop fees) is identical between windows.

## 5. Switzerland 2027 join (27 Jul – 10 Aug 2027)

Source: `legacy/switzerland-2027/PLAN.md` ("The join", "Ordering logic"); `research/trips/README.md`.

- **Current state (mid-July Dolomites):** Dolomites ends **Mon 26 Jul**; Switzerland begins **Tue 27 Jul** —
  a **clean same-day handoff**. The lane's recommended mechanics: drop the car in **Bolzano**, go
  **Bolzano→Innsbruck→Zurich on 27 Jul** (~5h30–6h30, saves half a day vs backtracking to Venice), and fly
  **open-jaw: YYZ→VCE out, ZRH→YYZ home** (the only all-nonstop pairing). Lucerne-first, Zermatt-last.
- **If Dolomites moves to 17–30 Aug:** Switzerland (27 Jul–10 Aug) now comes **first**, and the two trips
  **no longer touch**. Two problems:
  1. **A ~7-day gap, 10–17 Aug**, with nowhere to be — either filled (extra cost/nights) or you fly home and
     back (two transatlantic round trips, wasteful and expensive).
  2. **Compressing the gap** (starting Dolomites ~11 Aug off the back of Switzerland) drops you **straight
     into the Ferragosto peak** — the worst crowding/pricing of the year, and against the brief's intent.
- **Open-jaw also reshuffles:** chaining would become **YYZ→ZRH in / VCE→YYZ home** (still valid, Zurich is
  the Swiss nonstop), but you lose the tidy Bolzano→Innsbruck→Zurich transfer logic that made the mid-July
  handoff elegant.
- **Schengen:** fine either way — Italy+Switzerland cumulative ~28 of 90 days; ETIAS/EES caveats unchanged
  (`switzerland-2027/PLAN.md`).
- **Net:** the Switzerland chain is a **real, concrete reason to keep Dolomites in mid-July.** Moving it to
  late August only works if Nitin is willing to **decouple the two trips** and eat the gap or a second
  crossing.

## 6. What changes in the day-by-day plan if moved to 17–30 Aug

- **Wildflowers gone** — any meadow/bloom framing (Alpe di Siusi, Seceda ridge, Prato Piazza) loses its
  colour; swap emphasis to rock/light.
- **Earlier sunsets** push the evening shoots ~40–65 min earlier and shorten after-hike daylight; **earlier
  sunrises** make dawn starts easier. Re-time the `timing.md` §5 alarms ~40 min later.
- **Book earlier and expect 7-night minimums** — the 4+4+4+1 base split is the main casualty; consider a
  **2- or 3-base split** (fewer, longer stays fit peak-August minimums better and cut changeover friction).
- **Passo Gardena 2027 seasonal-closure risk** becomes live for the Ortisei/Selva block — have the
  Sella/Pordoi detour or bus 473 as the default.
- **No road-closing race to dodge** — the 4 Jul Maratona rest-day logic and the Coppa-d'Oro-fills-Cortina
  caution disappear; driving is freer.
- **Parking saturates earlier** at Braies/Tre Cime/Alpe di Siusi — tighten the "parked by 08:00" habit to
  "booked slot + arrive at open."
