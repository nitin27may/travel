### 1.4 Sassolungo "flying coffin" — Forcella del Sassolungo cable car (Passo Sella)
- Source: https://www.valgardena-groeden.com/en/leisure-activities/mountains-and-hiking/the-coffin-lift-in-the-dolomites/
- **2026 dates:** mid-June to early October (2026 season start mid-June). Exact 2027 dates must be confirmed — this lift has the latest opening of the Val Gardena group, so an early-season trip should re-verify.
- **Hours:** 08:15 – 17:00
- **Prices:** one-way **€14 adult**, return **€18 adult**
- **The experience:** cabins hold a **maximum of two people standing upright** — hence "flying coffins". No door-closing mechanism as on a normal gondola; you step into a moving cabin. The lift was slated for modernisation but has not been approved for environmental reasons; the narrow profile is needed to thread rock passages.
- **Journey/destination:** ~4–5 minutes up to the **Rifugio Toni Demetz** at Forcella Sassolungo, **2,695 m**, wedged in the notch between Sassolungo and Sassopiatto.
- **Not suitable for:** anyone with mobility issues, large packs, or vertigo. Dogs/bikes effectively impossible given the cabin format.
- Cross-ref: https://outdoor.valgardena.it/en/point/parking-lot/coffin-lift-forcella-sassolungo/39352526/

### 1.6 Lagazuoi cable car (Passo Falzarego → Rifugio Lagazuoi)
- Sources: https://lagazuoi.it/EN/Information-Prices-and-useful-information-page20-Lagazuoi-cable-car ; https://cortinadampezzo.it/en/news-en/summer-lift-openings/ ; https://www.altabadia.org/en/dolomites/getting-around-in-summer/alta-badia-summer-lifts
- **2026 dates — SOURCES DISAGREE:** lagazuoi.it and altabadia.org say **6 Jun – 18 Oct 2026**; cortinadampezzo.it says **30 May – 18 Oct**. Alta Badia lists hours as **09:00 – 16:40**; Cortina lists 09:00–17:00. Reconcile at booking time. Departures every ~15 minutes; last ascent 16:40, last descent 17:00.
- **Prices:** the official page does not publish a plain tariff table in the fetched version; it advertises **5% discount for credit-card payment**, online tickets with turnstile entry, and 1/3/5/7-day **Cortina Vertical Pass** options. Budget ~€25–30 return (comparable Cortina-area lifts). VERIFY. Parking at Passo Falzarego for the cable car was **free for the whole 2026 summer season**.
- Contact: +39 0436 867301
- **At the top (2,752 m) — this is the single best rainy-day-proof / low-effort culture+view combo in the Dolomites:**
  - **Rifugio Lagazuoi** — famous sun terrace, arguably the most photographed rifugio terrace in the range; also does a **sunrise breakfast** (see Food section)
  - Lagazuoi **Terrace Bar**
  - **WWI open-air museum** — restored Italian and Austrian trenches, posts and the **Lagazuoi tunnels (Galleria del Lagazuoi)**, which you can descend on foot back to the pass (helmet + headtorch + via-ferrata set recommended; the tunnel descent is NOT a casual stroll)
- Dogs permitted with conditions.
- **Technical data** (https://lagazuoi.it/EN/Discover-Services-page41-The-Lagazuoi-cable-car): in service since **February 1964**, rebuilt 1986–87. Reversible aerial tramway **with no pylons**; **2 cabins of 50 passengers**; speed 12 m/s (43.2 km/h); **journey time 2 min 50 sec**; **630 m of vertical**; 1,150 m span; from Passo Falzarego 2,105 m to 2,732 m, just below the 2,778 m summit. Cabin doors open vertically. This is the fastest big-vertical lift in the area — an easy "just go up for an hour" option.
- **Rifugio Lagazuoi extras** (https://www.rifugiolagazuoi.com/en/): ~100 covers, traditional Ladin cuisine with Venetian and South Tyrolean influences, vegetarian options. **A SAUNA at 2,752 m is open to day visitors 10:00–15:00, €30 per person for 60 minutes including towel.** Overnight bookings take a **non-refundable 50% deposit**; card required; cancellation inside 3 days or no-show forfeits. **GAP: the sunrise breakfast price/time/booking is still not published on either official site — call +39 0436 867303 (rifugio) or +39 0436 867301 (cable car ticket office).**

## 2. MUST-SEE SIGHTS AND VIEWPOINTS WITH LITTLE OR NO HIKING

### 2.1 Lago di Braies / Pragser Wildsee — THE ACCESS RULE IS THE WHOLE STORY
- Sources: https://www.prags.bz/en/ ; https://www.suedtirolerland.it/en/highlights/nature-and-landscape/lakes/lake-braies/
- **2026 restriction, verbatim:** *"access to the Pragser Tal valley between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. is only permitted by public transportation, on foot, by bicycle or upon presentation of an online reservation."*
- **Restriction period 2026: 1 July – 15 September.** **26–30 June 2026 was therefore OUTSIDE the restriction.** For a 2027 trip starting 26 June this matters enormously: if the same window applies, **26–30 June 2027 would be unrestricted and 1–9 July 2027 would be restricted.** RE-VERIFY — the restriction period has crept earlier in past years.
- **Outside the restricted hours (before 09:00 / after 16:00) access is free**, but parking is not guaranteed without a booking.
- **Bus:** lines **439 and 442**, online reservation required in peak season. **€13 adult, €9 child (6+), under 6 free.** südtirolmobil Pass holders get 10 reserved seats per trip.
- **Car combination ticket: €44** — includes the transit permit, parking, and a **€22 restaurant voucher** (only if booked in advance; same-day purchase excludes the voucher). **Motorhomes over 2.2 m: €49.**
- Public parking P1–P5 plus private operators. **Disabled visitors with a valid permit pass free at all times.**
- **Hiking and cycling in are unrestricted year-round.** Approach trails from nearby villages run 4.8–12.5 km.
- **Rowing boats:** available in summer **from mid-June** from the wooden boat house on the shore. Prices/hours not published on the fetched pages — VERIFY.
- **Practical:** the lake circuit is ~3.5 km, flat, ~1 hour. Best light is early morning (the classic boathouse shot). Given the 09:00 gate, arriving before 09:00 both dodges the rule and gets the light.

### 2.2 Lago di Misurina, Lago di Antorno, Cadini di Misurina
- **Lago di Misurina** (1,754 m) — roadside lake with the Sorapis/Cadini backdrop; hotels and cafés on the shore; a flat 2.6 km loop. No entry fee; paid lakeside parking. Fully drive-up. This is the classic "zero effort" Dolomite lake.
- **Lago di Antorno** — 1.5 km north of Misurina on the road to the Tre Cime toll gate; smaller, quieter, with a rifugio; free/cheap roadside parking; postcard reflection of the Cadini group.
- **Cadini di Misurina viewpoint** — the famous jagged-spires shot. Reached from the **Rifugio Auronzo** parking (top of the Tre Cime toll road) on trail 117 — roughly **25–35 minutes each way, mostly level with some exposed narrow sections and a short drop-off.** Not a hike in distance terms but it is exposed; not for anyone with vertigo. Sunrise/sunset are the reason people go.
- **Tre Cime toll road (SP49 to Rifugio Auronzo):** a **toll road** with a substantial per-car charge and a large car park at 2,320 m that fills by mid-morning in July. **GAP: the official dreizinnen.com / tre-cime.info pages did not return the toll tariff to the fetcher — VERIFY the 2027 toll, opening dates and hours directly.** The site redirects to https://www.dreizinnen.com/en/ . Historically the toll has been around €30 per car and the road opens roughly late May/June to late October, with an early-morning cutoff once the car park is full.

### 2.3 Lago di Carezza / Karersee
- Source: https://www.suedtirolerland.it/en/highlights/nature-and-landscape/lakes/lake-carezza/
- **"A 1.2 km long circular trail leads in 20 minutes round Lake Carezza, its shores are not accessible."** There is **a small platform at the lakefront** that gives the best view — the shoreline is fenced for conservation, so the postcard shot is taken from a designated boardwalk.
- Reachable by public transport and by car; **large car park on the opposite side of the road** (paid; underpass to the lake).
- The lake's Ladin name means "Rainbow Lake"; the local legend on the tourism board's page is of **a mermaid loved by the wizard Masarè**, whose shattered rainbow gave the lake its colours. (The King Laurin/rose-garden legend belongs to the Rosengarten massif above, not the lake itself — worth getting right.)
- **Best time:** morning, when the Latemar reflects and before tour buses arrive. Total time on site: 30–45 minutes. It is small; do not build a day around it — pair with Passo Costalunga and the Great Dolomites Road.

### 2.4 Lago di Landro / Dürrensee and Lago di Dobbiaco / Toblacher See
- Both sit directly on the **SS51 Alemagna between Dobbiaco/Toblach and Cortina** — pure drive-by stops with roadside parking.
- **Lago di Landro** is the one with the classic head-on view of the **Cristallo massif** and, from the right angle, the Tre Cime peeking through the Val di Rienza gap. Flat lakeside path. Frequently near-dry by late summer — late June/early July is a good time for water level.
- **Lago di Dobbiaco** has a flat 3.5 km circular boardwalk, a lakeside restaurant and boat rental; it is the gateway to the **Val di Landro** and the start of the Dobbiaco–Cortina cycle path on the old railway line.
- Both are free, unrestricted, and can be done in 20 minutes each while driving between Alta Pusteria and Cortina.

### 2.5 Val di Funes churches — Santa Maddalena and San Giovanni in Ranui
- Regional context: https://www.dolomiti.it/en/val-di-funes/ describes Santa Maddalena as "one of the most beautiful mountain villages in South Tyrol"; the small church of **San Giovanni in Ranui** is listed as a place of historical and cultural interest. Tourism board: **Società Cooperativa Turistica Odles, +39 0472 840 180, info@odlesdolomites.com**, https://www.odlesdolomites.com/

- **San Giovanni in Ranui (St. Johann in Ranui):** an onion-domed 1744 baroque chapel standing alone in a meadow. **CRITICAL: it stands on PRIVATE LAND belonging to the Ranuihof farm.** Access to the meadow is controlled and photography is regulated.

### 2.6 The Great Dolomites Road (Strada delle Dolomiti, SS241 + SS48)
- The classic route runs **Bolzano → Val d'Ega → Lago di Carezza → Passo di Costalunga (1,745 m) → Vigo di Fassa → Canazei → Passo Pordoi (2,239 m) → Arabba → Passo Falzarego (2,105 m) → Cortina d'Ampezzo**, roughly 110 km. Built 1895–1909 as the Kaiserstrasse. It strings together, in one drive, Carezza, the Rosengarten and Latemar, the Sella, the Sass Pordoi cable car, Falzarego and the Lagazuoi cable car.
- Allow a **full day, not a transfer** — it is 3 hours of driving without stops and every stop is worth 30–60 minutes. Motorcyclists and cyclists are everywhere in summer; expect slow, tight hairpins and full pass-top car parks by 11:00.
- Sources for the individual pass-top lifts and museums are given elsewhere in this document (Sass Pordoi §1.5, Lagazuoi §1.6, Carezza §2.3, Forte Tre Sassi §4.7).

### 2.7 The Sella Ronda loop by car (Pordoi – Sella – Gardena – Campolongo)
- A ~55 km circuit of four passes around the Sella massif: **Passo Pordoi (2,239 m) – Passo Sella (2,244 m) – Passo Gardena/Grödner Joch (2,121 m) – Passo Campolongo (1,875 m)**, linking Canazei, Selva/Wolkenstein, Corvara and Arabba. Drivable clockwise or anticlockwise in about 2 hours of pure driving; realistically a full half-day with stops.
- Each pass has a lift or a viewpoint at the top: Pordoi → **Sass Pordoi funivia**; Sella → the **Sassolungo "coffin" lift** and the Città dei Sassi/Steinerne Stadt boulder field; Gardena → **Dantercepies** and the Cir towers; Campolongo → Pralongià/Boè access from Corvara.
- **HAZARD: this exact loop is the course of the Sellaronda Bike Day and part of the Maratona dles Dolomites course. On those days the passes are closed to cars.** See section 6 — this is the number-one date conflict to check for the 26 Jun – 9 Jul window.

### 2.8 The individual passes
- **Passo Giau (2,236 m)** — arguably the single best pass-top viewpoint in the Dolomites: Ra Gusela and Nuvolau on one side, Pelmo, Civetta and Marmolada across. Rifugio Giau at the top. Reached from Cortina via a steep 29-hairpin road (SP638) or from Selva di Cadore. Free parking at the top, small and full by mid-morning; sunrise and sunset are the reasons to be there. Zero walking required for the main view.
- **Passo Falzarego (2,105 m)** — junction of the Cortina/Alta Badia roads, base station of the **Lagazuoi cable car** (§1.6), big free car park (free for the whole 2026 summer season per https://lagazuoi.it/EN/Information-Prices-and-useful-information-page105-Parking-lot-at-the-Lagazuoi-cable-car).
- **Passo Valparola (2,192 m)** — 2 km beyond Falzarego toward Alta Badia. Small **Lago di Valparola**, the **Forte Tre Sassi** WWI fort/museum (§4.7), and open WWI positions you can walk into from the road. Much quieter than Falzarego.
- **Passo Pordoi (2,239 m)** — Sass Pordoi funivia, a large car park, the **Ossario del Pordoi** (German WWI/WWII ossuary) on the Sella side, and the Terrazza delle Dolomiti above.
- **Passo Sella (2,244 m)** — the Sassolungo group at arm's length; the "coffin" lift; the **Città dei Sassi** boulder chaos; several rifugi. **Subject to car-free "Dolomites Vives" days in some years — check section 6.**
- **Passo Fedaia (2,057 m)** — the Marmolada dam pass between Canazei and Malga Ciapela. Direct head-on view of the **Marmolada north face and glacier** from the road. Not on most itineraries; combine with the Marmolada cable car at Malga Ciapela below.
- **Lago di Fedaia** — the reservoir at the pass, with the Marmolada glacier directly above. Roadside parking, flat lakeside walking, a couple of rifugi. One of the highest drive-up lakes in the Dolomites and the best free view of the glacier without buying a cable-car ticket.
- **Lago di Alleghe (979 m)** — formed by a landslide in 1771, in the Val Cordevole below the **Civetta** wall. Lakeside promenade, pedalos, a village with cafés. Lower and warmer than the high lakes; a good bad-weather or rest-day stop and a natural pairing with Val di Zoldo.

### 2.9 Prato Piazza / Plätzwiese
- A high grass plateau at ~2,000 m under the **Croda Rossa/Hohe Gaisl**, above Braies, with 360° views and huts (Rifugio Prato Piazza, Malga Vallandro/Dürrenstein hut) and a WWI fort.
- **ACCESS IS RESTRICTED.** The narrow road up from Ponticello/Brückele is closed to private cars during the day in summer (historically roughly **10:00–15:00**, with a shuttle bus running instead and a paid car park at the top). **GAP: the prags.bz Plätzwiese page 404'd to the fetcher and I could not confirm the 2026 dates/hours/fares. VERIFY at https://www.prags.bz/en/ or +the Prags/Braies tourism office.** Treat it as "book/plan like Braies".
- Once up there the walking is essentially flat — one of the best effort-to-view ratios in the Pusteria.

## 3. HIDDEN GEMS

*(Note on method: my WebSearch quota was exhausted early, so this section is built from direct fetches of official tourism-board and operator sites. Reddit and TripAdvisor forums block automated fetching entirely, so the "what locals say" layer the brief asked for is thin. Gaps are marked.)*

### 3.1 Bletterbach — the "Grand Canyon of South Tyrol" (GEOPARC Bletterbach, Aldino/Redagno)
- Source: https://www.bletterbach.info/en/ and https://www.bletterbach.info/en/geoparc/prices/
- **UNESCO World Heritage as part of the Dolomites listing** — and it is the *geological* justification for the whole listing: the gorge cuts down through the **Permian–Triassic boundary**, exposing 40 million years of rock, with **fossils, dinosaur footprints and sandstone formations**.
- **2026 season: 25 April – 31 October.**
  - **Visitor Centre, Aldino/Aldein: daily 09:30–18:00**
  - **GEO Museum, Redagno/Radein: daily 09:30–17:30** (note: *"Due to construction work at the Radein/Redagno site, the ticket office is currently located in a container."*)
- **Guided programmes:**
  - **Daily "adventure/experience" tour departing 10:30 from the Aldein visitor centre** — guided walk through the gorge, **200–250 m of ascent**
  - **Saturdays 14:30 — "Saurier Path" (dinosaur path)** nine-station loop on Permian geology
  - **Special GEO excursions on selected dates in July and August, 09:30 start** — full traverse of the gorge
  - **"Bletterbach for family", Wednesday mornings in July and August, ages 6–10**
- **GAP: ticket prices are not displayed on the site (online booking exists). Helmet: helmets are issued/required for the gorge walk in practice — VERIFY.** info@bletterbach.info / **+39 0471 886 946**. Address **Lerch 40, Aldino**.
- **Curiosity worth knowing:** the site is used by **ESA's PANGAEA programme to train astronauts in field geology for Mars missions.**
- **Verdict:** genuinely off the standard Dolomites circuit (it is south of Bolzano in the Val d'Ega/Oltradige hinterland, ~40 min from Bolzano, 1.5–2 h from Val Gardena/Alta Badia). Excellent for a hot day or a change of register, and completely different scenery from the limestone towers. Best combined with a Bolzano day or the Great Dolomites Road.

### 3.2 Malga Fane / Fane Alm (Valles/Vals, Gitschberg-Jochtal) — "the most beautiful mountain village"
- Source: https://www.gitschberg-jochtal.com/en/almenregion/vals/fane-alm
- A hamlet of **residential huts and haylofts, a small church and three inns** scattered across meadows in the **Pfunders mountains at the head of the Valser Tal**, with **more than 300 cows** grazing in summer; **locally made cheese for sale**.
- **ACCESS (this is the practical bit):** drive to the **Berg am Boden car park** at the end of the Valsertal; **a traffic-light system indicates whether the upper Fane Alm car park has space. When the Fane Alm lot is full, a shuttle service is activated from Berg am Boden.** Berg am Boden closes in winter.
- **On foot in summer:** the **"Milk trail" (Milchsteig)** from the valley, or take the **Jochtal lift to 2,007 m** and connect via the **Rotensteiner high-mountain trail**.
- **GAP: shuttle price and timetable, exact walking time, and hut opening dates not published — VERIFY.**
- **Verdict: strong hidden-gem candidate.** Not on the standard Dolomites circuit at all (Valles is off the Val Pusteria near Rio Pusteria/Mühlbach), photogenic in a completely different way to the rock towers, and the parking system means you can plan around it. Roughly 30–40 min from Bressanone, ~1 h from Val Gardena/Val Badia.

### 3.3 La Val / La Valle and the *viles* of Val Badia
- Source: https://www.altabadia.org/en/dolomites/hiking-village-la-val-dolomites
- **La Val (1,300 m)** is a Ladin village recognised as one of the **European Hiking Villages since 2015** — "meticulously mown meadows, centuries-old **viles** and more recent farmsteads". A *vile* is the traditional Ladin cluster-hamlet: several farms sharing a well, a bread oven and common land. This is the living cultural landscape that the Ladin museums explain.
- **Armentara meadows** — the high plateau above La Val inside the **Fanes-Sennes-Braies nature park**, traditional Ladin farming amid spruce, stone pine and larch, under the amphitheatre of **Sas dla Crusc (Sasso della Croce)** and **Cima Nove**. Dense stone-pine and larch forest, many orchid species, **60+ bird species**.
- **Santa Barbara church** is the village landmark. The Ladin concept the village markets itself on is *"bëgnodü"* — being welcome, being present.
- **Verdict:** the best low-effort *cultural* landscape walk in Alta Badia, and almost empty compared with the lift-served plateaus 10 minutes away. **Late June is peak wildflower and hay-meadow season here.**
- **Longiarù / Lungiarü:** **NOT one of Alta Badia's six villages** (Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa, San Cassiano, Badia, La Val — https://www.altabadia.org/en/alta-badia/villages) — it sits in the **comune of San Martino in Badia**, up a side valley, and is the classic *viles* village (Miscì, Seres, Vi, Alfarëi…). **GAP: the Alta Badia site does not cover it; look to the San Martino in Badia / Museum Ladin side (https://www.museumladin.it/en/) for information.** It is the most intact traditional Ladin settlement pattern in the Dolomites and it is quiet even in July.

### 3.4 Alpe di Fanes / Fanes-Sennes-Braies
- **Rifugio Fanes, 2,060 m**, on the Alpe di Fanes above San Vigilio di Marebbe — founded **1928**, Ladin-heritage interiors, traditional Ladin cooking (canederli, strudel), speck/cheese/Schüttelbrot snacks, playground with farm animals, rooms. **+39 0474 453001 / info@rifugiofanes.com** — https://www.rifugiofanes.com
- Access is from **Pederü** (end of the drivable road up the Val di Marebbe/Rautal): from there either walk up the old military road or take the **jeep shuttle** run by the huts. **GAP: shuttle price/timetable not published — call the rifugio.**
- The Fanes plateau is the mythic heart of Ladin legend (the Kingdom of Fanes, the Marmots) and is **inside the nature park, so it stays quiet**. Cascate di Fanes — see below.
- **Rifugio Scotoni** (§5.3) is also on this plateau's southern edge.

### 3.5 Cascate di Fanes and Cascate di Riva/Reinbach
- **Cascate di Fanes** — a waterfall in the gorge on the Cortina–Alta Badia side near the Armentarola/San Cassiano road; roadside viewing with a short path. **GAP: no official page retrieved.**
- **Cascate di Riva / Reinbachfälle (Campo Tures / Sand in Taufers, Valle Aurina)** — three staged waterfalls linked by a well-built path with the **St. Franziskus chapel** and the **Franziskusweg** stations. **GAP: taufers.com and ahrntal.it URLs 404'd or failed; no official data retrieved. VERIFY.** Note the region also has the **Klimastollen Prettau/Predoi** climate-therapy mine tunnel (confirmed as a Südtirol Jazzfestival venue, §6.4), which is a genuinely unusual rainy-day option.

### 3.6 Lago di Resia / Reschensee bell tower — EVALUATE: PROBABLY SKIP
- Source: https://www.reschensee.com/en/ (reschenpass.it redirects here). **+39 0473 310 600 / info@lago-di-resia.com**
- The **submerged 14th-century bell tower of old Graun/Curon** standing in the reservoir; *"the foundations of the old village rest on the lake bed."* The holiday region sits at the **tripoint of Italy, Austria and Switzerland** and includes **Glorenza/Glurns, "the smallest town in the Alps."**
- **VERDICT: this is NOT a Dolomites side-trip.** Lago di Resia is at the top of the Val Venosta, west of Merano — roughly **2.5–3 hours each way from Val Gardena/Val Badia and 3.5+ from Cortina.** It is a 20-minute photo stop at the end of a 6-hour round trip. **Skip it on a Dolomites-based itinerary.** It only makes sense if approaching or leaving via the Reschen Pass / Landeck / Switzerland, or combined with MMM Juval and Glorenza as a separate two-day loop.

### 3.7 Belluno Dolomites National Park — Lago del Mis and Cadini del Brenton
- Source: https://www.dolomitipark.it/en/ — **Parco Nazionale Dolomiti Bellunesi, Piazzale Zancanaro 1, 32032 Feltre (BL), +39 0439 3328, info@dolomitipark.it**
- The park runs information points, museums, mountain huts and bivouacs, nature/thematic/CAI trails, cycling routes, wildlife watching, plus motorhome and picnic areas; the site has an interactive map and itinerary planner.
- **Cadini del Brenton** are a series of turquoise plunge-pool potholes on the Brenton stream by **Lago del Mis** in the Val del Mis, reached by a very short signed path from the road — one of the genuinely easy, genuinely spectacular, genuinely uncrowded stops in the whole region.
- **GAP: the park site did not return access details, parking, fees or walking times for Lago del Mis/Cadini del Brenton — contact the park.**
- **VERDICT: this is the southern Dolomites, ~1.5–2 h south of Cortina near Belluno/Feltre.** Only worth it as part of an approach/exit day from Venice or a dedicated Val di Zoldo/Belluno loop — not as a day trip from Val Gardena or Alta Badia.

### 3.8 Val Fiscalina / Fischleintal, Val Casies, Lago di Anterselva, Lago di Valdaora
- **Val Fiscalina/Fischleintal** (Sesto/Sexten) — the classic view of the "Meridiana di Sesto" sundial peaks framing a flat green valley floor; a level valley-bottom walk from the Fischleinboden car park to Rifugio Fondovalle/Talschlusshütte, pushchair-friendly. Tourism board (https://www.dreizinnen.com/it) calls it *"one of the most appreciated starting points for excursions in the Dolomites"* with "unspoiled nature" — **but published no parking, shuttle or traffic-restriction details.** **GAP — VERIFY; the Fischleinboden car park is paid and the upper road is closed to cars.**
- **Val Casies / Gsieser Tal, Lago di Anterselva / Antholzer See, Lago di Valdaora** — all in the Val Pusteria side valleys. **GAP: no data retrieved.** Anterselva is a large forest-ringed lake at 1,642 m in the Rieserferner-Ahrn nature park at the foot of the Stalle/Staller Sattel pass into Austria (note: **the Staller Sattel road is one-way alternating on a timed cycle** — verify if crossing). All three are low-drama, low-crowd, family-grade alternatives to the honeypots.
- **Lago di Dobbiaco / Toblacher See** — see §2.4.
