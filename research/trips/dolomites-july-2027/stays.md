## 1. Ten-line summary

1. Party of 3 (2 adults + child 11), whole-home apartment with kitchen + parking, ~CAD 300 (~EUR 191–200)/night, car all 13 nights.
2. The planned days fall in **three geographic clusters** — East (Braies/Tre Cime/Sesto), Central (Ampezzo), West (Val Gardena/Sella) — so three bases is the natural fit; two is possible with longer hike drives.
3. **The decisive constraint is apartment minimum-stay:** formal South-Tyrol *Residences* overwhelmingly require **7 nights Saturday-to-Saturday** in July (confirmed, operator sources). A Tue-arrival/Mon-departure trip matches **no** clean Sat-Sat week.
4. Recommendation: **3 bases — San Candido 4n · San Vito di Cadore 4n · Selva/Santa Cristina (Val Gardena) 5n.** One fewer packing move than the current plan.
5. This **swaps pricey, ZTL-bound Cortina for San Vito di Cadore** (~15 min south): fits the apartment budget, easy free parking, only +~15 min each way on the 4 central-cluster days.
6. It **absorbs the awkward 1-night Bolzano** (no apartment rents for one night) into a 5th Val Gardena night; **Ötzi museum becomes a stop on the departure drive** (Val Gardena→Bolzano→VCE, evening flight).
7. **Runner-up: 2 bases — Alta Pusteria (San Candido/Dobbiaco) 7n · Val Gardena 6n.** One move only, best apartment economics, but the Ampezzo days run 45–60 min each way.
8. **Verified real operator stays with published 2027 prices exist** in San Candido (Appartamento San Candido **€190/night** mid-July; Residence Helga **€231–295**). San Vito, Dobbiaco, Val Gardena options are named and real but prices are enquiry/aggregator-only → marked estimated.
9. Inventory for July 2027 is **already bookable now** (Airbnb ≤12-month host window; several operators show 2027 rates). Dolomite July apartments typically fill by the previous autumn/winter → **book by end Dec 2026, hard stop ~Feb 2027**; Cortina-side even earlier.
10. Two South-Tyrol bases keep the **free Südtirol Guest Pass + Val Gardena Mobil Card**; San Vito (Veneto) has **no guest card** — unavoidable for the central cluster. Confirm the Guest Pass is included at booking.

### Recommendation: **Split B — San Candido 4n · San Vito di Cadore 4n · Selva or Santa Cristina 5n**

Why:
- **One fewer packing move** than the planned 4-base (2 vs 3), and it **removes the 1-night Bolzano stop**, which is the single worst fit for an apartment brief — essentially no whole-home/*Residence* rents for one night in July, so that night would force a hotel and a 4th unpack.
- **Cortina → San Vito di Cadore** is the highest-value swap for this brief: Cortina self-catering in July runs **€220–450+/night** with ZTL and paid/garage parking (aggregator range, *estimated*), while San Vito apartments are materially cheaper with **free/garage parking and no ZTL**, ~10 km / 15 min south. The only cost is +~15 min each way on the 4 central-cluster days (Sorapis, Lagazuoi, Cinque Torri, Monte Piana) — all still ≤45 min.
- **Absorbs Bolzano into a 5th Val Gardena night.** On departure day (Mon 26) the plan already drives west→VCE; routing **Val Gardena → Bolzano (Ötzi, ~50 min) → VCE (~2h40)** still fits an **evening** VCE→YYZ nonstop (dep ~19:10–21:10 per `logistics.md` §1.1). Confirm the 2027 flight time before committing.
- Keeps **two South-Tyrol bases** (San Candido, Val Gardena) = free **Südtirol Guest Pass** + **Val Gardena Mobil Card**. San Vito is Veneto = **no guest card** (unavoidable for the Ampezzo cluster; Cortina is the same).
- Daily-hike coverage stays "very good": a base sits in or beside every cluster.

**Key risk (applies to A, B, C alike): minimum-stay.** Formal South-Tyrol Residences require **7 nights, Saturday-to-Saturday** in July (operator-confirmed: Residence Helga "7 night minimum stay"; Linhea "minimum stay of 7 nights in high season … for short stays of 3 days we charge the high-season rates"). The trip's Tue-13→Mon-26 shape matches **no** clean Sat-Sat week. Practical consequences for Split B:
  - San Candido (Tue 13→Sat 17, 4n) and Selva/S.Cristina (Wed 21→Mon 26, 5n) are **non-weekly** → either book **independent apartments / Airbnb hosts that accept 3–5 nights** (fewer of them, book earliest), or accept paying the **weekly rate for a short stay**.
  - San Vito (Veneto) is generally **more flexible** on changeover than South Tyrol; it is the easiest of the three to fill.

### Runner-up: **Split D — Alta Pusteria (San Candido or Dobbiaco) 7n · Val Gardena 6n**

- **Only one packing move**, best apartment economics, and the **7-night Pusteria block is the closest any option gets to the weekly minimum** (though Tue-Tue, not Sat-Sat, so strict Sat-only residences still refuse it — target residences that enforce only a *minimum number of nights*).
- Trade-off: the four **central-cluster days run 45–60 min each way** from Pusteria (Sorapis ~50, Lagazuoi ~1h, Cinque Torri ~1h, Monte Piana ~30). On the **05:30 Sorapis start** that means leaving ~05:00. Acceptable for a car-based party that values unpacking once; tiring otherwise.
- Pick **E (Dobbiaco 6 / Val Gardena 7)** instead if you want the Val Gardena stay to be the one that reaches a full 7-night block and the shorter final drive to VCE.

**Not recommended:** the current **4-base (A)** — three moves plus a Bolzano night that no apartment will take, and Cortina blows the per-night budget. Keep it only if a dedicated Cortina base and Alta Badia access outweigh cost (they don't for this brief in mid-July, with the Maratona already past).
