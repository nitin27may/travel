## 6. Summary flags for the synthesizer
- Biggest constraint: Algonquin permits are 100% advance booking in fall; Dorset Tower needs a time slot; Sandhill Fall Festival needs advance ticket on Mon 12 Oct; Booth's Rock needs its own permit; Hardwood Lookout closed Oct 9-12.
- Monday Oct 12 is thin for food in Lake of Bays: Boiler Room, Cast Iron, LOB Baysville, Henrietta's, Family Place all closed on Mondays; Mill on Main and On the Docks (Huntsville) and Trading Bay (Dorset) are the likely open ones, but no primary source confirms holiday hours.
- The lodge's own Thanksgiving buffet ($85) is the zero-drive dinner option for Sat/Sun and needs a booking.
- Muskoka Heritage Place and Santa's Village are over the "worthwhile" line for this trip (train season ends Oct 10; Santa's is 70 min).
- Ragged Falls (27 min) and Dorset Tower (25 min) are the two closest scenic stops; Lions Lookout and Huntsville downtown murals are free, rain-independent-ish fillers.
