## 1. ANTELOPE CANYON, PAGE AZ

### 1a. Universal rules (all sections)
- **Guided tour is mandatory.** "All areas of the Lake Powell – Antelope Canyon locations are only accessible via Guided Tour. Tour Guides are mandatory to all Antelope Canyon locations." — https://navajonationparks.org/guided-tour-operators/antelope-canyon-tour-operators/
- **Navajo Nation entry/permit fee: $15 per person, per location, per day**, charged *on top of* the tour price. Same source as above. Confirmed independently by operator Antelope Canyon Navajo Tours: "$85 tour fee + $15 Navajo permit = $100/person total" — https://navajotours.com/tour-packages/
- **Fee increase effective 1 Jan 2026** across all Navajo Tribal Park locations, permits and entry fees — https://navajonationparks.org/guided-tour-operators/monument-valley-tour-operators/ (expect another increase before 2027; GAP: no 2027 fee schedule published).
- **Drones prohibited everywhere on Navajo Nation land** — https://navajonationparks.org/entry-fee/
- **Tripods / monopods / selfie sticks banned since 2019 in Upper, Lower and Canyon X.** "Tripods, monopods, and selfie sticks have been banned since 2019, operators restrict bags and backpacks" and "dedicated photography tours of Antelope Canyon no longer exist" — https://antelopeslotcanyon.tours/guides/antelope-canyon-photography/ (operator-run guide site, not the Nation itself; corroborated by each operator page below).
- **Bag rules vary by operator** — this is the rule most likely to catch you at check-in. Upper (Antelope Canyon Navajo Tours): "For the safety of all guests, bags must be transparent and viewable to our staff" — https://navajotours.com/tour-packages/. Lower (Ken's): "tripods, professional camera equipment, and similar gear are not allowed on any tour" — https://lowerantelope.com/faq/
- **Time-zone trap:** Page/Arizona does not observe DST; the Navajo Nation does. Confirm which clock your slot is on — https://adventurousantelopecanyon.com/ and https://antelopeslotcanyon.tours/guides/antelope-canyon-photography/

### 1b. October light — which section is worth it
- **Light beams form only in Upper Antelope Canyon**, "from late March to mid October, strongest roughly 11am to 1pm, and never on cloudy days." — https://antelopeslotcanyon.tours/guides/antelope-canyon-photography/
- **Implication for a late-Sept / early-Oct 2027 trip:** beams are still in season but at the tail end and weakening. A midday Upper slot is the only way to get one. After ~mid-October the beams are gone entirely and Upper loses its main advantage over Lower/Canyon X.
- Ken's Tours (Lower) states beams "most visible from late spring through mid-fall, typically between 10 AM and 1 PM" — https://lowerantelope.com/faq/
- Canyon X is the low-crowd option; Lower is the sculpted-wall / bounced-light option — https://antelopeslotcanyon.tours/guides/antelope-canyon-photography/

### 1f. SECRET ANTELOPE CANYON — the tripod loophole
**Operator: Horseshoe Bend Tours, on-site ops by Lii' Bikee' Slot Canyon Tours** — https://horseshoebendtours.com/slot-canyon-tour and https://www.horseshoebendtours.com/faq

## 2. HORSESHOE BEND, PAGE AZ

**Official operator: City of Page Parks & Recreation** — https://horseshoebend.com/parking-fees/

| Vehicle type | Fee |
|---|---|
| Passenger vehicle (car, truck, SUV, RV, motorhome) | **$10** |
| Motorcycle | $5 |
| Commercial van (up to 14 passengers) | $35 |
| Mid-size tour bus (15–35 pax) | $70 |
| Full-size bus (35+ pax) | $140 |

- "Fees are based on vehicle capacity, not how many people are in it."
- **Hours: daily sunrise to sunset, 365 days a year including holidays.** "You may stay past sunset to finish your visit and hike out."
- Payment: cash and card. **"During major US holidays (Memorial Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas), the booth may accept cash only."** Bring cash as backup. Note Labor Day falls just before a late-Sept trip start; not an issue for a late-Sept/Oct window.
- **America the Beautiful is NOT accepted.** "The Horseshoe Bend parking fee is collected by the City of Page, not the National Park Service." Confirmed independently by NPS: "They do not accept America the Beautiful or Glen Canyon passes for parking" — https://www.nps.gov/glca/planyourvisit/fees.htm
- Trail: 0.7 miles each way, unshaded. Left branch at the midway split is ADA-accessible with a fenced platform; right branch is the unfenced main overlook, ~1,000 ft drop. ~300 standard spaces. Restrooms at trailhead, no food/visitor center. No overnight parking. Drones banned. — https://slotcanyonguide.com/horseshoe-bend/parking-guide/ (third-party guide)
- **CONFLICT:** slotcanyonguide.com claims RVs/oversized pay $35 in a separate lot; the official City of Page page puts RV/motorhome in the $10 passenger-vehicle band. **Trust horseshoebend.com ($10); the $35 tier there is for commercial vans.**
- No advance booking exists — pay at the booth on arrival. Nothing to reserve.

## 3. ANGELS LANDING PERMIT, ZION NP

Sources: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm | https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675325 (Fall) | https://www.recreation.gov/permits/4675324 (day-before)

### Costs
- **$6 non-refundable application fee**, covers an application for **up to 6 people** including the applicant.
- **$3 per person permit fee**, charged only if you win.
- Example total for four people: $6 + $12 = **$18**.

### Seasonal lottery — 2026 published windows (Zion NP)
| Hike dates | Lottery opens 8 a.m. MT | Closes 11:59 p.m. MT | Permits issued |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 1 – May 31 | February 13 | February 25 | February 26 |
| Jun 1 – Aug 31 | April 1 | April 20 | April 25 |
| **Sep 1 – Nov 30 (our window)** | **July 1** | **July 20** | **July 25** |
| Dec 1 – Feb 28, 2027 | October 1 | October 20 | October 25 |

- **For a Sept/Oct 2027 trip the Fall seasonal lottery should open ~1 July 2027 and close ~20 July 2027, permits issued ~25 July 2027 — INFERRED from the stable 2026 pattern, NOT published. Zion has not posted 2027 lottery dates as of 2026-09-05. Verify in spring 2027.** (GAP)
- You rank **seven** days/times or windows of days and times. You may name one alternate. **Names must match photo ID and cannot be changed after submission.** Submitting multiple applications cancels all of them.

### Day-before lottery
- Opens **12:01 a.m. MT**, closes **3:00 p.m. MT** the day before the hike; permits issued **4:00 p.m. MT** the same day you apply.
- Same $6 + $3/person fee structure.
- **"You cannot change your permit. All fees are non-refundable."** Cancelled day-before permits do **not** return to the pool.
- Cancelled *seasonal* permits automatically roll into the day-before lottery — this is what makes the day-before pool worth playing.

### Refunds / weather
- **Seasonal:** cancel until 2 days before for a full refund of the $3/person fee (the $6 application fee is never refunded). You can also *reduce* group size until 2 days prior and get $3 back per person removed. You cannot increase group size.
- **Day-before:** nothing refundable, ever.
- **No weather-cancellation provision exists.** NPS explicitly says "Even if you have a permit, it may not be advisable for you to hike if the weather is bad." You eat the fee.

### Success rates (community data — no official NPS figure published)
- Fall (Sept–Oct) is one of the two most competitive seasons, roughly **20–30% success**; winter 50–87%; late June/August weekdays 40–50%. Mid-week beats Saturday in both lotteries. An aggregate 2024 study put combined success across both lotteries near 47%. — https://outdoorstatus.com/articles/angels-landing-lottery/ and https://thedwellings.co/2026/04/24/angels-landing-lottery-odds-how-to-actually-get-a-permit/ (**both third-party blogs — treat as indicative, not authoritative. GAP: NPS publishes no current success-rate table.**)

### Other hard facts
- **No minimum age is published** for the permit. (GAP — confirmed absent from both the NPS page and the recreation.gov permit pages.)
- Permits required 24/7, 7 days/week. Valid for a single day; start at the Grotto (Shuttle Stop 6) in your assigned time slot.
- **Hiking without a permit: fines up to $5,000 and/or six months in jail.**
- **The West Rim Trail to Scout Lookout needs no permit** — a genuine fallback if you lose both lotteries; you get the overlook view, just not the chains section.
- Hike: 5.4 mi round trip, 1,488 ft gain, ~4 hours typical.
- **Trail maintenance closure 5–8 October 2026 — no permits issued.** A comparable early-October maintenance window in 2027 is plausible and would land squarely in our trip. **Check before fixing dates.** (GAP: 2027 closure dates unpublished.)
- Park entrance fee is separate; no reservation needed to enter Zion itself.

## 4. KANARRA FALLS PERMIT (Kanarraville, UT)

Sources: https://www.kanarrafalls.com/ | https://kanarrafalls.com/faqs/

- **Cost: $15 per person.** Children 7 and under are free **but still need a ticket**. Listed as "All Ages".
- **Daily cap: "Limit 200 general admission tickets per day"** (homepage booking widget). Plus a separate **commercial carve-out of 20 tickets/day at $25/person including guides**, from 2024, requiring Tax ID + liability insurance. Commercial guiding companies cannot use general-admission slots.
  - **CONFLICT: a third-party source states 150/day** (https://visitcedarcity.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/outdoor-activities/kanarra-falls/ family of results). **The operator's own site says 200. Use 200.**
- **Tickets are released 1 January for the entire year** — so 2027 dates go on sale 1 Jan 2027. This is the single earliest booking action in the whole itinerary and it is cheap to secure.
- **Sell-out: "During peak season (April–October), MOST DAYS sell out a month in advance."** Weekends in spring/summer go several weeks out.
- **Refunds: "Changes are allowed up to 48 hours before the booking date. No Refunds!"** You can rebook or take a gift card. **Inclement-weather tickets can be rebooked with manager approval** — rebooking, not a refund.
- Season/hours: trail open dawn to dusk spring through fall, limited winter hours. **Staff present March through November** — a late-Sept/Oct trip is inside the staffed season. Summer kiosk hours 7am–7pm; a drop box covers off-hours.
- **Children under 18 require adult supervision** due to hazardous terrain and remoteness.
- No pets (service animals leashed). Two boot-washing stations in the parking area. No restrooms or trash beyond the parking area.
- GAP: no separate parking fee documented; no published age/height minimum beyond the supervision rule.
- Contact: 435-590-7490 / manager@kanarrafalls.com

## 5. FIERY FURNACE, ARCHES NP

Sources: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/explore-the-fiery-furnace.htm | https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm | https://www.recreation.gov/ticket/234668/ticket/116

### The decisive fact for this trip
**Ranger-guided Fiery Furnace tours run approximately mid-April through September and "will end for the season in September."** — recreation.gov ticket page + adventuresinmoab.com. **A late-Sept arrival is right at the cutoff and an October arrival misses ranger tours entirely.** The **self-guided permit is year-round**, so that is the realistic option for this itinerary.

### Self-guided permit
- **Booking window: up to 7 days in advance, and you must reserve at least 2 days prior.** Recreation.gov or 1-877-444-6777.
- Season: **year-round**.
- **Minimum age 5. Children under 5 are prohibited.** Maximum group size 6.
- Fees per NPS: **$6 (ages 13+), $3 (ages 5–12), $3 (Senior/Access Pass holders)**.
  - **CONFLICT: a 2026 third-party source states the self-guided permit is now $10/person and the ranger tour $16/person** — https://adventuresinmoab.com/fiery-furnace-permits/ and see also the NPS fee-increase project page https://parkplanning.nps.gov/SEUGpermitfeeincreases. **The nps.gov/thingstodo page was last updated May 2022 and is likely stale. Budget $10/person and verify on recreation.gov at booking.**
- Mandatory orientation film at the visitor center. **"All persons going on the permit must be present"** at pickup.
- Day-use only, sunrise to sunset. **Once you enter you must complete the route — no turning back.**
- **"Permits sell out very quickly during the busy season."**

### Ranger-guided tour (if a late-Sept date lands before the cutoff)
- **Booking window: up to 6 months in advance** per NPS; **the Arches permits page says 7 days in advance**; a third source says morning tours open for the following year on December 1. **These three statements conflict — GAP. Check recreation.gov's Fiery Furnace ticket page directly when 2027 dates load.**
- Fees per NPS: Loop Hike **$16 adult / $8 child**; Out-and-Back **$10 adult / $5 child**. Recreation.gov listing corroborates $16 adult / $8 child & senior.
- **Minimum age 5.** One adult required per child aged 5 or 6. Children 12 and under must be accompanied by a responsible adult.
- Duration ~2.5 hours, rated difficult and strenuous — requires using hands to navigate narrow cracks and ledges. Good hiking shoes mandatory; at least one quart of water per person.
- Physical tickets must be collected in person at the visitor center; same-day pickup **8:00–8:30 a.m. only**; 30–45 min drive to the trailhead.
- **Refunds: "No refunds for late arrivals."** Tours may be cancelled for extreme weather (lightning, flooding).

## 7. MONUMENT VALLEY NAVAJO TRIBAL PARK

Sources: https://navajonationparks.org/tribal-parks/monument-valley/ | https://navajonationparks.org/entry-fee/ | https://navajonationparks.org/guided-tour-operators/monument-valley-tour-operators/

### America the Beautiful — CONFIRMED NOT VALID
- Navajo Nation Parks & Recreation, verbatim: **"WE do NOT Accept the National Park Pass."** — https://navajonationparks.org/entry-fee/
- Reinforced generally: NPS states the America the Beautiful pass does not cover **tribal lands** — https://www.nps.gov/planyourvisit/passes.htm
- Monument Valley is a **Navajo Tribal Park**, not a National Park. Budget the entry fee separately.

### Entry fee — SOURCES CONFLICT, all on the Nation's own site
- `navajonationparks.org/entry-fee/` booking widget: **Entry Fee $10**, "All Ages", duration 8 hrs.
- `navajonationparks.org/entry-fee/` body text: **"$8 per person, per location."** (appears stale)
- `navajonationparks.org/tribal-parks/monument-valley/`: **$10/day per person + $15 per vehicle for the scenic drive.**
- Third-party https://visitmonumentvalley.org/hours-and-fees/ claims **$15/person + $20 vehicle**.
- **GAP: the Navajo Nation's own pages disagree. A fee increase took effect 1 Jan 2026. Budget ~$10–15/person plus a possible ~$15–20 vehicle fee for the loop drive and confirm by phone: (928) 871-6647 or (435) 727-5870.**
- Standard entry allows access for up to 8 hours.
- Entry is per location — Antelope Canyon is charged separately at $15/person/location.

### Hours (official, seasonal — the October change matters)
| Period | Fee booth | Visitor Center / Scenic Drive | Last scenic-drive entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 8 – Apr 30 | 7:00 am – 8:00 pm | 7:00 am – 8:00 pm | 3:30 pm |
| May 1 – Sep 30 | 7:00 am – 7:00 pm | 7:00 am – 7:00 pm | **4:30 pm** |
| **Oct 1 – Jan 31** | 7:00 am – 7:00 pm | **8:00 am – 5:00 pm** | **2:30 pm** |

- **A trip crossing 30 Sept / 1 Oct sees the last loop-drive entry jump forward two hours, from 4:30 pm to 2:30 pm.** This kills a casual late-afternoon self-drive of the loop in October. Plan the loop for the morning.
- Admin office Mon–Fri 8am–5pm year-round, closed 12–1 for lunch.
- Closed: Thanksgiving Day, Navajo Nation Family Day (day after Thanksgiving), Christmas Day, New Year's Day.
- **"CLOSURES MAY BE POSSIBLE, DUE TO INCLEMENT WEATHER."** The unpaved loop closes in flash-flood conditions.

### The 17-mile Valley Drive
- **Self-drive is allowed** but: "the valley drive has very rough terrain and deep sand dunes." **"RV's, Camper van's, motorcycles and Large SUV's or Off-Road Vehicles are Prohibited on the valley drive."** "An SUV or Truck would be preferable due to the rough terrain."
- **This is a real constraint if the trip vehicle is an RV, camper van, or an oversize SUV — you would be forced onto a guided tour.**
- Enter at your own risk. Dogs prohibited at all Navajo Tribal Park locations. **No drones. No rock climbing on the monuments** ("Navajo law will be strictly enforced on this issue"). Cremation/ash scattering prohibited.

### Guided / backcountry tours
- **Anything beyond the 17-mile loop requires a Navajo guide.** Backcountry permits (hiking/camping on designated trails and campgrounds) are obtained at the Visitor Center. Hiking or camping without one incurs removal and additional fees.
- **Wildcat Trail** (1.5 mi) is the one self-guided hike; sign in/out required. Trails open only during park hours 8am–5pm.
- **"There are tour guides available upon arrival at the lower level parking lot"** and "You can purchase Entry Fee and Guided tour upon your arrival" — **same-day guided tours are genuinely available; no advance booking is strictly required.**
- Authorized operator list (phone-only for most — no online booking): Blackwater Tours 435-727-3312; **Sacred Monument Tours (toursacred.com) 435-727-3218**; **Phillips Photography Tours (monumentvalley.com) 928-429-0040**; Explore Monument Valley 928-429-1959; Navajo Spirit Tours 435-444-7773; **RGJ Inc dba Goulding's Tour 435-727-3225**; Navajo Guided Tour Service MV 435-727-3312; Valley View Guided Tours 435-485-0103; Monument Valley Tribal Tours 385-347-6243; Monument Valley Holiday Tour 928-489-1732; Mitten View Tours 928-429-4672; **Monument Valley Hunts Mesa Edition 928-302-8398**; Tinhorn Tour 928-489-9128; MV Dineh Bekeyah Tours 928-409-0088; MV Twin Warriors Tours 928-429-8628; Sandstone Tours 435-200-5450; Monument Valley Rain God Mesa Tours 928-606-9422; Monument Valley Simpson's Trailhandler Tours 1-888-723-6236; Sisco's Tours 520-697-3586; Monument Valley Safari 435-220-0539. — https://navajonationparks.org/guided-tour-operators/monument-valley-tour-operators/
