## Summary
Official NPS pages give itinerary bands by hours, not by days. Only Canyonlands (1h / 2h / half day / full day / multi-day) and Arches (1.5h / 3h / half day / 2h hikes) publish explicit time-boxes; Bryce gives "1-3 hours" and "4+ hours"; Zion and Capitol Reef publish no recommended visit length. Utah.com (state tourism) publishes a 3 day / 2 night Zion itinerary. All "minimum / comfortable / diminishing" day counts in the final table are INFERENCES built from the official hour/distance figures, and are labelled as such. Arches has NO timed entry in 2026; Zion's only hard structure is the shuttle plus the Angels Landing permit past Scout Lookout. Shuttle/permit facts were verified against the 2026 NPS pages.

## Key Findings

### Zion
- FINDING: NPS does not state a suggested visit length for Zion. The Planning Guide gives no 1-day/2-day itinerary; it only says most visitors hike in Zion Canyon, and to check the shuttle.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/planning-guide.htm
- FINDING: Utah.com (Utah Office of Tourism) suggests 3 days / 2 nights: Day 1 arrive + Canyon Overlook (1 h, 1 mi rt = 1.6 km); Day 2 full day of shuttle hikes (Emerald Pools 1.2 mi = 1.9 km, Weeping Rock 0.4 mi = 0.6 km, Riverside Walk 2 mi = 3.2 km); Day 3 Angels Landing (4-5 h, "5 miles rt", permit required).
  SOURCE: https://www.utah.com/locations/national-parks/zion-national-park/visitor-information/itineraries-articles/
- FINDING: Zion Canyon shuttle is mandatory for the Scenic Drive (private vehicles barred in shuttle season). 2026: Spring/Summer schedule May 17 - Oct 24, first bus 7:00 AM from Visitor Center, last departure 6:00 PM from Sept 13-Oct 24 (last return from Temple of Sinawava 7:15 PM). Fall schedule Oct 25 - Nov 28, last departure 5:00 PM. Nine stops, every ~5-10 min. No ticket/permit/reservation needed to ride or enter. So mid/late Sept the canyon day ends at ~7 PM last return; no timed entry.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-shuttle-system.htm
- FINDING: Riverside Walk 2.2 mi (3.5 km) rt, ~1.5 h, 57 ft change, paved (per NPS Zion info guide as surfaced by search; page not directly fetched). Lower Emerald Pool 2 mi (3.2 km) ~1.5 h; Middle Emerald Pools 2.2 mi (3.5 km) ~1.5 h, 150 ft change. NOTE: Utah.com lists Riverside Walk at 2 mi and Emerald Pools 1.2 mi; NPS and Utah.com differ slightly.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/zion-canyon-trail-descriptions.htm (search snippet; individual trail table did not load) and https://www.utah.com/locations/national-parks/zion-national-park/visitor-information/itineraries-articles/
- FINDING: Narrows bottom-up: NO permit for day hike from Temple of Sinawava (shuttle stop 9). Riverside Walk ~1 mi (1.6 km) paved to river entry, then wading. Out-and-back to Big Spring = 9.4 mi (15.1 km) total, up to 4.7 mi (7.6 km) one-way; NPS duration "1-8 hours"; 334 ft (102 m) elevation gain; at least 60% of the hike is wading/walking/sometimes swimming; no bathrooms on the hike; closed-toe shoes, walking stick, drysuit/waders for cold water; park does not rent gear (private outfitters do); Orderville Canyon only up to 1/4 mile without canyoneering permit; flash flood risk requires weather check. Full Big Spring turnaround (15.1 km) EXCEEDS the 12 km ceiling, but you can turn back anywhere (a 6 km rt = 3 km in, roughly Riverside Walk + ~1 mi of river is within ceiling).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/narrowsbottomup.htm
- FINDING: Angels Landing: permit needed for anything past Scout Lookout; NO permit needed to Scout Lookout. NPS: 5.4 mi (8.7 km) rt, 1,488 ft gain, ~4 hours "some take longer"; to Scout Lookout 2.25 mi (3.6 km) with ~1,000 ft climbing (page wording does not say clearly one-way vs round trip; other aggregators quote 3.8-4.4 mi rt, so treat Scout Lookout rt as approx 4.5 mi / 7.2 km, ~2.5-3 h -- unverified on NPS). So Angels Landing is roughly a half day (about 4 h hiking plus shuttle/queue), Scout Lookout roughly 2.5-3 h.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm (Scout Lookout time from https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/hike-to-scout-lookout.htm search snippet; the 2.5-3 h figure appeared in a snippet mixed with AllTrails data, LOW confidence)
- FINDING: Angels Landing permits: seasonal lottery + day-before lottery on recreation.gov. Fall 2026 (Sept 1 - Nov 30 hikes) seasonal lottery ran July 1 - July 20, permits issued July 25 (already past as of today, 2026-09-24). Day-before lottery: apply 12:01 AM - 3 PM MT day before, results at 4 PM MT. Fees: $6 application (up to 6 people, non-refundable); $3/person permit. Lottery entry is not required for Scout Lookout. Child restrictions not stated on the page I fetched (GAP; NPS separately warns about chains/1,000 ft drop-offs).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/angels-landing-hiking-permits.htm
- FINDING: Junior Ranger: self-guided booklet, ages 4+, free at any visitor center or download.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/planning-guide.htm
- FINDING: Crowd advice: fewer visitors on weekdays; arrive early or after 3 p.m.; parking fills early year-round.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/zion/planyourvisit/planning-guide.htm

### Bryce Canyon
- FINDING: NPS suggests "1-3 hours" (Amphitheater and first 3 miles of road) or "4+ hours" (adds Southern Scenic Drive, extended hikes, ranger programs). Four main viewpoints: Bryce, Inspiration, Sunset, Sunrise. Main road is 18 miles (29 km), exceeding 9,000 ft. NPS gives no overnight recommendation; whether a 2nd night is needed is a GAP in official sources (inference in table).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/index.htm
- FINDING: Must-do hike: Queen's/Navajo Combination Loop 2.9 mi (4.6 km), 2-3 h, 625 ft (191 m) gain, moderate; NPS calls it "our most popular hike in the park and the one we most recommend to first-time visitors"; starts Sunrise Point via Queen's Garden, up Navajo Loop, returns by Rim Trail; use Two Bridges side of Navajo Loop in winter when Wall Street closed; up to 30% grades; shuttle available.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/thingstodo/queen-s-navajo-combination-loop.htm
- FINDING: Shuttle 2026: April 3 - Oct 18; May 9 - Sept 20/21 hours 8 AM - 8 PM; from Sept 22 to Oct 18 hours 8 AM - 6 PM (the 6 PM winter-end hours from a search summary of NPS; NPS fetch page said May 9-Sept 20 for extended hours -- 1-day discrepancy). 15 stops from Shuttle Station in Bryce Canyon City through Bryce Point; free all-day parking at Shuttle Station; shuttle does NOT go to Rainbow Point (past Bryce Point on Southern Scenic Drive); the 3-hour Rainbow Bus Tour is not offered. No reservations/timed entry. So mid/late Sept: shuttle ends 6 PM after Sept 21 (relevant for sunset/sunrise timing; own car allowed for sunset points).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/shuttle.htm and https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/hours.htm (hours page seen only in search results)
- FINDING: Junior Ranger: NPS says ask at visitor information desk. Ranger programs: geology talks, rim walks, astronomy, full moon hikes.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/index.htm
- FINDING: Visit Utah's 10-day Mighty 5 route treats Bryce as an overnight stop ("land at Bryce Canyon for the night"), i.e. 1 night in the official state itinerary.
  SOURCE: https://www.visitutah.com/plan-your-trip/recommended-itineraries/the-mighty-5-ultimate-journey (search snippet, page not fetched)

### Capitol Reef
- FINDING: NPS Plan Your Visit page gives NO suggested visit length. It says Fruita can be explored by car or on foot, "half a day or more"; Junior Ranger is listed under For Kids; permits only for canyoneering, climbing, commercial tours, etc.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/index.htm
- FINDING: NPS Trail Guide distances (this table is as printed; the NPS Cassidy and Grand Wash figures are one-way; Hickman as printed 0.9 mi):
  Hickman Bridge 0.9 mi (1.4 km) listed, 400 ft (122 m), moderate (aggregator/Utah.com: 1.8 mi rt, ~1.5 h); Grand Wash 2.2 mi (3.6 km) one-way, 200 ft, easy (out-and-back to the far end ~4.4-4.5 mi = 7.1 km); Cassidy Arch 1.7 mi (2.7 km) one-way, 670 ft (204 m), strenuous, i.e. ~3.4 mi (5.5 km) rt (2-3 h per aggregator); Cohab Canyon 1.7 mi (2.7 km), 440 ft, moderate; Capitol Gorge 1.0 mi (1.6 km), 80 ft, easy. NPS trail guide gives no times. Starting elevation ~5,000 ft (1,524 m) can make "easy" trails harder for visitors from lower elevations. Fifteen day hikes near Fruita, 0.25 mi to 10 mi (0.8-16 km).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/trailguide.htm and https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/hiking.htm (Hickman 1.8 mi rt/1.5 h from https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/capitol-reef/must-see-capitol-reef/great-hikes/hickman-bridge-and-navajo-knobs search snippet)
- FINDING: Fruita orchards: ~1,900 trees; fruit may be picked only when the orchard is posted open; check at visitor center; pay at self-pay stations. NPS/Visit Utah note September is apple season. NPS lists becoming a Junior Ranger as an activity for a "whole day" of Fruita.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/learn/historyculture/orchards.htm and https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/capitol-reef/must-see-capitol-reef/fruita-and-u-pick-orchards (search snippets)
- FINDING: NO official statement of how many days Capitol Reef needs. Adding up the four asked items from NPS numbers plus the aggregator times: Hickman ~1.5 h + Grand Wash ~1-2 h + Cassidy ~2-3 h + Fruita/orchards/Junior Ranger ~2-4 h = about 7-10 h, i.e. 1 full day plus a morning (inference).
  SOURCE: (derived) https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/trailguide.htm

### Arches
- FINDING: 2026: NO timed-entry reservations at Arches; enter any time during operating hours. Reservations still needed for Devils Garden Campground and Fiery Furnace hikes/permits. (This is a change from 2022-2025 -- verify before travel.)
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/index.htm
- FINDING: NPS itineraries. By car: 1.5 h (either The Windows Section OR Delicate Arch Viewpoint, stop at Wolfe Ranch); 3 h (drive whole park road, ~10 min per viewpoint); half day (whole road plus short walks at Windows, Delicate Arch Viewpoint or Balanced Rock). Hiking, 2 h options: Windows loop + Double Arch + Balanced Rock trail; Delicate Arch slickrock hike; Devils Garden to Landscape Arch; Sand Dune/Broken/Tapestry Arch area. Half day options: full Devils Garden to Dark Angel (with primitive return), or Klondike Bluffs/Tower Arch (unpaved road).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/itineraries.htm
- FINDING: Delicate Arch: 3 mi (4.8 km) rt from Wolfe Ranch, +480 ft (146 m), steady uphill slickrock, no shade/water; NPS warns rangers frequently rescue people who underestimated it. From the Delicate Arch Viewpoint lot the combined total is 5 mi (8 km) as printed (page wording ambiguous). Easy alternatives: Lower Viewpoint 100 yd level; Upper Viewpoint 0.5 mi (0.8 km) with stairs. Sunset is busy (hundreds of people).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/delicate-arch.htm
- FINDING: Devils Garden (search-result figures, from NPS-derived text/aggregators; not fetched on NPS): Landscape Arch 1.8 mi (2.9 km) rt, 30-60 min; Double O Arch 4.0 mi (6.4 km) rt, 2-3 h, NPS rates "difficult"; full loop 7.8 mi (12.6 km), just OVER your 12 km ceiling. Windows loop ~1 mi (1.6 km) rt, 30-60 min. Devils Garden is 18 mi (29 km) from the entrance.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/places/devils-garden-trailhead.htm and https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/the-windows.htm (search snippets; treat as MEDIUM)
- FINDING: Junior Ranger: "earn a badge for completing a junior ranger book". Fiery Furnace requires permit or ranger-guided tour (~2 mi, ~3 h, uneven terrain, drop-offs) -- likely unsuitable/optional for a child of 11; check NPS minimum age (GAP).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/index.htm and https://www.nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/fiery-furnace-permit.htm

### Canyonlands - Island in the Sky
- FINDING: NPS Island in the Sky itineraries: 1 hour = drive to Grand View Point; 2 hours = Grand View Point + Green River Overlook + 0.5-mile (0.8 km) loop to Mesa Arch; half day = the above plus mesa-top trails such as Whale Rock or Upheaval Dome, plus ranger programs; full day = multiple mesa trails or long treks like Syncline or Murphy loops (or White Rim Road 4WD, permit); multiple days = campground base/backpacking/White Rim (permit).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/itineraries.htm
- FINDING: NPS ISKY trail table (mi / km / time): Mesa Arch 0.6 / 1.0 / 30 min (easy); White Rim Overlook 1.8 / 2.9 / 1.5 h (easy); Grand View Point 1.8 / 2.9 / 1.5 h (easy); Murphy Point 3.4 / 5.5 / 2 h (easy); Upheaval Dome 0.6 / 1.0 / 30 min (moderate); Whale Rock 0.8 / 1.3 / 1 h (moderate); Aztec Butte 1.4 / 2.3 / 1.5 h; Lathrop to Canyon Rim 5 / 8 / 2-3 h; Neck Spring 5.6 / 9 / 3-4 h; strenuous: Gooseberry Canyon 4.6 / 7.4 / 4-6 h; Syncline 8.1 / 13 / 5-7 h; Murphy loop 10.8 / 17.4 / 5-7 h; Alcove Spring 11.2 / 18 / 6-7 h; Wilhite 11.4 / 18.3 / 6-8 h; Lathrop to White Rim Rd 11.8 / 19 / 5-7 h. Within your 12 km ceiling and child-appropriate: everything easy/moderate above plus Neck Spring (9 km) at the upper end; none of the strenuous ones fit except arguably none.
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/hiking.htm
- FINDING: Junior Ranger booklets free at a visitor center or download online. No timed entry; permits only for backcountry/4WD (White Rim etc.).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/cany/planyourvisit/index.htm

## Caveats / Gaps
- GAP: No official source states a recommended number of DAYS for Zion, Bryce, Capitol Reef, Arches or Canyonlands. NPS uses hours (Bryce, Arches, Canyonlands) or nothing (Zion, Capitol Reef). Utah.com gives 3 days/2 nights for Zion only. Day counts in the table below are the researcher's inference.
- GAP: Several NPS pages (Zion trail table, Arches hiking list, Bryce hiking page, Capitol Reef thingstodo, Zion one-day page) returned 404 or did not render dynamic content; some figures came via search snippets (flagged). Fetch outputs were summarized by a small model; exact figures should be spot-checked on the pages before booking.
- Discrepancy: Scout Lookout distance ambiguous (NPS wording 2.25 mi; aggregators 3.8-4.4 mi rt). Hickman Bridge NPS 0.9 mi vs 1.8 mi rt elsewhere (one-way vs round trip). Trail guide values for Cassidy and Grand Wash are one-way.
- Bryce shuttle end date differs by a day between sources (Sept 20 vs 21); Sept 22+ hours 8 AM-6 PM per search summary. Zion shuttle hours reduce Sept 13.
- Angels Landing fall 2026 seasonal lottery already closed; only the day-before lottery remains (or skip past Scout Lookout). Age limits not verified.
- Arches timed entry: 2026 has none; earlier years had it -- confirm at nps.gov/arch/planyourvisit/conditions.htm before travel.
- Fiery Furnace minimum age not verified.
- Mid/late Sept water level/flash flood conditions for the Narrows are checked daily at Visitor Center; not quantified here (GAP: flow thresholds).
