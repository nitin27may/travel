# Official Docs Research: Utah 5-parks route (LAS -> Springdale -> Bryce -> Torrey -> Moab -> SLC), UT-12 stops, fuel, closures

## Summary
Official (NPS/BLM/UDOT/State Parks) sources confirm: UT-12 is 123 mi and about 3 h Bryce-Torrey without stops, fully paved, peaks near 9,600 ft, and has a no-guardrail Hogback section. Lower Calf Creek Falls is a 6.0 mi round trip on a sandy trail with a $5/vehicle day-use fee. The UDOT SR-12 culvert job (MP 6-7) finished 22 Jun 2026 and I found no other posted 2026-27 UT-12 or UT-24 closures. Burr Trail is paved only to the Capitol Reef boundary, and Hell's Backbone is gravel, so both should stay out of the plan. Official sources give few per-leg drive times (mostly distances), so the leg table mixes sourced distances with aggregator/estimated times, each labelled. Factory Butte pull-out mile marker and Boulder fuel hours could not be verified.

### UT-12 stops
- FINDING: Lower Calf Creek Falls: 6.0 mi (9.7 km) round trip to a 126-ft waterfall; "moderately difficult due to sandy sections"; BLM calls it a good introductory Escalante hike suitable for families; $5.00 per vehicle day-use fee; restrooms at trailhead; can be intensely hot, bring ample water; dogs leashed in campground. Trailhead is at Calf Creek Recreation Area.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/lower-calf-creek-falls-trail
- FINDING: Calf Creek is 11.4 mi south of the UT-12/Burr Trail junction in Boulder and 14.4 mi northeast of Escalante; a paved spur (about 250 yd) leads to the day-use lot. Elevation gain about 505 ft and 2.5-3 h typical (search-result summary of BLM PDF / other sources, not fetched directly). Surface is mostly soft sand, often unshaded, with short steep sections.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/sites/blm.gov/files/uploads/BLMUtahLowerCalfCreekFalls.pdf (surfaced in search; PDF itself not fetched)
- FINDING: Age-11 suitability: BLM labels it family-suitable; effort is about 3 h round trip on sand. Plausible for a fit child of 11 with water and a start before midday heat; this is my inference, not an official age statement. September may still be hot on the exposed canyon floor.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/lower-calf-creek-falls-trail
- FINDING: Calf Creek Recreation Site reopened 27 Sep 2024 after a prior closure; check BLM page before going.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/lower-calf-creek-falls-trail
- FINDING: Escalante Interagency Visitor Center (755 W Main, Escalante) gives road/trail conditions; BLM page states Tue-Sat 9 a.m.-4 p.m., closed Sun-Mon. A search-result snippet claimed 8:00-4:30 daily in summer; the BLM page itself says Tue-Sat 9-4 (page fetched today, so trust it, and phone 435-826-5499 to confirm). Kanab center: Sun/Mon closed; Cannonville center: Mon/Tue closed (summer hours press release, earlier season).
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/escalante-interagency-visitor-center , https://www.blm.gov/press-release/grand-staircase-escalante-national-monument-announces-summer-hours-visitor-centers-0
- FINDING: Anasazi State Park Museum (Boulder): 9 a.m.-5 p.m. daily, closed Thanksgiving/Christmas/New Year; elevation 6,700 ft; plan 1-2 h; six-room replica dwelling and original site; exhibit renovation still a work in progress; day-use fee via Utah State Parks system (amount not stated on page).
  SOURCE: https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/anasazi/
- FINDING: Homer Hogback ("The Hogback", Escalante-Boulder): narrow slickrock ridge, no guardrails or shoulders, steep drops both sides for about 1.5 mi, at about 6,700 ft; road is paved and standard width along its length. Adds no time as it is on the route, but slow driving is prudent. (Wikipedia and tourism sources, non-official.)
  SOURCE: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_Route_12
- FINDING: Burr Trail: paved from Boulder on UT-12 to Capitol Reef's west boundary; inside the park an 8.5-mi graded dirt road with switchbacks (800 ft in 0.5 mi), not suitable for RVs/trailers. NPS current conditions (updated 9/24/26) list "Notom-Bullfrog & Burr Trail: High clearance 2WD". Only the first stretch out of Boulder (Long Canyon, paved) is passenger-car safe; the dirt section is high-clearance per NPS today, so drop it, consistent with your no-dirt rule.
  SOURCE: https://home.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/roads.htm , https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/conditions.htm
- FINDING: Hell's Backbone Road: about 38 mi of gravel (Dixie NF), about 2 h, high-clearance strongly recommended in tourism/USFS-linked summaries; closed by snow in winter. Not paved; drop it. It intersects UT-12 at about mile 83.27 near Boulder.
  SOURCE: https://www.visitutah.com/places-to-go/parks-outdoors/dixie-national-forest/hell-s-backbone-road-and-box-death-hollow , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_Route_12 , https://www.fs.usda.gov/r04/dixie/recreation/hells-backbone-road (surfaced in search; not fetched)
- FINDING: Kiva Koffeehouse: near milepost 73 on UT-12 between Escalante and Boulder, perched over the Escalante River canyon, reached down a dirt driveway (short), open 8:30-4:30 Wed-Mon, closed Tuesday (per Visit Utah snippet). Hours are from a search-result summary; confirm before relying on it, especially on a Tuesday.
  SOURCE: https://www.visitutah.com/articles/kiva-koffeehouse
- FINDING: Escalante Petrified Forest State Park, Kodachrome Basin and Head of the Rocks overlook are other on-route stops (not costed). Head of the Rocks: panoramic overlook near Boulder/Escalante.
  SOURCE: https://www.rubysinn.com/highway-12-utah-road-trip-guide/

### Fuel
- FINDING: Hanksville: Hollow Mountain Gas Station and Stan's Fuel & Foodmart (Hwy 95); hours not listed on the town page; UDOT fleet pump is fleet-card only. A search result also lists 24-hour pay-at-pump at 200 N Hwy 95.
  SOURCE: https://www.hanksvilleutah.gov/fuel
- FINDING: Torrey: Phillips 66 at 675 E Hwy 24 (gas, diesel, groceries). Escalante: Sinclair at 802 E Hwy 12. Wayro/Ruby's summary: services in Tropic, Escalante, Boulder, Torrey, Hanksville, but Escalante and Boulder have limited hours; fill up at Cedar City or Panguitch. Rates 10-20% higher in remote towns.
  SOURCE: https://www.rubysinn.com/highway-12-utah-road-trip-guide/ , https://stations.sinclairoil.com/ut/escalante/802-e-highway-12 , https://blog.wayro.ai/cedar-city-hanksville-utahs-scenic-highway-12-drive
- FINDING: Ruby's Inn guide states "a 40-mile stretch between Torrey and Escalante with zero services". This conflicts with Boulder being a town on that stretch (whether Boulder has a working pump is unverified: GAP). Practical rule: fill in Escalante or Torrey, and at Hanksville for the UT-24/I-70 leg (about 81 mi Torrey-I-70 with only Hanksville between).
  SOURCE: https://www.rubysinn.com/highway-12-utah-road-trip-guide/
- FINDING: Capitol Reef Country services page has no fuel listings (checked).
  SOURCE: https://capitolreefcountry.com/other-services/

### Factory Butte
- FINDING: Factory Butte is 12 mi west of Hanksville on SR-24, with a BLM recreation area (restroom, interpretive kiosks, no fee). It is visible from SR-24 for miles; nearest lodging is Caineville about 7 mi away. Close-up viewing needs unpaved Factory Bench Road (any vehicle "when dry") or Moonscape Overlook via dirt, which you are dropping. Exact paved-highway pull-out mile marker: GAP (not in the BLM page). BLM kiosk/restroom site is on the highway side and is the likely roadside pause; confirm on a map before the trip.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/factory-butte-recreation-area , https://www.americansouthwest.net/utah/factory-butte/index.html

### Closures / construction / weather
- FINDING: UDOT SR-12 box culvert and resurfacing project at MP 6-7 (Garfield County, near Red Canyon, west of Bryce): 17 Jun 2025 - 22 Jun 2026; "completed on June 22, 2026". Delays had been up to 20 min. Relevant only if you approach Bryce via US-89/UT-12 from Zion.
  SOURCE: https://udotinput.utah.gov/sr12boxculvert
- FINDING: UDOT 2026 construction announcement lists no SR-12, SR-24, US-191 or SR-9 projects; it does list I-15 St. George widening (Washington County, summer 2026 to winter 2028, $175M; relevant to LAS -> Springdale, expect slowdowns) and US-6 Spanish Fork Canyon widening (into 2027; relevant if Moab -> SLC uses US-6/US-89 rather than I-70/I-15 via Salina).
  SOURCE: https://connect.udot.utah.gov/2026/03/26/udot-announces-2-8-billion-in-new-and-ongoing-2026-construction/
- FINDING: A search result says a project is replacing the SR-24 bridge over the Fremont River (Wayne/Emery); location/dates not verified (GAP). Live UDOT map is the authority.
  SOURCE: https://udottraffic.utah.gov/map
- FINDING: Capitol Reef NPS conditions page (updated 9/24/26): park fully open, visitor center 8:00-4:30 spring/fall; Capitol Gorge closed; Scenic Drive and Gooseneck 2WD accessible; orchards open (apple/pear harvest $2.00/lb).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/conditions.htm
- FINDING: No rockfall notice for UT-12 found in official sources; "GAP: no 2026-27 UT-12 rockfall/closure notice located". NPS notes UT-12 can close temporarily after winter storms (Visit Utah). Check UDOT 511 before the day.
  SOURCE: https://www.visitutah.com/articles/the-all-american-road-scenic-byway-12 , https://udottraffic.utah.gov/map
- FINDING: Boulder Mountain summit of UT-12 is about 9,600 ft per NPS Capitol Reef; Visit Utah says "approximately 9,000 ft" and Capitol Reef Country says "over 9,000 feet". Use 9,600 ft (NPS).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/directions.htm
- FINDING: September weather (NPS): Capitol Reef avg high 80 F / low 55 F, 0.97 in precip, monsoon season runs July-September, flash flood risk. Bryce (8,000 ft): avg high 70 F / low 42 F, record low 16 F, about 4 thunderstorm days. Boulder Mountain at 9,600 ft will be cooler than either; no official Boulder Mountain September table found (GAP).
  SOURCE: https://www.nps.gov/care/planyourvisit/weather.htm , https://www.nps.gov/brca/planyourvisit/weather.htm
