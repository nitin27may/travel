# Utah & Arizona — 15 days on the Colorado Plateau

**14 nights · ~1,500 mi / 2,400 km · 8 bases · all five Utah national parks**
**YYZ → Las Vegas in, Salt Lake City → YYZ out.** Group: a family with a child of 11 (test age).
Hiking ceiling ~12 km round trip. **Dates open.**

> The 25-day, 17-stop version was dropped on 2026-09-06. This is now the only plan.
> It is **not** that plan truncated — the shape was re-optimised once the Rockies came out.

## What changed when this became the only version

| | Truncated 25-day plan | This plan |
|---|---|---|
| Capitol Reef | 1 night, after Moab | **2 nights, before Moab** |
| Salt Lake City | 2 nights | **1 night** |
| Ends on | Capitol Reef, then a drive | **Moab — Mesa Arch at dawn** |
| Fremont River country | split by a Moab detour | **grouped: badlands → Waterpocket Fold** |

Three deliberate changes:
1. **Capitol Reef moved before Moab.** The Hanksville badlands and the Waterpocket Fold are the
   same landscape, so they belong together — and it saves Arches and Canyonlands for the finish,
   which is stronger. The last full morning is now Mesa Arch at sunrise rather than a transfer.
2. **The second Salt Lake night was moved to Torrey.** SLC is an exit city; two nights there was
   an artifact of cutting a longer trip, not a choice. In Torrey it buys a full Capitol Reef day.
3. **Cost:** Torrey → Moab retraces 145 mi through Hanksville. That is the one stretch driven
   twice, and it is worth it.

## When to go — the window is now wide

Dropping Wyoming removes the early-October closure wall that pinned the long version to
September 2028. **Nothing on this route shuts in early October**, so this plan can move by
weeks or by years. **2027 is back on the table**, which the 25-day version could not manage
around the fixed Iceland dates (4–15 Sep 2027).

- **Best: second half of September.** Utah has cooled from 100–110 °F to roughly 75–85 °F,
  Antelope Canyon is still in beam season, and the Monument Valley loop still takes
  late-afternoon entries.
- **The one hard line is 1 October**, when three things worsen at once: the Monument Valley
  loop's last entry drops **16:30 → 14:30**, Narrows outfitters switch to dry suits as the
  water hits 3–7 °C, and Antelope Canyon's beam season ends.
- **Also workable: mid-May to early June** — though spring runoff can close the Narrows outright
  and Kanarra Falls runs cold and high.
- **Avoid July and August.** Moab at 105 °F, plus monsoon — the one thing that genuinely
  threatens the Hanksville badlands.

## What was dropped, and why it is not a loss

Durango, the Million Dollar Highway, Ouray, Breckenridge, the Tetons and Yellowstone — stops
11–16 of the original reel. They were only ever joined to the Utah half by a **570 mi / 8h30
transit** and a dead night in Wyoming. As their own trip they get proper time and stop being
hostage to Utah's schedule. Two natural successors: **Colorado San Juans from Denver, ~9 days**,
and **Tetons + Yellowstone from Jackson, ~8 days**.

## Assumptions taken

1. **Angels Landing → plan Scout Lookout, but enter the lottery anyway.** Sustained exposure above
   a ~1,000 ft drop. Scout Lookout gives the same view before the chains. $6 + $3/person, applied
   for 1–20 July; decide at the junction on the day. **Still yours to overrule.**
2. **Capitol Reef before Moab** — groups the Fremont River country and ends the trip on Mesa Arch.
3. **Two nights Torrey, one Salt Lake** — the second SLC night was an artifact, not a choice.
4. **Bryce: Queen's Garden out-and-back.** The Navajo Loop has been closed since Feb 2026 and the
   Queen's Garden/Navajo combination is the most-downloaded GPX in the park — it now leads to a
   barrier with a 600 ft climb back out. Re-check at the visitor centre.
5. **One $250 non-resident America the Beautiful pass**, covering holder + all passengers. The
   +$100 non-resident surcharge applies only to those 16+, so the child is exempt regardless.
   Not valid at Monument Valley or Horseshoe Bend.

## Vehicle

**National, "Full Size SUV AWD" (FFDR), Emerald Club, LAS→SLC.** Plain Standard/Midsize/Full Size
SUV classes are published as **2WD** — the AWD must be in the class name, and Emerald Aisle lets
you check the badge before taking the keys. NPS defines "high clearance" as **≥8 inches**.

**No major company permits UT-261.** A Nevada pickup narrows the exclusion — NRS 482.31555 limits
it to damage *directly caused by* the road, and NRS 482.3159 makes that non-waivable — but does
not remove it. The Moki Dugway is a bounded, deliberate risk. **Avis and Budget publish no Nevada
block; do not book them.** Add a Moab Jeep day (~$410) if you want the Shafer Trail covered.

**No booster seat is required for a child of 11** in Nevada, Utah or Arizona.

## Decision rules

**Wet clay, days 8–9.** Check the night before: Goblin Valley COOP gauge `USC00423254` via the
ACIS API for observed 72 h rain (**not KHVE** — null precipitation, record ended 31 Aug 2025),
then NWS zone UTZ130, then BLM Henry Mountains **(435) 542-3461** or the 24/7 Capitol Reef line
**(435) 425-3791 press 4**. BLM's wording is *"impassable for days"*. **If wet: skip the
Hanksville dirt, go straight to Torrey, and gain a half day at Capitol Reef.**

**Flash flood, day 3.** The Narrows closes on flow rate and forecast, not on local rain. If the
rating reads "Expected" or above, substitute the Watchman Trail and move Canyon Overlook here.
