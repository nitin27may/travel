# Research: Trails (agent output, Aug 23 2026)

## Stowe Pinnacle
- ~3.3-3.6 mi RT, ~1,450-1,520 ft gain, moderate (steep rocky upper third), 2-3 hrs. Summit 2,651 ft, CC Putnam State Forest.
- Trailhead: Upper Hollow Rd (via School St -> Stowe Hollow Rd), lot ~9 cars, FREE, fills before 9 AM even weekdays in foliage. Overflow: Pinnacle Meadows lot off Pinnacle Rd (easier alt route, 2.6 mi RT / ~1,062 ft).
- Blue-blazed; 0.5 mi flat then steep stair-like climb; light scrambling near top; note the fork ~1 mi up for descent.
- Payoff: open rock knob, Mansfield NW, Camel's Hump, Worcester Range, Sterling Range.
- Links: https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/stowe-pinnacle | https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/stowe-pinnacle-trail | https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/stowe-pinnacle | https://vermontexplored.com/stowe-pinnacle-trail-vt/
- No GMC closures/alerts (Aug 2026).

## Bingham Falls
- ~0.5 mi RT, ~100-150 ft DOWN to falls (climb out), 20-40 min. 25-ft plunge into gorge, cascades, pools.
- Trailhead: VT-108 ~6.4 mi N of village, dirt pull-offs both sides (~15 cars), free, fills midday. Alt: Mill Trail from Notchbrook Rd pull-off (gentler).
- WARNING: steep rock staircases, wet + slippery, worse with leaf-fall. No closures.
- Links: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/bingham-falls-trail | https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/bingham-falls | https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/mill-trail-to-bingham-falls

## Sterling Pond
- 2.2-2.5 mi RT, ~940-1,000 ft gain in ~1.1 mi — Trail Finder rates DIFFICULT; eroded rock staircase/ledge, slippery, muddy after rain. Optional 1.4 mi pond loop. Highest trout pond in VT (~3,000 ft).
- Trailhead: east side of the info booth at the TOP of Smugglers' Notch (VT-108, ~9.7 mi N of village). Tiny lot, most congested spot in the area during foliage — dawn or skip.
- ALERT: active bear situation around Sterling Pond shelter (Trail Finder) — no food out.
- Links: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/sterling-pond-trail | https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/smugglers-notch-mount-mansfield-east | https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/sterling-pond

## Moss Glen Falls (STOWE)
- 0.5 mi RT easy to falls; 100-125 ft cascade in mossy gorge, one of VT's tallest. C.C. Putnam SF.
- Trailhead: VT-100 N ~3 mi -> right on Randolph Rd -> FIRST right on Moss Glen Falls Rd -> gravel lot (~8-10 cars, free) 0.5 mi on left.
- Meadow + boardwalk, then fork: LEFT = short steep rooty climb to gorge-rim overlook — rim edge is eroded/hazardous, stay back. Rock steps slippery when wet. No closures. No weekday-construction issue found.
- Links: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/moss-glen-falls-2 | https://gostowe.com/things-to-do/outdoor-activities/moss-glen-falls | https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/moss-glen-falls
- DO NOT CONFUSE with Moss Glen Falls GRANVILLE: 35-ft roadside falls ON VT-100 in Granville Gulf, paved pull-off + 0.1 mi accessible boardwalk, 10-min stop; only on Route 100 backup day.

## Mount Pisgah (South Trail, Willoughby)
- South Trail out-and-back: ~4.4 mi RT, ~1,650 ft, Hard, 2.5-3.5 hrs. Summit 2,751 ft is FORESTED, NO VIEW.
- Views: Pulpit Rock spur ~0.9-1.0 mi up (classic perch ~650 ft over the lake); THE panorama is 0.3-0.4 mi PAST the summit at the North/West overlooks — continue past the first North Overlook to a second for the full-length lake view. Return same way (or car-spot North trailhead for 4.5-4.9 mi traverse).
- Trailhead: VT-5A just S of the lake, lots both sides of the road, free. High-use; ledges cold/windy/slick when wet; muddy start near beaver-pond boardwalk.
- Peregrine closures: statewide season Mar 15-Aug 1, all lifted by Aug; Pisgah NOT on 2026 closure list. Zero impact Oct 1-4.
- Links: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/us/vermont/mount-pisgah-trail | https://www.trailfinder.info/trails/trail/willoughby-state-forest-mount-pisgah | https://fpr.vermont.gov/willoughby-state-forest | https://adventuresofaplusk.com/hiking-to-mount-pisgah-vermont/
