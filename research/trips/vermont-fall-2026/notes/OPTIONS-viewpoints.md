### The Elmore gotcha
**Elmore State Park day use does not open until 10:00 AM.** This whole plan runs on pre-7 AM starts
because the lots are tiny. Mount Elmore cannot be a dawn hike — you would be sitting at a closed
gate. It only works as a **late-morning start**, which means it needs its own half-day, not a
bolt-on to an existing one. That is the reason it stays in reserve rather than being scheduled.

## 4. The overload problem, stated plainly

As written, the plan does **Stowe Pinnacle (1,500 ft) on Friday and Mount Pisgah (1,650 ft) on
Saturday** — 3,150 ft of climbing on back-to-back days with pre-7 AM starts, immediately after an
8.75-hour drive on Thursday. That is the thing to fix before anything gets added. **No third big
hike goes in.** Everything below trades hiking down, not up.

## 5. Where driving genuinely beats hiking — the two swaps that matter

### Swap A (strongest): Pisgah → Pulpit Rock only, then drive up Burke
The plan already concedes Pisgah's summit has no view and the good ledges are 0.3–0.4 mi *past* it.
So take the one thing Pisgah uniquely has — **Pulpit Rock, the ledge 650 ft directly above the
lake** — and stop there.

| | Effort | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Pisgah full *(current)* | 4.4 mi / 1,650 ft / 3 h 45 | Pulpit Rock + two overlooks |
| **Pulpit Rock + Burke toll road** | **~2.2 mi / 900 ft / 1 h 30, then $5 and a 10-min walk** | **The vertigo shot AND a 360° fire tower looking back at the same cliffs** |

Saves ~2 hours, removes the hardest hour of the trip, and adds a view the plan does not currently
have. Trade-off honestly: you skip the North/West overlooks. Burke's panorama is wider; Pisgah's
is more dramatic. This gets you both kinds.

Saturday would run: Pulpit Rock 7:35–9:15 → Willoughby South Beach picnic → **Burke toll road
10:30–11:45** → Craftsbury ~13:15 → **Barr Hill ~14:15** → home ~15:45. Less driving pressure than
the current version, more views, half the climbing.

### Swap B: if the weather is poor, drop the hike entirely
Wet rock is the plan's stated injury risk. On a wet Saturday: **Burke toll road + Willoughby South
Beach + Craftsbury + Barr Hill.** Four viewpoints, ~20 minutes of total walking, and Burke's tower
is often above the valley murk. This is a genuinely good day, not a consolation.

## 6. Making the 1 h 15 drive to the gaps pay

The gaps only justify themselves if the day is loaded properly. Route 100 south passes **Waterbury**
both ways, so the gaps day should absorb the Sunday stops:

**Two Gaps day, combined version** — Waitsfield (Great Eddy Bridge) → Warren village + Warren Falls
→ Lincoln Gap up the paved side → **Sunset Ledge, the only hike, 1 h 15** → back down → App Gap
overlook at golden hour → **Cold Hollow Cider Mill + Cabot Creamery on the way home through
Waterbury**.

That is six things on one road, one easy hike, and it **frees Sunday for an early border crossing** —
which the plan already flags as the leaf-season queue risk. The gaps day pays for itself twice.

**If nobody wants to hike that day: cut Lincoln Gap.** Its top is wooded and there is no view from
the car; without Sunset Ledge there is no reason to drive it. App Gap alone still works.
