# Iceland — when to go, and why early September wins

## The finding that set the dates

I computed sunrise, sunset and both twilight thresholds for eight points on the route using the
NOAA solar algorithm (`sun-times.py`, `dark-times.py` in this folder — reproducible, no API).
Iceland is **UTC+0 year-round**, so no DST correction applies.

**Iceland gets no astronomical darkness at all until roughly 3 September.**

| Date | Reykjavík — astronomical dark (sun below −18°) | Mývatn |
|---|---|---|
| 15 Jul | none — no civil twilight end either, light all night | none |
| 28 Aug | **none** | **none** |
| 1 Sep | **none** | **none** |
| 4 Sep | 21:53 – 23:10 (**1.3 h**) | none |
| 8 Sep | 21:04 – 23:57 (2.9 h) | 22:11 – 23:30 (1.3 h) |
| 12 Sep | 20:32 – 00:26 (3.9 h) | 21:19 – 00:18 (3.0 h) |
| 15 Sep | 20:12 – 00:43 (**4.5 h**) | 20:54 – 00:41 (3.8 h) |

So the aurora argument pushes **later** into September. Meanwhile three things push **earlier**:

1. **Campsites close.** ~57 sites open in May and 26 more in June; then **50 shut during September
   and another 22 in October**. Only about **23 campsites publish an all-year season** — roughly one
   in five of those that publish at all.
2. **The Iceland Camping Card expires 15 September**, every year. That is a hard edge.
3. **Weather degrades.** Rain on 18–22 days of the month, gusts routinely over 50 km/h in the south
   and south-east, and F-roads start closing mid-month on snow and wind.

**The two curves cross at roughly 8–15 September.** That is the window.

## Chosen window

**Saturday 4 – Wednesday 15 September 2027.** Eleven nights.
The first half of the trip is the south coast, where darkness doesn't matter. By the time the route
reaches Mývatn and the north (nights 7–9, 10–12 September) there is 1.3 → 3.0 hours of true
astronomical dark per night. The last camping night is 14 September — one day inside the Camping
Card's validity.

**Window B (in the page behind the A/B toggle): 11–22 September 2027.** Same route, one week later.
Buys 4.5+ hours of darkness and emptier roads; costs the Camping Card entirely, exposes the tail of
the trip to September campsite closures, and raises the odds of a wet, windy week.

## What early September costs you

**Puffins.** They leave Iceland from about mid-August. From 15 August operators stop selling combined
whale-and-puffin trips and run whale watching only. Dyrhólaey and Látrabjarg will have empty cliffs.
This is a real loss and it is not recoverable inside this window — puffins and aurora do not overlap.

## Weather to expect

- **5–12 °C**; daytime highs 10–13 °C, nights 4–7 °C. Above freezing nearly everywhere.
- **Rain on 18–22 days of the month**, but usually as passing showers rather than all-day rain.
- **Wind is the hazard, not cold.** Gusts over 50 km/h are normal; the south and south-east are worst.
- Roads are not icy in September. Route 1, the south coast, Snæfellsnes and the Golden Circle all
  stay open. Mountain snow appears late in the month.
