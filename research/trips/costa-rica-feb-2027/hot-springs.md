# Research lane: Hot springs and thermal/wellness (Costa Rica, Feb 2027)

Raw data, compiled 2026-10-06. Prices are USD, mostly pre-tax unless noted (CR tax 13%). Sources are web-search summaries and a few page fetches; no official site was fetched directly (GAP: confirm every price on the operator's own site before booking). Feb 2027 is dry/high season; Tabacon's peak window ends Jan 8, 2027 so Feb uses non-peak 2027 rates (see below).

## 0. Alec's Tabacon view (requested page)
- Author calls Tabacon a honeymoon splurge: 25+ pools, "fairy-tale" grounds; day pass plus dinner for two about $360; dinner "very yummy" but not fancy; water was cooler than ideal; some pools have dirt/rock bottoms, wear water shoes; ~30 min from downtown La Fortuna; Baldi mentioned as budget alternative at "only $80 per visit". Source: https://explorewithalec.com/things-to-do-in-costa-rica/
- Verification: $360 for two with dinner is stale/low. Current non-peak is $184 adult day pass + dinner (so ~$368 pre-tax for two) and Alec's figure roughly matches only if pre-tax; add 13% tax = ~$416 (GAP: confirm whether Tabacon quotes include tax). Source: https://sometimeshome.com/guide-for-tabacon-hot-springs-day-passes-in-costa-rica/ and https://www.fortunawelcome.com/la-fortuna-hot-springs/tabacon-costa-rica/ (search snippet).
- Baldi "$80" is the day pass with meal on some resellers; direct entrance is $40 and with meal $58 (see Baldi).

## 1. ARENAL / LA FORTUNA

### Tabacon Thermal Resort and Spa (natural, geothermal)
- Day pass non-peak 2026: $115 adult / $55 child; with meal $184 adult / $90 child. After Jan 8, 2027 adult day pass rises to $129 (so Feb 2027 = ~$129 adult; meal-inclusive price GAP). Christmas peak Dec 18 2026-Jan 8 2027: $175 adult / $75 child; $254 / $120 with culinary. Sources: https://sometimeshome.com/guide-for-tabacon-hot-springs-day-passes-in-costa-rica/ ; search result summary citing https://soakdestinations.com/tabacon-hot-springs/ and https://www.fortunawelcome.com/la-fortuna-hot-springs/tabacon-costa-rica/
- Hours 10:00-22:00 for day pass. Source: https://sometimeshome.com/guide-for-tabacon-hot-springs-day-passes-in-costa-rica/
- Includes: all thermal pools (22+ springs cited), set-menu lunch or dinner at Ave Restaurant (1 starter + 1 entree, drinks with meal) on meal passes, parking free; locker/towel with $50 refundable deposit; no outside food/drink. Same source.
- Temperatures: 72-105 F (22-41 C) across pools/river (same source). Toorizta says 5 thermal pools plus cold plunge (conflicts; GAP): https://toorizta.com/arenal-hot-springs-guide/
- Natural: free-flowing (non-pumped) hot river with built pools. Source: https://costaricatravelblog.com/best-la-fortuna-hot-springs/
- Reservations: recommended; daily visitor cap; Toorizta claims "essential", sells out 2-3 weeks ahead (peak). Cancellation 15 days (90 in peak). Sources above.
- Night: day pass open to 22:00, evening is described as best for ambiance (toorizta). Volcano glow: GAP (Arenal has not been visibly erupting/glowing since 2010; do not promise lava glow; stars only). Source for Arenal quiet: GAP.
- Hotel guests: free springs access, guests-only hours 08:00-10:00 (before day pass opens), and adults-only Shangri-La Gardens (18+, guests only). Source: search summary citing https://sometimeshome.com/... and https://www.entercostarica.com/hotels/arenal-hotels/tabacon-thermal-resort-and-spa
- Kids: allowed, free age 5 and under, under 16 need supervision; noise rules strict. Same sources.
- Crowd: GAP for hourly pattern. Typical inference (unverified): tour-bus arrivals mid-day; guests-only 8-10 and post-18:00 quietest.
- Spa: treatments $140-$239 (source: sometimeshome). Massage floor price GAP.
- Room rates: ~ $450-750/night double (search summary, https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g17484635-d293017-Reviews-Tabacon_Thermal_Resort_Spa-La_Fortuna_La_Fortuna_de_San_Carlos_Arenal_Volcano_Nationa.html); kayak/momondo "from $383", non-pool rooms ~ $500: https://www.momondo.com/hotels/la-fortuna/Tabacon-Thermal-Resort-Spa.mhd187137.ksp . Feb 2027 rate GAP. 14 adults-only suites with private plunge pools: https://www.americanindustrialmagazine.com/blogs/travel/tabacon-thermal-resort-spa-unveils-14-adults-only-rooms-and-suites-and-a-new-suite-category-with-private-plunge-pools

### The Springs Resort and Spa (natural geothermal, volcano views)
- Day pass: $125 + 13% = ~$141.25 pp most of year; $124.30 pp from Aug 17-Nov 20, 2026 and May 1-22, 2026. Valid 2 consecutive days; includes parking, lockers, towels; food/drink/tips not included. Feb 2027 price GAP (likely ~$141). Sources: https://mytanfeet.com/special-deals/club-rio-discount-costa-rica/ ; https://www.daypasscr.com/the-springs-resort-spa-day-pass-arenal-costa-rica/ ; https://www.thecostaricatoursite.com/arenal/tours/The-Springs-Resort-and-Spa-at-Arenal.php
- Hours 08:00-22:00 (same). ~18-25 pools cascading downhill, waterslides, multiple restaurants, sushi; best volcano views; day passes limited, book ahead (https://toorizta.com/arenal-hot-springs-guide/ , https://costaricatravelblog.com/best-la-fortuna-hot-springs/).
- Temperatures by pool: GAP. Kid policy: family-friendly with adult areas (toorizta); adults-only pool GAP.
- Spa massages from $80/45 min: search summary citing https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/arenal-springs-resort-spa-review/ (note: that page is "Arenal Springs", a different hotel that does not sell day passes; verify the $80 applies to The Springs, GAP).

### Baldi Hot Springs
- Entrance only $40 adult; $29 child 6-10; under 6 free. With lunch/dinner buffet $58 adult / $41 child; +13% tax. Hours 09:00-22:00; no time slots; kids' area and slides close 18:00; locker $6, towel $10 refundable. Sources: https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/baldi-hot-springs/ ; https://www.twoweeksincostarica.com/product/baldi-hot-springs-entrance/ ; https://soakdestinations.com/baldi-hot-springs/
- ~25 pools, swim-up bars, slides, kids' zone; busiest, can feel crowded in high season (toorizta). Reservations advisable in peak. Natural geothermal: GAP (pumped/mixed, GAP). Adult-only: none found. Spa: GAP.

### Paradise Hot Springs

## 4. Stay vs day pass economics (Arenal, 2 adults, Feb 2027, pre-tax)
- Tabacon day pass + dinner: 2 x $184 = $368 (non-peak 2026; Feb 2027 likely higher: day pass $129). Stay: ~$450-750/night (GAP Feb) includes unlimited springs, guests-only 08:00-10:00 and Shangri-La. One night stay ~$500+ buys springs for ~24 h, so stay premium over pass is ~$130-$380 for a night of lodging in the resort. Sources above.
- The Springs: 2-day pass ~ $141 pp = $282 for 2 days; room from ~$430/night (https://www.momondo.com/hotels/la-fortuna/The-Springs-Resort-and-Spa-at-Arenal.mhd193939.ksp).
- Mid/budget: Baldi with meal $116 for two; Paradise $162 with meal; Titoku/Ecotermales ~ $70-$118 for two; Orosi $90 for two.
- Rio Negro free for Guachipelin hotel guests; Titoku free for Arenal Kioro guests (sources above). Priority access for guests of other resorts: GAP.

## 5. Ranking (derived from above; inference, not sourced)
Quality-over-cost top 4 for a couple:
1. Tabacon (stay if budget allows for Shangri-La and 08:00-10:00 access; otherwise weekday evening pass with dinner).
2. The Springs (2-day pass, volcano views).
3. Arenal Ecotermales evening slot (capped, quiet; 17:00-21:30).
4. Hacienda Guachipelin Rio Negro (natural mud and river setting) or Borinquen (GAP details).
Best budget pick: Titoku (~$35, intimate, capped at 80) or Paradise ($47); Baldi if kids and slides; Rio Chollin for free but rough.
