## 1. RINCON DE LA VIEJA (2 nights)

### 1.1 Hacienda Guachipelin (owns Rio Negro springs)
- Own site: https://www.haciendaguachipelinhotel.com/ . Family Room 516 sq ft: 1 King + 1 Double + 1 Twin, garden view, deck/patio. Also Superior Suite 2 Kings (473 sq ft; rollaways on request); Honeymoon Suite with private pool.
- Breakfast included (buffet); free hot springs access; outdoor pool "weather permitting"; spa/sauna; children's playground; free self-parking; no elevators.
- A/C: aggregator says all rooms have A/C (https://www.tripadvisor.com/HotelsList-Rincon_de_La_Vieja-Hotels-With-Air-Conditioning-zfp17158878.html via search summary); Pura Vida Moms lists A/C as standard in the standard room (https://puravidamoms.com/hacienda-guachipelin-costa-rica/). Own-site A/C line: GAP.
- Rate: FROM $153 (https://www.vacationscostarica.com/rincon-de-la-vieja-volcano/stay/hacienda-guachipelin/); aggregators $119-206. Family Room rate: GAP. Feb quote: GAP.
- Distance: 35 min from Liberia airport (vacationscostarica link). Adventure park on site (zip, canyoning, tubing, bike, horses). A mother reported tubing as safe with 10- and 12-year-olds (Pura Vida Moms link); official age limits for zip/canyon: GAP (not stated on pages fetched).

Rio Negro public price 2026: adult (13+) $35, child (4-12) $30, under 4 free; 10 pools, ~1.5 h slots at 9:00-16:00 hourly; 350 m walk through forest; suspension bridges; mud. Source: https://www.guachipelin.com/relaxation/rio-negro-hot-springs-mud-baths/ . Night access: GAP (none found; slots end 16:00).

### 2.4 Night soak with a child (what the sources allow)
1. Tabacon day pass, open to 22:00; arrive about 15:00-16:00, eat (set menu at Ave), soak after sunset. Child pays $55 ($90 with meal). Guests of the hotel can soak until 22:00 (own site). Evening has fewer visitors (inference; GAP crowd data).
2. Ecotermales evening session 17:00-22:00 (child $25 entry-only; $39 with dinner) is capped and quieter; "Moon Thermal Experience" 20:15-22:45 includes a cocktail (adult-oriented; child eligibility GAP).
3. Baldi to 22:00, but kids' area/slides close at 18:00; after that pools are open to everyone.
4. Kioro's Titoku closes at 18:00 for guests, so Kioro's night soak is the on-site 7 pools (night hours: GAP).
5. Arenal is not erupting; there is no lava glow (research-hot-springs.md GAP; Tabacon says "deep in the rainforest" only).
- Staying overnight at Tabacon or Springs removes the post-soak drive on dark roads (darkness about 17:45; research-stays.md). Own-car night drive to Ecotermales is about 9 min from town (arenal.net).

### 2.5 Which Arenal base for 3 nights? (derived from the data above; recommendation = inference)
- Volcano side, Observatory Lodge: best for nature/trails/birding and cooler air, but no A/C, no springs, 16 km (about 30 min) from every other activity, and most hot springs close the child's evening before you could drive back. 10-year-old is chargeable. Fine for 1 night or a day visit ($22/$40), poor for 3.
- Near town (belt on the Tabacon/Arenal NP road, 10 min from town): springs on site, A/C, quiet, tours 10-25 min away, restaurants in town; best fit for 3 nights.
- Lake Arenal (El Castillo / Nuevo Arenal): quiet views and cheaper, but 45 min / $20 taxi each way to La Fortuna, no springs, fans in cabins. Better as a one-night transfer toward Monteverde than a 3-night base.
- RECOMMENDED: near town / volcano side belt, specifically Arenal Kioro (2 Queens + day bed, A/C, breakfast, 7 on-site pools plus Titoku free, in-room hot tub, free parking, $351+). Fallback: Arenal Springs (Master Suite with 2 bedrooms, kids' pool, guests-only springs, $355-396+). Use the Tabacon day pass (child $55) for one evening instead of paying $700+ to sleep there. Optional: day visit to Observatory Lodge ($22/$40) for trails.
