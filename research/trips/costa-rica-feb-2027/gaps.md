## 5. Entry form and rental-car CDW

- Entry form: no health or e-form is required for tourists. Canada's advisory (updated 31 Aug 2026, new chikungunya health notice) lists no health form; only yellow-fever proof if arriving from an at-risk country. https://travel.gc.ca/locations/costa-rica . visitcostarica.com lists passport, USD 100 per month of funds, onward ticket; no digital form. https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/entry-requirements . Costa Rica repealed the Covid Health Pass on 1 Apr 2022 (CRDA / ICT, snippets): https://costaricadreamadventures.com/travel-requirements-costa-rica/ . GAP: migracion.go.cr (immigration authority) and salud.go.cr not read.
- Canadian card CDW, hard facts: Scotiabank ScotiaGold Visa certificate says "in some countries (e.g. Australia, New Zealand, Costa Rica, and the states of New York and Illinois) the law requires" the rental company's coverage in the price, and the card then covers only any deductible, provided you waive the agency's deductible waiver. Max 48 days. Excludes vans other than mini-vans, and trucks/pick-ups. Claim within 48 hours. https://www.scotiabank.com/ca/common/pdf/credit_card/rental_car_insurance.pdf (read via saved PDF text)
- Visa Infinite (Visa's own LAC document, not a Canadian-issuer certificate): lists Costa Rica among countries that require buying local insurance, Visa cover sits on top; no cover in Ireland, Israel, Jamaica; 31-day cap; must decline the rental company's CDW. https://caribbean.visa.com/dam/VCOM/regional/lac/ENG/Default/Documents/PDFs/infinite-auto-rental-insurance-eng.pdf (snippet). GAP: Canadian Visa Infinite terms (RBC Avion, TD, CIBC, BMO) were not opened.
- Rental-company side: to decline the agency CDW you need a letter from the card issuer naming the renter and last 4 digits, confirming rental cover valid in Costa Rica; taking any agency coverage (CDW, zero-deductible) voids card cover. https://costa-rica-guide.com/travel/transportation/car/credit-card-insurance/ (snippet) ; https://vamosrentacar.com/car-rental-insurance-explained/ (research-logistics.md). Mandatory TPL/SLI cannot be covered by a card.
- GAP: Amex Canada and Mastercard World Elite Costa Rica terms, whether each issuer will actually issue a Costa Rica letter, and SUV class exclusions beyond Scotiabank's text. Practical line (DERIVED): ask the issuer for the letter 2+ weeks before travel, and check the vehicle class against the card's pick-up/van exclusions (a RAV4-class SUV is covered under Scotiabank's wording, a pick-up is not).
