# Peru — the destination map

Raw research. Every claim carries a source. `GAP:` marks anything unverified.

Peru is **1.28 million km²** — roughly the size of Ontario and Quebec combined — and it is not one
country to travel in. It is three parallel strips that run north–south and barely touch:

| Strip | What it is | Getting around |
|---|---|---|
| **Costa** | Desert coast, sea-level, Pan-American Highway runs its whole length | Fast buses, good roads |
| **Sierra** | The Andes, 3,000–6,700 m, where almost every famous thing is | Slow mountain roads, flights |
| **Selva** | Amazon basin, 60% of the national territory, almost no roads | Fly, then boat |

That geography is the whole answer to "how many days." You cannot cross between strips casually.
Lima to Cusco is a 1 h 20 m flight or a 21-hour bus. Iquitos, a city of 400,000, **has no road
connection to anywhere** — plane or river boat only.

## Tier 1 — the things nearly everyone comes for

### Cusco + Sacred Valley + Machu Picchu
The core. Cusco (3,399 m) was the Inca capital; the Spanish built their churches directly on top of
Inca walls, which is still visible at Qorikancha. The Sacred Valley below it (Pisac, Ollantaytambo,
Urubamba, Chinchero, Moray, Maras salt pans) sits 500–600 m lower and is the correct place to sleep
on arrival.

- **Minimum honest block: 5 nights.** 2 in the Sacred Valley (acclimatising), 1 Ollantaytambo or
  Aguas Calientes, 2 Cusco. Anything under that is a transit lounge with ruins.
- Machu Picchu itself is now the most rule-bound site in South America — see `research-logistics.md`.
- Source: https://www.peru-spirit.com/guides/cusco-altitude-vs-sacred-valley/

### Lima
Not a stopover any more. **Maido was named World's Best Restaurant 2025**; Central won the global
No. 1 in 2023 and has since moved into 50 Best's "Best of the Best" hall of fame; Kjolle, Mérito and
Mayta all rank. **The World's 50 Best Restaurants ceremony itself is being held in Lima on
4 November 2026** — the first time in the award's 24-year history it leaves for Peru.
Also: Larco Museum, MALI, Barranco, Miraflores clifftop, the Pachacamac ruins south of the city.

- **2 nights**, ideally one at the start and one at the end (flights out of LIM are mostly late).
- Sources: https://www.theworlds50best.com/stories/News/the-worlds-50-best-restaurants-2026-lima.html
  https://penthouse.pe/en/50-best-latam-lima-living-near-maido-central/

### Arequipa + Colca Canyon
Arequipa (2,328 m) is a white volcanic-stone colonial city under three volcanoes — and it is the
single best **acclimatisation staging post** in Peru because it is 1,000 m lower than Cusco.
Colca is the world's second-deepest canyon; the draw is the **Cruz del Cóndor** viewpoint at dawn,
where Andean condors ride the thermals out of the canyon.

- **Arequipa 2 nights + Colca 2 days** (the standard Colca tour is an overnight in Chivay).
- The Arequipa → Colca → Puno tourist coach is a legitimate way to link the two and see the
  4,800 m altiplano lookout en route.
- Sources: https://bushop.com/peru/guides/colca-canyon-without-the-stress-what-to-book-what-to-skip-and-typical-timelines/
  https://www.getyourguide.com/arequipa-l2859/colca-canyon-lake-titicaca-in-2-days-from-arequipa-to-puno-t119440/

### Lake Titicaca / Puno
**3,812–3,856 m** — the highest navigable lake in the world, and the highest place most itineraries
sleep. Uros floating reed islands (heavily touristed), Taquile, Amantaní homestays.

- **1–2 nights.** Most people find one night and a half-day lake tour is enough; the second night is
  for an Amantaní homestay, which is the part actually worth staying for.
- Blunt: Puno the town is not attractive. Sleep here for the lake, not the city.
- Source: https://bushop.com/peru/guides/puno-vs-arequipa-which-stop-makes-more-sense-in-your-itinerary/

## Tier 2 — the ones that change what the trip is

### Huaraz + Cordillera Blanca + Cordillera Huayhuash
This is the part the Instagram post is about, and it is correct that it is a different country from
Cusco. Huaraz (3,050–3,090 m) sits in a valley between the **Cordillera Blanca** — the highest
tropical mountain range on earth, with Huascarán at 6,768 m — and the **Cordillera Huayhuash**,
a compact 30 km ridge holding Yerupajá (6,634 m) and Siula Grande, the mountain from *Touching the
Void*.

Full detail in `research-trekking.md`. Headline: **the full Huayhuash circuit is 135 km, 10–12 days,
12 passes above 4,500 m, high point ~5,020 m** — and it needs 2–3 acclimatisation days in Huaraz
before you start.

- **Day-hike-only version: 4–5 days** (Laguna 69, Laguna Churup, Laguna Parón, Pastoruri).
- **Santa Cruz trek: 4 days** — the standard "first multi-day trek in Peru."
- **Huayhuash: 14–16 days door-to-door from Lima.**
- Sources: https://www.huayhuash.com/huayhuash_circuit.html
  https://www.explorandes.com/blog/santa-cruz-trek

### The Amazon
Three separate gateways, and they are genuinely not interchangeable:

| Gateway | Reach | Nights | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Puerto Maldonado** (Tambopata) | 55 min flight from Cusco | 2–4 | Easiest. Clip it onto Cusco with no backtrack. Macaw clay licks, oxbow lakes, giant otters |
| **Iquitos** (Pacaya-Samiria) | Flight from Lima only — no road exists | 3–5 | Big-river Amazon. Pink dolphins. Riverboat cruises. Genuinely remote feel |
| **Manú** | Long overland from Cusco, or charter | 6–8 | The most biodiverse and the most expensive. For people who care about the wildlife specifically |

- If you have 3 days: **Puerto Maldonado**. If you want the Amazon to be a real part of the trip:
  **Iquitos or Manú**, and budget a week.
- Sources: https://tourthetropics.com/guides/puerto-maldonado-vs-iquitos-vs-manu/
  https://www.rainforestcruises.com/guides/iquitos-or-puerto-maldonado

### The south coast — Paracas, Huacachina, Nazca
Ballestas Islands (sea lions, Humboldt penguins), Paracas National Reserve, the Huacachina oasis
with dune buggies and sandboarding, pisco bodegas in Ica, and the **Nazca Lines** — only properly
seen from a light aircraft.

- **2–3 days**, and it works as a loop out of Lima, so it slots in without disturbing the Andes.
- Honest note: this is the most "activity park" stretch of Peru. Huacachina is a bus-tour town.
- Source: https://conde.travel/packages/lima-ica-paracas-huacachina-nazca/

## Tier 3 — the north, which almost no foreign itinerary includes

Peru's north coast holds archaeology that predates the Inca by a thousand years, and it is empty
of tourists.

- **Trujillo** — Chan Chan, the largest adobe city in the Americas (Chimú); Huacas del Sol y de la
  Luna, Moche pyramids with polychrome friezes covering nearly a whole face; El Brujo and the
  tattooed Señora de Cao. Huanchaco fishing village with reed *caballitos de totora*.
- **Chiclayo** — the tomb of the **Lord of Sipán**, an unlooted royal Moche burial, plus Túcume's
  26 pyramids.
- **3–4 days for Trujillo + Chiclayo together.**
- Source: https://www.perunorth.com/trujillo-chiclayo-4d

### Chachapoyas (Amazonas region) — the strongest Tier 3 case
Cloud-forest kingdom of the Chachapoya, the "warriors of the clouds," conquered by the Inca only
decades before the Spanish arrived.

- **Kuélap** — a walled hilltop citadel of 400+ round houses, reached by a **20-minute cable car**
  from Nuevo Tingo. As of January 2026 the Ministry of Culture's PRIAK 2024–2029 programme reported
  south-wall recovery at **93%**. The cable car has had repeated operational interruptions —
  confirm before travelling.
- **Gocta** — a 771 m waterfall that was not documented to the outside world until 2005.
- **Karajía** sarcophagi, Revash tombs, Leymebamba Museum (219 mummies).
- Costs 2026: ~S/55–60 pp for Kuélap (cable car + entry), ~S/20 Karajía, S/10–15 community fees.
- **5 days practical minimum; 7 days to include Revash and Leymebamba plus a cloud-forest weather buffer.**
- Source: https://www.fertur-travel.com/blog/2026/chachapoyas-travel-guide/22498/

### Others worth naming
- **Choquequirao** — an Inca citadel believed **three times the size of Machu Picchu**, receiving
  about **30 visitors a day** because the only way in is a strenuous 4–7 day trek into and out of
  the Apurímac canyon. A cable car has been promised for over a decade.
  GAP: no confirmed construction status found for the Choquequirao cable car — treat as not built.
- **Ayacucho** — 33 colonial churches, one per year of Christ's life; the biggest Semana Santa in Peru.
- **Cajamarca** — where Atahualpa, the last Sapa Inca, was held for ransom and killed. Largely ignored
  by foreign visitors.
- **Máncora / Vichayito** — the north-coast beach strip. Genuine warm-water swimming, unlike the
  cold Humboldt-current coast further south.
- **Marcahuasi** — stone-forest plateau a few hours from Lima; camping, rock formations.
- Sources: https://www.touropia.com/underrated-destinations-in-peru/
  https://www.salkantaytrekking.com/blog/21-epic-off-the-beaten-path-destinations-in-peru/
