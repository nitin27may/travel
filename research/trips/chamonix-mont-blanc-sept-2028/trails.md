Pattern: Les Houches lifts (Prarion, Bellevue) close first, Brévent/Planpraz/Vallorcine next, Balme and Flégère gondola roughly a week after, Tramway and Montenvers last. In 2024 the Sunday cut was the 3rd Sunday; in 2025 and 2026 the 2nd Sunday (7 Sep Prarion, 14 Sep / 13 Sep the rest).

INFERENCE (not a source): for 2028 the closing Sundays will be either 10 Sep or 17 Sep. If 10 Sep, Prarion (7th Sunday-equivalent, 3 Sep) and Bellevue/Brévent/Planpraz/Index would all be shut for the last 4 days of the trip (11-14 Sep); Flégère gondola and Balme likely 17 Sep. Plan the lift-dependent hikes (Brévent, Index/Lac Blanc, Bellevue, Balme) for 1-9 Sep to be safe.

Conflict to note: seechamonix (https://seechamonix.com/lifts/dates) lists Brévent 13 Jun - 1 Nov in 2026, and en.chamonix.com lists Planpraz open 17 Oct - 1 Nov 2026; chamonix.net lists Brévent 31 Aug - 13 Sep. ### 0.2 Parking

Sources: https://www.seechamonix.com/parking ; https://hautesavoiemontblanc-tourisme.com/offres/parking-de-la-flegere-chamonix-mont-blanc-fr-5888607/ (via search snippet). A user tip (airial.travel, low quality) says arrive by 08:30. GAP: exact capacity (number of spaces) not found in any source.
- Planpraz/Brévent car park (Chamonix): covered, paid, EUR 2 per 2 hours, free under 1 hour, open all year (https://hautesavoiemontblanc-tourisme.com/offres/parking-du-brevent-chamonix-mont-blanc-fr-5887109/ via snippet). An older blog says EUR 10 for 8 h (Aug 2024). GAP: capacity.
- Chamonix town: about 4,000 spaces, fill fast; guaranteed only in off-season (Oct-Nov, late Apr-early Jun). Busy: Feb holidays, Mont Blanc Marathon week late June, UTMB week end of August (https://www.seechamonix.com/parking). UTMB 2026 was 24-30 Aug; GAP: UTMB 2028 dates not published in any source I found (https://reference-trail.fr/utmb-2027-quelle-date-pour-levenement-trail/ discusses a 2027 shift). If UTMB 2028 falls on the first weekend of September it overlaps 1-3 Sep; check utmb.world.
- Les Tines (Chapeau start): "Les Tines car-park" (en.chamonix.com/objet_touristique/893). GAP: cost/capacity.
- Merlet animal park car park (Aiguillette des Houches start): per official page. GAP: cost/capacity.
- Le Buet / Vallorcine (Bérard): hamlet car park at 1337 m (Randozone via search snippet); Chamonix Mobilité bus serves the waterfall (Decathlon Outdoor https://www.decathlon-outdoor.com/fr-fr/inspire/france/randonnee-cascade-de-berard-haute-savoie).
- Italian side: Val Veny and Val Ferret are traffic-limited; entry limited to about 260 cars per valley, LED sign at entrance, free summer shuttle (dates in the source "7 June - 29 September", year unstated); Val Ferret barrier at La Palud, paid parking at Planpincieux 09:00-19:00; Arnouva regulated July-August (entry before 08:00 until full, then shuttle from La Palud) per a 2026-updated page. ### 0.4 Alerts, closures and hazards found

1. TMB reroute, Switzerland: path between Prayon and Branche (Treutsebo sector, Orsières) diverted for rockfall; new route marked since Sat 11 Jul 2026; old route closed; camping/bivouac banned there. Source: https://www.ffrandonnee.fr/s-informer/actualites/deviation-du-sentier-de-grande-randonnee-tour-du-mont-blanc-orsieres-en-suisse (published 14 Jul 2026). Hits TMB stage 8/9 Champex-Trient area. Whether it persists in Sep 2028: GAP.
2. Refuge de la Croix du Bonhomme (TMB stage 2): major renovation from mid-Aug 2026, closure expected for several seasons, reopening projected about 2029. Source: https://www.ultimatefrance.com/hiking/french-alps/tour-du-mont-blanc . Likely closed in Sep 2028. Affects stage 2/3 overnight choice (use Les Chapieux, Refuge de la Nova, Mottets).
3. Casermetta Espace Mont-Blanc near Col de la Seigne closed all summer 2025 for renovation (same source). GAP: 2028 status.
4. Tramway du Mont-Blanc: new Nid d'Aigle terminus; "Access to the Nid d'Aigle is reserved for mountaineers with a refuge reservation" per en.chamonix.com. Day-walkers should treat Bellevue / Col de Voza / Mont Lachat as the realistic destination, and trains only went up to Nid d'Aigle from 4 Jul in 2026 due to snow (Bellevue terminus until 3 Jul). Tram rolled out Département-run operation from 18 May 2026 (Haute-Savoie press kit https://hautesavoie.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/DP_inauguration-TMB_270626.pdf , via search snippet; not read).
5. Grand Balcon Nord: the steep shortcut to Montenvers (left fork at 2,071 m) cuts through couloirs "dangerous in snow (for experienced hikers)" (official page below).
6. Chéserys to Lac Blanc stretch includes a ladder climb (AllTrails snippet, https://www.alltrails.com/poi/france/haute-savoie/chamonix-mont-blanc/lacs-des-cheserys); Tré-le-Champ section steep and rocky; Stage 10 TMB has ladders on the main route (https://tourdumontblanchike.com/tour-du-mont-blanc-etapes-stages/).
7. Nid d'Aigle on foot from Bionnassay: ladders (iron stairs against rock) in the last metres; rated difficult (https://hautesavoiemontblanc-tourisme.com/offres/le-nid-daigle-au-depart-de-bionnassay-saint-gervais-les-bains-fr-5866970/ via snippet).
8. Mer de Glace steps (glacier retreat): sources conflict. en.chamonix.com train page says "100 steps ... down until the ice cave"; gondola page says about 200 steps from the gondola arrival; one blog says about 580 metal steps (https://www.characrosstheworld.com/mer-de-glace-montenvers-glacier-chamonix-travel-guide/); an older explorersweb piece about Mer de Glace being "alpinists only" was not read. New gondola (since Feb 2024) goes to the cave level, drops walkers about 20 steps from the new station per one source. Ice cave is carved each year. GAP: current step count and whether cave reopens in Sep 2028. Ice cave closes in mid-May and reopens per season; 2026: cave closed 17 Oct-1 Nov.
9. Lac Blanc and Chéserys: swimming banned by prefectural order; dogs banned (Aiguilles Rouges nature reserve). Official pages above.
10. Planpincieux glacier (Italy, Grandes Jorasses): 2020 alert did not affect the TMB path Courmayeur-Bertone-Bonatti (search snippet). GAP: any 2026/2028 Planpincieux alert.
11. Mont Blanc normal route/Goûter huts: rockfall closures reported 2022 (https://explorersweb.com/mont-blanc-refuges-closed-due-to-rockfall-danger/); not relevant to walkers but a hazard-news signal. Two outdoorukraine.com items dated Aug 2026 appear to recycle 2022 stories; do not rely.
12. Colle/Fenêtre d'Arpette (TMB stage 8 variant, 2,665 m): good weather only.
13. Early September snow: GAP. No source gave a snow-line figure for early September. Sources only say snow can linger into July at Lac Blanc (AllTrails snippet) and that May snow persists Plan de l'Aiguille to Signal Forbes. Official pages say opening "subject to favorable snow and weather conditions". Use the Chamonix tourist office weather, MeteoFrance Chamonix bulletin, and OHM (Office de Haute Montagne) at check time.

### 1.1 Lac Blanc from Flégère (Aiguilles Rouges)
- Official: 7 km (4.3 mi), +500 m / -500 m, "Difficult (high gradient)", 3 h round trip, 1h45 one way; start 1,877 m, high point 2,352 m. Open 01/07-30/09/2026 daily, dependent on Flégère lift. Free. https://en.chamonix.com/things-to-see-and-do/sports-and-outdoor/hiking/lakes-waterfalls-and-gorges/hike-to-the-lac-blanc-from-la-flegere
- Loopi (tourist office data): 7 km, 546 m up / 71 m down listed (https://en.chamonix.loopi-velo.fr/parcours/walk/2b216d23-cbb1-4a84-8141-f02f1a0275ae/hike-to-the-lac-blanc-from-la-flegere , via snippet). Third-party: 5 mi, 1,657 ft gain, 4 h (theoutbound/blog; https://Theoutbound.com/france/hiking/hike-to-le-lac-blanc); one logged loop 8.05 km / 5.0 mi, +564 m, about 4 h (https://www.characrosstheworld.com/lac-blanc-hike-chamonix/).
- AllTrails: "Lac Blanc via Grand Balcon Sud" 5.4-5.5 mi (8.7-8.9 km), +1,922-1,929 ft (about 586 m), 4.8 stars, 1,630 reviews https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/haute-savoie/lac-blanc-via-grand-balcon-sud ; "Lac Blanc - Index de la Glière via La Flégère" loop 5.7 mi (9.2 km), +2,204 ft (672 m), 745 reviews https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/haute-savoie/lac-blanc-et-l-index-de-la-gliere-via-la-flegere ; "Grand Balcon Sud - Le Lac Blanc" out and back 4 mi, +1,758 ft, 803 reviews https://www.alltrails.com/trail/france/haute-savoie/le-lac-blanc-depuis-le-grand-balcon-sud .
- Trailhead: top of Flégère gondola (hiking sign on right). Drive Les Praz, signs for Martigny; or bus stop "La Flégère". Gondola saves 800 m of ascent.
- Lift: Flégère gondola, 2026 closes 13 Sep (Index) / 20 Sep (gondola only). Queue up to 40 min in peak summer per blog.
- Route: down into Combe de la Chavanne, past Chalets et Lac de la Flégère (2,027 m), below Tête de l'Aubuy, joins Argentière path, flat combe to Lac Blanc. Option: descend to Chéserys.
- Top: Lac Blanc, Mont Blanc massif reflected, Aiguilles Rouges flora. Refuge du Lac Blanc: season listed 3 Jun-29 Sep 2026 (tourism listing) vs 5 Jun-4 Oct 2026 (Ulysse); 38-40 places; cash only; half-board only; book via refugedulacblanc.fr or phone 07 67 56 74 14 from 1 June (https://hautesavoiemontblanc-tourisme.com/offres/refuge-du-lac-blanc-chamonix-mont-blanc-fr-5830887/ ; https://ulysse.com/news/aiguilles-rouges-col-montets-rando-sans-voiture-2026). Chalet du Col des Montets reopened June 2026 (Ulysse).
- Sept notes: refuge open to late Sep-early Oct; cooler, fewer crowds; confirm snow. GAP: snow line.
- Verdict: top-ranked day.

### 1.2 Lac Blanc from the Index chairlift (short route)

### 1.6 Lacs de Chéserys

## 3. Rank: "is this worth a day" (my judgement from the data above, ranked)

1. Lac Blanc from Flégère with Chéserys extension (3-5 h walking, Aiguilles Rouges). Best reward per effort; Mont Blanc reflected; Index variant shorter. Lift fragile: do before about 9 Sep.
2. Grand Balcon Nord (Plan de l'Aiguille to Montenvers) with Mer de Glace cave. Mont Blanc-side needles and glacier in one route; Aiguille du Midi mid-station needs a timed ticket; train and gondola run into late Sep.
3. Grand Balcon Sud (Flégère to Planpraz) plus a Brévent summit extension. Easier, everywhere visible, lifts at both ends; combine with the Brévent summit for the widest panorama. Brévent/Planpraz close about 13 Sep.
4. Col de Balme (and Albert 1er refuge for the longer version). Easy, long-lasting lifts (to about 20 Sep); good for a lighter or marginal-weather day.
5. Aiguillette des Houches (+ Bellachat extension). No lift needed; use as a spare day when lifts are closed or a Mont Blanc-view day without queues.
6. Val Veny / Bertone-Bonatti (Italy). Different face of Mont Blanc; needs early shuttle planning, Italian traffic limits; Lac Combal itself is a low, shrinking lake.
7. Cascade de Bérard + Vallorcine. Short, bus/train accessible, rainy-day filler.
8. Lac d'Émosson excursion. A day out with a train; hiking version is a hard scrambling day.
9. Tramway du Mont-Blanc / Bellevue to Nid d'Aigle. Interesting, but Nid d'Aigle top is reserved for refuge-booked mountaineers; day visitors get Col de Voza/Mont Lachat views.
10. Lac/Montagne de Péclerey. Local mid-mountain loop; name unverified.

### Must-do shortlist with reasons
1. Lac Blanc plus Chéserys: the single most famous Chamonix hike (AllTrails ratings 4.8 across several variants, hundreds to over 1,600 reviews each); official route 7 km, +500 m, 3 h; refuge on the lake.
2. Grand Balcon Nord: strongest face-on view of the Aiguilles and Mer de Glace; official 5.7 km, mostly downhill.
3. One lift-free day (Aiguillette des Houches or Grand Balcon Sud walked up from town): insurance against early-closing lifts.
4. Mer de Glace/Montenvers (half day, with ice cave): iconic glacier-retreat story; check the stair count and cave date.
