# Lane 4: Attractions and hours, Chamonix / Mont Blanc, 1-14 Sep 2028

Raw data for synthesis. Researched 2026-10-12. NO 2028 schedules or prices are published. Everything below is from 2026 (summer 2026 season) unless marked otherwise. Lift operators set September end-dates year by year, so 2028 dates are projections: treat the 2026 pattern as the best guide and verify in spring 2028.
Source tags: [S1] = fetched page text; [S2] = web search snippet only (weaker).

## 0. Key structural findings (read first)

- Early-mid Sept is the lift wind-down window. In 2026 several lifts closed between 6 and 20 Sep, inside the 1-14 Sep window. Ordering by closure date (2026 pattern): Prarion gondola 6 Sep; Brevent/Planpraz, Bellevue cable car, Vallorcine gondola 13 Sep; Balme/Le Tour 20 Sep; Tramway du Mont-Blanc 27 Sep; Gorges de la Diosaz 27 Sep. Aiguille du Midi runs to 1 Nov. Source: https://www.chamonix.net/english/lift-systems/dates-times [S1], https://en.chamonix.com/activities/practical-information-about-the-lifts/tramway-du-mont-blanc [S2], https://en.chamonix.com/activities/hiking/hiking-trails/gorges-de-la-diosaz [S2]
- Hours shorten from 31 Aug 2026 (AdM close 16:00 last ascent, Skyway 13:45 last from 7 Sep). Plan big-lift visits for the morning.
- Skyway Monte Bianco (Italy side): the Chamonix.net page lists it to 27 Sep 2026 but also "closed till 29/05/2027 for maintenance" (internally inconsistent; page itself flagged inconsistencies). Autumn maintenance closure pattern: 14 Oct-6 Dec 2025 closure; spring 2026 closure 20 Apr-22 May. So September is usually open but verify. Sources: https://www.chamonix.net/english/lift-systems/dates-times [S1]; web search results via https://www.lovevda.it/en/database/5/ski-lifts/courmayeur/skyway-monte-bianco/4137 and https://www.qualitytravel.it/skyway-monte-bianco-riapre-il-24-maggio/95397 [S2]

## 12. Climatology (the "clear Mont Blanc view" question)

- Data (1991-2020 normals, Chamonix, climatestotravel): September mean 12.8 C, avg low 6.2 C, avg high 19.4 C, precipitation 105 mm over 10 days, about 6 h sun per day (185 h/month). https://www.climatestotravel.com/climate/france/chamonix [S1]
- Other estimates (all third-party, periods unstated, flagged inconsistent): 6 h sun/day = 46% of daylight, 12 rain days, 57 mm (weather2travel, https://weather2travel.com/france/chamonix/september/ [S2]); about 10 rain days, highs 19 C, lows 9 C (Chamonet, https://www.chamonet.com/news/what-s-on-in-chamonix-september-698979 [S2]); 190 h sun, 15 rainy days, 120 mm (alternative site; URL not retrieved); 38% chance of rain on an average day (Wanderlog, https://wanderlog.com/weather/9982/9/chamonix-weather-in-september [S2]).
- FRACTION OF DAYS WITH CLEAR MONT BLANC VIEW: GAP: no published statistic. The sources searched did not report cloud-free or "summit visible" day counts. Proxy only: about 10 of 30 days have measurable rain (1991-2020), so roughly two-thirds of days are rain-free, and sunshine runs about 6 h/day (around 46% of possible). Rain-free does not mean summit visible (valley cloud, afternoon convection). This is derived arithmetic, not a measured clear-view fraction. For the 14 days of 1-14 Sep 2028 the same proxy gives about 4-5 rain days. Treat as an expectation of roughly 9-10 rain-free days of 14, with sustained clear-summit mornings on perhaps half of them (the half is a planner judgement, not sourced).
- Thunderstorms: GAP: no September thunderstorm-day count found. Summer is the rainy season because of afternoon thunderstorms (climatestotravel). Hiking guide: heatwave raising the isotherm above 4,500 m makes afternoon storms highly probable. https://hiking-hostel.com/en/blog/mont-blanc-mountain-weather [S2]
- Freezing level: GAP: no September normal. Nearest data: August 1991-2020 average about 3,800 m (cited via MeteoSuisse in a 2026 report); summer 2026 heatwaves reached about 4,200-4,500 m. https://www.the-ski-guru.com/2026/08/23/mont-blanc-rockfalls-record-2026/ [S2] ; https://www.espace-mont-blanc.com/asset/rapportclimat_eng.pdf [S2, not read]. Implication: at AdM (3,842 m) temperatures hover near or just below 0 C in a normal September, rising above in heatwaves; rockfall risk elevated in hot years (record rockfall in 2026 per the-ski-guru).
- Shoulder-season gotcha: wet or shortened days around a tail-end storm can shut lifts (weather dependent).

## 13. Ranking: is it worth a slot, with hours needed

1. Aiguille du Midi (plus Step into the Void). Must see. 3-4 h (more at peak). Needs a clear morning; book D-1 or earlier, first departure 08:10 in the 31 Aug-13 Sep band. 83 EUR adult return.
2. Montenvers and Mer de Glace (train, ice cave, Glaciorium). Must see for most families. 3 h (2-3 h per source) plus stair climb (hundreds of steps, count GAP). 49.70 EUR package. Works in cloud (the cave).
3. Brevent via Planpraz, with a Bergerie de Planpraz lunch. Must see on a clear afternoon. 3-4 h. Closing about 13 Sep (2026 pattern); use 1-12 Sep.
4. Grand Balcon walk (Planpraz-Flegere), 3-4 h plus lifts, needs both lifts on; clear day.
5. Tramway du Mont-Blanc to Nid d'Aigle. Worth it for a train and glacier-view day, 3-4 h. Last season day 27 Sep in 2026, so open for early Sept; hours short (to 15:00 last).
6. Skyway Monte Bianco / Courmayeur. Worth a half day to full day (4-6 h with Courmayeur lunch); 59 EUR Helbronner return; last ascents 13:45 from 7 Sep; Italian side typically clearer when the French side is clouded in (judgement, unsourced).
7. Gorges de la Diosaz. Worth 1-1.5 h; 7.50 EUR; open to 27 Sep.
8. Chamonix town (Saturday market, museum, church/cemetery stroll). 2-3 h.
9. Emosson and Mont-Blanc Express to Martigny. Half to full day; dam open to 31 Oct 2026 pattern.
10. Annecy day trip. Full day.
11. Megeve half day. Optional.
12. Saint-Gervais thermal baths / QC Terme Chamonix. 2-3 h. Rain-day filler.
13. Lausanne. Skip unless by car.

Must-see shortlist for a 14-day stay: AdM (3-4 h), Montenvers (3 h), Brevent/Planpraz (3-4 h), Tramway du Mont-Blanc (3-4 h), Skyway/Courmayeur (5 h), Diosaz (1.5 h), Emosson/Martigny (5-6 h), Annecy (full day).
