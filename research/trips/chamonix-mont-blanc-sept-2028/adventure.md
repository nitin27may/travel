# Research lane 2: Adventure and activities (Chamonix / Mont Blanc, 1-14 Sep 2028)

Raw data for synthesis. Researched 2026-10-12.

## Reading rules

- Every operating window, price and policy below is the **2026** published figure. No 2028 timetable or tariff exists anywhere. Treat 2026 September windows as a PATTERN, not a promise.
- Lift calendars shift year to year. The lift pattern is the same shape each year (late-Aug/early-Sep summer timetable, a mid-Sep step-down around 13-14 Sep, then autumn timetable). The 2026 step-down dates (7, 13/14, 20/21, 27/28 Sep) are the best proxy for 2028.
- Day-of-week arithmetic (my calculation, not sourced): 1 Sep 2028 = Friday, 14 Sep 2028 = Thursday, 28 Aug 2028 = Monday.
- `GAP:` = unverified, with the failed source named.
- Tool note: the search tool returns summaries. Where a figure came from a fetched official page it says "fetched". Where it came from a search summary only it says "search summary".

## 0. Cross-cutting findings that change planning

1. **Mid-Sep 2026 lift step-down falls inside a 1-14 Sep window.** In 2026: Prarion gondola summer period ends 6 Sep; Panoramic Mont Blanc (Helbronner) goes to a short 09:00-13:45 timetable 7-27 Sep; Index chairlift stops after 13 Sep; Flegere gondola only 14-20 Sep; Aiguille du Midi and Montenvers switch to autumn hours 14 Sep; Chamonix Luge closes 15 Sep-17 Oct except weekend openings 20-21 and 27-28 Sep. Source (fetched): https://www.chamonix.net/english/lift-systems/dates-times
   - Consequence: a 2028 trip of 1-14 Sep straddles the step-down. Days 1-13 look like 2026 "1-13 Sep"; day 14 (Thu 14 Sep 2028) may be on the autumn timetable. Anything that needs the full-day timetable should be placed in the first ~10 days.
2. **Helbronner/Panoramic Mont Blanc has the tightest hours of any lift** (09:00 first, last round-trip departure 13:45 in 7-27 Sep 2026). This contradicts any plan that puts the Italy crossing in the afternoon or as a "weather-permitting backup afternoon".
3. **Parc de Merlet is announced as closed for all of 2027** for meadow rehabilitation; no source says it reopens in 2028. Source (fetched): https://www.tourisme-haute-savoie.com/actualites-de-haute-savoie/ouverture-2026-parc-de-merlet/ . GAP: reopening status in 2028, source above silent.
4. **Grands Montets (Plan Joran gondola) closed all summer 2026 for major renovations.** Source (fetched): https://www.chamonix.net/english/lift-systems/dates-times . GAP: 2028 status.
5. **Brevent cable car (top, 2,525 m) is closed 21 Sep-16 Oct 2026.** In 2026 it runs daily 31 Aug-20 Sep. Source (fetched): https://en.chamonix.com/activities/practical-information-about-the-lifts/brevent-cable-car . Fine for 1-14 Sep, if the pattern repeats.
6. **Aiguille du Midi weather rule is the same everywhere: "Subject to favorable weather."** No official refund policy for weather closure was found for direct lift tickets (see 1.1).

## 8. Altitude and safety considerations

### 8.1 Aiguille du Midi (3,842 m) and Helbronner (3,466 m)
- Lower station 1,033 m to summit 3,842 m (fetched, official page). Mid-station 3,777 m is the Panoramic Mont Blanc departure point, so the AdM rise is about 2,800 m in one ride. Ride time GAP (not stated on fetched page).
- Official page says only: "Bring warm clothing, sunglasses and sun cream" and "children under 5 are not recommended", no separate altitude warning.
- Third-party guide (search summary): altitude sickness can appear from about 2,500 m when ascent is rapid; typical symptoms nausea, loss of appetite, drowsiness, fatigue, dizziness, rapid pulse; fit people are not protected; officially visits are discouraged with severe colds or heart problems; staff are trained to help; keep the visit short if sensitive; symptoms often ease on return to the valley. Source: https://northabroad.com/?p=15983 . Low authority. A French medical article cited also says altitude sickness is unpredictable at 1,500-3,000 m (https://www.pourquoidocteur.fr/Articles/Question-d-actu/23856-Au-ski-mal-montagnes-touche-au-dessus-200-metres ).
- GAP: any official medical guidance (Compagnie du Mont-Blanc, medical association) on pregnancy, heart disease, children, and acetazolamide. No source found; consult a doctor.
- Practical: Helbronner is lower than the AdM summit, but a Chamonix-to-AdM-to-Helbronner-to-Courmayeur day takes a visitor above 3,400 m for several hours, then via the Skyway down. Sequencing the AdM early in the stay versus acclimatising first: GAP, not sourced.
- Contradiction with weather planning: 7-27 Sep Helbronner closes its last return departure at 13:45. AdM summit cloud and wind close the lift without notice; "subject to favorable weather".

Sources: https://hautesavoiemontblanc-tourisme.com/en/offers/refuge-du-gouter-ffcam-saint-gervais-les-bains-en-5829931/ (search summary, page 404'd on direct fetch of the .../5829931 ID) and https://www.explore-share.com/blog/new-regulations-to-climb-mont-blanc/ (fetched, dated 2019 regulations: personal reservation mandatory, hut capacities 20/74/40/120 for Nid d'Aigle/Tete Rousse/Tete Rousse camp/Gouter, reservation proof checked by Garde Blanche and gendarmes).
- Booking window: sources conflict (online up to 10 days before; older UIAA says until 10:00 on the arrival day). GAP: current rule and 2028 season opening of reservations.
- Hazards named in sources: the Grand Couloir (rockfall) reportedly claimed "over a hundred lives" (commercial guide, no dated primary source) and the route's low PD grade is "exactly the trap" (same guide). GAP: official fatality statistics. 2018 prefecture decree restricted access for overcrowding (https://www.seechamonix.com/news/mont-blanc-access-restrictions-extended-to-august-15th-centre-719399 ).
- Why it is not a casual add-on (synthesis of cited facts): 2-3 days minimum; summit 4,808 m, well above AdM; hut booked 9-12 months ahead; needs acclimatisation days and crampon/ice-axe skills; weather windows are narrow; the season, per the one operator's 25 May-15 Sep availability, may end around the middle of the trip's window; cost EUR 1,750-2,850 per person minimum. GAP: whether September 2028 guide availability continues past 15 Sep. The operator's 2026 window ends 15 Sep.
- Conclusion for the planner: **a Mont Blanc ascent cannot be added inside a 14-day family or leisure trip without dedicated prior acclimatisation and a booking made 9-12 months before.** Not verified for 2028; confirm with the hut booking system (FFCAM).
