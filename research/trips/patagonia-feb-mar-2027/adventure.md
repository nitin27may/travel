## 1. EL CALAFATE

### 1.1 Minitrekking (Hielo y Aventura), Perito Moreno
- Operator: Hielo y Aventura. Official: https://www.hieloyaventura.com (detail pages did not load: GAP for official terms).
- Price: ARS 320,000 adult from, winter promo ARS 256,000; with transfer up to ARS 384,000 [C26, May 2026]. Reseller: ARS 500,000 per person [NOM-MT, undated]. USD: Nomades search snippet quoted US$320 adult until 31 May 2026 and US$370 for 1 Aug 2026 - 31 May 2027, children 8-15 cheaper (snippet only, page itself shows ARS; UNVERIFIED). Year: 2026.
- Duration: about 9-9.5 h day, about 1 h on ice [C26, NOM-MT]. Pickup 08:00-17:00 [NOM-MT].
- Season: open all year, weather permitting [NOM-MT]. Late Feb/March: expected operating. Official confirmation GAP.
- HARD LIMITS: age 8 to 65, "no exceptions" [C26, NOM-MT]. Height: GAP. Weight: GAP. Fitness: medium; uneven terrain, crampons; not for pregnancy or mobility/coordination limits [C26].
- Booking: advance; sell-out timing GAP.
- Cancellation (reseller Nomades): free until 24 h before; later no refund; free reschedule to 24 h; weather suspension full refund [NOM-MT]. Operator's own terms GAP.
- Included: pickup (urban), shared transport, guide, crampons [NOM-MT]; navigation + helmet per [C26]. Not included: park entry (ARS 25,000 residents / ARS 50,000 non-residents per NOM-MT, subject to change), food.
- OK for age 11: Y (min 8).

## 3. USHUAIA

### 3.1 Tren del Fin del Mundo (OFFICIAL, high confidence)
Source: https://www.trendelfindelmundo.com.ar (fares for Oct 2026 - Mar 2027, i.e. the trip window).
- Clase Turista: ages 13+ AR$ 80,000; ages 4-12 AR$ 40,000; 0-3 free. Foreigners pay the same rate as nationals (international category). Site has a USD option; USD figures not shown (GAP).
- Clase Premium: ages 13+ AR$ 250,000; 4-12 AR$ 125,000; 0-3 free.
- Note: ages 4-12 pay the child fare; 13 and over pay adult.
- Schedule Sep 1 - Apr 30: trains 09:30, 12:00, 15:00; station open 08:30-17:15. Buy ahead; office tickets subject to same-day availability. Check-in 45 min early, last 10 min before departure. Park entry to Parque Nacional Tierra del Fuego not included and required.
- Duration: GAP on the page. Cancellation terms: linked on site, not read: GAP.
- Earlier 2026 winter press (iProfesional/La Capital, Jul 2026): Turista ARS 70,000 / 35,000, Premium ARS 217,000 / 110,000 (prior-season prices; shows ~14% rise to the Oct 2026 list).

### 3.3 Isla Martillo penguin colony WITH LANDING (Pira Tour / Pira Pira)
- Only operator reported as authorized to land at Martillo (via Estancia Harberton): Pira Tour, Ushuaia [DP] (blog; "when they went"). Whether another operator now lands: GAP. "Pira Pira vs Piratour" comparison: GAP (could not find a source separating them; Piratour's site https://www.piratour.com.ar lists only a "Caminata en Pingüinera" product with no description; phone (+54) 02901 435557).
- Format [DP]: about 7:35 start, 1.5 h bus to Harberton, short boat to a pebble beach, about 1 h walking a fixed path; stay 4 steps from birds. Daily visitor numbers strictly limited; forum report: max 20 on island at once, 80/day (FlyerTalk, https://www.flyertalk.com/forum/17646020-post7.html, older). Spots sell out fast Dec-Feb; book ahead online (higher price) or at the office a day or two before.
- Price: blog says ARS 195,000 (year unclear) plus Harberton entry; Nomades older resale US$240 adult / US$144 child 3-11, Dec 2023 - Jan 2024; Harberton entry ARS 10,000 cash [NOM-1264]. 2026/27 price: GAP.
- Season: Oct 1 - Mar 31 [DP, NOM-1264]. Magellanic penguins start migrating north in late March [DP]. Early March: Magellanics should still be present but numbers falling and adults/chicks moulting is plausible (UNVERIFIED). Gentoo: a small permanent colony, sightings not guaranteed (blogger saw none) [DP]. So Gentoo in early March: PRESENT YEAR-ROUND but not guaranteed.
- Min age: GAP (none stated anywhere). Not suitable for pregnant or reduced mobility [NOM-1264]. Cancellation (Nomades resale): 100% to 48 h before [NOM-1264].
