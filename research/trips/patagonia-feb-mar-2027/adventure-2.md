## 1. PERITO MORENO / EL CALAFATE

### 1.1 Los Glaciares park: hours and ticket (OFFICIAL)
- Corredor Mitre-Glaciar Moreno is the only sector with set access hours. Summer 1 Sep - 30 Apr: entry 08:00-18:00, may stay until 20:00. Winter 1 May - 31 Aug: entry 09:00-16:00, stay to 18:00. Source: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/patagonia-austral/parque-nacional-los-glaciares/horarios-como-llegar-0 . So the trip window (26 Feb - 11 Mar) = summer hours, 08:00-18:00 entry, 20:00 out.
- Closure days (25 Dec / 1 Jan): GAP, official page does not mention any.
- Ticket (daily pass, "General" column = non-resident foreigner): ARS 50,000, valid from 1 Jun 2026; children 0-6 inclusive free. Source: https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/tarifas . Press cross-check (4 Jun 2026): Argentines ARS 25,000, Santa Cruz residents ARS 8,000, international ARS 50,000: https://promociones-aereas.com.ar/guia/nuevos-precios-de-entradas-a-los-parques-nacionales-de-argentina-2026-guia-completa-de-tarifas-c . USD: GAP (Civitatis once showed ARS 45,000 = US$30 on an older page, implying ~1,500 ARS/USD then; not an official rate).
- Gate: paid access on RP 11, 80 km from El Calafate (same official page). Park info: 02902-492504, informespnlg@apn.gob.ar (https://www.argentina.gob.ar/parquesnacionales/losglaciares).

- Best time of day: GAP (no source states it). Inference only, not sourced: the ice face is on the Lago Argentino side, so check sun angle on the day; boat departures run 11:30-15:30 (see 1.3), so midday-to-afternoon park arrival pairs with the boat.
