# Patagonia Feb-Mar 2027: logistics research (raw data)

Researched 2026-10-13. Raw data lane, not a report. Every claim carries a source URL; `GAP:` marks what could not be verified. Many aggregator figures disagree; where they do the conflict is shown rather than resolved. Search tool returned secondary sources far more often than airline/official pages, so airline schedule facts for Feb-Mar 2027 are mostly GAP.

## 1. Flights

### 1.1 Toronto (YYZ) to Buenos Aires (EZE)
- Great-circle distance YYZ-EZE: 8,975 km (idealo https://flights.idealo.com/route/Toronto-YYZ/Buenos-Aires-BUE); YTO page 8,966 km; Cleartrip 8,947 km (https://www.cleartrip.qa/flight-schedule/toronto-buenos-aires-flights.html). One blog says ~10,622 km, discarded as inconsistent.
- Nonstop time: roughly 15 h (Trip.com listings: 15h05-15h30, via search summary; https://in.trip.com/flights/airport-yyz-eze/). Cleartrip says ~13h55, an outlier. No time difference issue: Toronto is UTC-5 in Feb/Mar until DST starts 14 Mar 2027 (GAP: DST date from memory, not sourced); Argentina is UTC-3 all year, so +2 h in Toronto winter time.
- Air Canada nonstop YYZ-EZE exists. Launch 3 May 2018 on a Boeing 787-9, 3x weekly Mon/Wed/Fri, dep 19:40 arr next morning; return dep EZE 08:55 arr YYZ 19:00 (https://airlinegeeks.com/?p=22513, 2018 article, may be stale). An Aviacionline snippet conflicts (Tue/Thu/Sat, "instead of four") and a 2022 Aviacionline piece shows AC090 with a Sao Paulo stop (https://www.aviacionline.com/air-canada-alters-its-schedule-to-buenos-aires). GAP: 2027 days, times and whether it is seasonal. Not confirmed on aircanada.com; check the schedule tool for 26 Feb and 11 Mar 2027.
- Fares quoted on Air Canada's own page (https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-to-buenos-aires-ar, "collected in last 48 hours", fetched 2026-10-13, retrieval date not printed on the page): YYZ-BUE/EZE round trip economy from CAD 1,341 for 12-17 Feb, 21-28 Feb and 2-9 Mar 2027 windows; YYZ-AEP from CAD 1,391 (20 Jan-3 Mar 2027). Points: 202,752 + CAD 527 for 7-21 Feb 2027. These are "from" fares, not a quote for 26 Feb-11 Mar. Other Air Canada landing pages show "from CAD 1,087 / 1,102 / 1,116" (https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-from-canada-to-argentina, https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-to-buenos-aires-ar); undated, treat as indicative low points.
- One-stop options (via GRU, SCL, PTY, MIA, JFK etc.): GAP, not researched from airline pages. Typical range for dates: GAP beyond the Air Canada "from" fares above.

### 1.3 Baggage
- Aerolineas Argentinas domestic: checked bag max 15 kg in Economy/Premium Economy (https://www.infoviajera.com/?p=173069, updated May 2026; https://www.turismocity.com.ar/promociones_aereas/p-cuanto_cuesta_agregar_equipaje_en_cada_aerolinea-3525.html). From May 2026 the lowest domestic fare no longer includes a free carry-on, only a personal item up to 3 kg (Infobae https://www.infobae.com/economia/2026/05/04/aerolineas-argentinas-ya-no-incluira-el-carry-on-dentro-de-su-tarifa-mas-baja-cuanto-costara/). Whether the lowest fare includes a checked bag is disputed between sources; GAP: official AR baggage page.
- Flybondi: free personal item up to 6 kg under seat; 9 kg overhead (55x35x25) paid; checked 12 kg or 20 kg paid; online at booking is up to 60% cheaper than the airport (https://aifly.one/guides/flybondi-baggage-allowance/, third party). GAP: peso fees and official source (flybondi.com).
- JetSMART baggage: GAP, not found.
- Practical flag: 15 kg checked limit on AR is below international norms for hiking gear; weigh bags.

### 1.4 Aerolineas foreign-resident pricing
- Residency-based fares (non-resident higher) are still in place; a federal appeals court upheld the practice, distinction is residence not nationality (https://www.diariojudicial.com/news-79353-pasajes-para-locales-y-visitantes, undated in result). AR ran non-resident promo fares (up to 45% off) in 2025 and a Europe window 1 Mar-25 May 2026 (https://bloomberglinea.com/latinoamerica/argentina/aerolineas-argentinas-ofrece-tarifas-promocionales-para-incentivar-llegada-de-turistas-al-pais/). GAP: whether the non-resident surcharge applies to domestic legs sold outside Argentina in 2027 (historically yes, cheapest fares were resident-only). Verify by pricing on aerolineas.com.ar with the ".ca" and ".com.ar" points of sale.

### 1.5 Wind and weather delays
- No source found on wind-specific cancellations at FTE or USH. Found snow closures at USH (Aug 2023, winter, https://www.cadena3.com/noticia/sociedad/fuerte-temporal-de-nieve-en-ushuaia-cerraron-el-aeropuerto-y-cancelaron-vuelos_365243). ### 2.2 FTE to El Chalten: real figures vs the "2 h" claim
- Distance about 215 km, fully paved via RN40 and RP23 (https://www.rexby.com/is/instaxtraveler/t/getting-from-el-calafate-to-el-chalten-a-practical-transport-guide). The same guide says 3 to 3.5 h. Bus timetable aggregators: 2h40 (https://www.checkmybus.com/el-calafate-sc/el-chalten-sc, Rome2Rio https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Cerro-Torre/El-Calafate). No source supports 2 h; the video's "2 h" is optimistic. Plan 2h45 to 3h30 with wind and photo stops. The El Calafate airport (FTE) is ~20 km before town (GAP: unsourced; confirm).
- Bus: Cal Tur and Chalten Travel (Chaltur) run it. Fare conflicting: USD 39-40 (CheckMyBus/Busbud https://www.busbud.com/en/terminal-de-omnibus-de-el-calafate/s/11062), ARS 35,500 (Rome2Rio), ARS 50,000 one way with terminal fees ARS 3,000 (Calafate) and ARS 2,000 (Chalten) (https://nomadicsamuel.com/travel-blog/how-to-get-to-el-chalten-from-el-calafate-bus-stops-times). GAP: current official fare; check caltur.com.ar. Departures ~08:00 and ~17:00 per CheckMyBus.
- Wind: strong crosswinds on RN40; warning for high-sided vehicles (rexby guide). Fuel: limited; carry spare per 2019 article (https://batimes.com.ar/news/culture/magical-ruta-40-the-long-and-winding-road.phtml says nothing precise; the 2019 vakantio blog https://vakantio.de/it/blog/leavingtoreturn/vierter-stopp-argentinien-part-1-wandern-und-viel-chaos). GAP: fuel at La Leona (about 80 km past the RN40 junction, a roadhouse stop). Fill the tank in Calafate before leaving.

## 3. Entry and documents (Canadian citizens)
- No visa for tourism, stay up to 90 consecutive days (Argentine Consulate Montreal page updated 28/10/2025, https://cmrea.cancilleria.gob.ar/en/node/64645).
- Passport: valid for expected stay (Global Affairs Canada https://travel.gc.ca/locations/argentina); many sites say 6 months; airline may be stricter. Suggest 6 months as safe. GAP: official Argentine rule.
- Health insurance: the Argentine page says all foreign citizens must have insurance covering hospitalization and medical assistance; documents may be in English (same Montreal page). GAP: how often enforced at the border.
- Biometrics (fingerprints, photo) on arrival and departure; may be asked for onward ticket and proof of funds (travel.gc.ca).
- Reciprocity fee for Canadians: suspended from 1 Jan 2018 (https://www.bal.com/immigration-news/argentina-to-waive-visa-reciprocity-fee-for-canadians/; Cancilleria https://ecana.cancilleria.gob.ar/en/node/37483). No reinstatement found; GAP: confirm for 2027.
- DDJJ electronic sworn declaration: abolished in August 2022 (Disposicion 1975/2022; https://www.sirchandler.com.ar/2022/08/se-elimino-la-declaracion-jurada-para-entrar-al-pais/). GAP: no 2026 confirmation of anything new. No ETA scheme found for Canadians. Yellow fever proof not required; hepatitis A and B recommended (travel.gc.ca).
- Minors under 18 born or resident in Argentina need consent from non-travelling parent; not relevant unless a child holds Argentine status (travel.gc.ca). Other passports: TO CONFIRM per passport (US/Australia reciprocity fee also suspended per BAL; others GAP).
- Safety: Argentina "normal precautions"; Greater Buenos Aires "high degree of caution" (travel.gc.ca).

## 4. Money
- Rates, Sept 2026 (Rio Times https://www.riotimesonline.com/argentina-blue-dollar-convergence-expats-2026/): official ~1,480/1,530, blue ~1,525/1,545, MEP ~1,517/1,525 ARS per USD, gap 1-3%, tightest in years. Indicadores.ar 24 Jul 2026: blue 1,545, official 1,520, MEP 1,531.63 (https://www.indicadores.ar/dolar-blue-hoy). Conflicting older guide said ~1,100-1,200 (https://argentinavisalaw.com/guides/money), stale. Implication: the old "bring cash USD for blue rate" edge is mostly gone; do not plan around it. Foreign card purchases at MEP-linked rate ("foreign tourist dollar") confirmed only to April 2024 (https://buenosairesherald.com/argentina-101/foreign-tourist-dollar-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-preferential-exchange-rate); GAP: 2026-27 status. Withdrawals do not get that rate.
- ATMs: foreign-card withdrawals have low limits and high fees; use indoor ATMs (travel.gc.ca). GAP: current limit in ARS and fee. Card acceptance in Chalten, cash dependence, tipping norm (commonly 10%): GAP, no source found.

## 5. Connectivity
- Signal intermittent on RN40 and in trekking areas; Los Glaciares coverage very limited outside visitor centres (https://www.viajaresim.com/blog/?p=576).
- Personal eSIM can be bought online before arrival; Claro tourist SIM (25 GB, ~USD 15) needs an in-person purchase with passport; Movistar weakest rurally (https://blog.yesim.tech/mobile-internet-and-sim-for-argentina/). Sources disagree on best Patagonia carrier; SIM registration is required. GAP: carrier coverage map for Chalten. Download offline maps (Maps.me, Google offline, Wikiloc); WhatsApp works over Wi-Fi in town.

## 8. Holidays and crowding
- Carnival 8-9 Feb 2027 and 24 Mar (Memory Day) are non-movable; Good Friday 26 Mar; Veterans day 2 Apr (https://hacecuentas.com/feriados-argentina-2027, not official). No national holiday falls inside 26 Feb-11 Mar. Tourism bridge days for 2027 not yet published (same page). GAP: official Boletin Oficial confirmation. School holidays: Argentine summer break ends late Feb/early Mar in most provinces (GAP, no source); Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego dates GAP. Crowding: February is peak, March shoulder; GAP quantitative source. Chalten and Calafate accommodation fills in Feb; book early (derived, unsourced).
