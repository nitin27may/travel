# Days needed: Canadian Rockies, September 2029 (planner's synthesis)

Written 2026-10-12 from the six research files and `data/*.json` in this folder. No 2029 timetable, price or closure list exists. Every date and figure below is a 2026 (or older) figure used as a pattern.

Tags used here:
- **INFERENCE**: my reasoning, not a source.
- **GAP**: not verified; the failed or missing source is named where known.
- **Matrix**: a drive time from `data/bases.json`. The researcher estimated every value in that matrix from general road knowledge, rounded to 5 minutes, with no routing source. Only four legs have a source: Calgary-Banff about 90 min (Rome2Rio, 1h25-1h45), Banff-Lake Louise 40 min (Canadream), Banff-Jasper 3.5-4 h, Golden-Jasper 4h34 (wanderlog, possibly a different route). Treat every matrix figure as INFERENCE.

Party assumed: a family with a young child. The adventure research assumed a small young child; the height and weight rules decide three activities (below), so check them against the actual child.

**Verdicts (INFERENCE)**
- **8 nights** is the right answer only if school absence must stay at one week. It is the best value per day but it is not the Canadian Rockies so much as Banff and Yoho.
- **10 nights** is the weakest: it buys Jasper by deleting all slack and ends with a 4-5 h drive on departure day, or a one-way fee to Edmonton.
- **12 nights** is the honest fallback. It keeps every headliner and loses the Robson day and one of the two Kananaskis days.
- **14 nights** covers every park the brief asked for except Waterton, keeps Parkway driving to two days, and puts the trip's only realistic family larch walk on the second-to-last day.
- **16 nights** only if Waterton matters to you. In a late-September window Waterton is thinning out (Goat Haunt cruise ended 20 Sep and the border cruise ran one sailing a day, 14:00, from 8 to 20 Sep in 2026). If Waterton is a must, it belongs in the earlier window instead.

## 3. Recommendation: 14 nights, Sat 8 to Sat 22 September 2029

| Base (`bases.json` id) | Nights | Dates | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `banff` | 2 | Sat 8, Sun 9 | Walkable town, first night at 1,383 m before the Sunshine day at 2,159-2,385 m (researcher: altitude risk low if the first night is at Banff). |
| `lake-louise` | 1 | Mon 10 | Starts the Parkway 35 min closer than Banff (Lake Louise-Jasper 175 vs Banff-Jasper 210, matrix) and at Bow Lake before the wind. |
| `jasper` | 4 | Tue 11-Fri 14 | The far end of the loop; three full days and the most closure exposure, so it gets the slack. Lodging is the tightest in the Rockies after the 2024 fire (20% of rooms lost, about 96% summer occupancy). |
| `lake-louise` | 4 | Sat 15-Tue 18 | Moraine, Lake Louise and Yoho, each a split day. Moraine is put on the last of the three days for the best chance of first larch colour. |
| `kananaskis-village` | 3 | Wed 19-Fri 21 | Peter Lougheed lakes and Highwood Pass, then 70 min to Calgary airport (matrix). |

Base split check: 2 + 1 + 4 + 4 + 3 = 14 nights. Five check-ins, four distinct bases.

**Why not Canmore.** Canmore is cheaper and has the best family hotels with pools (Pocaterra Inn waterslide, Canmore Inn and Suites), but it is 60 min from Lake Louise and 235 min from Jasper (matrix). It enters the plan only as the Day 12 stop for groceries and Grassi Lakes. If Banff prices are out of reach, swap the first two nights to Canmore (25 min to Banff, matrix).

**Why Lake Louise twice.** The single night on Day 3 saves 35 min on the longest drive day and gets the family to Bow Lake around 08:15. The four-night block on Days 8-11 is there for the Moraine shuttle, which leaves from the Park and Ride at the Lake Louise ski resort. Field is the fallback if Lake Louise prices are too high (25 min, matrix; lodging GAP).

**Why Calgary in and out.** One rental, no one-way fee (GAP), and the loop shape means the Parkway is driven once in each direction with different stops. Edmonton out is in the open questions; it saves nothing on this shape because the trip ends in Kananaskis.

**Recommendation: Window B, Sat 8 to Sat 22 Sep 2029.** The child's experience barely differs between the two windows, because the family walks are the same either way. The adults gain three things in Window B: no Labour Day weekend in Banff, a real chance of first colour on the Larch Valley and Ptarmigan Cirque days, and quieter trailheads once Alberta schools are back. The costs are a borderline Moraine canoe, no Kananaskis raft, colder mornings (Lake Louise's September mean low is already -0.8 C, Parks Canada) and more chance of snow on the two highest adult hikes. One extra school day separates them.

**How the plan changes in Window A (Sat 1-Sat 15 Sep):**
- Same route and base order, every date seven days earlier.
- Day 2 (Sun 2 Sep) is the Sunday of the long weekend at Sunshine. Gondola up at opening.
- Day 3 is Labour Day Monday. Be on the Johnston Canyon trail by 07:30 or skip it for Marble Canyon and Paint Pots only.
- Day 11 Moraine (Tue 11 Sep): the canoe should still be running.
- Day 14 Highwood Pass (Fri 14 Sep): Ptarmigan Cirque is green, not gold. Swap it for the Kananaskis River raft (2026: 13:30 daily, minimum age 5 and 18 kg, so weigh the child first) and keep Ptarmigan Cirque as the morning.
- Lodging for 1-3 Sep is the most expensive and the first to sell out. Book it before anything else.
- If Waterton is wanted, it fits better in Window A: services are still at summer level in the first half of September.

## 5. What would change this recommendation

- **School says one week maximum:** take 8 nights, drop Jasper.
- **Waterton is a must:** take Window A and the 16-night shape, or swap the Kananaskis block for Waterton (Kananaskis Village-Waterton 200 min vs Lake Louise-Kananaskis Village 100 min, matrix).
- **The child walks 8-10 km comfortably:** several split days become family days (Kinney Lake, Rawson Lake, Chester Lake and possibly Lake Agnes are within reach), and the plan needs fewer solo adult hikes.
- **Jasper lodging cannot be found at a sane price by early 2029:** fall back to Hinton (about 1 h east) or Valemount (about 1h25 west; matrix gives 85 min), or cut to 12 nights with 3 Jasper nights.
