# Lane 6: timing, hazards, light, days needed (raw data)
Trip: Sat 1 Sep to mid Sep 2029, a family with a young child. Researched 2026-10-12. No 2029 data exists; years noted per figure. Tags: CALC = computed, INFERENCE = derived by me, GAP = unverified.
Method note: sources came via web search summaries plus page fetches. Where I only saw a search snippet, I say so.

## 0. Biggest planning flags (INFERENCE from data below)
- Sentinel/Larch Valley peak (~15-25 Sep) mostly falls AFTER 1-8 Sep. A trip of 1-15 Sep catches the START of larch colour at best; 2025 Sunshine gondola closed 21 Sep, 2026 closes 20 Sep (so likely ~mid/late Sep 2029; GAP exact 2029 date).
- Snow dusting in September at altitude is normal (see section 2). Lake Louise and Sunshine saw first snow 24 Aug 2025, ~30 Aug 2026, ~7 Sep 2023.
- Group of 4 hiking rule at Larch Valley/Sentinel Pass has been used by Parks Canada in past years in response to bears (year unconfirmed; a group of fewer than four would not qualify). GAP: current status.
- Lake O'Hara shuttle is a random draw (applications 2-23 Mar 2026); children 1-16 must be on the reservation; season ends ~4 Oct. For 2029 expect application window in March 2029 (INFERENCE).

## 1. What peaks when

### 1a. Larch colour
General window sources:
- Peak usually 20 Sep to 5 Oct, lasting 7-10 days; early frost/snow can end it. https://thebanffguide.com/larch-season-banff-guide/ (year not stated)
- Mid-Sep to early Oct; heat-stressed larches turning earlier than usual. https://curiocity.com/larch-season-alberta-2026/ (2026, search snippet)
- Colour moves from high elevations downward over about 2-3 weeks; mid to late Sep typically best (search summary of https://banffbound.com/blog/fall-larch-season-banff, https://banffjaspercollection.com/canadian-rockies/stories/larches-in-banff-where-to-see-them-this-fall; snippet only).
- Destination Canada says larch needles start changing in the last two weeks of August (high elevation). https://travel.destinationcanada.com/en-us/things-to-do/alberta-fall-colours (snippet only, treat cautiously)
- A German-language guide: peak usually ~20-30 Sep, ~a week. https://loudavymkrokem.cz/de/larch-valley-trek-kanada/ (2026)
- Autumn gale or early snow can strip needles. (same sources)

By site:
- Larch Valley / Sentinel Pass (~2,400 m): first trees yellow ~mid-Sep, absolute peak ~10 days later; among the first in Banff to change. https://loudavymkrokem.cz/en/larch-valley-canada-hike/ (2026 guide). Moraine Lake road is shuttle/guided/lodge only. https://thebanffguide.com/larch-season-banff-guide/ Moraine Lake road typically open 1 Jun to mid-Oct; Parks Canada shuttle ran 1 Jun to 12 Oct 2026. https://www.travelbanffcanada.com/moraine-lake-at-sunrise/
- Sunshine Meadows (gondola village 2,159 m, Standish chair 2,385 m): season "typically mid to late September, colours fade by late Sep" (Sunshine post, 2025; page now 404, seen via search snippet: https://skibanff.com/explore/news-blog/summer-2025/larch-season-meadows). Gondola closed 21 Sep 2025 per https://thebanffguide.com/larch-season-banff-guide/ and runs 6 Jul to 20 Sep 2026, 8:00-18:00, no ticket pre-booking needed: https://www.skibig3.com/summer/gondolasandlifts/ (snippet). Elevations from https://thebanffblog.com/sunshine-village-gondola/ (snippet).
- Lake O'Hara: GAP for peak dates (no source gave them). Photographer/guide sources say late September is best (https://explorersweb.com/larch-madness/ snippet; https://autumnsky.zenfolio.com/rockies/yoho/lakeohara). - Arnica Lake: listed among favourite larch hikes (Go Outside substack above). Dates GAP.

### 1c. Elk rut
- Banff Parks Canada bulletin issued 28 Aug 2026, valid to 31 Oct 2026: stay at least 30 m from all elk, never get between bull and cows, don't park between them (bulls charge vehicles), keep dogs leashed, rut to mid-Oct. Report to Banff Dispatch 403-762-1470. https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/bulletins/868a8410-7ef6-4f87-a4d3-f8dc44f335aa
- Timing: bugling, sparring, herding from mid-Sep to mid-Oct (search summary of https://banffbound.com/wildlife-calendar); other sources say rut runs from late Aug/September to mid-Oct (https://edmonton.ctvnews.ca/elk-rut-in-jasper-national-park-prompts-cautionary-reminder-1.6543443, snippet). So 1-15 Sep 2029 is the early rut (INFERENCE).
- Jasper: same 30 m rule; Jasper dispatch 780-852-6155 (search snippet from Jasper article). Where: GAP for specific sites (Banff townsite/Bow Valley and Jasper townsite are widely known; not source-verified here).
- A Banff wildlife specialist suggests doubling to ~60 m in rut (personal advice, quoted in rmotoday article https://www.rmoutlook.com/banff/dangerous-elk-rutting-season-underway-5784137, snippet).

- Bighorn rut is Nov-Dec, not September. https://hikejasper.com/Bighorns-Gather-for-Mating-Season-Nov-2024.html (snippet). Sheep on Hwy 16 east of Jasper and on the Icefields Parkway near Tangle Falls (snippet of search).
- Goats: Mount Kerkeslin "Goat Lick" viewpoint on Icefields Parkway and Disaster Point on Hwy 16 east; lick use peaks June-Aug, fewer by Sept (study snippet: https://bioone.org/journals/Western-North-American-Naturalist/volume-70/issue-2/064.070.0207/Mineral-Lick-use-by-Gps-Radio-Collared-Mountain-Goats-in/10.3398/064.070.0207.full). ### 1e. Bears, berries, closures
- Buffaloberry is key; bears feed up to 200,000 berries a day in hyperphagia (search snippet, rmotoday: https://www.rmotoday.com/banff/buffaloberry-season-leads-to-bear-advisory-for-residents-visitors-5614544). Blanket berry-season warning in Banff, Yoho, Kootenay in past years (undated snippet).
- Precedent: Lake Louise Tramline closed where grizzlies fed on buffaloberries (undated snippet, https://www.rmotoday.com/local-news/bear-closures-in-place-in-banff-1569391). Kananaskis 2025 bumper berry crop led to many bear warnings/closures (https://www.ctvnews.ca/canada/article/number-of-bear-warnings-closures-in-albertas-kananaskis-country-rises-due-to-bumper-berry-crop/, 9 Aug 2025).
- 2026: Minnewanka trail, Aylmer Pass and campgrounds closed 15 Jun-7 Jul 2026 for black bears. Parks Canada says some temporary wildlife closures are marked only in the field with no web notice (bulletin valid 1 Apr 2026 to 31 Mar 2027). https://parks.canada.ca/banff-bulletins
- Larch Valley/Paradise group-of-4 rule: past enforcement, fines possible. https://www.rmotoday.com/local-news/moraine-lake-hiking-restriction-in-place-2-1565780 (undated snippet). GAP current.
- INFERENCE: bears are most active on valley berry patches and avalanche slopes in Sept; expect at least one closure or group-access order somewhere by mid-Sep.

## 3. Safety with a young child

Bear spray (Parks Canada): not allowed on any commercial flight, even checked; carry clip on, in trunk or sealed container in vehicle; legal labelled "for animals", max 500 ml, recommended 225 ml, 0.75-1% capsaicin ~10 s spray; use only if bear within ~5 m and acting aggressive; check expiry; do not spray gear/tent. https://www.parks.canada.ca/pn-np/mtn/ours-bears/securite-safety/gaz-spray
- Rent/buy (2025-2026 blog and Reddit, low confidence): rental ~C$10-12/day at Wilson Mountain Sports Banff, Ultimate Sports Banff; Lake Louise: Wilson (Samson Mall), Chateau Mountain Sports; buy C$35-65 (Canadian Tire Canmore, Atmosphere Canmore); some shops refund ~25 on return. Sources: https://banffbound.com/bear-spray ; https://redlib.groet-infra.nl/r/Banff/comments/1ufgqco/where_to_get_bear_spray/ (Jun 2026). Prices GAP-verify. Jasper rental shops: named in search but no URL.
- Child-specific bear advice (secondary, not Parks Canada official): keep child within arm's reach, pick up and back away, don't run, group of 4+ where required, make noise. https://www.authentikcanada.com/ca-en/blog/what-to-do-bear-encounter ; https://braggcreek.ca/kananaskis/wildlife-encounter/ . Official Parks Canada bear/cougar pages not retrieved this session: GAP. Cougar advice (general): keep child close, pick up, don't run, face it, fight back. UNVERIFIED by official source.
- Group-size rules: see 1e. Moraine area precedent rule of 4+.
- Dogs: leash at all times, max ~3 m in Banff; some trails no dogs. https://www.parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/visit/faune-wildlife/chiens-dogs ; https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/securite-safety/regles-rules
- Drones: recreational use prohibited in all national parks (fines up to C$25,000 per secondary source). https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/securite-safety/regles-rules ; https://banffbound.com/blog/banff-drone-rules
- Rivers: GAP, no Parks Canada source retrieved. INFERENCE: glacial water is cold and fast; keep child back from Bow Falls, Athabasca Falls, Johnston Canyon edges.
- Glaciers: Athabasca Glacier toe is in Jasper NP; crevasse fatality 12 May 2026 (backcountry ski group, above the icefall; https://www.jasperlocal.com/2026/05/22/crevasse-fall-on-athabasca-glacier-claims-life/); 1990 child death precedent (https://publications.americanalpineclub.org/articles/13199100400/Fall-into-Crevasse-Unroped-Public-on-Glacier-Alberta-Rocky-Mountains-Athabasca-Glacier). Stay behind signs/ropes; use guided or Ice Explorer option. Rockfall: GAP official, use trail-report closures.
- Altitude: Sunshine village ~2,159 m, Standish top 2,385 m; Lake O'Hara ~2,000 m (GAP exact). Children get altitude illness at similar rates to adults, symptoms in first 24 h, small children report poorly; check heart/lung conditions with doctor. https://childrenscolorado.org/just-ask-childrens/articles/altitude-sickness ; https://services.thebmc.co.uk/advice-for-children-at-altitude . INFERENCE: risk low at these heights; sleep at Banff/Canmore (1,400 m) first.
- Sun: GAP source. INFERENCE: UV at 2,000+ m and on snow/water is high even in Sept; hats and SPF.
- Mosquito: GAP (nothing found). Ticks: wood ticks active spring (Mar-Jun, to mid-Jul), not found in valley bottoms/shade/alpine per a guide author; by September low risk (INFERENCE). https://canadianrockiestrailguide.com/?p=3105 (snippet) ; https://www.pestworld.org/pest-guide/ticks/rocky-mountain-wood-ticks
- Elk safety: see 1c; the rut is the main Sept hazard for a small child near townsites and roadsides.
