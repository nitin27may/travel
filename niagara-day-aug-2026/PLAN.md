Base: Whitby, Ontario. ~380 km, ~4 h 35 driving. Out 8:00 AM, home ~11:50 PM.

## What changed from the draft, and why

1. **Both claimed trail closures are real — confirmed, not assumed.** Whirlpool Trail is
   closed, and the Terrace Trail is severed between the Cobblestone and Trillium Trails
   (north entrance to Marker 6) for stair repairs, "until further notice," per Niagara
   Parks' own maintenance page. Sticking to Main Loop + River + Cliffside is the right call.
2. **Nature block extended to 3:15 PM**, per your ask. The Glen now gets 2 h 5 min on the
   trail instead of 85 minutes, and Queenston Heights gets 85 minutes instead of 75.
3. **Dinner is now in the plan, not spontaneous.** Apna Pind, 1650 Derry Rd E, Mississauga.
   The deciding fact is that it runs to **2:00 AM on Saturdays** — a 9:50 PM arrival has no
   closing pressure at all. Karahi Point (11 PM) and Mughal Mahal were the runners-up;
   Karahi Point's reviews are inconsistent (4.5 Google vs 2.5 TripAdvisor) and it sits on
   the wrong side of the city for the drive home.
4. **Sunset is 7:58 PM, not 7:56.** Computed, not looked up. Blue hour runs to 8:26 PM.
   Golden hour starts about 7:15 PM, which is what sets the Queen's Royal Park window.
5. **You only pay for parking TWICE all day, not five times.** Niagara Parks lets you
   **re-park free at any other Niagara Parks lot the same day on one receipt** — so paying
   ~$24 at the Glen covers Queenston Heights as well, provided the receipt goes on the dash.
   Old Town is a different authority (Town of NOTL), so that's the second and last payment,
   and it can be $0 on Ricardo Street. **Day total ~$24-47.** Full breakdown below.
6. **The Sunday concert doesn't apply.** The draft flagged a free 2-4 PM concert at
   Queenston Heights on Sunday Aug 30. We go Saturday, which should be the quieter day there.
7. **Weather is good** — mix of sun and cloud, 22 C, no rain. The draft's "shorten if wet"
   caveat almost certainly won't trigger, so the full route is realistic.

## Footwear — the one real safety item

The Glen is the only part of the day with any risk, and it is not mud. It is ~80 metal
stairs plus exposed rock, roots, loose talus and uneven footing on the gorge floor.

- **Trail shoes or any lugged rubber sole:** fine everywhere.
- **Smooth-soled sneakers, canvas shoes, sandals:** do the rim overlook and skip the descent.
  Everything else on the day — Queenston Heights, Old Town, Queen's Royal Park — is flat
  and paved and needs nothing special.
- The climb out is where tired legs plus poor grip causes trouble, not the way down.

## Parking — park twice, that's it

Two authorities. A receipt from one is worthless to the other.

### Payment 1 — Niagara Parks, covers BOTH morning stops
Niagara Parks' own policy: *"Guests can re-park on the same day for free at any other Niagara
Parks paid parking lot as long as they retain their original parking receipt and display it
on the dash."* It is **receipt-based, not plate-based**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Pay at **Niagara Glen**, ~3 h @ $8/hr | **~$24** |
| **Queenston Heights** — put the Glen receipt on the dash | **$0** |

**Do not bin that receipt.** Leave it face-up on the dash at Queenston Heights, readable
from outside the car. It is the most valuable piece of paper of the day.

### Payment 2 — Town of Niagara-on-the-Lake, covers the whole evening
The Glen receipt does nothing here. But Old Town is compact enough to park once and walk
everything: **Queen's Royal Park is about two blocks from Queen Street.**

| Option | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Ricardo Street** — unmetered (Parks Canada's Navy Hall page confirms it), north end of Old Town, walkable to both Queen St and Queen's Royal Park | **$0** |
| Heritage District meter, 3:45-8:00 PM @ $5.25-5.75/hr | ~$23 |
| Anywhere metered, after 8:00 PM | $0 |

Definitive free/paid boundary — check this on your phone before leaving the car:
https://notl.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=dbde74a8c7b943bbb39d0bbad94e18a7

| Scenario | Cost |

### Rejected
- **Niagara Parks Daily Pass**, $31.86 + HST (~$36) — more than paying hourly for a 3-hour stop.
- **Annual Niagara Parks Pass**, $50 to Dec 31 2026, two vehicles — only wins on a second trip this year.
- **Free parking at the Whirlpool Aero Car lot** — genuinely free, but 1.7 km straight-line
  (~2 km on the paved recreation trail) from the Glen. That is 25 min each way added to a hike
  that already ends climbing 60 m out of a gorge, and without a Glen receipt you would then pay
  full freight at Queenston Heights. Saves ~$12 for ~50 min of walking. No.
- "NOTL parking is free" — true only from **December 1**, when the Town waives Heritage
  District fees. Not in August.

## Gotchas

1. NOTL meters run 10 AM - 8 PM, $5.25-5.75/hr, actively enforced. Free from 8 PM. Park on
   the lake side instead and pay nothing.
3. Whirlpool Trail and part of Terrace Trail are closed — don't improvise onto them.
4. Pur & Simple closes at 3 PM. It is breakfast only; it cannot be a fallback later.
5. This is a 16-hour day. 8:00 AM out, ~11:50 PM back. The late dinner is the cost of
   keeping the sunset.
6. Apna Pind is on Derry Rd E near Dixie, north of the 401 — set the GPS to the address,
   not the name, as there is a second Apna Pind on Telford Way.

## Decision rules

- **Running late out of the Glen?** Cut Queenston Heights to 45 minutes (monument + cairn,
  skip the gardens). Do not cut Old Town — it is the second half of the point.
- **Tired by 5 PM?** Drop the 6:00-7:15 golden-hour second lap and just sit in Simcoe Park
  until 7:15. You lose the least by cutting this.
- **Want an earlier night?** Leave Queen's Royal Park at 8:00 instead of 8:25 — you keep the
  sunset itself and lose only the blue hour. Saves ~25 min and gets you home by ~11:20.
- **Weather turns wet** (not forecast): shorten the Glen to the rim overlook and the Main
  Loop only. Wet metal stairs and wet talus are the two things worth respecting.
