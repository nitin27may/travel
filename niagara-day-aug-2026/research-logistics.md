## Parking — CORRECTED Aug 28 after challenge; three different authorities

### Niagara Parks Commission lots (Niagara Glen, Queenston Heights)
**$8.00 per hour at BOTH.** Neither is free. An earlier draft of this research wrongly
recorded Queenston Heights as free parking — that was from a weak secondary source and is
wrong. Official: https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit-niagara-parks/plan-your-visit/getting-around/

Day cost: Glen ~3 h = ~$24, Queenston Heights ~1.5 h = ~$12. **~$36 total.**

- **Niagara Parks Parking Pass: $50**, valid to Dec 31 2026 regardless of purchase date,
  covers up to two household vehicles, works at almost every Niagara Parks lot along the
  56 km Parkway. Excludes the Parks Police building and the Oakes Garden Theatre metered lot.
  No parking between 12:00 AM and 6:00 AM. Physical pass required — no screenshots.
  https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit-niagara-parks/plan-your-visit/parking-pass/
- **Verdict for a single day trip:** pay hourly ($36 < $50). The pass only wins if there is
  any second Niagara visit before Dec 31, at which point it wins immediately.
- **Free Niagara Parks lots exist** but only two: the **Whirlpool Aero Car** lot and
  **Old Fort Erie**. The Aero Car lot is the nearest free option to the Glen, on the same
  Parkway and linked by the paved recreation trail — but it adds a walk at both ends of a
  hike that already finishes with a 60 m climb. Noted for completeness, not recommended.
- No daily flat rate or daily maximum is published for 2026. Older sources quoting
  $1.25/hr with a $10 cap are stale (that was ~2016 pricing).

### Town of Niagara-on-the-Lake (Old Town / Queen Street)
**Metered 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily, $5.25-5.75 per hour**, actively enforced. Machines take
credit/debit/coin; Honk Mobile and ParkedIn also work.
https://www.notl.com/town-services/parking/where-park-how-pay

**But free parking does exist in Old Town, and this is the useful find:**
- There is **free street parking on the Lake Ontario side**, around Simcoe Park and toward
  Front Street — outside the metered Heritage District core.
- The Town waives Heritage District fees entirely **from December 1** each year. That is the
  "NOTL parking is free" people remember. It does not apply in August.
- Residents with a permit get 3 free hours anywhere. Not us.

**Plan change:** park free on the lake side and walk in to Queen Street, rather than metering
on Queen. We are walking to Queen's Royal Park at 7:15 PM anyway, so parking at that end of
town costs no extra walking and saves about $23.

Verify on the Town's paid-parking map before committing — the free/paid boundary matters.
