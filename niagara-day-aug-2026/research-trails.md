## CLOSURES — verified against Niagara Parks' own maintenance page, Aug 28 2026
- **Whirlpool Trail: CLOSED.**
- **Terrace Trail: section CLOSED** between the Cobblestone and Trillium Trails
  (north entrance to Marker 6) — stair repairs and risk mitigation, "until further notice."
- Everything else open: Main Loop, River, Cliffside, Woodland, Cobblestone, Trillium, Bouldering.
- Source: https://www.niagaraparks.com/repairs-maintenance/

## Practical
- Nature Centre: 3050 Niagara Pkwy, Niagara Falls. Guided hikes leave 11:00 AM and 2:00 PM.
- Parking: **$8/hour**, Niagara Parks lot. Large lot in front of the Nature Centre (~100+
  spaces) — this is NOT a 9-car trailhead; capacity is not the constraint here, unlike the
  Vermont trip. Cost is the constraint instead: ~$24 for our 3-hour stop.
- Phone: (905) 354-6678
- Terrain: stairs, exposed rock, roots, uneven footing, loose talus near the river. The
  climb OUT is the hard part — same elevation, tired legs, no shade in places.

## Queenston Heights
- 14184 Niagara Parkway, Niagara-on-the-Lake.
- **Southern terminus of the Bruce Trail** — Canada's oldest and longest marked footpath.
  Stone cairn sits in a parking lot, ~160 m from Brock's Monument.
- Brock's Monument: built 1853, William Thomas, Neoclassical, Queenston limestone,
  16-ft statue of Maj. Gen. Sir Isaac Brock, killed at the Battle of Queenston Heights,
  October 1812.
- Tennis/pickleball courts recently rebuilt (4 new pickleball, 4 resurfaced tennis).
- **Parking is $8/hour (Niagara Parks lot) — NOT free.** Corrected Aug 28; an earlier note here said free, which was wrong.
- No closures affecting the park itself.
- https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit/nature-garden/queenston-heights/
