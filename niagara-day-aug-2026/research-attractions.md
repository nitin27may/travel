# Research: Hours, food, verification (Aug 28 2026)

## Breakfast — Pur & Simple, St. Catharines
- 290 Glendale Avenue, St. Catharines, ON. (905) 227-4955
- **Hours: 7:00 AM - 3:00 PM, daily.** Verified open — the user's draft claim was correct.
- Weekend gotcha: they run an **online waitlist** on Sat/Sun. Join it from the car and you
  get a text when the table is ready, instead of standing in the lobby.
- https://pursimple.com/restaurant/st-catharines/

## Dinner — Apna Pind, Mississauga (chosen)
- 1650 Derry Road E, Mississauga, ON. (905) 678-2100
- **Saturday hours: 9:00 AM - 2:00 AM.** This is the decisive fact: a 9:50 PM arrival has
  zero closing pressure, unlike almost every other option considered.
- Punjabi / North Indian. Well regarded for traditional dhaba-style cooking.
- Position: Derry Rd E near Dixie, just north of the 401 — genuinely on the way home from
  the QEW, not a detour into downtown Mississauga.
- https://apnapindcanada.com/

## Niagara-on-the-Lake Old Town
- Queen Street heritage district; Clock Tower cenotaph mid-street; Niagara Apothecary
  (5 Queen St) is a restored 1869 pharmacy museum.
- Simcoe Park green space adjacent to Old Town.
- Metered parking 10 AM - 8 PM, $5.25-5.75/hr (see logistics).

## Queen's Royal Park
- 45 Front Street, Niagara-on-the-Lake.
- White gazebo (a film prop left in place, now the town's signature photograph), lawns,
  benches, shoreline.
- Sightline: this is where the **Niagara River empties into Lake Ontario**. Fort Niagara
  sits directly across the river mouth on the American side.
