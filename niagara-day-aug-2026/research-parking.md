# Research: Parking, verified in depth (Aug 28 2026)

Two separate authorities. A receipt from one is worthless to the other. That single fact
organises the whole answer.

## 1. Niagara Parks Commission — Niagara Glen AND Queenston Heights

Official rate page: https://www.niagaraparks.com/visit-niagara-parks/plan-your-visit/getting-around/

| Lot | Rate |
|---|---|
| Niagara Glen & Nature Centre | **$8.00/hour** |
| Queenston Heights Park | **$8.00/hour** |
| Whirlpool Aero Car | **FREE** |
| Old Fort Erie | **FREE** |
| (for reference) Rapidsview Lot C | $10/day |
| (for reference) Rainbow Bridge | $12/hour |

### THE KEY FINDING — one payment covers both our stops
Niagara Parks policy, quoted from their own site:

> "Guests can re-park on the same day for free at any other Niagara Parks paid parking lot
> as long as they retain their original parking receipt and display it on the dash."

The system is **receipt-based, not licence-plate based**. So:
- Pay at Niagara Glen for our ~3-hour stop = **~$24**
- Drive to Queenston Heights, put the Glen receipt on the dash = **$0**
- **Total Niagara Parks cost for the day: ~$24, not $36.**

**Do not lose or bin that receipt.** It is the single most valuable piece of paper of the day.
Leave it face-up on the dash at Queenston Heights, readable from outside.

### Options considered and rejected
| Option | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly at the Glen + re-park receipt | **~$24** | **Chosen.** Cheapest real option. |
| Daily Parking Pass (re-entry to most lots, one calendar day) | $31.86 + HST (~$36) | More than paying hourly for a 3-hour stop. No. |
| Annual Niagara Parks Parking Pass | $50, valid to Dec 31 2026, 2 vehicles | Only wins on a second Niagara trip this year. |
| Park free at Whirlpool Aero Car, walk to the Glen | $0 + walk | **1.7 km straight-line, ~2 km on the paved Niagara River Recreation Trail.** That is ~25 min each way, added to a hike that already ends with a 60 m climb out of the gorge. And with no Glen receipt you would then pay full price at Queenston Heights. Saves ~$12 for ~50 min of extra walking. Not worth it. |

## 2. Town of Niagara-on-the-Lake — Old Town

**Completely separate from Niagara Parks. The Glen receipt does nothing here.**

- **Heritage District meters: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM daily, $5.25-5.75/hour.** 30 machines on
  Queen Street and the Old Town side streets. Credit, debit, coin; Honk Mobile and ParkedIn apps.
- **Free after 8:00 PM**, every day.
- **Official paid-parking map (the definitive answer on the free/paid boundary):**
  https://notl.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=dbde74a8c7b943bbb39d0bbad94e18a7
- The "NOTL parking is free" belief is real but refers to the Heritage District **fee waiver
  that starts December 1** each year. Not August.

### Free / unmetered spots — what is actually confirmed
| Where | Status | Source confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Ricardo Street** | **Unmetered** | Good — Parks Canada's own Navy Hall page states there is "unmetered parking on Ricardo Street" beyond the 30-space Navy Hall lot. |
| Byron Street | Free overnight; no bylaw banning it | Medium — local press. |
| Queen's Royal Park lot | Conflicting. One secondary source says metered at $1.50/hr, which does not match the Town's own $5.25-5.75 range and looks stale. | **Low — treat as paid.** |
| Anywhere metered, after 8 PM | Free | High — Town's own hours. |

**Honest limitation:** the Town does not publish a plain-text list of free streets. The ArcGIS
map above is the only authoritative source, and it should be checked on the phone before
leaving the car. Ricardo Street is the best-supported free option and it sits at the north
end of Old Town near Navy Hall, close to both Queen Street and Queen's Royal Park.

## 3. Answering the real question: how many times do we pay?

**Twice. Not five times.**

| Block | Park once at | Payments |
|---|---|---|
| Niagara Glen + Queenston Heights | Glen lot; receipt on dash at Queenston | **1** (~$24) |
| Old Town + light food + golden hour + Queen's Royal Park | Ricardo St (free) or a Heritage District meter | **1** ($0-23) |

Old Town is compact enough that this genuinely works: **Queen's Royal Park is about two
blocks from Queen Street**. Park once at the north end and every evening stop is on foot,
which is what the plan already assumed.

### Day total
| Scenario | Cost |
|---|---|
| Glen receipt + free Ricardo St | **~$24** |
| Glen receipt + metered Queen St 3:45-8 PM | **~$47** |
| Worst case, paying each lot separately | ~$59 |
