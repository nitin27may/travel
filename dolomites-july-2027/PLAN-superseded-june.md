# SUPERSEDED — see PLAN.md (dates moved to 13–26 July 2027)

13 nights, 14 days on the ground. Toronto (YYZ) → Venice (VCE) nonstop, rental car.
Four bases: Alta Pusteria (5) → Cortina (4) → Val Gardena (3) → Bolzano (1).

**Year assumed 2027** (26 June 2026 is past). Confirm before booking anything.

---

## Scope verdict

**14 days is well matched to the Dolomites core — it is not loose.** Three distinct
sub-ranges (Sesto/Pusteria, Ampezzo/Falzarego, Gardena/Siusi) each hold 6–8 days of
genuine material. Fourteen days covers each properly with two soft days and no
day that requires more than ~1h30 of driving.

**Do not spend nights of this trip going south.** Every southern night is taken from
an already-full core. See "If you can add nights" below.

## What changed from a naive plan

1. **Maratona dles Dolomites is Sunday 4 July 2027 — officially confirmed.** Roads shut
   from 04:45; Corvara/La Villa/San Cassiano/Valparola do not reopen until 16:00–17:00.
   The 4th is planned as a no-car day inside Cortina.
2. **Lago di Braies is free and unrestricted until 30 June.** The car ban (09:00–16:00,
   mandatory €40 prepaid booking) starts 1 July. Braies is therefore day 2, not later.
3. **Rifugio Locatelli opened 28 June in 2025.** Tre Cime is scheduled for 29 June, not
   day 1 or 2.
4. **Fedare chairlift (Passo Giau) does not open until 13 July** — the standard lift
   approach to Nuvolau/Averau is unavailable. Start from Bai de Dones instead.
   **Faloria opens 11 July** — unavailable all trip.
5. **Seceda's famous view is not at the top station** — it is further along the ridge
   toward the Fermeda towers. Seceda now needs a pre-booked 30-min slot, costs €74
   return plus a €5/person ridge turnstile, and is excluded from DolomitiSuperSummer.
6. **Passo Gardena may close to non-resident cars for the 2027 season** (a Sept–Oct 2026
   pilot was explicitly a trial for it). The Cortina→Gardena transfer has two backups.
7. **Alpe di Siusi is done by cable car, not car** — €30 return, free base parking,
   sidesteps both the 09:00–17:00 driving ban and the €30 pre-booked P2 slot.
8. **Last night is Bolzano, not Ortisei** — takes an hour off the airport drive and adds Ötzi.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Sat 26 Jun. Venice → Alta Pusteria
VCE → San Candido, ~2h30 / 195 km via A27 then SS51. No pass driving on arrival day.
On-route stops: Pieve di Cadore; **Lago di Landro** roadside (head-on Cristallo).
Arrive ~16:00. **Groceries today.** Evening: Lago di Dobbiaco boardwalk (3.5 km flat)
or San Candido old town and its Romanesque collegiate church.
Watch for: Lavaredo Ultra Trail ran 24–27 June in 2026 around Cortina — verify 2027.

### Day 2 — Sun 27 Jun. Lago di Braies at first light + Val Fiscalina
04:00 alarm. Dobbiaco → Braies 20 min. In position 04:45; sunrise 05:21.
**Alpenglow hits the Seekofel 15–20 min before sunrise.** No booking, no fee, no ban.
Boats are moored at the boathouse only before 08:00. Lake loop 3.7 km, ~1.5 h.
Boat rental (La Palafitta) €20 shared / €55 private per 45 min, 08:00–18:00 from 20 June,
walk-up only — queues reach 2 h later in the day.
Afternoon: **Val Fiscalina** — cars restricted mid-June to early Oct; shuttle 440/446, or
park Fischleinboden (€8–18). Flat 20–30 min to Talschlusshütte under the Croda dei Toni.

### Day 3 — Mon 28 Jun. Prato Piazza + Monte Specie
Toll road from Ponticello/Brückele. **Hard cap of 100 cars; 10:00–15:00 the valley is
public-transport or pre-booking only** — be up before 10:00 or take the shuttle.
**Monte Specie / Strudelkopf, 2,307 m** — ~350 m, 3–4 h round trip, easy-moderate, no cables.
Payoff: the Tre Cime north faces seen from the *west*, a different angle from the Auronzo side.
Plateau wildflowers at peak.
Rain plan: MMM Ripa in Brunico castle (closed Tuesdays, so Monday works) or Cron4 spa.

### Day 4 — Tue 29 Jun. Tre Cime di Lavaredo
**Book the toll slot 30 days ahead.** €40/car, 12 h validity from the booked slot.
Loop 9.5–10.5 km, 400–480 m, 3.5–4.5 h, wide gravel, no exposure.
**Decision rule** — settled forecast: take a 12:00 slot and walk clockwise, reaching
Rifugio Locatelli mid-afternoon when the north faces are lit and the 09:00–12:00 crush
has cleared. Unsettled: earliest slot, off the loop by 14:00.
Side trip: **Cadini di Misurina viewpoint**, 30–40 min out on trail 117 — an exposed rocky
spur ending at a drop-off. Do not continue onto the Bonacossa; it becomes via ferrata.
Confirm Locatelli is open (it opened 28 June in 2025). Cash only. Auronzo/Lavaredo are safer.

### Day 5 — Wed 30 Jun. Drava cycle path into Austria
Papin Sport, San Candido (at the station). Your dates fall in the cheap band ending 26 July:
trekking €25/day, e-bike €50, helmet €2, **one-way drop €7**.
**San Candido → Lienz: 43.4 km, +15 m / −517 m, ~2h50.** Cross at Prato alla Drava.
Drop the bike at Papin's Lienz station and take the train back as a foot passenger
(cross-border, so buy the combined bike+person ticket at Lienz — südtirolmobil fares don't apply).
Deliberate recovery day between Tre Cime and the Cortina block.
Rain plan: Acquafun San Candido, €16 pool, 13:00–20:30 (closed Tuesdays, so Wednesday works).

### Day 6 — Thu 1 Jul. Transfer to Cortina + Monte Piana
San Candido → Cortina ~45 min. En route: **Monte Piana** from Lago d'Antorno —
9.2 km, ~600 m, 4–5 h, or take the jeep shuttle from Misurina. The whole summit plateau is
a WWI open-air museum: restored trenches, tunnels, gun emplacements, from a battle with
~14,000 casualties. Small headlamp useful.
Note: Malga Ra Stua's access road closes to private cars today for the season.

### Day 7 — Fri 2 Jul. Lago di Sorapis
**05:30 departure.** The Passo Tre Croci lot holds ~20 cars and fills by 06:00–06:30.
11–12.6 km, 460–630 m, 4.5–6 h. Only ~114 m net to the lake — it is a traverse, not a climb.
Path 215 has metal ladders, boards and cabled ledges; the **Cengia del Banco** is a genuine
drop and is bad for vertigo. Do not take the 216 descent as a loop — steep rubble, exposed
ledge, no cables.
Water saturates turquoise 09:00–11:00, which is also peak crowd; reviewers report passing
~900 descending hikers and 10-minute waits where the path is too narrow to pass.
Rifugio Vandelli serves lunch 11:30–14:30, **cash only**.
Afternoon: rest in Cortina.

### Day 8 — Sat 3 Jul. The Falzarego road — Lagazuoi, Cinque Torri, Limides
Everything today is within 10 km on one road, and **Falzarego parking is free all summer**.

**Morning — Lagazuoi tunnels (Percorso 6).** Cable car 2min50, 630 m, first up 09:00.
Descend 2,752 → 2,105 m, ~647 m over ~3 km, 1–1.5 h in the tunnels plus 30–45 min out.
**Headlamp mandatory** — it is pitch black. Helmet recommended. Cold, damp, steep rock-cut
stairs with a cable handrail. Not for claustrophobia or serious vertigo. The payoff is
*inside* the mountain, mid-descent.

**Afternoon — Cinque Torri.** Chairlift from Bai de Dones (open since 6 June, ~€27.50–30
return). The WWI open-air trench museum is flat and free at the top — the best
history-for-no-effort site in the range. For the fit: continue to Rifugio Averau
(~45 min) and Nuvolau. Book Averau, +39 0436 4660.

**Sunset — Lago di Limides.** 20–30 min off the Falzarego road, a small tarn reflecting
Tofana di Rozes. It dries out later in summer, so early July is the better window.
Needs dead calm. Sunset 21:08, colour to ~21:35 — headlamp for the walk out.

**Groceries today** — Sunday is both a retail closure and the Maratona.

### Day 9 — Sun 4 Jul. MARATONA DAY — do not drive
Official closures: SS48 Pocol–Passo Falzarego shuts 09:40, reopens **15:10**.
SS638 Selva–Passo Giau–Pocol shuts 09:00, reopens **14:00**. Everything west and south
of Cortina is sealed most of the day; Cortina↔Alta Badia is severed entirely.

**Plan: a no-car day in Cortina.** Freccia nel Cielo from the town centre to
**Cima Tofana, 3,244 m** — €45 return, running since 13 June, 09:00–17:00, last ascent
to the summit 16:50. Dogs ride free but need a muzzle. Highest lift-served point of the trip.
Then Corso Italia, lunch in town, and the race atmosphere without the traffic.
Altitude note: valley to 3,244 m in minutes. Expect 20–30 min of breathlessness. Dress warm —
it can be near freezing up there while Cortina is 28°C.

### Day 10 — Mon 5 Jul. Cortina → Val Gardena, the scenic way
~1h30 via Falzarego → Valparola → San Cassiano → Corvara → Passo Gardena.
**Verify Passo Gardena first.** A pilot from 1 Sept 2026 capped non-resident day-trippers at
150 pre-booked slots and was described as a trial for a full 2027 seasonal closure.
Backups: Campolongo–Pordoi–Sella (~2h), or north via Brunico and Bressanone (~2h).
Stop: **La Val / La Valle** — European Hiking Village, 1,300 m, centuries-old *viles* hamlets,
and the **Armentara meadows** beneath Sas dla Crusc at peak wildflower. Almost nobody goes.
Optional lunch: **Ütia de Bioch**, 30 min on foot from Piz Sorega, 08:30–17:30 — Josper grill,
1,700 wine labels. Boards €19.50, tortelli €20.50, strudel ~€7.50.

### Day 11 — Tue 6 Jul. Alpe di Siusi
**Cable car from Siusi/Seis — not the car.** €30 return, 08:00–19:00, free base parking,
15 min up. Sidesteps the 09:00–17:00 driving ban and the €30 pre-booked P2 slot entirely.
Europe's largest high alpine pasture, at peak bloom — gentians, orchids, alpine roses,
mid-June to mid-July. By August the meadows are cut.
Choose by appetite: easy 6.8 km rolling loop · **Bullaccia/Puflatsch and the Witches' Benches**
(4.8 km, 152 m, 1.5–2.5 h) · **Forcella Denti di Terrarossa** 2,499 m under the Rosszähne
spires (6–8 h, ~1,000 m).
Do not confuse the Terrarossa walk with **Via Ferrata Maximilian** on the ridge above —
serious exposure and long unprotected scrambling despite its "beginner" billing.

### Day 12 — Wed 7 Jul. Seceda, then Val di Funes at sunset
**Seceda requires a pre-booked 30-min slot**, non-refundable. €74 return / €49 one-way,
plus **€5 per person** at the ridge turnstile. **Not covered by DolomitiSuperSummer** —
only the Gardena Card includes it.
Cheaper and quieter: **Col Raiser from Santa Cristina, €34 return**, then ~1 h uphill.
**The famous photograph is not at the top station** — walk the ridge toward the Fermeda
towers. A 100–400 mm telephoto is the lens that matters, not a wide angle.
Meadows are off-limits and rangers enforce. Drones illegal in the nature park, fines €3,000+.
Lifts run 08:30–17:30 — too late for sunrise, too early for sunset. The ridge is empty at
both ends of the day, but only on foot.

Late afternoon: **Val di Funes** (~40 min). Barriers block non-residents from driving to
Santa Maddalena; park in the village and walk 20–30 min to the panorama viewpoint.
**Santa Maddalena wants sunset** — 70–200 mm to compress church against the Odle wall.
**San Giovanni in Ranui** sits on private Ranuihof land with a designated paid viewing point.
The exact rule and fee could not be pinned down — call +39 339 604 46 85 first.

### Day 13 — Thu 8 Jul. Adolf Munkel Weg, then Bolzano
Morning: **Adolf Munkel Weg** under the Odle/Geisler wall — 7.5–9 km, ~400–420 m, ~3 h,
zero exposure. The payoff is the entire trail: continuous close-up of Sass Rigais and the
Furchetta above meadow and larch. Lunch at Geisler Alm / Rifugio Odle at the midpoint.
Zanser Alm parking runs on a **traffic light** — green means drive up and pay on arrival,
red means pre-booked only. If red, park at Ranui or in the village and take **Bus 330**.
Afternoon: **Bolzano** (~50 min). **Ötzi**, South Tyrol Museum of Archaeology — €13,
10:00–18:00, last entry 17:30, open daily in July. Reservations recommended.
Evening: Piazza Erbe and the arcades. Cantina Bolzano is in the city — Italy's first
climate-certified cellar.
Bolzano has an active ZTL. Park in a garage and walk.

### Day 14 — Fri 9 Jul. Bolzano → Venice
2h40 / ~280 km, A22 then A4. Tolls ~€20 at roughly €0.07/km. Pay by card in the blue
"Carte" lanes; **never enter a yellow Telepass lane.** Fill up before the airport.

## If you can add nights

**Add them at the front, not the back.** San Martino di Castrozza is 2h20 from Venice —
the closest Dolomite base to VCE — so a 3-night southern leg costs no extra transfer,
gives a gentler-altitude first day for jet lag, and is dramatically less crowded.

What it buys: **Val Venegia and Baita Segantini** (Cimon della Pala reflected; stroller-easy
from Pian dei Casoni) · the **Rosetta cable car** onto the lunar Pale plateau (lifts open
20 June, €34 combined return, closed 13:00–13:40) · **Val Canali and Rifugio Treviso**
under Sass Maor · **Laghi di Colbricon** with WWI fortifications and Mesolithic finds.

Caveats: the Val Venegia road is closed to private cars 15 June–15 Sep and the 2026 shuttle
started 27 June — verify the 2027 start date. The Pale plateau at ~2,700 m carries the
highest late-June snow risk in the southern Dolomites; confirm with Rifugio Rosetta.

**If you would rather stay in the core**, +2 nights in Val Gardena is the better buy —
it recovers Sassolungo, Puez and Piz Boè.

**Do not day-trip Venice.** ~6 hours of driving for ~6 hours in a hot, crowded city that now
charges a day-visitor access fee. Attach it as an overnight at one end or skip it.

**Not extensions, different trips:** Brenta Dolomites / Madonna di Campiglio, Lake Garda,
Trento (2–2.5 h from Cortina). Also skip Lago di Resia's sunken bell tower — 2.5–3 h each
way, and the tourism board's own copy calls it hype.

## Packing that is not obvious

Microspikes · boots with a heel welt (not trail runners) · headlamp — mandatory for the
Lagazuoi tunnels and for walking out after a 21:35 sunset · waterproof jacket **and** pants ·
warm hat and gloves for dawn at 2,500 m, where it is near freezing in July · polarizer for
Braies, Sorapis and Carezza · 100–400 mm telephoto for Seceda and Santa Maddalena ·
cash for rifugi (many are cash-only and card terminals fail on connectivity) ·
a TIM-leaning Italian eSIM (~USD 33 for 30 days vs ~CAD 230 roaming); coverage is ~80%
while hiking, with dead zones in inner Val Fiscalina, the Fanes interior, the Sorapis
traverse and the north side of the Tre Cime loop. Download offline maps.
Emergency: **112**, works with no SIM. Confirm your insurance explicitly covers
helicopter mountain rescue and repatriation — many policies exclude both.
