# Dolomites 2027 — Research Lane: ADVENTURE & ACTIVITIES

Researched 29 Aug 2026. All season dates and prices below are **2026 figures — re-verify for
2027 in spring.** Bases for reference: San Candido 13–16 Jul, Cortina 17–20 Jul,
Ortisei/Selva 21–24 Jul, Bolzano 25 Jul.

The existing lanes cover trails, rifugi, lifts and transport well. This lane exists because
they were thin on the thing that actually decides who in a group can do an activity: the
**hard limits — age, height, weight — plus price and booking channel.**

---

## 1. GUIDED VIA FERRATA — the headline activity, and the pricing model is unusual

Source: https://www.guidedolomiti.com/en/rates/ (Cortina + Alta Badia mountain guides)

**The guide fee is per GROUP, not per person, and is split among participants.**
That inverts the usual budgeting: a solo climber pays the whole thing, four people pay a
quarter each. Worth knowing before assuming a per-head price.

| Item | 2026 rate |
|---|---|
| **Via ferrata, guided** | **€380–500 per group**, divided among participants |
| Max group, medium/difficult ferrata | **3–4 per guide** |
| Max group, very easy ferrata | **5–6 per guide** |
| Guided day hike | €380 up to 5 participants |
| Multi-day trek | €420/day |

- **Technical equipment — harness, helmet, via ferrata set — is INCLUDED in the guide fee.**
- **Lift and cable-car tickets are NOT included.** Budget separately; several ferrata approaches
  start from a paid lift.
- **Prices exclude 5% VAT.**
- **GAP: minimum age.** The rates page does not state one. Ask when booking — this is the single
  most important unknown in this lane.

**Self-guided alternative:** full kit — harness, helmet, via ferrata set — rents for
**€25–35/day** from sports shops in Cortina. Only sensible for people already competent on
cables; the existing `research-trails.md` is explicit that several routes here are EEA-graded.

Other guide services: https://www.altabadiaguides.com/en/via-ferrata-dolomites/ ,
https://www.internationalalpineguides.com/dolomites-italy-via-ferrata

## 2. FUNBOB, MONTE BARANCI — San Candido, i.e. base 1 (FULLY VERIFIED)

Source: https://www.dreizinnen.com/en/the-cable-cars/the-cable-cars/funbob

**The longest rail toboggan run in South Tyrol, and it is at your first base.**
1,739 m of track, **314 m vertical drop, max 40 km/h**, 18.5% average gradient. New
"Mountain Coaster 2.0" sleds from the 2025/26 season. Four-seater chairlift from San Candido
village up to 1,500 m.

| | Adult | Junior (<18) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ride | **€21.50** | €15.00 |
| 3 rides | €48.50 | €34.00 |
| 5 rides | €66.50 | €46.50 |
| Unlimited, mornings | €60.00 | €43.50 |

- **Season 23 May – 18 Oct 2026.** Hours in the 21 Jun – 30 Aug window: **09:00–18:30** —
  which covers the 13–16 Jul stay.
- **Age: under 3 not permitted. Under 8 must ride with someone 18+. From 8 they ride alone
  (ID required).**
- **GAP: height limit, and whether the chairlift is priced separately from the ride ticket.**

## 3. PARAGLIDING — Gardenafly, Ortisei / Seceda (base 3)

Sources: https://www.valgardena.it/en/addresses/detail/base/poi/ortisei-val-gardena/paragliding-gardenafly/E5AD3BDBAE4449AB89A7C7AA153D0A65/ ,
https://www.val-gardena.net/en/paragliding.asp

- Flying areas include **Seceda**, which is the signature Val Gardena view.
- Three products: **Panoramic, Thermal, Superior.**
- **Age from 4, with no upper age limit.**
- Tel **+39 392 1286600**. Booking site: www.paragliding-flights-dolomites.com
- **GAP: price in euro, weight limits, and the weather-cancellation terms.** Call before the trip.

Cortina also has certified tandem operators (confirmed by cortinadampezzo.it) but no
fetchable price page — **GAP**.

## 4. RAFTING & CANYONING — Val Pusteria, reachable from San Candido

Sources: https://www.pustertal.org/en/leisure-activities/sport-offers/rafting/ ,
https://www.rafting-club-activ.com/en/touren/

- **Rienza river (Val Pusteria): minimum age 6.**
- **Isarco / Eisack river, advanced grade: minimum age 14.**
- Operator: **Rafting Club Aktiv**, Valle Aurina — info@rafting-club-activ.com. Also
  Dolomit Activ, info@dolomitactiv.it
- **GAP: all prices, durations and cancellation terms.**

## 5. CORTINA — lifts, bike park, no zipline found

Sources: https://www.freccianelcielo.com/en/what-to-do-in-summer/summer-hours-and-prices/ ,
https://cortinadampezzo.it/en/news-en/summer-lift-openings/

- **Cortina–Col Drusciè gondola and Col Drusciè–Ra Valles cable car: 13 Jun – 29 Sep 2026,
  09:00–17:00.** Covers the 17–20 Jul stay.
- **Cortina Bike Park Dolomiti: 31 km of track, 4,000 m of vertical, 13 Jun – 29 Sep 2026.**
- **No zipline could be found in Cortina.** Searched repeatedly; the region's adrenaline
  offering is via ferrata, bike park and paragliding rather than ziplines. Do not assume one
  exists.

## 6. ALPINE COASTER GARDONÈ — verified, but OFF-ROUTE

Source: https://obereggen.com/en/ropeways/summer/alpine_coaster_gardone

980 m circuit, 28.3% max drop, includes a full spin. **€6.00 adult, €4.50 child (8–17).**
Season opens 13 Jun 2026.
**Height/age: minimum 105 cm. Between 105–135 cm, or under 8, must be accompanied by
someone 18+. Over 135 cm or aged 9–14, accompanied by someone 14+.**

**Caveat: Gardonè is at Obereggen/Latemar, south of Bolzano — off every base except possibly
the single Bolzano night on 25 Jul, and even then it is a detour.** The Funbob at San Candido
is longer, closer and already in the itinerary's first block. Listed only so it is a known
option, not a recommendation.

booking, every time.
