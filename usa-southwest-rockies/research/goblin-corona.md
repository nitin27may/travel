## Summary
Goblin Valley is a 12-mile paved dead-end spur off UT-24 (junction about 24 mi south of I-70, about 20 mi north of Hanksville). It lies on the direct Torrey->I-70 route, so the detour is only the 24-mile round trip plus visit time. Day-use is $20 per vehicle. Trails are short (Three Sisters 250 yd, Carmel Canyon loop 1.5 mi), with no shade. Corona Arch is a free BLM hike: 3 mi round trip, about 2 hours, 250-440 ft gain (sources disagree), with a railroad crossing, two cables, steps and one short ladder. The trailhead is about 14 mi from Moab on UT-279. Several requested items (2026-27 closures, cell coverage, ATB pass validity, official drive times) were not verifiable from official pages; they are listed as GAP.

## Key Findings

### A) Goblin Valley State Park
- FINDING: Route. From UT-24, turn at the signed Temple Mountain junction and follow paved road about 12 mi to the park. The junction is 24 mi south of I-70 (Green River side) and about 20 mi north of Hanksville. The route is paved the entire length; if GPS sends you onto dirt, it is the wrong route. Roads: Temple Mountain Road from UT-24, then left onto Goblin Valley Road.
  SOURCE: https://www.utah.com/locations/state-parks/goblin-valley-state-park/ (24 mi + 12 mi paved); https://wanderlustphotosblog.com/2022/04/20/the-ultimate-goblin-valley-state-park-guide-for-visitors-to-southern-utah/ (via search summary: road names, Hanksville 20 mi, "paved entire length, dirt = wrong route"; community source, not official)
- FINDING: Extra distance. Because the route Torrey -> Hanksville -> UT-24 north -> I-70 already passes the junction, the detour is 12 mi in + 12 mi out = about 24 mi extra (derived from the 12 mi figure above). The park does not connect north to I-70 by paved road; you return to UT-24. (No official source describes a paved northern link; GAP: absence not confirmed by official directions page, which returned 404.)

- FINDING: Trails (official). Easy: Three Sisters 250 yd one-way (iconic formation); Curtis Bench 1.5 mi round trip. Moderate: Entrada Canyon 1.5 mi one-way; Carmel Canyon Loop 1.5 mi round trip ("colorful narrows", rock scrambling required). Strenuous: Toadstool Canyon 2.7 mi loop (scrambling, rockfall possible); Goblin's Lair 1.5 mi one-way to an 89-ft slot canyon, additional scrambling. In km: 250 yd = 0.23 km; 1.5 mi = 2.4 km; 2.7 mi = 4.3 km; Goblin's Lair round trip 3 mi = 4.8 km. Valley of the Goblins is a free-roaming area of nearly 3 sq mi with three valleys; the Third Valley needs backcountry navigation/GPS.
  SOURCE: https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/goblin-valley/hiking-and-rappelling/
- FINDING: Climbing hoodoos. Official pages fetched do not state a rule. Community sources say climbing is allowed on sturdy goblins (5-15 ft), and the park brochure asks visitors not to climb fragile hoodoos or disturb cryptobiotic soil. Confirm at the visitor center.
  SOURCE: https://www.familycantravel.com/goblin-valley-state-park-with-kids/ (community); brochure 2025 PDF https://stateparks.utah.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2025/06/Goblin-Valley-Brochure-2025-1.pdf (returned 404 to fetch; quote known only via search summary)
- FINDING: Flat terrain, no shade, full sun; a family did 1.3 mi in 90 min in the goblin valleys. Rattlesnakes and scorpions are mentioned. (community)
  SOURCE: https://www.familycantravel.com/goblin-valley-state-park-with-kids/
- FINDING: Water and facilities: water, restrooms, vault toilets, showers, picnic tables, gift shop. Official heat advisory: temps reach 90-100 F; carry at least 32 oz water per person per hour. Flash flood: check forecast at visitor center; avoid canyons and washes when rain is possible.
  SOURCE: https://stateparks.utah.gov/parks/goblin-valley/
- FINDING: International Dark Sky Park; disc golf is free.

### B) Corona Arch
- FINDING: Trailhead is on the north side of UT-279 (Potash Road), about 10 mi south of the US-191 junction, which is 4 mi north of Moab; BLM gives about 14 mi from Moab total. Fee: none. Roped activities prohibited. About 80,000 hikers a year.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/corona-arch-trail
- FINDING: Distance 3 mi round trip (4.8 km), "moderately strenuous" per BLM. Allow at least 2 hours (Discover Moab). Elevation gain: 440 ft (Discover Moab) vs 250 ft (Visit Utah); blog sources say 482 ft. GAP: no single authoritative figure; use about 130-150 m.
  SOURCE: https://www.discovermoab.com/places-to-go/outside-the-parks/corona-arch/ ; https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/hiking/moab-hiking/corona-arch
- FINDING: Trail features: crosses railroad tracks early; old roadbed through a rim gap, then a wash with cairns; two safety cables, steps cut into slickrock, one short ladder over a ledge; "mild exposure to heights"; final stretch is an easy walk on a broad slickrock bench. Passes Pinto Arch and Bowtie Arch before Corona (opening 140 ft wide x 105 ft high). Visit Utah warns of drop-offs, especially with children.
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/corona-arch-trail ; https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/hiking/moab-hiking/corona-arch
- FINDING: Timing: sun all day; Visit Utah advises doing the hike first thing in the morning in warm months; Discover Moab says early morning or early evening. Trailhead GPS 38 34'28.0"N 109 37'56.5"W.
  SOURCE: https://www.visitutah.com/things-to-do/hiking/moab-hiking/corona-arch ; https://www.discovermoab.com/places-to-go/outside-the-parks/corona-arch/
- FINDING: Along Potash Road: a paved pull-out on the river side has a petroglyph panel (about 25 ft up the wall, about 125 ft long), and Goldbar Arch is visible en route. Jug Handle Arch: GAP, not confirmed in an official source. (petroglyph detail is community source)
  SOURCE: https://www.allmoab.com/trails/corona_arch_trail.php (via search summary); https://www.discovermoab.com/places-to-go/outside-the-parks/corona-arch/
- FINDING: BLM page showed no alerts or closures at fetch time (2026-09-24).
  SOURCE: https://www.blm.gov/visit/corona-arch-trail
