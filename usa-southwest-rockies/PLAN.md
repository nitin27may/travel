# Utah's five national parks — 11 days

**10 nights · ~800 mi / 1,300 km road · 5 bases · Zion, Bryce, Capitol Reef, Arches, Canyonlands**
**YYZ → Las Vegas in, Salt Lake City → YYZ out.** 2 adults + 1 child (11). Hiking ceiling ~12 km
round trip. **Dates open.** Every leg is paved.

> Revised 2026-09-24 from the 15-day Utah + Arizona plan, which is kept in
> `PLAN-15day-superseded.md` (old page in `archive/`). Cut: Page, Antelope Canyon, Horseshoe Bend,
> Monument Valley and the Hanksville badlands (4 nights). Added: Scenic Byway 12 with Lower Calf
> Creek Falls as the Bryce → Torrey route.

## How the split was decided

No official source publishes a recommended number of days for any park. The split comes from NPS
hour and distance figures (`research-days-official.md`), about a dozen trip reports
(`research-days-community.md`) and leg times (`research-days-route.md`).

- **Zion 3 nights** — Utah.com says 3 days / 2 nights; community says 2 is mainstream and 3 only
  if the Narrows is a firm goal. It is, so 3.
- **Bryce 1 night** — near-universal in every source; NPS gives hour bands, not days.
- **Capitol Reef 2 nights** — the park people most often wish they had given more; nobody regretted
  the second night. Official trail list fills about 1.5 days.
- **Moab 3 nights** — modal split. On official figures Arches is ~1 day and Island in the Sky ~0.5,
  so **2 nights would work and make this a 10-day trip**; kept at 3 because Mesa Arch at dawn and
  Dead Horse at sunset cannot share a day comfortably.

## Optional stops (2026-09-24, `research-goblin-corona.md`)

Goblin Valley (day 7) and Corona Arch (day 10) are **optional**, not part of the core route; neither adds a night.
Goblin Valley is a state park: America the Beautiful pass probably not valid. Corona Arch elevation gain differs by
source (250–482 ft). Skip either if the group is tired or it is hot.

## What was cut, honestly

Antelope Canyon, Horseshoe Bend and Monument Valley are among the most photographed places in the
Southwest. What they cost: 4 nights, two Navajo/Arizona clock changes, the trip's hardest sell-out
(The View Hotel) and a Navajo-time booking. A Page-only add-on would cost the Highway 12 day, so it
is a swap, not an addition.

## Dates

Second half of September is best. **The one date that bites is 1 October** (Narrows dry-suit cold).
Nothing else in the research pins the dates. Also workable: mid-May to early June. Avoid Jul–Aug.

## Assumptions

1. **Scout Lookout, not Angels Landing** — but enter the 1–20 July lottery anyway.
2. **UT-12, not a retrace** — Lower Calf Creek is optional.
3. **Standard SUV is enough** — AWD was needed only for the Moki Dugway and Hanksville dirt.
4. **Bryce: Queen's Garden out-and-back** — Navajo Loop closed since Feb 2026.
5. **One $250 non-resident America the Beautiful pass.** Child exempt from the +$100 surcharge.
   Does not cover Dead Horse Point (state park) or Lower Calf Creek ($5/vehicle, BLM).
