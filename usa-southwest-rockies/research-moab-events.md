# Moab recurring fall events — Sept/Oct

Closes the "all Moab Sept/Oct event dates" gap logged in `research-hours-closures.md`.
**GAP CLOSED 2026-09-05.** Moab is a three-night base and the town books out entirely
around these; a collision means no rooms at any price.

Method limitation: the Discover Moab calendar is a JS/Simpleview widget whose REST endpoint
returns `Invalid credentials` and is unusable programmatically. The workable aggregator is
**moabhappenings.com** (server-rendered yearlong calendar). Prior-year patterns were derived
from Wayback snapshots. City of Moab / Grand County CivicEngage feeds are short-horizon and
near-empty — **not** usable as a permit register.

## The Moab 240 is the one to never book into
240-mile point-to-point; every runner brings a multi-person crew occupying rooms and RV
sites for several nights across a wide radius. **8–12 Oct 2027 is confirmed** and is the
worst lodging week of the Moab autumn.

## Verdict against both windows
- **Window A** — Moab on trip days 9–11 = **28–30 Sep 2027: clear.** Arts festival 24–26 Sep
  (we're in Page/Monument Valley), Outerbike projects 1–3 Oct (we leave 30 Sep), Moab 240
  8–12 Oct. Clears everything, but only by a day either side of Outerbike.
- **Window B** — Moab days 9–11 = **18–20 Sep 2028**: Skinny Tire Fall Festival projects
  ~16–18 Sep, clipping the first night. Minor pressure, not a blocker.
- **Neither window is badly hit. This lane does not decide A vs B.**

## Confirmed DEFUNCT — do not re-research
- **24 Hours of Moab** — organizer domain is now a squatted gambling site; absent from the
  2026 yearlong calendar.
- **Rally on the Rocks** — domain squatted; was a May event regardless.
- **The Other Half** half marathon — `moabhalfmarathon.com` now redirects to Mad Moose,
  which has no such page. Canyonlands Ultra took its late-Oct slot.
- **Moab Century Tour** — explicitly retired, replaced by Skinny Tire Fall Festival.
- **There is NO fall Jeep Safari.** Easter Jeep Safari is March (20–28 Mar 2027). The fall
  4x4 events are the Jeep Jamboree and the Ho-Down, both late October.
