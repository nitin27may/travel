## 6. Border / ID / entry requirements
- France is Schengen; Switzerland is not EU but in Schengen; Italy Schengen. (General knowledge, no URL retrieved.) Carry passport; Canadians need no visa for stays up to 90 days in 180.
- EES (biometric Entry/Exit System): fully operational at all external border crossings from 10 Apr 2026. | https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/en/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/ees-le-nouveau-systeme-europeen-de-gestion-des-frontieres-entre-en-service-le-10-avril-2026
- ETIAS: earlier sources said launch Q4 2026; a July 2026 report (credited to The Points Guy) says the EU delayed ETIAS to 2027 with no firm date, citing EES technical problems. Fee EUR 20 (previously planned EUR 7); valid 3 years or until passport expires; stays up to 90 in 180 days; Canada is on the visa-exempt list covered. Possible fee exemption under 18/over 70 came from an Australian source (unverified for Canadians). | https://insideflyer.com/posts/eu-postpones-etias-travel-authorization-to-2027/ ; https://www.diplomatie.gouv.fr/en/presse-et-ressources/decouvrir-et-informer/actualites/ees-le-nouveau-systeme-europeen-de-gestion-des-frontieres-entre-en-service-le-10-avril-2026 (Q4 2026 claim, now stale) ; https://thepointsguy.com/guide/europe-travel-visa-etias-what-to-know
