### Chamonix centre (~1,035 m INFER)
- Car-free: partly (pedestrianised core, but cars allowed around; covered car parks above). Access: train/bus/car; Chamonix Sud bus station and SNCF station.
- Noise: INFER train line runs along the east/southern edge of centre and through the valley, N205/D1506 road traffic; no sourced noise data. Search returned a single review that a central hotel away from main street traffic was calm (https://www.powderhounds.com/europe/france/chamonix/accommodation.aspx).
- Day-tripper churn: high, evenings busy with restaurants and bars (powderhounds describes it as best for atmosphere/nightlife). Last train/bus see section above.
- Trade: best food, shops, buses, lifts to Aiguille du Midi and Brevent; most expensive, loudest, parking costly (EUR 19.90/24 h). Honest verdict (INFER): fine for 2-3 nights for logistics; not the best for a quiet week.

### Les Praz (~1,060 m INFER)
- Quiet residential hamlet at the Flegere base gondola; powderhounds mentions calm (https://www.powderhounds.com/europe/france/chamonix/accommodation.aspx). Station with TER stops (no service during autumn 2026 works). 15-20 min on foot to Chamonix centre (INFER). Trade: small, pleasant, golf/lake, little food choice; good base for Lac Blanc/Flegere.

### Argentiere (~1,250 m)
- Car-free: no. TER stop at 1,244 m and buses. Elevation sourced above. Near Grands Montets base (closed to lifts summer 2026, so check 2028); good for Col de Balme / Aiguille Rouge, Lognan walks. Camping Glacier d'Argentiere open 13 May-30 Sept 2026 (see prices). Trade: more local and quieter than Chamonix, steeper valley, sun lost earlier (INFER north-facing under the Aiguille Verte/Drus), grocery limited. Good for hikers (powderhounds: quieter, walkable).

### Les Bossons / Les Pelerins
- Les Pelerins: Super U supermarket, train stop (https://everyrail.com/sv/frankrike/chamonix/les-pelerins.md). Les Bossons: Camping Les Deux Glaciers (open 1 Jan-1 Nov 2026). Bossons glacier base. Bus every 30 min to 20:00 (see above). Trade: cheap lodging, close to the A40/N205 road and rail (INFER noise), closer to Mont-Blanc-facing slopes so later sun (INFER). Short walk-up to Chamonix is 2-3 km.

### Les Houches (~1,000 m INFER)
- Quiet at night per a resort guide, buses run all evening, spread out so lift access needs bus/car (https://www.alpineanswers.co.uk/ski-resorts/france/resort-guide-chamonix-les-houches). Listed as an affordable and last-minute availability option (powderhounds). Start/end point of the TMB; Bellevue cable car to 14 Sept 2025; Prarion. GAP: noise from railway/N205 per street. Trade: cheaper, calmer, 15 min to Chamonix, but evenings are the quiet.

### Vallorcine
- Named quietest base (powderhounds). Near Swiss border; Mont-Blanc Express terminus area (Le Chatelard-Frontiere next); Col des Montets, Aiguilles Rouges. Ulysse reports the Chalet du col des Montets reopened June 2026 for a 3-day Aiguilles Rouges loop without a car (https://ulysse.com/news/aiguilles-rouges-col-montets-rando-sans-voiture-2026). Trade: very quiet, little shopping, train and road shut by weather/works more often (INFER). GAP: last train; shops.

### Servoz
- GAP: nothing sourced. INFER: small village at the valley mouth, cheapest in the valley, on Mont-Blanc Express (free with carte d'hote), N205 passes nearby. Verify.

### Saint-Gervais / Le Fayet

## 5. TMB booking system

- Official platform: https://www.montourdumontblanc.com (run for the Association des Gardiens de Refuges et Gites du Tour du Mont-Blanc). Only member huts can be booked there; others must be contacted directly. Account, cart, "My bookings", terms of sale linked.
- Season opening: 2027 season opens 15 Oct 2026; ideal season mid-June to mid-September; July-August fill months ahead (https://www.montourdumontblanc.com/en). 2028 opening date: GAP, not published. Prepare for 15 Oct 2027 (INFER).

## 6. Recommended splits (INFER; reasoning, built on the facts above)

Common logic: Chamonix centre is the wrong place to sleep all week (noise, parking EUR 19.90/day, day-tripper evenings, early sun loss). Use a quiet hamlet as the main base, one hut night for height, and the Swiss/Italian sides only if time allows. Lifts shut from mid-September, so do lift-dependent days (Flegere, Brevent, Balme) in the first week. Book hut nights when the 2028 platform opens.

| Nights | Split | Reason |
