# Iceland — the Ring Road by camper van
**Saturday 4 – Wednesday 15 September 2027 · 11 nights · ~2,190 km · first van trip**

Open-jaw is not needed: KEF in, KEF out, one vehicle, one loop.
Flight out is the evening of **Friday 3 September** from Toronto — YYZ→KEF is a 5 h 35 m overnight
arriving between 06:00 and 09:00.

---

## What the research changed

**1. The dates moved, and the reason is arithmetic, not taste.**
I computed the sun angles for the whole route. **Iceland has no astronomical darkness at all before
about 3 September** — in mid-July it doesn't even reach the *end of civil twilight*. So an aurora is
physically impossible on a summer van trip, no matter how good the solar activity is. Meanwhile the
campsite network starts shutting during September and **the Camping Card expires on 15 September**.
Those two curves cross in a narrow band. This window sits in it.

**2. The van costs you the highlands, permanently.**
A 2WD camper is **not allowed on F-roads** — doing it voids the whole insurance policy — and **no
Icelandic insurer sells river-crossing cover at any price**. Landmannalaugar, Þórsmörk and Askja are
out. Not a scheduling problem; a vehicle problem. Accepted deliberately: those are not places to
learn what a van does.

**3. The van also costs you the thing vans are normally for.**
Sleeping outside a marked campsite is illegal for vehicles in Iceland. You cannot wake up at a
trailhead. **Every day below is built around where the campsite is**, which is the reverse of how the
other plans in this repo are ordered.

**4. Puffins are gone.** They leave from mid-August; operators stop selling puffin trips on 15 August.
Choosing the aurora means giving them up. There is no window that has both.

**5. Reynisfjara changed this year.** A landslide and severe erosion in February 2026 narrowed the
beach and largely destroyed one viewing platform. Access is now managed by safety zone and the cave
is closed whenever the red light flashes.

## Orientation — what Iceland physically is

Iceland is the only place on earth where a **mid-ocean ridge sits above sea level**. The
Mid-Atlantic Ridge runs the length of the island, and a mantle plume sits underneath it — the
combination is why there is land here at all rather than 2,500 m of water. The North American and
Eurasian plates are pulling apart at roughly 2 cm a year, and at Þingvellir you can walk down into
the resulting rift. Everything else follows from that: the volcanic fissures, the geothermal fields,
the black sand (basalt ground down by ice and sea), and the glaciers that sit *on top of* active
volcanoes, which is why Iceland has a word for a glacier-burst flood.

**Why it is famous, specifically:** not "waterfalls" — the combination. Nowhere else can you stand
between two continental plates in the morning, walk on a glacier tongue in the afternoon, and sleep
next to a lava field that was liquid within living memory.

**What you cannot get elsewhere:** Þingvellir is both the rift valley *and* the site of the Alþingi,
founded in 930 AD and the oldest surviving parliament in the world. And the geothermal pool culture —
every town has a heated outdoor pool with hot pots, open late, costing about a tenth of the tourist
lagoons. In a van, that is your daily shower and your evening out.

**Sub-regions covered:** Reykjanes & the Golden Circle · the South Coast · Vatnajökull and the
glacier lagoons · the East Fjords & Mývatn · Snæfellsnes and the west.

**What to know before the days start:**
- **Campsite-only sleeping.** Not negotiable, actively enforced.
- **Wind, not cold, is the hazard.** Never open a van door into it.
- **No F-roads.** The interior is off the table in this vehicle.
- **Get the heater.** Nights run 4–7 °C.
- **The Camping Card expires 15 September** — the trip is built to end inside it.
- **Petrol and food:** stock up at Bónus or Krónan in Selfoss on Day 1. Prices climb sharply once
  you leave the southwest.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Sat 4 Sep · KEF → Reykjanes → Selfoss · ~130 km
Land at dawn on aeroplane sleep. Deliberately the lightest day of the trip.
Collect the van, learn where everything is in the car park before driving anywhere.
- **Gunnuhver** — a geothermal field of boiling mud on the Reykjanes tip
- **Brimketill** — a lava rock pool the Atlantic breaks straight into
- **Bridge Between Continents** — literal, slightly silly, two minutes
- **Big shop at Bónus/Krónan, Selfoss.** Do this properly; it sets the food budget for 11 days.
- **Sleep: Selfoss.** Pool in town.
- *Volcano note:* Grindavík and the Blue Lagoon are open, but Svartsengi is inflating. Check
  **safetravel.is** the morning of, and treat the whole peninsula as a bonus, never a dependency.

### Day 2 — Sun 5 Sep · Golden Circle · ~160 km
- **Þingvellir** — walk Almannagjá, the rift wall. The Alþingi site is at the bottom.
- **Silfra snorkel** *(optional, book ahead)* — age 12+, 150–200 cm, 45–120 kg, medical form
  mandatory, competent swimmers only. See `research/route.md` for the disqualifying conditions.
- **Geysir / Strokkur** — Strokkur goes every 5–10 minutes; Geysir itself is dormant
- **Gullfoss** — two-tier, into a canyon. Upper and lower viewpoints are different pictures.
- **Kerið** — crater lake, small fee, ten minutes
- **Sleep: Laugarvatn.** Fontana geothermal baths on the lakeshore.

### Day 3 — Mon 6 Sep · South coast · ~150 km
- **Seljalandsfoss** — walk behind it. You will get soaked; waterproofs, not a jacket.
- **Gljúfrabúi** — 500 m along the same path, hidden in a cleft. Most people miss it.
- **Skógafoss** — 60 m. Climb the stairs for the top-down view.
- **Kvernufoss** — the quiet one behind the Skógar museum
- **Sólheimajökull glacier walk** *(optional, book ahead)* — easy 3 h tour from age 8 (~US$105),
  advanced 5 h from age 12 (~US$162)
- **Dyrhólaey** — the sea arch. Cliffs will be empty of puffins in September.
- **Reynisfjara** — **the dangerous one.** Stay high on the beach. Cave closed under the red light.
- **Sleep: Vík.** Krónan across the road from the campsite.

### Day 4 — Tue 7 Sep · Vík → Skaftafell · ~140 km
- **Fjaðrárgljúfur** — 100 m mossy canyon. Lower lot 1,000 ISK, upper free.
- **Eldhraun** — the moss-covered lava from the 1783 Laki eruption, which killed a fifth of
  Iceland's population and cooled European harvests
- **Svartifoss** — basalt columns like organ pipes. 5.4 km return / ~120 m, or 7.1 km / 310 m
  for the full Sjónarnípa circuit.
- **Falljökull glacier hike** *(optional, book ahead)* — Tröll base at Skaftafell, groups of ten,
  minimum age 12
- **Sleep: Skaftafell campsite**, inside the national park at the trailhead.

### Day 5 — Wed 8 Sep · The glacier lagoons → Höfn · ~130 km
- **Fjallsárlón** first — smaller, quieter, and you will have it nearly to yourself
- **Jökulsárlón** — the big lagoon. Boat trips run in September; book same-day if the weather holds.
- **Diamond Beach** — icebergs on black sand. Entirely dependent on what has washed up. If it is
  bare, it is bare; don't spend an hour waiting.
- **Stokksnes / Vestrahorn** — private land, small fee, and the best mountain photograph in Iceland
- **Sleep: Höfn.** Langoustine town. Pool.

### Day 6 — Thu 9 Sep · The East Fjords · ~270 km · long day
The transit day, and the one most itineraries sprint through. Don't.
- **Djúpivogur**, **Fáskrúðsfjörður** (the French fishing station), **Stöðvarfjörður** and Petra's
  extraordinary private stone collection
- Hairpin fjord roads, almost no traffic
- **Egilsstaðir** then over the pass to **Seyðisfjörður** — rainbow street, blue church
- **Sleep: Seyðisfjörður.** *Decision rule:* if the pass forecast is wild, stop at Egilsstaðir
  instead and see Seyðisfjörður on the morning of Day 7.

### Day 7 — Fri 10 Sep · North across the interior edge → Mývatn · ~260 km
- Route 1 climbs onto **Möðrudalsöræfi**, high open desert. This is the emptiest road on the trip.
- **Dettifoss** — the most powerful waterfall in Europe by volume. **Take road 862 (west, paved),
  not 864 (east, gravel)** in a camper.
- **Ásbyrgi** — a horseshoe canyon that Norse myth attributes to Odin's horse
- **Sleep: Mývatn** (Vogar or Hlíð). **First real aurora night: dark from ~22:11.**

### Day 8 — Sat 11 Sep · Mývatn · ~60 km · rest day
Deliberately low mileage after two long ones.
- **Hverir / Námafjall** — boiling mud, sulphur, no vegetation. Smells like it looks.
- **Dimmuborgir** — lava pillar labyrinth
- **Hverfjall** — crater rim walk, ~3 km around, 1.5–2 h total
- **Grjótagjá** — hot spring in a lava cave. Look, don't swim; it runs too hot.
- **Mývatn Nature Baths** in the evening — the Blue Lagoon without the queue or the price
- **Sleep: Mývatn.** Second aurora night.

### Day 9 — Sun 12 Sep · Húsavík → Akureyri · ~180 km
- **Húsavík whale watching** — humpback and minke in September. Book the morning sailing.
- **GeoSea** — clifftop seawater baths over the bay
- **Goðafoss** — right on Route 1, no walking, still excellent
- **Akureyri** — the north's real town. Botanical garden, proper restaurants, first
  laundry opportunity.
- **Sleep: Akureyri (Hamrar).** Best facilities on the route — showers, laundry, ~US$18/adult.

### Day 10 — Mon 13 Sep · The long west run · ~390 km · the big one
Five hours of driving. Break it deliberately.
- **Hvítserkur** — basalt sea stack that looks like a drinking animal
- **Hvammstangi** — seal colony
- **Hraunfossar** — a waterfall that seeps out of a lava field along a kilometre of cliff
- **Deildartunguhver** — Europe's highest-flow hot spring
- **Sleep: Grundarfjörður**, under Kirkjufell.

### Day 11 — Tue 14 Sep · Snæfellsnes → Reykjavík · ~260 km
"Iceland in miniature," and the highest value-per-hour on the whole loop.
- **Kirkjufell** and Kirkjufellsfoss
- **Djúpalónssandur** — black pebble beach with the 1948 trawler wreck still lying on it, and the
  four lifting stones fishermen were graded by
- **Snæfellsjökull** — the glacier-capped volcano Jules Verne used as the entrance to the earth
- **Arnarstapi → Hellnar** coastal walk, basalt arches and cliffs
- **Búðakirkja** — the black church on the lava field
- **Sleep: Reykjavík, Laugardalur.** Year-round site, next to the city's biggest pool, short hop to
  the van depot in the morning.

### Day 12 — Wed 15 Sep · Reykjavík → KEF → home · ~60 km
- **Hallgrímskirkja** tower for the coloured-roof view, Harpa, the old harbour
- **Sky Lagoon** if the flight is late enough — better designed than the Blue Lagoon and on the way
- Return the van with a **full tank**; refuel before the depot, not at it
- Fly home
